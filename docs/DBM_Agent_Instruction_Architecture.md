# Agent Instruction Architecture

Design basis for the D-GOV-41 four-role replacement. The runtime contract is
`AGENT_WORKFLOW_RUNTIME.md`; the component standard is
`WORKFLOW_COMPONENT_STANDARD.md`.

## Roles and methods

Chirality separates the way an agent contributes from the undertaking it
performs. HELP_HUMAN maintains the relationship and overall alignment.
HELPS_HUMANS develops understanding and design with the human. WORKING_ITEMS
organizes implementation and integrates its contributions. TASK executes one
bounded assignment.

Those roles can participate in many workflows. A workflow can compose many
instances, subject to the role hierarchy and effective permissions. Methods
retain their specialized knowledge, artifact contracts, decisions, and recovery
without each method requiring its own persistent agent persona.

## Four sections

PROTOCOL evokes characteristic conduct. SPEC supplies standards of judgment.
STRUCTURE expresses relationships and forms of contribution. RATIONALE explains
why the contribution matters. Together they provide a compact role instruction.
Runtime metadata, schemas, permission configuration, and method detail have
separate operational homes.

Ontology, epistemology, praxeology, and axiology describe complementary concerns:
what is being distinguished, how claims are warranted, how action is organized,
and what commitments give it direction. They inform the coherence of a design;
the four instruction headings serve a different, practical purpose.

## Reflection and design

HELPS_HUMANS makes the human's developing intention inspectable through inquiry
and concrete proposals. It can examine its own categories and respond to the
patterns the human's corrections reveal. The interpretation remains revisable;
recognition is not proof, and the human's commitments remain theirs.

This reflective contribution serves the conception of workflows, tools, and
projects. It develops a design sufficiently clear for WORKING_ITEMS to carry
into implementation and remains available when implementation changes the
question.

## Operational homes

The registry defines role eligibility and capability ceilings. Briefs bind
context, authority, scope, outputs, and checks to runs. Workflows provide methods
loaded when selected. Tools perform deterministic operations. Shared governance
remains in its governing documents and accepted amendments.

The human may engage either manager directly. The hierarchy coordinates
execution and responsibility; it does not prescribe a mandatory conversational
route for every undertaking.
