# Chirality App v4 thesis

This thesis examines an application environment for authoring workflows and making a general agent act within and for a host application. It shares the Root thesis's kernel, expressed
through its particular objects, operations and conditions of use.

[Shared conjecture and definitions](appendix_d_framework_s.md) preserve the
formal argument and its prose. The necessary meaning at issue is recognition
of the necessity of contingency; contingency means temporal dependence.
G remains undefined. The application account is grounded in inspectable
requirements, implementation and evidence.

This is explanatory research under the owner's direction, not a new product
contract, permission or acceptance act. Current requirements remain in the
[PRD](../PRD.md) and its adopted companions.

The [Root thesis](../../../../docs/thesis/README.md) develops the common framework.
The [frozen App foundation](../../foundation/thesis/README.md) retains its
historical source basis unchanged; this is the current App-specific thesis.
