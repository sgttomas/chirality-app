# Chirality App v4 thesis

## From General Capability to Situated Participation

**Why an application for making methods is the first exemplar of Chirality**

[Read the thesis](THESIS.md).

The App thesis asks why general agent capability needs a relation to an
application's objects, operations and consequences, and why workflow authoring
is a useful first expression of that relation. It argues from the purposes of
human–agent work, using App v4's design, implementation and observed failures
as evidence and counterexamples. It is not an expanded product specification.

The argument develops through conversation and method, meaningful perception
and action, human judgment, continuity, native supplier capability, application
re-derivation and transfer to domain applications. Its evidence distinguishes
requirements, inspected code, defined tests and observations of actual use.

The [shared conjecture](appendix_d_framework_s.md) preserves the formal argument
with shared definitions and revised interpretation. The necessary meaning at issue is S's recognition of the
necessity of contingency; contingency means temporal dependence. N is
existential affirmation, and G remains undefined. This is the shared origin of
the three theses, without requiring assent to the conjecture to examine their
practical arguments.

This is explanatory research under the owner's direction, not a new product
contract, permission, acceptance act or qualification claim. Current
requirements remain in the [PRD](../PRD.md) and its adopted companions.
Implementation evidence is bounded to repository revision
`a602024416ace9652f61bb2043889c307fd4e09d`; later development may change its
standing. No product tests were rerun to author this thesis.

The [Root thesis](../../../../docs/thesis/README.md) concerns the shared means
of development. The [SWBPIPE thesis](../../../chirality-piping/docs/thesis/README.md)
examines their particular engineering instantiation. The
[frozen App foundation](../../foundation/thesis/README.md) retains its
historical source basis unchanged; this directory is the current App-specific
thesis.
