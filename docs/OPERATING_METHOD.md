## 1. Direction and scope

D-12 originally proposed retaining the decomposition/execution structure and
guiding work graphs through the Project Management manual according to the
Agent User Manual. Later owner direction A/C makes the three manuals core
working governance. Accepted HTML decisions 01 and 07 select focused
responsibility/contract renewal and staged coexistence. U4 expressly permits
decomposition and project definition with open matters carried forward.

The human governs the undertaking. Root hosts shared ideas, methods and
tools; their location or historical application does not automatically give
them v4 authority. The accepted purpose, selected method, scoped brief and
actual host permissions guide the particular work. Historical handoff
instructions to seek acceptance of everything again are superseded at that
scope by U4; their records remain unchanged.

## 4. Feedback on the method

The owner's aim remains to try the practices, examine them in use and improve
through feedback (OD-08). Their prescribed practices and illustrative
examples do not prove efficiency or operation at the intended scale.

- **V4-OPS-20** When practice helps, does not fit or is ambiguous, record the
  useful observation at its work-graph node, citing the relevant manual
  section and observed conditions. Reuse the record that already carries the
  work; a separate report for every action is unnecessary.
- **V4-OPS-21** Bring consequential method observations to the applicable
  selected-method decision or stage discussion. Their treatment can be a
  proposed manual revision, a scoped departure or no change. Preserve who
  decided and what the decision actually covers.
- **V4-OPS-22** **[open method detail — PRD OQ-05]** Where selected guidance
  does not cover an issue, identify the consequence and governing scope.
  The earlier draft proposed recording the treatment and bringing
  consequential gaps to the human; it did not establish a universal silence
  policy. Resolve any missing criterion before relying on it, while
  independent authorized work continues. Do not infer that all estimation,
  scheduling or risk practice is absent from every selected method.
- **V4-OPS-23** **[open assignment detail — PRD OQ-05]** Manual revisions need
  an identified author, evidence, review and adoption scope. The earlier
  draft's assumption that the owner personally edits all revisions is not a
  settled authorship allocation. The human retains consequential direction
  and adoption; agents can prepare changes when separately authorized.

## 5. Proportion and continuity

- **V4-OPS-30** Actual host permissions and available isolation mechanisms
  determine enforced boundaries. A brief or role restriction may be
  instruction-asserted; independent review checks its observance. A worktree
  alone is not proof of a complete sandbox. Report actual capabilities and
  limits accurately (L-04).
- **V4-OPS-31** Keep records that serve a decision, reliance, examination or
  recovery. Git history and reviewed pull requests are primary change
  records; avoid duplicating them in parallel evidence systems. Preserve
  actual supplied basis and candidate identity where the work needs them.
- **V4-OPS-32** Give each maintained record a consumer or use, such as entry,
  a review or a selected stage decision. A record required to preserve a
  human act or historical evidence retains its purpose even when not read
  during every run.
- **V4-OPS-33** The owner has accepted a basis stable enough to proceed with
  decomposition and project definition, including open matters (U4). Carry
  each unresolved input with owner, consequence and point of need. Do not
  demand empirical proof of the whole product before conceptual definition;
  do not treat this acceptance as product implementation or release.
- **V4-OPS-34** Independently review identified candidates, with the original
  D-13 preference for a Codex reviewer and the stated different-model Claude
  fallback. Report actual reviewer separation and model identity when
  exposed; a fresh native context is not evidence of a different family.
  Source-fidelity review of this post-act consolidation checks its relation
  to the accepted composite, not a retroactive human review of these bytes.

Staged coexistence preserves complete thesis bytes and standing, historical
records, current work and continuing obligations. Keep current applicability
findable. Account for instruction packaging, tools, paths and consumers before
changing active shared material. The human's eventual fallback replacement
act does not automatically retire every old project or authenticate a
professional work product (B-HTML 07; PRD §8).

## 6. External coordination and increments

SWBPIPE implementation remains with the external session; the human relays
coordination and authorizes files as the medium (U1/U5). The App manager may
prepare shared/consumer contracts, questions and received-result records. It
must not claim a receiving commitment or delivery from its own draft, send
messages to the outside session on presumed authority, or undertake its
implementation. The manager owns the handoff and dependency account.

The first connected engineering activity can proceed without PEC or Domains.
Domains develops alongside and joins the database-research-to-design-candidate
activity in a subsequent increment (U2/U3). Keep provider, query tool,
research workflow and host contributions at their actual points of need;
provider owner and interface details remain open. A domain-specific agent
expression does not add a standing role. PEC consumption is independent and
optional to start. Without it, agents/managers do more source comparison and
coordination, and the human carries more cross-undertaking context.
