## Prospective amendment

1. Chirality retains exactly HELP_HUMAN, HELPS_HUMANS, WORKING_ITEMS, and TASK.
   HELP_HUMAN is the new-chat default; HELP_HUMAN, HELPS_HUMANS, and
   WORKING_ITEMS are direct-entry roles. Role identity remains distinct from
   Agent Type.
2. Canonical skills use `.agents/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`. This tranche adopts
   `chirality-change`, `preparation`, `researcher`, `software-code-review`,
   `software-defect-diagnosis`, `deliverable-consistency`,
   `drawing-titleblock-page`, and `proposal-format`. Optional execution
   companions retain applicable restrictions without becoming mandatory
   canonical skill metadata. `chirality-change` remains specific to work on
   this repository and is excluded from the App's general-purpose bundle.
3. Central workflows are `project-setup`, `project-decomp`,
   `software-decomp`, `domain-decomp`, `research-orchestration`, and
   `scope-change`. Their central status gives selector attention and does not
   make selection compulsory. Other existing workflow packages retain explicit
   legacy status unless separately reviewed.
4. Method identity is `{source, sourceRootId, kind, name}`. Unqualified lookup
   is project, then user, then bundled. Collisions expose origin and never
   silently replace an already selected method. Runtime owns production
   discovery and resolution; Root Python generates and validates the shared
   metadata and fixtures.
5. Ordinary context contains Root `AGENTS.md`, applicable project
   instructions, the active role instruction, and available skill
   descriptions. Selected method bodies and needed resources load on demand.
   Other full role instructions, broad governance excerpts, and unselected
   method bodies are excluded. Actual supplied origin, hashes, basis changes,
   selections, resources, and native-plan revisions remain replayable without
   rewriting earlier history.
6. Selection, role compatibility, coordination, and execution are distinct.
   Multiple ordered methods may be used in one conversation. Replacement
   occurs at a recorded safe boundary; any provider successor remains under
   the one visible conversation. Method loading grants no capability.
7. `interactionMode` is separate from `permissionMode`. Native Plan Mode is
   available only through a qualified adapter, remains in the same
   conversation with revision history, and has no ordinary decomposition
   checkpoints. Unsupported adapters expose it as unavailable. Explicit
   project-file export is separate from default conversation storage.
8. PROJECT and SOFTWARE decomposition use three grouped checkpoints: basis,
   normalized scope, vocabulary, and objectives; proposed Packages and
   Deliverables with coverage findings and exceptions; audited final
   decomposition accepted for downstream use. DOMAIN adds one combined
   source-admission/fidelity checkpoint before atomization, then three groups:
   normalized scope and meaning; Category/Knowledge Type/Knowledge Subject
   structure; audited final acceptance. SCOPE_CHANGE uses proposed change and
   impact; exact amendment and propagation plan; audited poststate acceptance.
   Agents prepare evidence and proposals before decisions. Only affected
   decisions reopen after a material basis change.
9. Historical role and method identifiers, legacy `TaskSkill`/`Workflow`
   conflict handling, existing sessions/events, and flat project workflow
   documents remain compatible without byte rewriting or silent
   reinterpretation.
