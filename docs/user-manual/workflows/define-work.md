# Define work

Use this method when a task is too vague to assign or check. Its result is a
usable definition of the work. That may be a short brief; it need not be a new
formal document.

Source: [define-work](https://github.com/sgttomas/chirality/blob/main/workflows/define-work/WORKFLOW.md).
This guide is a draft. App packaging and the installation procedure remain to
be verified for the release.

## Bring

Your intended result, the person who needs it, relevant requirements and source
material, permitted changes, and any decision or resource limits. An existing
adequate brief can be used without rewriting it.

## Example

> Help me define the work to prepare a monthly project update. The project
> manager needs a draft for Friday's review. Use the approved plan and this
> month's progress notes. Identify missing information. Define the work and
> its checks; do not prepare or send the update yet.

Ask the agent to explain the proposed work, inputs, responsibilities, handoffs
and checks. Discuss important gaps and choices. Separate what is known from
what still needs confirmation. Keep the resulting brief where its producer
and reviewer can use it.

## Check the result

Can someone carry out the assignment from this brief? Can the receiver tell
whether the result meets the need? Are missing inputs and decisions visible?
Does it count the same shared work more than once? Does it add steps or records
that serve no useful purpose?

Change one premise, such as the review date or a missing progress report, and
ask what must change. This tests whether the brief explains the work well enough
to adapt. The completed definition does not authorize or complete the work it
describes; commission that work explicitly when ready.
