# Fleet coordination fixture (DEL-09-05, V4-EXM-13)

The small invented undertaking on which exit criterion 5, coordinating
delegated work, is observed on an identified candidate. It realises the
conditions of EXAMINATION V4-EXM-13 and sets no staffing, duration or
performance target.

- `invented-inputs.json`: the constructed material, the two files staged
  before the run, the work and the decided matter fixed in advance, the change
  of basis, and the first message sent unchanged.
- `steps.json`: the steps in run order, each an action and what is observed,
  in the scope's terms. `F-9` is performed at the act control and is marked
  as such on its step record; every other step is operated through the
  candidate's shipped controls or is preparation named as preparation.
- `stimuli.json`: the arranged conditions, where each is staged and observed,
  and whether a recorded exchange could stand in.

What the run must show and a prepared file must not: every brief, graph
revision, log line and decision package comes from the conversation under the
guidance the candidate ships. Nothing under `.chirality/` or
`project/decisions/` is staged, and a record supplied beneath the interface
fails the step it was supplied for. A missing or malformed record is
observed as the view shows it and is a finding, not something to repair by
hand.

Results use DEL-09-01's result record, one per planned step, each naming the
candidate by its identification record. Each step record also says who
operated the step and by what means, what was done outside the controls and
whether that was preparation or independent observation, and where the step
needed knowledge the interface did not give.

The run needs a model turn with delegation available, so it needs the
owner's sign-in. Nothing here is a result.
