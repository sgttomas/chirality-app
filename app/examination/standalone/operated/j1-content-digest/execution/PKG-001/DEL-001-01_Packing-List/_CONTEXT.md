# DEL-001-01 Packing List

Invented examination material for the App's J1 examination. It describes no
real project, site, person or organisation.

## Identity

- Deliverable: DEL-001-01
- Name: Packing List
- Package: PKG-001, Invented Field Kit
- Discipline: Logistics
- Type: Document
- Responsible: the invented kit coordinator

## Description

A packing list for one invented two-day field kit: every item that goes into
the kit, its quantity, which crate it travels in and who checks it at loading.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every item in the kit has a line with its quantity and its crate.
- Each crate's total mass is stated and is at most 25 kg.
- The list names who checks each crate at loading.
- Items needing a battery say which battery and how many spares.
