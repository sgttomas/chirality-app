# Specification: DEL-001-01 Packing List

Invented examination material.

1. Each item is listed once, with its quantity and the crate it goes in.
2. No crate exceeds 25 kg when packed.
3. Water is carried at 3 litres per person per day.
4. Every battery-powered instrument names its battery type and spares.
5. The list states who checks each crate at loading.
