# DEL-09-01 — Candidate examination infrastructure and evidence protocol

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable makes every examination of an App candidate honest and comparable. It contributes the invented fixtures and recorded exchanges that make seams repeatable, the records that bind each result to its candidate, configuration and date with one of five outcomes, the rules that reopen results when a candidate changes and keep criteria intact during repair, the record of independent review, and the routes on which interface and native evidence are obtained and kept apart. The journey owners in PKG-09 and the packaging owner use this support to produce their own witnesses; it produces none of them.

It serves OBJ-008 (candidate-specific verification that does not overstate unrun or partial evidence) and OBJ-010 (accountable independent review). Its source commitments are PRD V4-CST-06, EXAMINATION §§1–2 (V4-EXM-01…05), ARCHITECTURE M-7 and OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-34. The 4.0.0 release target names no exit criterion for this deliverable. It serves each criterion that must be met "on the identified candidate" by supplying the candidate identification and the result record those criteria are stated in.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — Reusable invented-data and recorded-exchange fixture support for repeatable seam tests, each fixture carrying its origin, its supplier pin where the supplier is involved, a standing label and a digest. Supports SOW-103 and SOW-191; OBJ-008. [CLM-001; CLM-002]
- **OUT-002** — Candidate identification and evidence capture: the tools and record formats that identify a built candidate and write result records distinguishing actual observations, missing inputs, and verification from validation. Supports SOW-189 and SOW-190; OBJ-008. [CLM-001; CLM-002; CLM-006]
- **OUT-003** — The outcome, reopening, criterion-protection and independent-review protocol, with the review and change-impact records and the checks that apply the protocol's rules to them. Supports SOW-190, SOW-192, SOW-194 and SOW-229; OBJ-008 and OBJ-010. [CLM-001; CLM-002; CLM-003; CLM-004; CLM-005; CLM-006]
- **OUT-004** — Execution support for the WebKit and Chromium interface routes and for native packaged-application smoke checks, recording the actual engine, platform and package identity of each observation. Supports SOW-155 and SOW-193; OBJ-008. [CLM-001; CLM-003]

Terms the product implements:

- **Outcome.** Passed: the criterion was evaluated on the actual subject and met. Failed: evaluated and not met. Blocked: the case was attempted and a stated precondition or dependency stopped it; the cause is recorded. Not run: the case was planned for this candidate and not attempted; what is missing is recorded. Inconclusive: execution occurred and the evidence is insufficient or ambiguous.
- **Run basis.** A definition check runs the rules on a model or prototype. A rehearsal uses a test double or a replayed recording without a candidate. A candidate run uses an identified App candidate. Only a candidate run's record can stand for a scenario or a verification criterion, whatever its outcome.
- **Activity.** Verification is an agent- or person-operated scenario against a candidate; validation is a practitioner's actual use. Neither substitutes for the other.
- **Route.** Each evidence reference says where it was obtained: on a model without the product, at a seam from a replayed recording, at a seam live, in the interface on WebKit, in the interface on Chromium, natively on an unpackaged build on macOS Apple Silicon, or natively on the installed DEL-01-06 package.
- **Provenance.** Each evidence reference says what kind of evidence it is: a test definition, a recorded replay, a static inspection, a live observation, a person's act, a host receipt, a review record or a feature owner's check.
- **Currency.** A result is current, historical (its subject is superseded) or reopened (same subject, changed configuration, fixture or rule). A historical or reopened result keeps its outcome and evidence.
- **Records.** The result record, the review record, the change-impact record and the candidate identification record.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — This deliverable owns reusable examination support and its integration: fixtures, evidence capture, the protocol and its records, and execution support. The independent reviewer of a candidate is separate from that candidate's author. Feature owners keep their focused tests; this support joins their seams and replaces none of them.
- **CLM-002** — Verification uses invented material and binds each result to candidate, configuration and date with one of the five outcomes. Practitioner validation is a different activity with the owner as principal practitioner, and may use invented or owner-controlled material. No real client or engineering data is required. Recorded real protocol exchanges are preferred to live models where a recording can show the criterion.
- **CLM-003** — An earlier pass supports only the candidate it was observed on. A code change reopens the affected checks, and a repair leaves the criterion as it was. Interfaces are examined in both WebKit and Chromium, with a native smoke witness for each targeted packaged application. Both engines are kept for upkeep and portability evidence; shipping is macOS first and no Windows release follows from them.
- **CLM-004** — Candidates are reviewed independently under OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-34, with the reviewer preference and fallback stated there and a different model family where one is available. The review reports the separation that actually held and the model identities that were exposed. A fresh context alone shows no difference of model family.
- **CLM-005** — Other deliverables own the witnesses this support serves, and feature owners own feature production and focused tests: `DEL-01-06` the macOS package and its distribution evidence, and `DEL-09-02`, `DEL-09-05`, `DEL-09-06`, `DEL-09-07`, `DEL-09-09`, `DEL-09-10`, `DEL-09-11` and `DEL-09-12` their own qualification, examination or validation, as REQ-008 lists. Within `app/examination/`, this deliverable owns record checking, admission, candidate and support identity, package and distribution receiving and the interface runner; DEL-09-02 owns standalone preparation, native-step forms, native operation support and the receiving of qualification records.
- **CLM-006** — SWBPIPE is implemented by its own project, and coordination with it is relayed by the human. A host offers, records and presents acts; the person performs them. Applying, proposing, accepting an edit, checking, approving and accepting professional reliance are separate acts with separate actors and evidence, and one never establishes another. An observed human act may be recorded faithfully with the actor distinct from the recorder. Operation policy that is still undecided is decided by its owners (TBD-001); this protocol carries their decisions.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — Fixture support shall use invented engineering material for verification, trace recorded exchanges to their actual origin and applicable configuration, and permit replay for repeatable seams where feasible. Live-model runs remain few and deliberate without inventing a numerical budget. Invented fixtures and replay evidence retain their nature and do not certify an unobserved live host. [CLM-002; EXM V4-EXM-02]
- **REQ-002** — Every examination result shall name its candidate, date, harness/model versions and model server configuration, identify the criterion and actual evidence, and retain passed, failed, blocked, not-run or inconclusive standing. Passed/failed report observed evaluation against the criterion; blocked records the preventing condition; not-run states that execution has not occurred; inconclusive preserves insufficient or ambiguous evidence. Missing product inputs shall not be reported as an executed product pass or failure. [CLM-002; EXM §§1–2]
- **REQ-003** — Evidence capture shall distinguish agent-operated verification from actual practitioner validation and preserve the actor and scope of any recorded human act. It shall faithfully record an actually performed act while keeping the recorder distinct from the actor, and shall not infer acceptance, checking, approval or professional reliance from execution success. No ordering dependency between independently evidenced acts is invented by this protocol. [CLM-002; CLM-006; HTML 03]
- **REQ-004** — Candidate changes shall reopen affected scenarios; earlier passes remain attached to their original candidate. The protocol shall connect the changed candidate, affected checks, rerun evidence and remaining uncertainty without presenting an old pass as current. [CLM-003; EXM V4-EXM-03]
- **REQ-005** — Repairs shall preserve the examined criteria and their source identity. A failure is diagnosed and fixed; deleting the check, lowering the criterion or calling an unresolved policy settled shall not constitute repair. Any separately authorized criterion disposition retains its explicit decision and supersession; the examination author does not make that decision. [CLM-003; CLM-006; HTML 04]
- **REQ-006** — Independent review shall bind the actual examined candidate and identify reviewer separation from its author, exposed model identity and any availability limitation. It shall preserve the Codex preference and stated different-model Claude fallback, seek a different model family where available, and make no unsupported family-diversity claim. Findings and their dispositions remain distinct from human acceptance or product release. [CLM-004]
- **REQ-007** — Interface execution support shall cover WebKit and Chromium, including the macOS WebKit environment, and short smoke checks of each targeted packaged application on its in-scope platform. Browser results and native packaged results shall be separately identifiable; the former shall not substitute for a required native or actual-host witness. Targeted platform scope stays explicit and introduces no Windows shipping commitment. [CLM-003]
- **REQ-008** — The harness shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: package production/distribution by `DEL-01-06`; standalone qualification by `DEL-09-02`; fleet qualification by `DEL-09-05`; connected-activity agreement/workflow round trip by `DEL-09-06`; local host qualification by `DEL-09-07`; external control/catalog-extension qualification by `DEL-09-09`; connector qualification by `DEL-09-10`; later run reconstruction by `DEL-09-11`; and practitioner validation/feedback coordination by `DEL-09-12`, each with its actor in CLM-005. It supplies reusable support and evidence interfaces to those owners. Feature implementation/focused tests remain with feature owners under CLM-001 and CLM-005; external host implementation remains with the outside SWBPIPE session, human coordination/acts and unresolved policy decisions with the respective actors under CLM-006 and TBD-001. Evidence records shall not imply that these excluded acts occurred merely because support or a handoff exists.

- **AC-001** — Every verification fixture is invented, labelled as such, and carries a usable origin and configuration reference. No fixture requires real client or engineering material, and none selects a Domains corpus or admission permission. Verify by VER-001. [REQ-001; CLM-002]
- **AC-002** — A seam can be examined repeatably from a recorded real exchange. Each record marks replay apart from live observation, a replay never stands for a live or joined witness, and each live run states why no recording could show its criterion. No run quota is set. Verify by VER-002. [REQ-001; CLM-002]
- **AC-003** — A result record carries candidate, harness, model and model-server configuration, date, criterion and evidence, with exactly one of the five outcomes in the meaning defined above. The candidate is named by an identification record that can be checked against the built application (source revision, build identity, supplier pin). A record with a missing product input is refused as an executed pass or failure, and a record whose run basis is a definition check or a rehearsal is refused as a scenario or criterion result. Verify by VER-003. [REQ-002]
- **AC-004** — Records keep verification apart from practitioner validation and the actor apart from the recorder. An actually performed human act is recorded faithfully. A record that cites a human act names its actor and its recorder separately and refers to the evidence of that act and its scope; a record that cites an act without such evidence, or that infers acceptance, checking, approval or reliance from tool success or silence, is refused. Verify by VER-004. [REQ-003]
- **AC-005** — After a candidate change, each affected result is marked historical or reopened and cites that change, an earlier pass stays attached to its own candidate, and each affected case has current evidence or an explicit unresolved standing. Verify by VER-005. [REQ-004]
- **AC-006** — A failure is carried through diagnosis, repair and rerun against the same criterion identity. A rerun against a changed criterion counts as a repair only when it cites a separately authorized disposition; a deleted or weakened criterion is reported as a finding. Verify by VER-006. [REQ-005]
- **AC-007** — A review record binds the reviewed candidate and states author and reviewer separation, the model identities that were exposed, availability limits, findings and their dispositions, and states whether the preferred reviewer or the fallback of REQ-006 was used. A claim of a different model family is refused unless both identities are exposed and differ. Verify by VER-007. [REQ-006]
- **AC-008** — The interface runs in WebKit and in Chromium, and each targeted packaged application has a native smoke check, with engine, platform and package identity recorded. Each native record identifies the macOS WebKit environment the interface ran in, kept apart from the runner's WebKit engine. A part that needs native evidence passes only on a native route; a missing native or host input is blocked or not-run and is never replaced by a browser pass. Verify by VER-008. [REQ-007]
- **AC-009** — Every output keeps the boundaries of REQ-008: it consumes focused feature evidence without producing it, carries each undecided choice with its owner, and presents no support artifact as a journey, a validation, a release or a policy decision. Verify by VER-009. [CLM-001; CLM-005; CLM-006; TBD-001]

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Records have one defined form, and a record is relied on only after it is checked against that form and the protocol's rules. An examination begins with an identified candidate, the criteria that apply, the selected configuration and the contributions available; a case planned for a named candidate always gets a record, whatever happens. A step that is a person's act is performed by the person and never by automation. A native step is recorded on DEL-09-02's native-step form or, where it includes no human act and is automated, by an automation tool the record identifies. Interface scenarios run in a cross-engine runner against a supplier double or recorded fixtures; the runner's WebKit engine is portability evidence and the App's own web view is covered by the native routes. Results and their limits return to the journey owner and to the independent reviewer.

- **VER-001** — Inspect the fixture set and its origins against PRD V4-CST-06 and the seam contracts it serves. Confirm invented content, no required client data, and a named admission responsibility where a later research fixture is involved.
- **VER-002** — Replay identified recorded exchanges through the seam support and inspect repeatability, replay provenance, pin binding and the stated reason for each live run.
- **VER-003** — Run the record checks over valid, invalid and rule-violating result records covering all five outcomes, missing inputs, the candidate identification and records made on a definition check or a rehearsal. Compare each verdict with the outcome definitions in EXM §1.
- **VER-004** — Run the record checks over a faithful record of an evidenced human act with actor distinct from recorder, and over records that manufacture acceptance, checking, approval or reliance from execution or silence.
- **VER-005** — Take results for an earlier and a changed candidate, write the change-impact record and check that each result relying on the changed thing is marked and cited, and that the unaffected basis is stated.
- **VER-006** — Follow one failure through diagnosis, repair and rerun, comparing the criterion identity before and after. Check that a changed criterion without a cited disposition is reported.
- **VER-007** — Check an independently produced review record against its author and candidate basis: separation, exposed model identity or its unavailability, preference and fallback treatment, findings and closure limits.
- **VER-008** — Run the interface candidate in WebKit and Chromium and smoke-check the actual targeted packages. Keep engine, platform and configuration outcomes apart and report an unavailable native or host input as such.
- **VER-009** — Review the output interfaces against CLM-005 and CLM-006: list each excluded act with its owner, and each undecided choice with its owner and the criterion it bears on.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — An examination record says only what was observed. Source inspection, a planned test, a historical pass and execution on the current candidate are four different facts and are recorded as such. Better means a reader can check more of a result from its record alone (candidate, configuration, date, criterion, evidence with route and provenance); never by recording something that was not observed.
- **AX-002** — Criteria come from the accepted scope and its sources, and are not rewritten to fit an implementation. Better means fewer live-model runs for the same seam coverage; never by letting a replay, a browser pass or a component check stand for a live, native or joined witness.
- **AX-003** — Records exist for candidate review, decisions, reliance and recovery. A record with no such reader is not kept, and no measurement, saving or approval ritual is invented to fill one.
- **TBD-001** — Undecided matters this protocol carries and does not decide: the criterion for automatic catalog extension (owner with the host contract owner); any essential host beyond the App and SWBPIPE (owner); the period and activities of the owner's validation in use (owner); additions to the reserved human acts for a specific connected operation (owner with the App and SWBPIPE contract owners); and the admission arrangements for a later Domains research fixture (owner with the Domains, SWBPIPE and App receiving owners). Each bears only on the dependent criterion or fixture. The supplier pin a result is bound to is the one the candidate was built with.
