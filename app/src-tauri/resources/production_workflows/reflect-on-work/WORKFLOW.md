# Reflect on work

Use this method when experience raises a consequential question about what the
work is for, how it is understood or how it is being carried out. The starting
point is a concrete finding or question and the person's intended result, not a
scheduled ceremony or a search for things to change. HELP_HUMAN holds continuity
with the human; HELPS_HUMANS can help examine and design a response. The
implementation owner retains the work through authorized repairs. TASK may
contribute bounded evidence through its caller; it does not independently own
this human-dialogue method.

[Chirality in Practice (first edition, source 360a2d5050)](https://github.com/sgttomas/chirality/blob/360a2d5050302e27c143e6c76bd367f2d94b0072/docs/alignment-manual/Chirality_in_Practice_Edition_1.md) offers a reflective account of reasons and experience that can inform this inquiry; reading it adds no execution obligations.

Read the current purpose, commitments, selected source basis and the actual
finding with its evidence and limits. Use the scopes, the code and the review
record; a particular file format is not required. Distinguish the
human's commitment from the agent's interpretation, an observation from an
explanation, and unavailable evidence from evidence of absence. If the premise
is disputed, inspect the consequential source or affected use before building
on it. State what cannot yet be established and continue unaffected work.

Frame the smallest question that could change a useful decision. Does the
finding show a repair needed under existing commitments, an inadequate
explanation, an unsuitable practice, or a purpose/commitment that needs the
human's reconsideration? Compare what the work says exists, what warrants its
claims, what actions it directs and what those actions serve. Use these
perspectives where they sharpen the question; no matrix, score or full corpus
audit follows from them. Consider affected inputs and uses, not just the local
output. Ask whether the cost and interruption of monitoring are justified by
the decision or failure it helps address.

Develop a concrete retain/change choice with the human and Agent 0. Explain the
reason, consequences, uncertainty and useful next action. Retaining a method
because its application was at fault is a valid result; so is repairing a
method when the framing creates repeated friction. A passing check does not
settle fitness for intended use. Human judgment remains necessary for changed
purpose or commitments, risk acceptance and other reserved decisions. Apply
already-given decisions within their scope; do not ask again merely because the
work has moved to another contributor. Hold only the action needing a missing
decision, and make that decision reviewable rather than silently relaxing a
criterion. Keep actions within the authority given by the owner and the project.

Carry authorized consequences to the source that governs them through its
responsible owner. For ordinary result/document disagreement under current
commitments, use [bounded reconciliation](../bounded-reconciliation/WORKFLOW.md)
where the repository maintains deliverable commitments, work and needs. That
comparison and this examination have different questions; neither replaces the
other. For reusable method design use [create-workflow](../create-workflow/WORKFLOW.md)
under its preceding basis. Coordinated knowledge work can organize contributors
but is not a substitute for the human's reflective judgment.

Check that the reason still follows from the evidence, the affected uses were
considered, and the authorized consequence reached its owning material (or that
retention has an explicit reason). Return the conclusion, its basis and limits,
and any unresolved decision with its consequence. A decision edits the text it
governs, and the PR says at a high level what changed. Do not
create a reflection report. On interruption, recover that question, basis, decision
standing, usable work and next action before continuing. A discussion or a
labelled example does not establish observed execution, product acceptance or
phase advancement. Stop when the bounded question supports a warranted next act.
