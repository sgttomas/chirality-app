# Integrated engineering estimate and schedule

Prepare one coupled engineering estimate and resource-loaded schedule whose effort, price, technical handoffs, resource assumptions and consumer outputs agree. Use this method to construct, reproduce or revise that case. A bounded revision can reuse compatible validated counterpart inputs/results; it still returns the effects and limits on the coupled basis. Establish actual engineering readiness, scope acceptance, resource commitments and any execution baseline through their applicable evidence and decision owners before relying on them.

Use [Methods](METHODS.md) for the modeling detail. Supply applicable project tools, any executable planning engine and implementation adapters through the assignment.

## 1. Establish the intended use and authority

Take the objective, authorized changes and stop conditions from the conversation. Identify whether the task is to reproduce a saved case, revise one, or construct a new model. Preserve earlier reviewed versions; do not infer a backlog from historical plans.

Define the coupled basis and requested consumer outputs before calculating: reference or remaining work; included/excluded scope; effort versus internal cost, charge-out or complete fee; currency/price year; exact milestone subjects; resource/calendar assumptions; selection objective; required editing and native-tool behavior. State unavailable claims and limits explicitly. A limited packet is not a forecast for an undefined whole milestone.

The coupled case needs compatible effort, skills, calendars and availability. Missing rates limit priced totals but do not justify dropping hours or substituting zero. Honor the selected tool's actual input contract: do not invent values or bypass checks to make a route run. Complete authorized supported portions and identify any concrete capability or input limit without presenting an incomplete comparison as a complete coupled result.

Read the applicable project entry, accepted identities, commitments and affected input/use conditions. Query the affected neighborhood and external interfaces. Reuse applicable source warrants; do not reread every source merely because it exists. Distinguish source facts, owner rulings, estimating judgments, assumptions, proposals and acceptance. Apply settled owner rulings from their governing text without asking again. Do not replace the source or dependency masters with this model's basis.

Keep scope/acceptance changes, new risk acceptance, explicit holds and releases with their actual decision owners and competent authorities under confirmed delegation. For a genuinely new reserved relationship choice, show its actual work, inputs, checks, consequence and alternatives. Continue unaffected authorized work.

## 2. Consume the work definition

Use an adequate existing accepted definition, brief or owning work record. Project-definition and dependency-master workflows have a separate owner; this cost/schedule method consumes their inputs rather than designing, performing or editing those workflows/masters. Where an engineering project has accepted Deliverable parents or reviewed child functions, retain their identities and qualifications. Those are supported representations, not universal prerequisites. Keep shared production separate from a receiver's distinct integration/checking contribution; a reference to the same work is not another production allowance.

When constructing or refining the coupled network, identify coherent workfronts, purpose/maturity handoffs and exact output/milestone subjects before date arithmetic. A workfront may aggregate many effort units; split it when release, input maturity, qualified skill, independence or receiving use differs. Include supplier/owner interfaces and qualified future-availability assumptions. Native-only dependency views are partial.

Refine derived model working order/purpose-maturity handoffs only within accepted inputs, citing relevant needs/rulings and new assumptions. A sufficiently specified, explicitly authorized provisional upstream hypothesis can have a conditional numerical preview without editing masters; unhandled structural impacts remain uncalculated and promotion awaits proper disposition.

Produce inspectable coverage of every in-scope family and conditional/excluded/unpriced disposition in the owning basis. A separate matrix file is optional. Coverage is a substantive review outcome, not a count of rows or a certificate that inputs are suitable.

## 3. Develop distinct coupled projections

Use the actual selected effort recipe and operative parameter/profile library. Preserve original methods separately from authorized quantity/unit/event or rule adaptations. Owning model inputs retain values and their known reasons, units, evidence/judgment status, applicability, limits and affected uses. Group related choices where appropriate; retain unknown historical reasons as unknown. New cases retain prior/new values and source lineage. Generated consumer views are not an independent tuning master.

Develop effort/pricing and the technical network as distinct projections of the same work, with feedback rather than deriving schedule logic from cost rows. Reuse unchanged counterpart inputs/results only where their identities and applicable validation permit it. Report supported effects on the coupled model, not an isolated favorable delta that conceals other impacts.

- **Effort and charge-out:** quantities, complexity, iterations, role work units, checking, ordinary friction and additional event mechanisms, rates and evidence quality. Give each included unit one effort/cost home and preserve exact source/task/role lineage. Disclose residual and unpriced scope. Use only supported uncertainty claims.
- **Technical network:** produced and checked native work, suitable external returns, purpose-limited early handoffs, complete applicable final handoffs, external waits and current-revision reconciliation. Never convert accounting stages into calendar gates or treat first data, a header, a PO or administrative completion as a completed technical answer.

Keep three joins distinct: source work → effort home; effort → compact workfront allocation; workfront handoff → consumer/use condition. Their reconciliation does not make them interchangeable. Reuse applicable warrants for unchanged relationships and check affected ones. Use the engineering mechanisms and focused examples in [Methods](METHODS.md).

Resource loading needs a reconciled effort budget, but its row count does not set network size. Timing changes alone create no effort saving. A norm change needs a substantive source/work-method reason, including enabling costs or suitability conditions.

The owned scenario model may persist activities, working-order, capacity and other planning choices. Native input/use conditions and settled rulings stay in their owning records; bind references and regenerate affected views when that meaning changes. This model is different from a transient agent-coordination query view under `construct-local-work-graph`. Do not use that method as a persistent model writer or create a second editable master of native needs.

## 4. Check meaning and coupled claims

Inspect the actual basis and saved coupled result; this is a working check, not a new method-approval pause. Use only the hierarchy needed; a bounded TASK does not delegate.

## 5. Evaluate qualified finite resources and scenarios

Define the coupled case's calendar, skill/department pools, availability periods, independence, concurrency limits and external lag assumptions. Availability is not attendance, funded demand or committed appointment. Allocate each selected case's included effort once and reconcile it to that case's source budget. Preserve hours and prices across comparisons only where the declared change leaves them unchanged; retain and explain authorized differences.

Diagnose a miss before changing the model: false technical logic, excessive aggregation, actual resource queue, external timing, calendar, or unsupported effort assumption require different repairs. Trace observed driving handoffs and queues; do not claim a unique critical path where resource-blocking logic is absent.

Vary only legitimate variables within the owner's limits. State what changed and why; keep an interpretable comparison. If first fit is the objective, continue until the first adequately checked allowed fit or an actual unresolved reserved choice/capability boundary prevents progress. Do not impose an arbitrary trial cap, silently reduce hours, relax limits or change the milestone subject. A fit does not clear unknown critical inputs, null readiness, technical holds or other missed milestones.

Where continuing services are modeled, compare demand with its funded cadence and horizon. Spreading fixed funded hours over a longer horizon does not buy continued cadence or guaranteed staffing. Report unpriced extension exposure, or explicitly price authorized incremental quantities at their owning home. Keep this distinct from pool availability.

## 6. Generate the requested consumer outputs

For each requested report, workbook, Gantt or native-tool exchange, specify selected case/version, aggregation, additive totals versus included subsets, editable input home, semantics retained/lost and required consumer behavior. Generate from the selected reconciled model; output adapters are not another master.

Publish exact milestone subjects and typed unknown/conditional status consistently in machine and human outputs. Preserve included delivery/support subsets, separate conditional extensions, external fees and unavailable internal cost. A date window may contain gaps; duration, effort and continuous staffing are different quantities.

Keep editable assumptions/rules in their declared owning inputs. A workbook pricing edit is a standalone variant unless an explicit validated import promotes it into the joint model. Otherwise edit owning inputs and regenerate; do not imply an unimplemented round trip.

Report whole-model effects on the requested coupled basis, including other milestones/null subjects, all department demand, changed distributions, coupled controls, unchanged findings and unmodeled effects. Reuse applicable checks for routine previews; material technical/numerical changes receive proportionate independent scrutiny.

Human keep or try-else is separate from calculated fit. Retain immutable candidate inputs/reasons/results and parent bindings; unselected cases need no maintained reject/branch ledger. Under an actual keep instruction, use one cheap programmable operation to validate/materialize the next immutable basis and atomically update the single chosen reference, recording the supplied instruction honestly without claiming authentication. Do not require hand-edited acceptance/status fields or side registers. Preserve current work-unit reasons and verify that the kept basis supports the next preview. Optional proposal preparation is an inspection aid, not a mandatory phase.

## 8. Establish the remaining warrants and integrate

Separate substantive source/model correctness, numerical conservation, saved representation behavior and preservation through integration. Check the actual saved artifacts, not only a successful builder or stale preview. Bind reviewed versions to their source/case identities, selected code/inputs and numerical runtime/seed settings where relevant; use hashes or equivalent precise identification. Retain a cross-session entry that distinguishes current project basis from available general recipes and points to actual values, reasons, limits and rerun commands. Inspect changed prose as well as pins.

Exercise only the consumer behavior required by the selected claim: meaningful workbook formulas and restored representative edits, native recalculate/save/reopen where required; Gantt appearance/interaction; native schedule import where claimed. Probabilistic analysis and native-tool exercises are not prerequisites for unrelated outputs. When a required tool is unavailable or policy blocks it, retain the concrete limitation and deliver only supported claims. Static checks are not a native or visual pass. Do not bypass host restrictions.

On failure, locate the actual defective artifact and its producing input. Repair at the owning locality, regenerate affected downstream projections and repeat only checks whose claims changed or remain unresolved. Source changes during generation invalidate the affected candidate; never update a pin merely to silence a failure. Preserve applicable passing evidence.

Integrate the checked version into its authorized home and reconcile affected references, needs and qualifications without rewriting unchanged declarations. Use the undertaking's actual review/change record; if another selected method prescribes a host-specific record, make the authorized adaptation explicit rather than silently ignoring its contract. If interrupted, leave partial work explicitly unusable where needed and one replaceable recovery note only if continuity requires it: exact version, checks, remaining obstacle and next step. Do not create routine receipts or parallel handoff chains.

Return usable artifacts, their case/basis, checks and material use limits, including any reserved choice. Stop when the authorized result is established. Clean generated scratch/build material that has no named consumer, review or recovery need. Acceptance and release remain with their actual decision owners and competent authorities.
