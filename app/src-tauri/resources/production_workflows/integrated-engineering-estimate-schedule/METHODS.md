# Modeling and output methods

Apply these engineering mechanisms to the coupled case's claims, adapting detail to scale and risk and reusing valid unaffected inputs/results. Consume the existing work definition; refer genuine gaps to its upstream owner, who can select bundled `define-work`. This planning method does not take over those masters or their workflows. No fixed activity count, phase percentages, role roster, distribution, software schema or output format is mandatory.

## Effort, price and uncertainty

Construct a work allowance from identifiable populations and role contributions. A useful form is quantity × reference role-hours × justified complexity/iteration factors, with explicitly separated ordinary friction and additional event mechanisms. Explain what a factor already includes before adding another. Shared production is priced once; recipient-specific checking/incorporation is priced separately only for actual distinct work. Preserve author/check independence where required. Project management/coordination has real service quantities and products rather than an automatic percentage added to all work.

Appraise the basis: measured/project-specific observation, analogous norm, published source, expert judgment or unsupported placeholder. Record applicability, boundaries and uncertainty in the owning assumptions. A requested magnitude is a challenge to examine the model, not calibration evidence. Method-dependent reductions require suitable controlled sources/workflows; either price enabling implementation, retain the original allowance for unsupported subsets, or disclose the condition.

For each selected effort unit preserve its quantity, unit, role and work/effort home. For priced units also preserve rate, currency/price year and cost lineage. Reconcile units and products before rounded reporting. A role's billing rate is not necessarily a staffing department's rate or internal employment cost. Missing rates or unknown fees remain unavailable/unpriced with affected totals qualified; do not substitute zero or a convenient discipline rate. Supported hours remain distinct from pricing completeness.

Use point estimates, explicit alternative cases/ranges or probabilistic analysis according to evidence and consumer purpose. Additional response-event expectations are capacity allowances, not certain future incidents. A simulation requires defensible distributions/dependence and explicit coverage limits. Do not transfer old quantiles after changing workload assumptions without re-establishing their meaning. Unsupported uncertainty remains disclosed, including unmodeled external clocks and residual conditional scope.

When probabilistic outputs are requested and supported, separate quantity/unit-allowance uncertainty, event occurrence × affected extent × additional work, and external waiting. Preserve common-cause dependence and once-only work homes. Appraise actual observations, analogs and expert judgments for applicability; earlier estimates are not observed training actuals. Retain model/version and reproducible seed, examine simulation convergence and sensitivity, and report mean, mode where meaningful, and model-conditional quantiles. Name the interval's target and interpretation: a 2.5th–97.5th percentile range generated from assumptions is not a frequentist confidence interval. Demonstrated predictive coverage requires applicable validation; Monte Carlo sampling error is separate from project uncertainty. Completion uncertainty requires propagating draws through the technical network and finite-resource allocator: finish calculated at mean hours is not expected finish, and cost intervals do not transfer to completion dates. This optional branch requires neither a new engine nor simulation when the basis cannot support it.

## Parameterized recipes and decision continuity

Capture the actual method before adapting it. A phase-template recipe may sum reference phase P50s, apply batch/effective-count credit, select composite log-spread from explicit base/factor/settings and calculate lognormal task metrics. Its count domain, floor/override policy and aggregation interpretation are operative parameters/rules, not hidden code choices. Preserve source-formula/narrative discrepancies and placeholder/incomplete families explicitly. An original compatibility recipe and a later rule or driver/role adaptation must have distinct named identities; source template values are not observed productivity.

Own operational values in parameter/profile libraries and per-case selections/overrides with exact value, units, reason where known, basis/source/judgment, applicability, limitations/status and affected uses. Coherent vectors can share one explanation. Distinguish a specific value's stated reason from inherited context and unknown historical intent. Technical/owner rulings stay at their actual source home. A read-only indexed view can expose historical values/reasons without becoming a competing editable decision master.

A new tuning case retains the prior value/case identity and actual reason for change; desired totals and earlier estimates are not calibration observations. Retain exact selected input snapshots and precise source/code identity, with runtime/seed/batch metadata where relevant. Keep one entry identifying the current project basis, available recipes, material open limits and safe new-output command. For a code-backed joint model, generated spreadsheets consume its inputs and results rather than own parameter tuning. A separate workbook variant has only its documented coupling; do not imply an unimplemented import or round trip.

Driver populations and explicit role work units may construct a reference function instead of a fixed phase template. Avoid multiplying a fully quantified function again by a whole-function count without an explicit repetition basis. Role composition can move with one task draw; partial known prices remain a priced subtotal while complete charge-out stays unavailable. An independent quantity/unit/event construction is a separate appraised recipe, not a default second sigma uplift. Sum distributions require declared dependence and joint draws rather than sums of task percentiles; exact sum mode may be unidentified. Exercise original-source formula fixtures, changed-rule cases, invalid/incomplete inputs, persisted rationale retrieval and representative batch scale before claiming reusable operation.

## Workfronts, maturity and dependency meaning

Choose the smallest coupled network that preserves important work and release distinctions. Effort/cost atoms may remain granular for audit while many allocate into a coherent engineering workfront. Splitting each atom into an activity creates spurious gates; aggregating every family into one final batch hides valid concurrency. Split on actual work/input/use conditions, retaining positive production and meaningful checks rather than zero-work headers as readiness proof.

A relationship explains the applicable provider product, case/revision, maturity and receiving use. Store its authority/reference with the model basis; the actual source commitment or settled ruling remains in its owning project text. A handoff is not suitable merely because a file exists or its planned date passed.

For supplier/native interfaces distinguish initial requests and suitable pre-order information; authentic offers and their technical appraisal; purpose-limited working returns; complete applicable final returns and native incorporation; purchase authority; later progress/delivery records. Model supplier internal work as external unless explicitly in scope. Source rights, responsible confirmation, appointment and critical fields remain use conditions. Unknown timing is uncertainty, not a project-start given. Shared subject records do not establish physical order counts.

For progressive design, identify what can be produced and primarily checked on a qualified preliminary envelope, what final comparison/update remains, and where independent final verification protects current use. A stable-envelope assumption can justify a bounded delta; material departure invokes affected substantive rework/hold. Never force all final work into a nominal delta or serialize the whole primary body behind a late paper return.

Readiness subjects need their actual relevant sets. A partial arrangement can have a date while integrated readiness remains null. Workshop attendance, purchase-relevant findings/disposition and full action/report closeout have distinct products and gates. A controlled model-geometry issue need not await unrelated later nongraphic records, but must incorporate actual relevant current geometry. Quantities require produced appropriate geometry/calculations, current extraction and checking before controlled CWP use.

## Bounded checks that test these claims

Use the actual saved paths, positive allocations, dates and input conditions relevant to the coupled claim. Static tracing can establish some claims; selected controlled perturbations establish sensitivity where needed. State which was done. A counterfactual changes a copy/candidate, never the reviewed source. Select applicable rows below; this is not a universal checklist or a reason to rebuild unchanged checked relationships.

| Claim | Focused evidence and failure signal |
| --- | --- |
| Primary work on preliminary data, final delta protects issue | Trace qualified preliminary envelope → positive primary author/check work; final return must not block that whole body. Trace actual current final return → positive delta author/check → affected issue. Check concurrency is allowed, work conserved and outside-envelope handling retained. Fail if bulk work is secretly after the final return, or delta is a zero header. |
| First data opens work without earning completion | Trace relevant subset → legitimate positive early work, then complete applicable suitable bundle → remaining production/incorporation/check → final handoff. Inspect or delay a controlling missing return. Fail if first arrival licenses the whole completed output, or all early work unnecessarily waits for all suppliers. |
| Current MTO depends on produced geometry | Trace geometry/calculation producer and case/revision → positive extraction/current reconciliation/check → relevant CWP. Fail if only a methods register feeds it or no producer exists. |
| Shared analytical check has one substantive home | Trace actual candidate into independent scrutiny and returned disposition to issue. Reconcile its effort once. Fail for absent check, duplicate master production or a fictitious checker-of-checker. |
| Tiny numerical residual cannot create a false workday | Exercise almost-exhausted author budget before independent QA at finite capacity. Preserve all positive hours within justified tolerance while closing the booking correctly. Fail if residual hours disappear or an insignificant remainder introduces a spurious extra author day. |

Inspect all modeled families for a reasoned coverage disposition and material missing producer/use paths; use bounded actual examples for distinct mechanisms instead of blindly Cartesian-linking all records. Passing structural scans does not establish engineering suitability.

## Finite resources and scenario diagnosis

Calendar capacity is qualified person-time, with separate skills for author/reviewer where required. State whether holidays, overtime, onboarding, part-time availability or named appointments are modeled. Compute daily/period loads against actual pool limits and check all source work allocated or explicitly undated. A department pool is an availability ceiling, not a minimum attendance charge. Unknown capacity or unsupported allocation remains a limit on feasibility claims.

Start with an interpretable reference profile. For a miss inspect the actual last technical handoffs, resource waits and governing assumptions. Test whether an input is controlling before buying capacity or shortening lags. Correct false logic or inappropriate batching on substantive grounds, then evaluate legitimate staffing/availability or external-input alternatives. Revise effort only from its work basis, not to meet dates. Report exact scope of the achieved target and the remaining misses/unknowns. First fit is neither optimization nor a minimum-staff proof.

For continuing services distinguish fixed funded pacing from required cadence over the full scenario horizon. Report priced periods and incremental exposure. Authorized added cadence becomes explicit extra population and effort/price; unpriced extension stays visible. Do not stretch funded reservations and describe them as full ongoing service.

## Projection contracts and methods

Each requested adapter declares its source case, source identities, grouping/allocation rule, rounding, edit ownership, retained qualifications and required behavior. Reconcile outputs to their selected owning inputs, including budget and dated allocations where used. Preserve detailed lineage where a compact representation aggregates work. Native or probabilistic checks apply only to the claims that require them; they are not gates on unrelated supported outputs.

| Output | Method and meaningful checks |
| --- | --- |
| Estimate/basis report | Explain scope, work methods, quantity/norm/role/rate basis, included and excluded/conditional work, evidence quality and uncertainty. Reconcile all families/shared homes and exact h×rate products; summarize judgments and material sensitivities rather than dumping every atom. |
| Cost workbook | Preserve useful template layout and meaningful formulas. Derive phase or period costs from actual selected allocation dates when the requested phase is temporal; accounting tags alone do not establish timing. Included delivery/support subsets are nonadditive; conditional extensions remain separate. Trace rollups back to source roles/rates. Check formulas, cached/native values, representative edit/restoration, saved reopen and changed print views to the extent claimed. An edit creates only the variant its implemented data path supports. |
| Manpower/resource view | Aggregate actual assignments by qualified department/skill and period using declared hours/FTE. Distinguish demand from available pools and commitments; include gaps and unknown/unmodeled exposure. Reconcile total assignment hours and peaks without treating peaks as continuous staffing. |
| Deliverable Gantt | Allocate shared work to its actual deliverable shares once. Show attributed workdays or explicitly label outer windows including gaps. Preserve exact milestone subjects and unknown/conditional status. Check data attribution and visual/interaction behavior separately. |
| Native schedule exchange | Declare whether the artifact is a date snapshot, a dependency plan or a fully reschedulable resource model. Preserve IDs, links, units, calendars and imported fields to the claimed extent. Inspect actual native import/recalculation when claimed; disclose losses such as daily contours, skill mix, capacities or manual dates. CSV validity alone is not equivalence. |

## Change, failure and recovery

Use source and case pins to identify reviewed bytes and upstream relationships. Check inputs before and after generation when source stability matters. If a source changes, quarantine the affected candidate, establish changed meaning at its owner and regenerate impacted consumers. A matching hash binds identity; it does not prove meaning.

A failing saved formula, stale display, unsupported import or missing consumer field requires identifying the actual producer and affected claim. Repair the owning input/adapter, preserving previous checked versions, then rerun affected checks. If only prose changed, inspect its semantic consistency and update its binding; do not restart every numerical review automatically. If a native/visual check cannot run, deliver only the representation claims established and name the remaining limitation.

Delegation may separate source reasoning, implementation, numerical review and saved-output review when useful. Give each contribution a bounded write home and one integration owner; combine compatible warrants and reuse valid evidence. Do not substitute a checklist, review label or opaque PASS for the actual evidence needed by the consumer.

## Sourced feedback and accepted-model continuity

An accepted planning model is a useful fallible basis, not proof of technical truth or closed conditions. Attribute feedback and its evidence/claim/directive status before mapping it to actual cost/schedule inputs. Keep definition/dependency masters with their separate owner; this method owns derived model order and numerical projections. Exact-before patches preserve shared homes, source units/roles/rates and conditional boundaries. An unsupported method or new mapping needs a concrete adapter/interface; a sufficiently specified provisional upstream hypothesis can still have a conditional preview without editing the master.

Check neutral adapter parity against accepted saved results before reporting deltas, and bind caches to actual inputs/code/runtime. Preserve exact dates and all booked hours within stated negligible floating tolerances; do not invent byte equivalence. Price-only schedules may reuse checked results only under exact semantic input identity; changed demand/time requires whole-model resource allocation. Show distributional/other-milestone effects even when headline finish is unchanged, and do not assign isolated causality to coupled controls.

Persist candidate feedback, patch, source/unit reasons, objective impact and selected parent/source/code bindings. Do not turn each preview into a manually maintained status or selection register. Unselected immutable cases remain recoverable. A single explicit adopt operation can validate and materialize the next immutable basis and atomically change one chosen reference under the actual human keep instruction. Record that supplied instruction as provenance without claiming authentication; do not hand-edit multiple lifecycle fields. Preserve prior accepted files, current work-unit reasons and source qualifications without false old-model equivalence or a mutable old-pointer pin. Test an isolated adopt/next-preview cycle. Optional proposal preparation is not a mandatory gate.
