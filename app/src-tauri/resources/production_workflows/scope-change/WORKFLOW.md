# scope-change

Amend accepted decomposition and propagate the consequences through their owning workflows.

WORKING_ITEMS coordinates this undertaking and assigns bounded contributions to TASK.

## Select the project route

For current PROJECT/SOFTWARE localities using `ScopeOfWork.md` and
`deliverable.yaml`, use [the current-project amendment profile](resources/current-project.md).
It preserves three grouped human decisions, stable IDs, exact amendment/write
boundaries, independent poststate scrutiny and propagation ownership without
requiring legacy lifecycle files. Read the project instructions to select the
route; file presence alone does not adopt a lifecycle.

For DOMAIN or explicitly adopted legacy phase/control-file projects, use the
[retained contract](resources/contract.md) and [retained method](resources/method.md).
Their `_STATUS.md`, reopening, snapshot/register and DOMAIN-specific duties
remain applicable to those selected projects, not to every current locality.

## Shared course of work

1. Prepare the requested change and impact for the first grouped decision.
2. Prepare the exact amendment and propagation plan for the second decision.
3. Apply only accepted candidate edits and obtain independent scrutiny.
4. Present the audited poststate and remaining obligations for the third decision.
5. After acceptance, apply the listed acceptance-conditional edits and hand
   affected setup/propagation to the selected `project-setup` or
   `project-setup-retained` route. Examine actual closure with
   `audit-scope-closure`.

A changed PRD or other governing carrier retains its own authority. Its exact
edit must be included in the accepted write boundary or returned to its owner.
Ordinary factual reconciliation under unchanged commitments is not a scope amendment.
