# From an existing project to its next product basis

Use this guide when the assignment calls for a successor PRD and supporting
documents. These are adaptable contents, not mandatory filenames, tables, or
new global schemas. Keep the source account compact where it can support the
design directly.

## Source roles and preservation

The human's direction identifies the next undertaking. A previous PRD or Scope
of Work explains intended commitments in its own scope. Implementation and
observations show what exists under stated conditions. Review and test records
support particular claims on particular revisions. Decisions and their history
explain why a choice was made and whether its basis still applies.

A thesis or other conceptual text can preserve the reasons behind the work.
A project-management or agent manual can express practices worth trying again.
Read the passages relevant to the new design, including their limitations and
historical context. Explain which ideas inform the successor and how its use
will provide feedback. Neither the existence of a publication nor its transfer
proves universal effectiveness.

Where the brief designates material for unchanged transfer, keep a source and
destination map with content identities. Preserve its stated status and source
attribution. Document application-specific interpretation and changed link
destinations outside the protected text. Verify equality when transfer is
performed; a planned copy remains outstanding until that happens. The PRD may
cite preserved conceptual material without declaring it an executable product
contract or copying every implementation choice described within it.

## Explain an exemplar's value

A compact entry can describe:

- The activity or problem it addresses and its users.
- The observed behaviour or proposed design idea, with source locations.
- Why it is useful, whose assessment that is, and the evidence limits.
- Dependencies, conditions, costs, and meaningful counterexamples.
- What should carry forward and what could change, with decision status.
- What later feedback would justify keeping or revising it.

For example, a retained conversation while a person examines an artifact may
help them keep the request, proposed change, and reasons connected. That is a
candidate value to investigate. It does not alone prescribe a sidebar, a
separate desktop application, or a new conversation runtime. Show the actual
experience and evidence, then develop the new expression with the human.

The workflow may identify a useful pattern that the original software never
implemented. Preserve its value as a design proposition, along with the missing
evidence. Successful code, unrealised intent, and a failed experiment can each
contribute differently to the successor account.

## The successor document set

The PRD explains the proposed product, intended users and activities, scope,
capabilities, connected behaviour, constraints, and what an adequate result
would allow people to do. Supporting documents give substantial shared subjects
an identifiable home when needed. A possible arrangement is:

| Subject | What a reader needs |
| --- | --- |
| Product vocabulary and state | Objects, distinctions, lifetimes, and meaningful transitions. |
| Behaviour and interfaces | Operations, observations, permissions, results, failure and recovery. |
| Architecture basis | Chosen boundaries and responsibilities; reasons, alternatives, and supplier assumptions still open. |
| Examination | Principal scenarios and means of checking requirements and fitness for use. |
| Operating and project methods | Selected practices, responsibilities, conditions, and the feedback through which they may improve. |

The main account should work as a whole. Refer to the source project where
evidence matters; do not make every new reader reconstruct the product from
the old decomposition. Clearly identify the documents or portions proposed
for acceptance and the role of preserved background material.

For a capability expressed in several host applications, describe a connected
activity in at least the hosts essential to the proposed claim. Identify
common meaning and host-specific behaviour. Where a standalone interface is
secondary, explain its purpose and relationship to the shared capability
without making its layout the definition of that capability.

## Readiness and feedback

Use a reader exercise to expose gaps: can someone explain an intended activity,
its constraints and consequences, and the decisions still needed from this
document set alone? Identify where they need additional evidence or invention.
The next use determines how much must be settled. A supplier choice may remain
open if its required behaviour is sufficiently bounded; an unresolved product
boundary may change the entire division of work.

Independent examination should check that selected exemplars survived with
their meaning intact, proposed departures work together, preserved material
remains unchanged, and historical evidence is not reported as proof of the
successor. Return readiness, partial scope, and pending checks explicitly.
Prepare the actual candidate before the human's decision.

Carry forward an explanation of what selected practices are expected to help
and what later experience could challenge that expectation. For example, an
artifact-linked conversation can be examined for whether a user can recover the
reasons for a proposal without losing the current engineering task. Record
observations and proposed improvements at their owning sources. Avoid adding
measurement work that has no bearing on a real decision.

Where the project's chosen route is Conceptual → FEED → 30% → 60% → 90% → 100%,
this workflow supplies the conceptual product basis. FEED develops the accepted
division and setup; work toward 30% establishes production contracts, evaluation
arrangements, dependencies, and an initial DAG. Detailed development proceeds
toward 60%, integrated deliverables toward 90%, and product examination and
delivery toward 100%. These describe positions of the work, not percentages
of tasks completed. Later evidence can reopen the affected part of the basis.
Use the project's actual adopted method for those phases.
