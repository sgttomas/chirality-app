## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The App continuity owner assembles the focused account from the accepted composite, the PKG-10 applicability/active-consumer contribution and received adoption status. Identify the affected coexistence scope before checking its content. Reuse existing records by reference, expand investigation only where a stated gap affects a specific preservation or retirement claim, and leave unreceived inputs explicit. The following methods define later production examination; their definition here does not claim those checks have been executed or decisions performed.

- **VER-001** — Compare OUT-001 against REQ-001 and the B7 preservation list. Inspect source/recovery routes and standing for the identified fallback, old projects/archives, reviewed methods, work, obligations and evidence. Record examined coverage and gaps; inspect that existing active lanes remain scoped and are not treated as automatically replaced. Evidence: focused coverage and retention findings bound to the account revision.
- **VER-002** — Starting from OUT-001, follow the current selector for each listed scope/consumer to its identified source and compare with the supplied PKG-10 map and DEL-11-02 receiving status. Examine a historical pin and absent/uncertain adoption case to ensure neither becomes current authority by file presence or run selection. Evidence: source/selector trace and unresolved-input findings; no new navigation technology is prescribed.
- **VER-003** — Compare the full thesis file set and bytes with the preservation basis stated in CLM-007; retain a reproducible comparison and inspect the attribution/standing record. In a non-authoritative test fixture or in-memory comparison, demonstrate that omitted, added or changed content is detected. Evidence: identified basis/candidate, complete comparison coverage, identity results and attribution/standing check. Do not mutate the thesis to run a negative case.
- **VER-004** — For the useful material named in OUT-001, compare preserved content identity to its identified original basis and record the compared set, results and gaps. Exercise a missing/changed item case without changing authoritative content; confirm historical research or checks are not reported as evidence of an unexamined current candidate. Evidence: candidate-bound identity results and limitation account.
- **VER-005** — Trace OUT-003's obligations to the supplied active-work/consumer record and their accountable owners. For each intended retirement, inspect its continuing-obligation dispositions and actual decision evidence; include unresolved obligation and fallback-only replacement cases, which must not establish retirement. Evidence: obligation-to-disposition trace, open owner/point-of-need account and any explicit hold on affected retirement.
- **VER-006** — Compare an actual sourced human act with its account entry: actor, subject, scope, evidence and transcription/observation limits remain faithful while recorder stays distinct. Compare proposed or absent acts and ensure none is recorded as performed. Check that replacement, adoption, execution, checking, approval and professional reliance are neither conflated nor forced into an unsupported dependency sequence. Evidence: positive faithful-recording and fabrication/conflation-negative findings bound to the account; use existing records or clearly labelled fixtures, not fabricated live acts.
- **VER-007** — Review each act enumerated in REQ-005 against CLM-003 through CLM-006 and D1/D2. Confirm the local account only references the separately owned adoption, renewal and replacement contributions, carries unresolved choices, and supplies continuity evidence without performing those acts. Evidence: semantic responsibility-resolution findings, including any limitation of deterministic owner checking.
