# DEL-07-02 — Connector limitation and source-file recovery paths

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Provide the App's receiving behavior and accountable source-file route when optional PEC or Domains material is absent, stale, partial or failing. Work with a sound independent basis can proceed; connector-dependent questions retain their missing inputs. Later qualified PEC consumption can join the work without becoming a new starting condition.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The App shall show the source identity, standing, available coverage/freshness information and limitations of connector-supplied material. It shall distinguish qualified/current use from absent, stale, partial and failing conditions and identify which conclusions lack support. Missing metadata shall remain an explicit limitation; it shall not be normalized into fresh, complete or authoritative material. Connector-specific meanings come from the receiving owners in CLM-003 and unresolved terms in TBD-001 and TBD-002 (CLM-001, CLM-002).
- **REQ-002** — For an affected question under each absent, stale, partial or failing condition, the App and recovery document shall expose an explicit route to underlying sources and the work needed to reconstruct the answer. The route shall identify available records/revisions and gaps, or state that the necessary source is unavailable and who must obtain or resolve it. Total absence shall be handled by the receiver without requiring a response-carried fallback signal. Reading files shall not itself establish authority or a sound answer; unresolved evidence remains unresolved (CLM-002, CLM-003).
- **REQ-003** — Limitation and recovery results shall not infer empty work, readiness or permission from a failed or missing feed. Reconstructed execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance shall retain their actual actor, subject and evidence or remain unknown. Successful recovery or execution shall not synthesize another act, nor impose an unsupported acceptance-before-other-acts sequence. Applicable prerequisites remain in their owning policy and records (CLM-002, CLM-005).
- **REQ-004** — The source-file route shall work through ordinary project records before later fleet software is available. For limited or absent PEC, agents shall locate and compare the relevant graphs, decisions and revisions; managers shall maintain review and integration ownership; the human shall carry necessary cross-undertaking coordination. The route shall state those actual duties and outstanding work without presenting a connector, a prepared brief, a source read or a future fleet view as proof that an actor performed them. It shall use the existing source-record interfaces and handoff to fleet views when available without making those views a prerequisite to file reconstruction (CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-003).
- **REQ-005** — PEC and Domains shall have independent receiving and fallback paths: either connector may be consumed within its own established contract without the other, and independently supported v4 development shall be able to begin and continue with both unavailable. A connector-dependent feature or question may wait for its actual missing input while supported work proceeds. This continuation shall not mark the missing input satisfied or bypass the receiving/data-boundary decisions in TBD-001 and TBD-002 (CLM-002, CLM-004).
- **REQ-006** — When qualified PEC capability becomes available, this contribution shall connect its recovery path to the App envelope whose qualification, release and deliberate receiving adoption are established by the App PEC receiving owner in CLM-003. Covered use shall show its actual limits; uncovered, stale, partial, failed or absent material shall retain the source route. This join shall neither restart independent v4 work behind a blanket gate nor infer release/adoption from contract maturity, fixture success or D108's exact-byte acceptance. Particular unsupported work remains subject to its own evidence needs (CLM-002, CLM-004).
- **REQ-007** — The fixture/evidence set shall exercise the accepted limitation and independence behavior and map results to the criteria below. It shall distinguish simulated receiving inputs from actual qualified/released/adopted operation, retain the same affected question across limitation cases and show actual fallback responsibility. Unavailable provider or source inputs and unexecuted checks shall remain pending with their reason. ## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Define the receiving-condition and responsibility map from the accepted source clauses, then document recovery against actual project files and their known gaps. Prepare representative fixtures with explicit assumptions. Integrate connector-specific semantics only when their receiving contracts supply them, and bind actual qualification/release/adoption before describing operational PEC use. File-based agent/manager/human work remains usable throughout. Production checks below are future verification obligations; this INIT's schema/checklist checks do not execute them.

- **VER-001** — Inspect App receiving behavior and presentation against the accepted limitation clauses; compare supplied source identities, standing, coverage/freshness and missing metadata with displayed results for qualified/current and each limited/absent condition. Use the relevant agreed connector meanings, recording any undecided comparison as pending.
- **VER-002** — Follow the same affected question through absent, stale, partial and failing fixtures, including no response and unavailable underlying sources. Inspect the visible source route, locate the cited files/revisions when available, and compare conclusions/gaps and the responsible recovery path with their source support.
- **VER-003** — Inspect failure and recovered records for inferred empty work, readiness or permission. Compare each execution/acceptance/checking/approval/reliance assertion with its actual subject, actor and evidence, including cases with only one act evidenced and others unknown. Check that applicable prerequisites are sourced and no generic acceptance-before-other-acts dependency is introduced.
- **VER-004** — Walk the recovery document using only available project graphs, decisions, revisions and other source records, with PEC and later fleet views unavailable. Trace agent reconstruction, manager review/integration and human coordination assignments, then compare claimed performed duties with actual records; list any remaining source or actor input.
- **VER-005** — Exercise the four independent-availability combinations in AC-005 against the receiving contracts or explicitly simulated equivalents. Compare each connector's limitation/recovery result, verify independent supported continuation and inspect that waiting questions keep their actual unsatisfied inputs and data-boundary constraints.
- **VER-006** — Examine a file-route-to-PEC join with the App DEL-07-01 evidence account: compare declared coverage, current limitations, qualification, release and App adoption with the consumed envelope. Repeat with each prerequisite absent or unsupported and inspect continuation/source routing. Treat simulated joins as development evidence; actual adoption requires its own evidence, including any accepted provider limitations.
- **VER-007** — Audit fixture and execution records for candidate/source/input binding, same-question continuity, criterion coverage, performed outcomes, simulation labels and pending inputs. Compare them with V4-EXM-30's receiving/limited/absent examination, without claiming this local fixture set completes the separately owned joined connector witness.
- **VER-008** — Review each excluded act in REQ-008 against the cited owner claims, accepted allocation and receiving code/documentation/test assertions. Check named App deliverable owners and the external/manager/human owners that deterministic ID matching cannot establish; inspect for invented Domains allocation, provider delivery, policy ruling or fleet prerequisite.
