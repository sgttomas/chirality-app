# DEL-07-02 — Connector limitation and source-file recovery paths

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable contributes the App's truthful treatment of optional connector material. Whenever the App shows or records something a connector supplied, the person sees whose it is, whether a basis for relying on it has been established, what state it is in against its sources, what it covers and which conclusions it cannot support. Whenever that material is absent, stale, partial or failing, the question it would have answered can still be answered from the project's own files, along an explicit route that names the sources read, the gaps left and who does each part of the work. Work that has a sound basis without the connector goes on, and a missing feed is never read as empty work, readiness or permission.

It serves OBJ-007, "Consume optional PEC and later Domains capability through explicit receiving contracts with truthful fallback", by supplying the limitation and fallback behaviour common to every connector. The connector-specific contracts and the examination on a candidate belong to the deliverables named in CLM-003. The decomposition row DEL-07-02 in the basis named above defines it as a backend feature slice of PKG-07. Its sources are PRD §2.3 and V4-CON-02…04, HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-61…63, ARCHITECTURE V4-ARC-22 and EXAMINATION V4-EXM-30. The draft 4.0.0 release target names no exit criterion for this deliverable and places Domains in a later increment.

SOW-246 asked for qualified PEC capability to be adopted when available. PEC delivery and adoption are retired (PRD V4-CON-02), so no PEC join is owed; what this deliverable keeps of it is that retained PEC material actually consumed shows its standing and limits (REQ-006).

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The App's connector receiving behaviour. Every item of connector material the App shows or records carries its standing, its source identity, the coverage and freshness supplied with it and the conclusions it does not support; for an affected question the App says, part by part, whether the connector supports reliance or the source-file route is needed. It applies to Domains and to retained PEC compatibility material, each independently. Supports SOW-032, SOW-033, SOW-034, SOW-035 and SOW-036; OBJ-007.
- **OUT-002** — The source-file reconstruction and responsibility route: a statement, readable by the person and by an agent working without the App, of how an affected question is answered from a project's ordinary files and who does each part of that work; and the route account the App records and shows for a question taken along it. Supports SOW-033, SOW-035 and SOW-245; OBJ-007.
- **OUT-003** — The connector availability and fallback case set: constructed inputs for each limitation condition and each independent-availability combination, on one affected question, used to examine OUT-001 and OUT-002. Supports SOW-032, SOW-033, SOW-034, SOW-035, SOW-036 and SOW-245; OBJ-007.

Terms:

- **Connector.** Domains, or retained PEC compatibility material (`DEL-07-01`). Every standing names its connector.
- **Standing.** What the App states about one item of connector material. It has three facets, and each facet value carries the basis it was assessed on.

  | Facet | Values | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|
  | Envelope | `adopted`, `not_adopted`, `outside_coverage`, `unknown` | Whether the receiving owner has established a basis for relying on this connector's material: established for this subject; material exists but the basis is not established; the subject lies outside the adopted coverage; no material, or the basis cannot be determined |
  | Condition | `current`, `stale`, `partial`, `failing`, `absent`, `unknown` | The material's state against its sources: supplied for the revision asked with no stated limitation or failure signal; built from an earlier state of a source that has since changed; some needed coverage is missing; the provider reports failure or its response is unusable; no response at all; the state cannot be determined |
  | Claim tier | PEC: `record`, `presence_advisory`. Domains: `admitted`, `located_not_admitted`. Either: `unknown` | The kind of item, as its connector's receiving contract defines it: `DEL-07-01` for the PEC tiers, `DEL-08-01` for the Domains tiers. A tier not stated is `unknown` |

  When several conditions hold, the one shown follows the order absent, failing, partial, stale, unknown, current, and every one that holds is listed with its reason. Nothing is `current` by default: a facet with no assessment is `unknown`. Absence is established by the receiver, since no response can carry a signal, and with no response the envelope and tier are `unknown`. A tier that belongs to the other connector is refused. Source identity, coverage and freshness are shown as supplied; a value not supplied stays missing. An assessment built from constructed input is marked simulated and stays marked wherever it is shown or recorded. The standing has a published form with an identified version, stating for one item its connector, the three facets, whether reliance is supported and the reasons; a reader validates a standing against that form and treats one that does not conform as `unknown`.
- **Reliance.** An item supports reliance only when its envelope is `adopted`, its condition is `current` and its tier is its connector's reliable tier: `record` for PEC, `admitted` for Domains. Every other combination supports none. A relied item reports only what its cited record states at its revision.
- **Prohibited conclusions.** Connector material, its limitation or its absence never establishes, for any item, that no work remains; that it is ready to start, may be dispatched or is permitted; that it is complete; or that anything is correct because of a presence fact. "Satisfied" is said only of a need for connector material, and such a need is satisfied only when reliance is supported. A satisfied need is not readiness.
- **Receiving record.** What a connector's receiving contract has recorded for one response to a question, or for no response: the standing of the response, a standing for each claim it carries, and whether the source-file route is needed. The common content is defined here; what else a record carries is `DEL-07-01`'s for PEC and `DEL-08-01`'s for Domains.
- **Affected question.** A question that connector material was to answer, identified with the revision asked and, for a question about change, the revision since. Each part of it either has connector reliance for every item it requires or needs the source-file route; a part with no covering material needs the route. A part whose only source is the connector waits, with its missing input named.
- **Source-file route.** Answering an affected question from the project's ordinary files: its graphs, decisions, revisions and other records. Reading files is how the answer is found, not what makes it authoritative.
- **Route account.** The App's record of one affected question taken along the route. It holds the question and its revisions; the trigger, naming the connector and why the route was needed; each source read, with its path, revision, the SHA-256 of the bytes read and its role; what was found, each finding with its source and place; each gap, with its effect and who is responsible for closing it; the conclusions the route supports, those it does not, and the four prohibited ones; each duty with its standing; and the recorder and time.
  - A needed source that cannot be read is a gap, never a guessed answer. Sources that contradict each other are both recorded, and the conclusion stays unsupported until its owner resolves it.
  - A revision is recorded as observed. Content read from the exact Git object at a full commit identity is bound to that commit; content read from the working copy carries no revision claim, whatever label is supplied with it.
  - Accounts are records the App alone writes, so they are kept in the App's records store, in the project's subtree, and never in the project folder. An account is written once and never rewritten; a later reading, or a connector that recovers, produces new records.
  - An account names its format and version. A reader validates it against that version and never reinterprets or upgrades it. An unknown version, an unreadable file and two accounts with one identity are each shown as such, and no winner is chosen. A missing accounts location means no account was found there, and incomplete discovery is shown as incomplete; neither means there is no work.
  - Everything in an account is a recorded claim. Showing it establishes neither that the sources say so, nor that a duty was performed, nor that a need is satisfied. Records the account cites keep their own standing.
  - Another deliverable refers to an account by its location in the store, its identity, its format version and the SHA-256 of its bytes. An identity alone is not a location.
- **Duties.** The work of the route: `locate_compare`, an agent finds the sources and compares them; `review_integrate`, a manager reviews the result and integrates it; `cross_undertaking_coordination`, the person carries whatever crosses undertakings. Each has a standing: `prepared`, `performed`, `outstanding`, or `not_required` with its reason. `performed` requires the actor and the evidence. A brief, a source read, a saved account or a fleet view is never evidence that someone performed a duty.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The standing is a projection of assessments that each connector's receiving owner supplies: this deliverable derives no adoption, admission or freshness of its own and reads no provider wire data. The standing and the route account have published forms with identified versions, as HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-63 requires of coordination formats. Source content is identified by SHA-256 and a Git revision by its full commit identity. Cases use constructed inputs whose expected results are worked from the rules of this scope and never taken from the product's own output.

- **VER-001** — Executable check (observes the standing, reasons, supplied metadata and unsupported conclusions the App shows and records for constructed material in every combination of envelope, condition and tier, against values worked by hand from the facet table, the precedence order and the reliance rule; validates each standing against its published form; and observes, for each response and for no response, that the receiving record carries the response's standing, a standing for each claim and whether the source-file route is needed).
- **VER-002** — Executable check combined with comparison with a named reference (the project's files and Git objects, read without product code): carries one affected question through no response, stale, partial, failing and unavailable-source cases, observes the route shown, the sources and revisions recorded and the gaps with their responsible parties, and compares each recorded content identity and revision with the file or Git object itself. It observes on the file system, without product code, where each route account was written, that none lies in the project folder, and that an account's bytes and SHA-256 are unchanged after later readings and after a later account of the same question. It supplies an account of an unknown format version, an unreadable account and two accounts with one identity and observes what is shown for each. It resolves a reference by its location, identity, format version and SHA-256, compares the SHA-256 with that of the file's bytes, and observes that an identity alone resolves to no account.
- **VER-003** — Executable check (searches everything the App shows and records in the limitation and recovery cases for a prohibited conclusion, and observes that each act keeps its own actor, subject and evidence or is shown unknown, including cases where only one act is evidenced).
- **VER-004** — Judgment by a named role (an independent reviewer who did not write the route statement): follows it on a project's ordinary files with connector material and fleet views unavailable, and judges whether the sources, gaps and duties of the question could be established and whether any duty reads as performed without its actor and evidence.
- **VER-005** — Executable check (derives each connector's standing in the four combinations of AC-005, derives it again with the other connector's inputs removed and observes that the two are identical; observes that App functions using no connector material run with both connectors absent and that a waiting part keeps its missing input named).
- **VER-006** — Executable check (observes what the App shows for constructed PEC compatibility material that is not adopted, adopted in simulation, outside coverage, stale, partial, failing and absent: its standing, its limits, its simulated marking and the route).
- **VER-007** — Judgment by a named role (an independent reviewer who did not write the cases): compares the case set with the conditions and combinations AC-001…AC-006 name, checks that every case is on the one affected question and every constructed input is marked simulated, and that each unexercised case is reported with its reason.
- **VER-008** — Judgment by a named role (an independent reviewer): reads this deliverable's code and what it records against each act REQ-008 excludes and against the allocation in CLM-003 and CLM-005.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-002** — An undecided connector term holds only the work that depends on it. Better means more of the common behaviour built and examined while terms are open; never by guessing a missing term, and never by letting a constructed case settle one.
- **AX-003** — Connectors are optional and independent of each other. Better means less work waiting on a feed: more of an affected question answered from source files, with fewer gaps left without a responsible party; never by making a connector a condition of starting or continuing soundly based work, and never by marking a missing input satisfied.
- **AX-004** — Acts stay with their actors. Better means fewer places where something shown or recorded could be read as a manager's or the person's act; never by leaving an evidenced act unshown, and never by recording a duty as performed without its actor and evidence.
- **AX-005** — A limit shown truthfully is worth more than an answer supplied. Better means more of what the material does not support visible to the person at the point of use, with fewer facets left `unknown` where a basis could have been stated; never by showing `unknown` as `current`, a missing value as complete, or a prepared duty as performed.
