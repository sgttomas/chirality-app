# Workflow-making workspace and registration

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Through the standalone App a person works out a method with an agent, turns what worked into a workflow draft, tries it, reviews it, registers it, finds it again in a later session, runs it on new inputs and refines it; and a person can instead pick a workflow that already exists and run it to completion. This deliverable contributes the workflow library and its registration, drafts and their trials, source-qualified selection, the text by which a selected workflow is given to the agent and the check that it arrived, and the guidance that carries the person through both journeys. It owns the complete result across the contributions of its neighbours, so that no step of the journey is left without an owner.

It serves OBJ-001 (the complete standalone workflow-making loop; PRD §1, §2.1, V4-APP-01; EXAMINATION V4-EXM-10), OBJ-002 (usable behaviour within the chosen App architecture; ARCHITECTURE §3) and OBJ-003 (stable origin and explicit registration; PRD V4-WF-02, V4-WF-03).

Terms the product implements:

- **Library.** A place that holds registered workflows for one origin and source root: a project library, the user library, the App's bundled workflows of one release, or a host library. A **slot** is one workflow name in one library. Workflow identity and the revision method are DEL-02-01's.
- **Revision series.** A slot holds an ordered series of registered revisions. Each revision's bytes are kept unchanged; none is overwritten or deleted. A **published copy** of the slot's latest revision is kept at `.chirality/workflows/<name>/` for readers outside the App and is never the authority.
- **Draft.** A package folder under `.chirality/workflow-drafts/<name>/` in a project or in the user's library, written by the agent, the person or the App. A draft has no workflow identity; it is named by its location and name, and its content by the same content identity a revision would have. Its **base** is the registered revision the App made it from, if any.
- **Review.** The App's presentation of one captured snapshot of a draft or of an existing library entry: its target slot and what registering would do, its content identity and files, `WORKFLOW.md`, what the declared part reads as, package findings, the difference from the prior revision and from the base, its lineage, the same name in other libraries, and whether a clean trial of this version exists. A review is not an act.
- **Registration.** The person's act, captured by DEL-01-04's act control and bound to the exact reviewed content, that makes a revision available in a library. Each registration names the reviewed content it registers and the prior revision of its slot. Its outcome is a new workflow, a new revision of the slot the draft was made from, a registration in place of content already in the slot, a re-confirmation of an identical registered revision, or a refusal (name taken by a workflow this draft was not made from, identical content, package not valid, changed since review, slot moved on).
- **Library standing.** What the App reads for a library entry from its registration ledger, its registration act record and its bytes together: registered; present without a registration record; changed outside registration; registration record incomplete; bundled; a shipped revision held in this library; host-listed; or not established. Files alone are never registration.
- **Selection.** The person's explicit choice of a full identity with its revision. It stays on that revision; a newer revision of the slot is shown, and following it is a new selection.
- **Workflow approval policy.** The person's persistent choice of what admits an unchanged registered revision in a later session. Under "Allow disk records", a fresh check that the ledger line, the registration act record and the stored bytes agree admits it, labelled "Disk consistency checked; approval authenticity unverified". Under "Require verified approval", a registration act captured in this App process or an approval whose keychain seal verifies on this installation admits it; otherwise the App says "Session confirmation required; persistent verification unavailable" and offers re-confirmation. Each selection and run records the policy requested and the evidence applied (disk consistency, keychain-verified approval, or an act in this process).
- **Run text.** The text that gives a selected revision to the agent as the first text element of the turn that starts a run: a line saying an earlier run in the conversation has ended (when there was one), a line naming the workflow, its origin, source root, revision and run, a line telling the agent how to report the workflow finished or propose the next one, a line naming the folder and files of the revision other than `WORKFLOW.md`, and `WORKFLOW.md`'s bytes exactly between a begin and an end marker that carry the name and revision. Each run text is recorded with the content identity of its exact bytes. When a run ends and no run starts with the next turn, one **run-end line** tells the agent that no workflow is in force.
- **Supply check.** The comparison of the run text the App sent with the text the supplier's history returns for that turn. Only an exact match is recorded as supplied; any other result is recorded as supplied but not verified, with its reason. Supplied is never adopted.
- **Proposal and finished report.** The only agent statements the App reads are a completed agent message's last line of the exact form `Next workflow: ‹origin›:‹name›` and a line of the exact form `Workflow finished: ‹origin›:‹name›`. Each yields an offer to the person; neither selects, starts or ends anything.
- **Trial.** Trying one version of a draft. Its **trial text** is one header line naming the draft, its content identity and the trial, saying that it is not registered and not a workflow run, followed by the run text a registered run of that exact content would receive. A **delegated trial** asks the agent of the authoring conversation to run the trial text through one sub-agent and report; a **clean trial** sends it in a fresh conversation. A trial is never a workflow run, a registration, a checking or an acceptance.
- **J1, use an existing workflow.** Through the App a person sets up a project, selects an existing workflow, supplies its inputs, runs it to completion and finds the results, without an agent interpreting the application's engineering controls.
- **J2, create, save, return and reuse.** Through the App a person does useful work, turns it into a workflow draft, trials, reviews and registers it, returns to unfinished work after closing and reopening, and later runs the saved workflow with new inputs under their workflow approval policy.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The App cannot stop an agent writing into a library's folders, so files are never treated as registration: standing is computed from the registration ledger, the registration act record and the bytes together, and a selection is resolved from the stored revision, never from the published copy. A review shows a captured snapshot and registration publishes that snapshot, so the bytes registered are the bytes reviewed whatever happens to the draft afterwards. Run texts and trial texts come from one composer, so a trial shows what a run would receive. A workflow is given to the agent as a text element of the run-start turn and is never placed in the conversation's standing instructions or a skill list, so each run's workflow is visible in the supplier's history and can be checked there. Run preparation, the supply check, trial links and library writes are done by the host process; the web view lists and presents.

- **VER-001** — Judgment by an independent examiner: on an identified build with a real supplier, carries out the journey from an empty project folder through two refinements, and records each native transition, the declared part shown, the identities used and any join that failed.
- **VER-002** — Executable check (observes library standing, selectability and the stored bytes): new and changed drafts are run with review absent, registration absent and both performed, and one existing library entry is registered in place; the draft and library states and the bytes on disk are tested against REQ-002.
- **VER-003** — Executable check (observes the selection's identity, the resolved bytes and the admission result): registration collisions, same-name definitions across the project, user and bundled origins (and the host origin, for AC-013, when a host library exists), a newer revision, an edited and a removed published copy are introduced after a selection, and a relaunch is made under each approval policy with and without a verifying seal, with a tampered settings file and with an unreadable one, before and after keychain protection is set up; overwrite, rebinding, admission and the settings-file disclosure are tested separately.
- **VER-004** — Comparison with a named reference: the run text is compared byte for byte with the text the supplier's history returns for the turn and its body with the stored `WORKFLOW.md`; then requirement, checkpoint and unknown-outcome results supplied by their owners are fed in and what the journey shows is compared with them.
- **VER-005** — Comparison with a named reference: a registration the examiner watched (made at the control by a person or, in an agent-operated journey examination as the release target defines it, by the examining agent on examination material, whose capture is then a synthetic examination artifact that establishes no person's decision) is compared with its record and display (actor, recorder, content, scope, purpose); the four unperformed-act cases and an independent checking act are then applied and the display inspected.
- **VER-006** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: reads the receiving and reuse account and the check results against the accepted decomposition's row, CLM-001…CLM-006 and ARCHITECTURE §3, traces each web-view command to the person's initiating event, and confirms the results name their build and limits.
- **VER-007** — Executable check, with a comparison against the supplier's history: one draft with a supporting file is tried both ways; the trial text is compared with the run text composed after registering the same content; linking and fidelity are read from the supplier's history, including a constructed case where the sub-agent's input differs; a clean trial and a real run are brought back and two trials compared; and the absence of any run record, start offer, registration or review gating is tested.
- **VER-008** — Judgment by an independent examiner who did not build the journey: carries out J1 on an identified build with real execution using only the App's controls and words, and records every point where a state was unclear, a next action was missing or an engineering record had to be read.
- **VER-009** — Judgment by an independent examiner who did not build the journey: carries out J2 across two App sessions in the same way, under each workflow approval policy, and records the same points and the source and revision the later run used.
- **VER-010** — Judgment by an examiner who did not build the views: on the build the journeys and examinations of criteria 1 to 6 and 15 are observed on, compares each view a person meets in them with the corresponding view of v3.0.1 as installed, and records each departure, each inconsistency between views and each thing that reads as unfinished or out of place. Whether the App feels good to use is the owner's verdict and is not this comparison.
- **VER-011** — Comparison with a named reference: the selected Library release's method index and package files, read from the release archive whose digest the project's reference record of the release gives, are compared with the bundled catalogue and with the inventory of the bundled workflow resources, by name, file and byte. No workflow is run.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Required behaviour is fixed and the technical means are open; earlier code is reused only where it meets the same criteria. Better means fewer steps in J1 and J2 at which a person must read an engineering record or have an agent operate a control; never by weakening review, registration, identity or the truth of a shown state.
- **AX-002** — The complete standalone result keeps one integration owner while its contributions are separately owned, and selection, supply, adoption, observed execution and human acts each need their own evidence. Better means more of a run's chain, from selected revision to supplied text, verified against something the App did not write; never by reporting a link as verified on the App's own record of it.
- **AX-003** — Registration is the person's explicit act on reviewed content, and the App can record and present it without becoming its actor. Better means fewer repeated acts asked of the person for the same assurance, as in reuse across sessions; never by counting a file, a tool success, a trial or an agent's statement as registration, and never by lowering the approval policy without the person's own act.
