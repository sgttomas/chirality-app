## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The standalone workspace shall support conversational planning and revision, trial through real native Codex tool execution, conversion of the useful method into a draft, review and explicit registration, reuse on new inputs, and further refinement. A draft is tried through a trial (REQ-009), which is not a run of any workflow identity; only registered revisions run (`APP-V4-DESIGN-PASS-3-20261001-DECISION-K3` K-7, as revised by the owner's direction of 2026-10-10 recorded in Design §4.2). Native inputs remain recognizable through their receiving interfaces, and the workflow declaration remains available in the journey. Workflows are chained within one conversation, one run at a time: sequentially, the person starting the next after one ends, or on the agent's proposal with the person confirming (`APP-V4-DESIGN-PASS-3-20261001-DECISION-L` L-2). A run in the chain ends only as `DEL-01-02` defines ending a run, distinct from interrupting a turn or stopping Codex, and a registration attempt interrupted by a quit or a loss of the App process is reconciled at App start; this workspace consumes those definitions and that App-start event and does not define them. [CLM-001; CLM-002; CLM-003; P1 §§2.1 and 4.1; E1 V4-EXM-10]
- **REQ-002** — A new or changed workflow shall remain a draft until the person reviews the identified content and explicitly registers it. Workflows shipped with an App release are registered by the release (origin bundled); a library entry byte-equal to a shipped revision is recognized as that revision; other library content without a registration record is registered in place by the person's act, several entries per act allowed, each entry's bytes bound (`APP-V4-DESIGN-PASS-3-20261001-DECISION-L` L-4). Registration behavior shall preserve that distinction for later revisions as well as first creation. Draft creation, successful trial execution or an agent's recommendation shall not stand in for either actual review or explicit registration. Explicit registration is performed by the person through the App act control of `DEL-01-04` and is bound to the exact reviewed content; a draft changed after review needs a new review (`APP-V4-DESIGN-PASS-3-20261001-DECISION-K3` K-8). [P1 V4-WF-02; CLM-004]
- **REQ-003** — Registration shall not silently overwrite an existing workflow. The workspace shall make the collision and resulting disposition visible and preserve existing content unless the actual disposition authorizes its change. A draft made from a registered workflow registers as a new revision of it; earlier revisions are kept and every run cites the revision it used; a same-name draft with no such origin is refused with a request for a new name; nothing is overwritten (`APP-V4-DESIGN-PASS-3-20261001-DECISION-K3` K-6). [P1 V4-WF-02; L1 SOW-048]
- **REQ-004** — Each workflow shall retain source-qualified identity across project, user, bundled and host-supplied origins. A selected workflow shall never silently rebind to a same-named definition from another origin when discovery or catalog contents change. [P1 V4-WF-03; L1 SOW-049 and SOW-050]
- **REQ-005** — The workspace shall receive declaration, capability/checkpoint and content-bound record behavior through CLM-003 and CLM-004. It shall preserve the identity of the draft or selected content actually supplied at its native interface and distinguish resolved source, supplied/provider-adopted basis and observed execution. Missing capability, required human checkpoint or unknown outcome shall remain visible in the joined journey. Registration or file portability alone shall not be presented as compatible execution. [B2 DEL-02-02 interfaces; A1 §3; E1 V4-EXM-14; P1 V4-WF-04–05]
- **REQ-006** — Presentation and recording shall distinguish workflow review and registration from execution success, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance. Each attributed human act requires its own actor and evidence; no such act is inferred from another, and no universal ordering between independently evidenced acts is invented. An actually performed human act may be faithfully recorded by the App or agent, with the human decision actor distinct from the recorder and its content, scope and purpose preserved. [CLM-004; P1 V4-AUT-03 and V4-REC-05; H1 V4-HI-25 and V4-HI-30–31; D1 decision 03]
- **REQ-007** — The implementation and receiving account shall retain catalog, reviewed draft-registration and native interaction behavior while assessing prior catalog/UI code only as optional reuse against actual receiving contracts and candidate checks. It shall use the selected stock Codex App integration; no second App engine, generic translation/adapter layer or common service is inferred from this workspace's scope. Responsibility and implementation placement still open in the accepted basis shall remain explicit until the relevant owner decides. [A1 §3 and V4-ARC-20; C1; CLM-002; CLM-003]
- **REQ-009** — A trial shall mirror a real run: for the draft's exact content, the App composes the run text a registered run of that content would receive, under a visible trial header naming the draft and its content identity and stating that it is not registered and not a workflow run. Supporting files reach a trial as they reach a registered run. While iterating, the person can ask the authoring agent, in the conversation where the workflow is being made, to run the trial through one sub-agent and report (a delegated trial); where the App can read the sub-agent's input it shows whether the workflow was given verbatim. At the person's choice, a trial runs in a fresh conversation (a clean trial), whose outcome the person can bring back to the authoring conversation to read and assess. Every message the App prepares for a trial is pre-filled, editable and sent only by the person. Each draft lists its trials by content identity, linked to their conversations; at the person's initiative the App offers trying again, bringing a trial or a real run back to the authoring conversation, and comparing two trials or a trial and a real run. A clean trial is optional: the registration review shows whether one exists for the reviewed version and never requires it, and registering, using and revising a workflow after real use without one remains open to the person. A trial is never a workflow run, registration, checking or acceptance, and offers no workflow start. [Owner direction 2026-10-10 on U-WR-24, Design §4.2; CLM-001; CLM-002; P1 V4-WF-02; E1 V4-EXM-10]
