# DEL-02-01 — Portable workflow contract and shared allocation

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable gives every consumer one meaning of a portable workflow. A workflow is a package whose `WORKFLOW.md` holds prose method guidance and one declared part that a product can observe: the inputs the method expects, the tools it needs, the checkpoints where a human act is required, and the outputs and evidence it returns. The deliverable contributes that declared part's open schema and its reader, the workflow's source-qualified identity with its content-identity method, the four role meanings as the App and a host's single agent seat receive them, and the allocation of each shared contract part to its owner and consumers. The App workspace, workflow execution, records and host consumers build on this one meaning without each defining its own.

It serves OBJ-003 (portable workflows and four-role guidance with stable origin and truthful checkpoints; PRD V4-WF-01, V4-WF-03, V4-SHR-01, V4-SHR-02, V4-ROLE-01, V4-HOST-05, V4-HOST-06), OBJ-004 (accountable shared contracts between App and hosts; PRD V4-SHR-03, ARCHITECTURE V4-ARC-20) and OBJ-005 (human acts stay attributable; PRD V4-WF-05, V4-AUT-03, V4-REC-05). The accepted decomposition assigns it as the API contract of PKG-02. The journeys of publication punchlist P1 and P2 run on its declared part and identity.

Terms the product implements:

- **Workflow package.** A folder whose entry point is `WORKFLOW.md`. Its prose is the method; its declared part states what the method expects, needs, stops for and returns. A declared part grants no tool, permission or act and is not evidence that any expectation was met. A package without one is a readable, selectable method whose requirements are undeclared.
- **Declared part.** The content of the one top-level fenced code block in `WORKFLOW.md` whose info string is `workflow-declaration`: UTF-8 JSON (RFC 8259) whose top level is one object described by the open schema (JSON Schema draft 2020-12). Front matter is skipped; a block quoted inside another block is not read; no block means undeclared; two or more means the declared part is malformed and none is read.
- **Categories.** `expected_inputs`, `required_tools`, `checkpoints`, `returned_outputs` and `returned_evidence`, with `compatible_roles`, `tool_restriction` and `declaration_contract_version`. A category whose member is omitted is **undeclared**; an empty list is **declared empty**; only the second supports "this workflow declares none".
- **Reading.** What the reader reports for the declared part, each category and each element: undeclared, declared empty, recognized, invalid or not established. An unrecognized element or a newer contract version is preserved unchanged and reported; in the required-tool or checkpoint category it makes that reading not established, never a pass.
- **Required tool.** A reference of class `host_operation`, carrying the host catalog's operation identity and version as the catalog writes them, or of class `harness_capability`, carrying one of the portable capability names (`shell-command`, `file-change`, `web-search`, `agent-delegation`, `mcp-tool-call`, `dynamic-tool-call`, `person-input-request`, `image-view`, `image-generation`, `plan-update`), each of which resolves to the supplier facts DEL-01-01 reports for its selected supplier version. Each states its purpose, its necessity with any fallback, and the stages that use it. Versions are compared for equality only.
- **Requirement outcome.** The names a consumer uses when it checks a declared requirement against a host: present; present, currently unavailable; missing; version mismatch; not exposed on this surface; channel not enabled; not established. A check **passes** when every required reference is present or present but currently unavailable and the workflow's compatible roles and delegation need can be met; a required reference that cannot be evaluated makes the check **not established**; any other shortfall means it **does not pass**. Optional references never block a pass. An undeclared workflow's check is not established, never passed and never "no requirements".
- **Checkpoint.** A declared point where one human act is required. It names exactly one required act, which is one of the five checkpoint acts among the kinds DEL-04-01 defines (marking checked, accepting a proposal, engineering approval, professional reliance, changing the grant), when it is reached (before a named tool is dispatched, when a named output is produced, or on a named host outcome), its subject, scope and purpose, its actor (the person or the accountable professional), the path on a negative decision, a mixed decision or an absent subject, the evidence expected of the act, the actions it holds, and whether it is governed and whether a fresh act is required.
- **Checkpoint disposition.** The names every consumer uses for what is known of a checkpoint in a run: not reached; waiting (reached, act not yet recorded); performed; resolved negatively; lapsed (a performed act's bound content changed after the run ended); unknown (the deciding observation was lost). Unknown and waiting are never shown as performed.
- **Returned output and evidence.** What the method promises to produce: a host change, a file, a message, an input to another workflow or a human-act standing, with its destination and promised standing; and the evidence it will return by reference. A promise is not an observation.
- **Identity.** {kind, origin, source root, name, revision}, with **derived-from** for an adapted workflow or a revision made from another. Origin is project, user, bundled or host. A name and origin without a revision name a library slot, not content. The **holding library** is where a copy is held; it is recorded beside the identity and never takes part in identity equality.
- **Revision.** The content identity of every regular file beneath the package folder. The App's method is designated `chirality.app.workflow-package.sha256/v1`: the lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 of the ASCII designation and one NUL byte, the file count as an unsigned 64-bit big-endian integer, then for each file, ordered by the UTF-8 bytes of its `/`-separated relative path, the path's byte length, the path bytes, the content's byte length and the stored content bytes, each length an unsigned 64-bit big-endian integer. Nothing is normalized or excluded. A link, another non-regular entry, an unreadable file or a path that is not UTF-8 makes the revision not established. A host uses its own designated method or adopts this one; values under different designations are not comparable.
- **Identity chain.** Listed, selected, resolved, supplied, adopted by the provider and observed behaviour are separate facts. A matching name at one link establishes nothing about another; supplied is not adopted; a link without evidence is unknown.
- **Carried and adapted.** A workflow carried unchanged into another library keeps its identity. An adaptation is a new identity whose derived-from is the original; it never edits the original's history.
- **Roles and the host seat.** HELP_HUMAN (alignment with the human), HELPS_HUMANS (design), WORKING_ITEMS (managed execution) and TASK (bounded execution, does not delegate). A host presents one agent seat and the selected workflow and need not offer a role choice; the role meaning under which the seat runs is recorded with the run, or recorded as unknown.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The contract keeps the four role meanings and the host's single seat, and keeps `WORKFLOW.md`, `SKILL.md` and `AGENTS.md` as open guidance a person can read in an ordinary editor without Chirality software, generated indexes or a running host. Every declared element carries or sits beside a short readable statement of its meaning. A host is not required to offer the App's role choice, and a host keeps its own workflows, skills and tools. [SOW-021; SOW-022; SOW-038; PRD V4-HOST-05/06, V4-SHR-01/02]
- **REQ-002** — The declared part distinguishes expected inputs, required tools, checkpoints requiring human acts, returned outputs and returned evidence, with the prose method kept. Host-operation requirements carry the catalog's own operation identity; harness-capability requirements carry a portable name that resolves to the supplier's reported facts. The schema is open and selects no host or supplier wire field. The reader reports a reading for every category and element and never rejects a whole package for one element's failure. [SOW-039; SOW-042…SOW-045; PRD V4-WF-01; ARCHITECTURE M-3]
- **REQ-003** — A checkpoint declares one required human act and never states that an act was performed. Execution success, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance are not interchangeable: each has its own actor, subject and evidence, evidence of one establishes none of the others, a routine tool permission satisfies no checkpoint, and no rule requires proposal acceptance before another independently evidenced act. In the current phase a declared checkpoint is plan guidance: the declaration still states the required act, when it is reached, its subject and the actions it holds, and the act is recorded only when the person performs it. [SOW-037; SOW-044; PRD V4-WF-05, V4-AUT-01/03/05, V4-REC-05; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-31/33/42]
- **REQ-004** — Declaration meaning and identity are the same for App and host consumers. The identity is what a selection names: it carries enough to resolve a selection to the same bytes, and a matching name is never the same workflow. Every discovery that finds a name in more than one origin reports all of them, with their holding libraries, as a collision. What a declared part promises to return stays distinguishable from observed production and from human acts: no reading reports a promised output or evidence as produced, and an inference from success to an act is reported as incompatible. Keeping the App's selection from rebinding is DEL-02-02's. [PRD V4-WF-01/03/06, V4-EXE-03]

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The declared part is carried as one fenced block inside `WORKFLOW.md` so that a single file travels with the package and the agent that reads the method receives the declaration with it. It is JSON under a JSON Schema with Chirality's own snake_case member names, so that any language's standard library reads it. Values owned elsewhere are carried as their owners write them: act kinds from DEL-04-01, operation identity and version from DEL-03-01, outcome names from DEL-03-02. The reader retains failures as findings instead of rejecting a package, so a prose-only or partly declared workflow stays usable. The revision is computed over a captured snapshot of the package, so the identity names exactly the bytes that were read.

- **VER-001** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: reads the contract and a host-origin and an App-origin package against PRD V4-HOST-05/06, V4-SHR-01/02 and V4-ROLE-01…03, and confirms role meaning, host guidance and origin are kept and no App role choice is required.
- **VER-002** — Comparison with a named reference: the schema and its examples are validated by a JSON Schema 2020-12 validator that imports no product code, block recognition is compared with an independent CommonMark parser on cases with indentation, tabs, nesting and quoted examples, and the reader's categories and readings are compared with those results.
- **VER-003** — Executable check (observes the reader's reading of checkpoint elements): declarations with no act, two acts, an act DEL-04-01 defines that is not one of the five checkpoint acts (proposing, applying, requesting), an act DEL-04-01 does not define, a success-as-act claim and a checking checkpoint without prior acceptance are read, and each reading is tested against REQ-003.
- **VER-004** — Comparison with a named reference: revisions are compared with digests computed from the stated framing by a script that imports no product code; then same-named packages from different origins are discovered and the collision report is compared with the packages placed, an identity is resolved again and its bytes compared with the snapshot it named, and the reading of returned outputs and evidence is inspected for any statement of production.
- **VER-005** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: reads the allocation against each named consumer's own scope and stated confirmation, ARCHITECTURE V4-ARC-20 and PRD V4-SHR-03, and reports any part without a consumer, any confirmation inferred from a document's existence and any placement recorded without its owners' decision.
- **VER-006** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: checks REQ-006 act by act against CLM-002, CLM-003 and CLM-004 and the accepted decomposition's rows for those owners.
- **VER-007** — Executable check (observes the check results and the second reader's output): the check inventory is run on identified packages, its coverage is compared with REQ-001…REQ-006, the second reader's extracted declared part is compared with the product reader's, and cases not run are listed with the missing input.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Priority is maintainability, then functionality, then local models and data privacy. Implementation is shared only where a repeated consumer responsibility and its maintenance justify it. Better means fewer places where two consumers read the same declaration or identity differently; never by imposing a common service, library or placement its owners have not agreed.
- **AX-002** — Guidance stays open and readable, and identity names actual content. Better means more of a workflow's expectations observable by a product from its declared part; never by making a package unreadable without Chirality software, by rejecting a prose-only package, or by treating a matching name as the same workflow.
- **AX-003** — Execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance stay separately attributable; hosts offer, record and present, and people perform. Better means fewer checkpoint declarations whose act, subject or moment is not established; never by letting success or one act's evidence stand for another act.
- **AX-004** — What is undecided stays stated as undecided. Better means more consumers confirmed against the contract by their own owners; never by recording a placement, a seat mapping or a confirmation that its owner has not given.
