# Workflow execution compatibility and round-trip support

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

When a person runs a selected workflow, this deliverable makes what the workflow declared actionable. It tells the person which of the workflow's required tools the current host provides and which are missing. It opens and ends the run and keeps the run's own history. It records each arrival at a declared checkpoint, the agent's request for the act where one can be identified, and the act only when the person performs it. And it carries a workflow between the App and a host, in either direction, with its identity, declared requirements and act history intact. The result is that a person can see, for any run, what the workflow needed, where it paused for them, and what they actually did.

It serves OBJ-003 (portable workflows with truthful checkpoints; PRD V4-WF-04, V4-WF-05, V4-WF-06) and OBJ-005 (human acts and run provenance stay attributable and reconstructable; PRD V4-AUT-03, V4-REC-05; EXAMINATION V4-EXM-14, V4-EXM-22). The accepted decomposition assigns it as a backend feature of PKG-02, with host execution supplied by the external host owner. The journeys of publication punchlist P1 and P2 run as its runs.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — CODE: the requirement check with its report, the run and its history, and checkpoint recording. Covers SOW-051, SOW-052 and SOW-053; supports OBJ-003 and OBJ-005.
- **OUT-002** — The App/host workflow transfer and adaptation contract and the App behaviour that implements its App side: what is carried, what each side reports, and the trace that keeps original and revised identities apart. Covers SOW-054 and SOW-055; supports OBJ-003.
- **OUT-003** — TEST: missing-tool, checkpoint-recording and source-preserving round-trip checks, including hold cases for a workflow that takes up the governance phase. Covers SOW-051, SOW-052, SOW-053, SOW-054 and SOW-055; supports OBJ-003 and OBJ-005.

Terms the product implements:

- **Run.** One use of a selected revision in a conversation, from the person's start to its end, named by a run reference and tagged on its conversation. A run ends only by the person's explicit end of it. A turn interrupt, a stop or exit of Codex, an App quit, a relaunch and a lost observation never end a run: the run is interrupted and is recovered as the same run. An ended run is never resumed; carrying on is a new run that inherits no arrival, disposition or act.
- **Requirement check.** An evaluation of the selected workflow's declared required tools against the capability information of the current host on the surface the agent acts through. Its **report** names the workflow, the host, the catalog edition and the surface, and gives each requirement's outcome with its purpose and reason, and the result. Outcomes, the pass rule and the three results (passes, does not pass, not established) are DEL-02-01's. A report is evidence of the one evaluation it records.
- **Arrival.** The App's observation that a run reached a declared checkpoint: the checkpoint's reached-when condition observed among the supplier's items the App saw live, or reported by a host. An arrival is recorded with its ordinal, time, bound subject, purpose and scope.
- **Checkpoint record.** The entries this deliverable writes in the run's own log: each declared checkpoint listed at run start with whether its arrival can be evaluated on this surface; each declaration finding; each arrival; a request for the act where it is a structured item the App observed; each act counted toward an arrival, and each act on the subject that did not count with its reason; a decline; a lapse; a lost or recovered observation; an act after the run's end; the run's end; and any interval in which nothing could be written. Dispositions are DEL-02-01's names and can be rebuilt from the entries. What an entry means when shown is stated here: an arrival with no act recorded is reached and not yet answered, never a held, blocked or paused run, and an interrupted run is interrupted, not ended. `DEL-04-02` defines the overlay that shows a disposition, and `DEL-01-04` places it and owns its behaviour in the App.
- **Current phase and governance phase.** In the current phase a declared checkpoint is plan guidance: the person and the agents manage any pause, and nothing holds the run. In the governance phase a checkpoint declared governed holds the run: none of its held actions is taken until the act. That definition of a hold is kept so that a loop or a host route can realise it for a workflow that needs one (TBD-006).
- **Transfer trace.** The separate facts recorded when a workflow moves or is used: what was selected and resolved, what was supplied, what the provider adopted and what behaviour was observed. Each says whether it concerns the original or the revised identity; a revised identity never inherits a fact observed for the original, and a fact without evidence is reported as absent or unknown.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The selected workflow's declared required tools are checked against the current host's capability information, and the person is told which requirements are missing or unsupported, each with its purpose and reason, and on what workflow, host, catalog edition and surface the check was made. A portable file or a registration is never evidence of compatible execution: a pass is stated as a passed requirement check on its basis, never as "compatible" or "will run", and an undeclared workflow is shown as requirements undeclared with the check not established, never as having no requirements. [SOW-051; PRD V4-WF-04; EXAMINATION V4-EXM-14]
- **REQ-002** — At a declared checkpoint the required human act is requested, and it is recorded as done only when the person performs it, even where the applicable autonomy otherwise allows direct application. In the current phase the agent carrying out the workflow requests the act; the App and a host's loop give the agent the checkpoint, offer the means to act, and record the arrival, the request where it can be identified and the act. They neither request in the agent's place nor otherwise react to the arrival: they do not pause, interrupt, refuse, prompt or add guidance because of it, and they do not report a workflow unsupported because a hold cannot be enforced. An earlier act of the required kind whose bound content is still current counts toward an arrival and is cited with its time, and several acts may answer one arrival together, each citing what it covers; a workflow that takes up the governance phase may require a fresh act instead. Holding the run until the act is the governance-phase definition, kept for workflows that need it (TBD-006). A run interrupted by a Codex stop, an App relaunch or a lost observation is recovered as the same run, and its history keeps each checkpoint's identity and actual disposition; what was not observed is recorded as unknown, never filled in. [SOW-052; SOW-053; PRD V4-WF-05; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-42; EXAMINATION V4-EXM-14, V4-EXM-22]
- **REQ-003** — Evidence of a performed checkpoint act is received and recorded with DEL-04-03's binding to content, scope and purpose, the person as actor kept apart from the recorder. Execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance each need their own actor and evidence: success, a queued proposal, a receipt, a routine tool-permission answer, an answer to a supplier question, a conversation statement or evidence of another act supplies none of them. No independently evidenced act needs proposal acceptance first. When bound content changes, the act is recorded as lapsed against the affected content and is never carried onto the revised content; in the current phase nothing is held again. [SOW-053; PRD V4-AUT-03, V4-REC-03, V4-REC-05; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-31, V4-HI-32, V4-HI-33]
- **REQ-004** — A workflow registered in the App can be carried into a host for adaptation. The carriage keeps its source-qualified identity and revision with its method, every file of the package, its declared tool and checkpoint meaning, and the trace from the content selected and resolved to the content supplied, adopted and observed. Carried unchanged it keeps its identity; adapted it is a new identity derived from the original. A receiving capability the host lacks is recorded explicitly. [SOW-054; PRD V4-WF-03, V4-WF-04, V4-WF-06; EXAMINATION V4-EXM-14]
- **REQ-005** — A host's workflow can be opened and refined in the App. Opening keeps its origin and revision; a refinement is a draft that goes through DEL-02-02's review and registration; the host's run and act history is shown as history under the host's identity and never becomes an act or disposition of an App run. Adaptation never rebinds a selection to a same-named source and never manufactures a past checkpoint act. [SOW-055; PRD V4-WF-02, V4-WF-03, V4-WF-05, V4-WF-06; EXAMINATION V4-EXM-14]
- **REQ-006** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: declaration and shared-allocation design (`DEL-02-01`) and workflow review and registration (`DEL-02-02`), as CLM-001 cites; catalog meaning (`DEL-03-01`), proposal and outcome meaning (`DEL-03-02`), grant display (`DEL-04-02`), the external adapter and its carriage (`DEL-03-03`), supplier observation (`DEL-01-01`), custody and tags (`DEL-01-02`), the App act control (`DEL-01-04`), operation policy (`DEL-04-01`) and the act and run record format, writer and reader (`DEL-04-03`), as CLM-002 cites; host-loop receiving design (`DEL-05-01`) and the joined round-trip witness (`DEL-09-06`), as CLM-003 cites. - **AC-001** — For an identified selected workflow and host, the check reports each required tool that is available and names each that is missing, at another version, not exposed on the surface, or on a disabled channel, with its purpose and reason and the catalog edition and surface checked. A workflow that registered successfully but lacks a requirement is reported as not passing; an undeclared workflow as not established. Verify by VER-001. [REQ-001]

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Recording is observation, not enforcement: the recorder reads what the App observed and writes it through DEL-04-03's writer, and its working state is disposable because every disposition can be rebuilt from the entries. The check is evaluated per occasion and its report is kept, so an earlier evaluation is never rewritten when the host's catalog changes. A run is found again after a relaunch through the tag DEL-01-02 keeps, so interruption never needs a second run. Host execution, the host's catalog and the host's act facility are supplied by the external host owner; where they are not available the corresponding results stay not established.

- **VER-001** — Executable check (observes the report and what the person is shown): an available-tool case and a missing, version-mismatched, not-exposed and disabled-channel case are run against a host catalog the product did not write, including a workflow that registered and lacks a requirement and an undeclared workflow, and each row and result is tested against the declared requirement and that catalog.
- **VER-002** — Comparison with a named reference: a run is driven to a declared checkpoint under direct-application autonomy with no act, then interrupted, replayed, relaunched and finally answered by the person's act, and the recorded arrivals, requests and dispositions are compared at each step with the supplier's recorded items and the act control's capture; an arrival is answered by an earlier act of the required kind on content still current, and another by two acts together, and the citations are read; a turn interrupt and a quit are made and the run is read as open, then the person ends the run and carries on, and the new run's record is inspected for any inherited arrival, disposition or act; hold cases are run only for a workflow that takes up the governance phase.
- **VER-003** — Comparison with a named reference: an act the examiner watched the person perform is compared with the checkpoint record (actor, recorder, content, scope, purpose), then the no-act, other-kind, success-only, tool-permission and changed-content cases and an independent checking act are applied and the record inspected.
- **VER-004** — Comparison with a named reference: an App-registered workflow is carried to a host fixture and its identity, revision and bytes at the destination are compared with the source by a digest computed outside the product, the declared part is read there, an adaptation's derived-from is inspected, and an unsupported receiving case is run; live-host evidence that is unavailable is reported as such.
- **VER-005** — Executable check, with a byte comparison against the host package: a host-origin workflow is opened, refined and registered in the App with a same-named App workflow present and with interruption and replay in its history; origin, revision, derived-from, selections and act history are tested before and after.
- **VER-006** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: examines this deliverable's code and outputs, act by act, against REQ-006, CLM-001…CLM-003 and the accepted decomposition's rows, inspects each result that depends on TBD-003…TBD-006 for a filled-in default, and reads what is produced for any statement that host construction or the joined witness is done.
- **VER-007** — Executable check (observes the check results and their coverage): the check inventory is run on an identified build, its coverage is compared with REQ-001…REQ-005, and cases not run are listed with the missing input.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Shared meaning allocates no common service; stock Codex stays the standalone App's executor, and host construction stays with its owner. Better means more surfaces on which a declared requirement or a checkpoint arrival can be evaluated at all; never by adding an App engine, interposing on the supplier's calls, or claiming a hold the product cannot enforce.
- **AX-002** — A tool result, a passing check or a successful transfer is evidence only of its own subject, and a performed act is recorded with its true actor. Better means fewer checkpoints and requirements left unknown or not established in a run's history; never by recording an act that was not observed, or by filling a gap in observation with an inferred event.
- **AX-003** — Recording serves the person's own judgement of where the plan paused and what was done. Better means a run history from which more of what happened can be rebuilt after interruption; never by reacting to an arrival in the current phase, or by letting a record say performed without the act.
