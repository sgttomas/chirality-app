# Workflow execution compatibility and round-trip support

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

When a person runs a selected workflow, this deliverable makes what the workflow declared actionable. It tells the person which of the workflow's required tools the current host provides and which are missing. It opens and ends the run and keeps the run's own history. It records each arrival at a declared checkpoint, the agent's request for the act where one can be identified, and the act only when the person performs it. It records each point the agent places in conversation and shows the conversation what took effect. And it carries a workflow between the App and a host, in either direction, with its identity, declared requirements and act history intact. The result is that a person can see, for any run, what the workflow needed, where it paused for them, and what they actually did.

It serves OBJ-003 (portable workflows with truthful checkpoints; PRD V4-WF-04, V4-WF-05, V4-WF-06) and OBJ-005 (human acts and run provenance stay attributable and reconstructable; PRD V4-AUT-03, V4-REC-05; EXAMINATION V4-EXM-14, V4-EXM-22). The accepted decomposition assigns it as a backend feature of PKG-02, with host execution supplied by the external host owner.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — CODE: the requirement check with its report, the run and its history, checkpoint recording, and the record of a point placed in conversation. Covers SOW-051, SOW-052 and SOW-053, and REQ-008 from PRD V4-AUT-07 and V4-AUT-08 and release target criterion 2; supports OBJ-003 and OBJ-005.
- **OUT-002** — The App/host workflow transfer and adaptation contract and the App behaviour that implements its App side: what is carried, what each side reports, and the trace that keeps original and revised identities apart. Covers SOW-054 and SOW-055; supports OBJ-003.
- **OUT-003** — TEST: missing-tool, checkpoint-recording and source-preserving round-trip checks, including hold cases for a workflow that takes up the governance phase. Covers SOW-051, SOW-052, SOW-053, SOW-054 and SOW-055; supports OBJ-003 and OBJ-005.

Terms the product implements:

- **Run.** One use of a selected revision in a conversation, from the person's start to its end, named by a run reference and tagged on its conversation. A run ends only by the person's explicit end of it. A turn interrupt, a stop or exit of Codex, an App quit, a relaunch and a lost observation never end a run: the run is interrupted and is recovered as the same run. An ended run is never resumed; carrying on is a new run that inherits no arrival, disposition or act.
- **Requirement check.** An evaluation of the selected workflow's declared required tools against the capability information of the current host on the surface the agent acts through. Its **report** names the workflow, the host, the catalog edition and the surface, and gives each requirement's outcome with its purpose and reason, and the result. Outcomes, the pass rule and the three results (passes, does not pass, not established) are DEL-02-01's. A report is evidence of the one evaluation it records.
- **Arrival.** The App's observation that a run reached a declared checkpoint: the checkpoint's reached-when condition observed among the supplier's items the App saw live, or reported by a host. An arrival is recorded with its ordinal, time, bound subject, purpose and scope.
- **Checkpoint record.** The entries this deliverable writes in the run's own log: each declared checkpoint listed at run start with whether its arrival can be evaluated on this surface; each declaration finding; each arrival; a request for the act where it is a structured item the App observed; each act counted toward an arrival, and each act on the subject that did not count with its reason; a decline; a lapse; a lost or recovered observation; an act after the run's end; the run's end; and any interval in which nothing could be written. Dispositions are DEL-02-01's names and can be rebuilt from the entries. What an entry means when shown is stated here: an arrival with no act recorded is reached and not yet answered, never a held, blocked or paused run, and an interrupted run is interrupted, not ended. `DEL-04-02` defines the overlay that shows a disposition, and `DEL-01-04` places it and owns its behaviour in the App.
- **Current phase and governance phase.** In the current phase a declared checkpoint is plan guidance: whether it asks the agent to pause is the person's to define and the agent can set it too (PRD V4-AUT-07), such a pause is the agent's cooperation, and nothing holds the run. In the governance phase a checkpoint declared governed holds the run: none of its held actions is taken until the act. That definition of a hold is kept so that a loop or a host route can realise it for a workflow that needs one (TBD-006).
- **Transfer trace.** The separate facts recorded when a workflow moves or is used: what was selected and resolved, what was supplied, what the provider adopted and what behaviour was observed. Each says whether it concerns the original or the revised identity; a revised identity never inherits a fact observed for the original, and a fact without evidence is reported as absent or unknown.
- **Recorded point.** A point (PRD V4-AUT-07) that the agent places in a conversation through the App's point tool, version 1, a dynamic tool the App offers on every conversation it starts. An admitted call is recorded once, as a record of kind `point_placed` written through `DEL-04-03`'s writer in the part of the App's records store that belongs to the conversation's App project, bound to that part by the store's stable project identity. The record holds the terms as the App admitted them, their digest and its method, the call it answers (App session generation, conversation, turn, call and request identity), the App project and the store's project identity, the run reading, and what the App observed: that the conversation's agent called the point tool, whether the App started that turn at a press of the person's, and that whether a delegation covers the placement is not established. Through `DEL-01-02`'s tags the conversation keeps an offer pointer when it is first indexed under its project; before its first record is appended, an offer pointer that also carries the App project and the store's project identity, which binds the conversation's points to those records; and for each record a pointer holding only identities (the point, the record, the call, the App project and the store's project identity) and the digest. No pointer holds the terms, the run reading or the observation. The terms, their closed values included, are the agent's supplied text: never a control label, an identity, an authority or the App's own statement. The **run reading** is the run's reference where a run is open in the conversation, no run open in this conversation in this App session, or not established with its reason: a run's start sent and not yet seen open; Codex restarted in this App session while a run of the conversation was open or starting; or a conversation that began in an earlier App session, whose runs the App cannot tell. A record is **sound** when it is found before any limit in its log, is unique in its log by point reference, by record identity and by call, holds a record identity the store's project-wide index finds once in the project's records, did not come from an imported records package, is valid for version 1, and its digest recomputes equal. The **point recorder** is the one worker of each App session that places points, checks a resumed conversation's records and reads a points log again, fed by a bounded queue; each reading it makes is **published** with the stamps of the log and of the project's link to the store. The App's own limits on points are its own choice: 16,384 bytes of canonical terms for a point, 100 points for a conversation, the 20 latest refusals of a conversation kept for its view in an App session, 16 jobs waiting for the point recorder, and a published reading read again at least once a minute while it is in use.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Recording is observation, not enforcement: the recorder reads what the App observed and writes it through DEL-04-03's writer, and its working state is disposable because every disposition can be rebuilt from the entries. The check is evaluated per occasion and its report is kept, so an earlier evaluation is never rewritten when the host's catalog changes. A run is found again after a relaunch through the tag DEL-01-02 keeps, so interruption never needs a second run. Host execution, the host's catalog and the host's act facility are supplied by the external host owner; where they are not available the corresponding results stay not established.

- **VER-001** — Executable check (observes the report and what the person is shown): an available-tool case and a missing, version-mismatched, not-exposed and disabled-channel case are run against a host catalog the product did not write, including a workflow that registered and lacks a requirement and an undeclared workflow, and each row and result is tested against the declared requirement and that catalog.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Shared meaning allocates no common service; stock Codex stays the standalone App's executor, and host construction stays with its owner. Better means more surfaces on which a declared requirement or a checkpoint arrival can be evaluated at all; never by adding an App engine, interposing on the supplier's calls, or claiming a hold the product cannot enforce.
- **AX-002** — A tool result, a passing check or a successful transfer is evidence only of its own subject, and a performed act is recorded with its true actor. Better means fewer checkpoints and requirements left unknown or not established in a run's history; never by recording an act that was not observed, or by filling a gap in observation with an inferred event.
- **AX-003** — Recording serves the person's own judgement of where the plan paused and what was done. Better means a run history from which more of what happened can be rebuilt after interruption; never by pausing, interrupting or refusing because of an arrival in the current phase, or by letting a record say performed without the act.
