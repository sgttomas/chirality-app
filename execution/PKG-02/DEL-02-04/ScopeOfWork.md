# DEL-02-04 — Additive role selection and supply

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

A person chooses how the agent in a conversation relates to them: as HELP_HUMAN, HELPS_HUMANS or WORKING_ITEMS, or with no role. The App adds the chosen role's guidance to the harness's own instructions without replacing them, gives delegated children their role and TASK guidance the same way, and tells the truth about which role limits are only stated and which the harness enforces. This deliverable contributes the App's shipped guidance documents and role set, the role choice, the composition and supply of guidance at a conversation's start, the record of what was actually supplied, and the account of role limits and of observed departures from them.

It serves OBJ-003 (the same four roles, supplied additively, with a bounded executor that does not delegate; PRD V4-APP-03, V4-ROLE-01, V4-ROLE-02, V4-ROLE-03), OBJ-001 (the guidance contribution to the native workflow-making loop) and OBJ-002 (the guidance contribution to the chosen App architecture; ARCHITECTURE §3). The accepted decomposition assigns it as a backend feature of PKG-02. No exit criterion of the draft 4.0.0 release target names this deliverable; every conversation of the journeys in criteria 1 and 2 starts with its supply.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — CODE: the shipped guidance documents and role set, conversation-role selection, TASK guidance for delegation, and additive composition and supply through the supplier's native inputs. Covers SOW-013, SOW-057, SOW-058 and SOW-126; supports OBJ-001, OBJ-002 and OBJ-003.
- **OUT-002** — The supplied-guidance and enforcement-limit account the App keeps and shows: the supply record of each conversation start, the limit account shown where a role is chosen, and the observations of a stated limit not kept. Covers SOW-057, SOW-059 and SOW-126; supports OBJ-001, OBJ-002 and OBJ-003.
- **OUT-003** — TEST: role-selection and supplied-basis checks, including unenforced limits and the treatment of reused code. Covers SOW-013, SOW-057, SOW-058, SOW-059 and SOW-126; supports OBJ-001, OBJ-002 and OBJ-003.

Terms the product implements:

- **Role set.** The four roles as data shipped with the release: for each its name, meaning, guidance document, whether it may delegate, the child roles it offers and whether a new conversation preselects it. HELP_HUMAN offers HELPS_HUMANS, WORKING_ITEMS and TASK as children; HELPS_HUMANS and WORKING_ITEMS offer TASK; TASK offers none. The role set is not editable.
- **Guidance documents.** The product guidance (`AGENTS.md`) and one document per role. They are the App's own documents, maintained as versioned source in this repository, and the packaged copy equals that source byte for byte.
- **Guidance store.** The person's editable copy of the shipped guidance in the App's own data folder. Each file is in one state: default (equal to the shipped default), modified, missing or unreadable.
- **Composition.** The text supplied at a conversation's start: the product guidance's bytes, then, when a role is chosen, a heading line naming the active role and the role document's bytes. Nothing is normalized. Each part's source, byte range and content identity are recorded, the identity designated `chirality.app.exact-bytes.sha256/v1`: the lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 of the exact bytes.
- **Supply record.** One record per conversation start, including a refused start, and per fork: the selection, the role set's identity, the composition's identity and length, each part with its source and state, the child-role entries, what the supplier reported, the outcome, and adoption recorded as unknown.
- **Limit account.** For each role limit, its standing: stated and not enforced; enforced by the supplier through a named mechanism; or unknown. The limits are that a task agent does not delegate and that work stays within a brief's write targets.
- **Limit observation.** The App's record that a child started with TASK guidance delegated.
- **The facts kept apart.** Selection, source resolution, supply, provider adoption and observed behaviour are separate facts about guidance; a consumer's adoption of guidance is a sixth, recorded by its own owner.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — There are exactly four standing roles: HELP_HUMAN for alignment with the human, HELPS_HUMANS for design, WORKING_ITEMS for managed execution and TASK for bounded execution. HELP_HUMAN, HELPS_HUMANS and WORKING_ITEMS are offered as conversation roles, at a conversation's start and in "Continue as". TASK is not a conversation role: it is the role a manager assigns bounded work to, and its guidance is supplied for that delegation. A conversation may also run with no role, which is not a fifth role and is supplied the product guidance alone. A new conversation's preselection follows the role set's default as data, shown and clearable. A conversation's role is fixed for its life; another role is a new conversation, and a fork keeps its source's role and guidance. A domain-specific expression such as the SWB Piping Designer stays within one of the four roles. An agent cannot choose or change a conversation's role. A role set that does not name exactly the four roles, or marks more than one default, refuses every start. [SOW-013; PRD V4-APP-03, V4-ROLE-01, V4-ROLE-03]
- **REQ-002** — The selected role's guidance is supplied in addition to the harness's own instructions, which stay intact. The composition is supplied once, at the conversation's start; nothing is supplied again on resume or fork, and no workflow is ever placed in it. Delegated children receive the product guidance and their role through the supplier's native agent-role configuration where the supplier supports it, added without replacing the person's own definitions; a child started without a role type has unknown guidance, and the account says so. Role supply changes nothing else in the person's Codex configuration. No earlier product default, packaging location or implementation stands as present authority for what is supplied. [SOW-057; SOW-126; PRD V4-ROLE-02]
- **REQ-003** — TASK guidance states that a task agent does not delegate. The harness's actual enforcement is described separately: the task limit is presented as "Stated, not enforced" unless the supplier enforces it through a named mechanism; any delegation a task agent makes is recorded and shown; and the App does not override the person's Codex configuration to enforce it. The person's configured delegation depth, approval policy, sandbox and a worktree are never reported as enforcement of a role limit. A modified role document makes that role's limits unknown. The other roles keep native delegation as the person's configuration allows. [SOW-058; SOW-059; PRD V4-ROLE-03; ARCHITECTURE §3]
- **REQ-004** — Supplied role guidance is traceable to its source identity and actual bytes, and selection, source resolution, supply, provider adoption, observed behaviour and consumer adoption are kept apart (CLM-004). Provider adoption is recorded as unknown. The supply record and limit observations are handed to the record and adoption owners of CLM-002 in a form they can use without defining their formats. A changed file reaches new conversations only; an open conversation whose guidance has since changed shows "Guidance changed since this conversation started", naming the file, and nothing is sent to it. ## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The composition is carried in one place only, the supplier's additive developer-instruction input at the start of a thread, and never in its base-instruction input, so the harness's own instructions cannot be displaced. Nothing is sent on resume or fork because the supplier keeps the guidance a thread started with. Child roles are carried as agent-role entries of the thread's own configuration, so no process-wide setting is changed. Guidance is composed and records are written by the App's host process; the web view shows the role choice and the store's state. Guidance bytes are read once and the same bytes are hashed and supplied, so identity and content cannot differ. Child-role supply is used only at a supplier version that this deliverable has examined for the agent-role configuration, identified by DEL-01-01's verified version identity record. At any other version, children are withheld and shown as not supplied.

- **VER-001** — Executable check (observes the offered roles and the start result): each conversation role and no role is chosen at start and in "Continue as", a TASK start is attempted, a domain-specific guidance expression is started and its recorded role is read, the preselection is cleared, a role change is attempted after the first message, a fork is made, and invalid role sets are loaded; each result is tested against REQ-001.
- **VER-002** — Comparison with a named reference: for each role and no role, the bytes observed at the supplier boundary are compared with the guidance files on disk and with digests computed by a script that imports no product code, the request is inspected for any base-instruction input, and resume and fork requests are inspected for guidance; a child started with and without a role type is examined against the supplier's own report of it.
- **VER-003** — Comparison with a named reference: the supplied TASK document is read for the limit statement, a child started with TASK guidance is made to delegate, and the supplier's own events of that delegation are compared with the limit observation recorded and shown; the person's configuration is compared before and after, and a manager role's delegation is run as the negative.
- **VER-004** — Executable check (observes the limit account): the account is read under the default configuration, with a restricted delegation depth, a restrictive approval policy and sandbox, inside a worktree, and with a modified role document, and each reading is tested against REQ-003 and the supplier's documented controls.
- **VER-005** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: traces one conversation from selection to observed behaviour in the account, checks each fact against its own evidence, inspects a publication-only case for any adoption claim and an evidenced adoption for its actor and scope, and confirms the record and adoption owners can read what is handed to them.
- **VER-006** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: identifies reused paths in the code, confirms they are covered by the checks of AC-001…AC-005 and AC-007, examines the code act by act against REQ-006, CLM-002, CLM-003 and the accepted decomposition's rows, and looks for any common service, precedence order or App interface taken from earlier practice.
- **VER-007** — Executable check, with a byte comparison against the maintained source: the store is seeded into an empty folder and into one holding an edited file, a file is edited between two conversation starts, each unreadable-file case is applied, and the packaged guidance is compared with its source.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — The four roles have one meaning everywhere, the harness's instructions stay intact, and TASK stays a bounded executor. Better means more of what an agent was actually given traceable to exact source bytes; never by replacing the harness's base instructions, adding a fifth role, or changing the person's Codex configuration.
- **AX-002** — Enforcement is reported as it is. Better means fewer role limits whose standing is unknown; never by presenting a stated limit, the person's own settings or a worktree as enforcement.
- **AX-003** — Guidance is the person's to read and edit, and adoption is each consumer's own act. Better means fewer conversations running on guidance the person cannot see or has not been told changed; never by overwriting the person's edited copy, substituting a default silently, or counting publication as adoption.
- **AX-004** — Choosing a role grants nothing: it settles no permission and confers no professional authority. Execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance keep their own actors and evidence under DEL-04-01.
