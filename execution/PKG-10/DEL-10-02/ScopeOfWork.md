## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The controls shall keep one current file-based work graph per undertaking and use it to select and carry authorized work. Recover the selected outcome, applicable basis, deliverable mapping, actual inputs/holds, node owner, write boundary, completion check, returned evidence, review/integration position and next action sufficiently for continued work. Maintain meaningful changes and returns under an identified maintainer. Keep production dependencies, chosen route and executing state distinct; a planned node or written brief does not prove execution. Under B5, dependency-based availability requires warranted evidence and closure/cycle treatment; INITIALIZED signifies defined-contract maturity only. Independent authorized definition can continue before the future project DAG is accepted. Once a current DAG is accepted, selection uses its scope and currency. Basis: CLM-001, CLM-003; B2 V4-OPS-02; B4 Field Book §5 and User Manual §9; B5.
- **REQ-002** — The controls shall use the four-role responsibilities in CLM-001 with teams, native/managed children or solitary work as the authorized undertaking needs, without a mandatory instance per role, package or node. Bounded assignments identify purpose/use, actual supplied basis and context, parent and mechanism, authority/tools, exact writes, checks, expected return and recipient. Returns identify outputs, candidate, executed checks, limitations, unresolved matters and continuation; the receiving manager examines the return against the brief and current receiving state before claiming integration. Basis: B2 V4-OPS-03; B4 Field Book §§2/5 and User Manual §§3/10.
- **REQ-003** — Each maintained record or emitted register shall name its reader and consumption point, and preserve only detail serving decision, reliance, examination or recovery. Use Git history and reviewed PRs as primary change records, linking existing evidence and actual decisions instead of duplicating their substance into parallel tracking systems. Preserve content/scope/actor and custody for an actually recorded human act, distinct from the recorder. Execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance retain their separate actor/evidence; no one act proves another or creates a synthetic prerequisite before an independently evidenced act. Basis: CLM-001; B2 V4-OPS-31/32; B4 User Manual §4 and Consolidated §7.4; B5 actual decision custody.
- **REQ-004** — The enforcement account shall distinguish host-reported or observed filesystem/tool/network/approval capabilities and available isolation from the role/brief's permitted operations, and identify material unknown or untested limits. Include actual checkout/shared-resource conditions relevant to the work. An available tool is not authority to exceed the brief; a successful operation, separate worktree or complied-with instruction is not proof of a complete sandbox. No restriction shall be described as mechanically enforced merely because it was followed. Basis: CLM-005; B2 V4-OPS-30; B4 User Manual §§3/4/10.
- **REQ-005** — The independent-check account shall identify the actual reviewer separation, supplied basis, candidate/content identity, checks performed, results and open findings; bind any repair/backcheck to the affected candidate. Record actual model identity only when exposed and distinguish an intended allocation from observation. A fresh native context does not prove model diversity or independent filesystem isolation. Keep author checks, independent review and human decisions separate. Basis: CLM-005; B2 V4-OPS-30/34; B4 Field Book §5 and User Manual §10.
- **REQ-006** — When a selected practice proves useful, ill-fitting or ambiguous, the controls shall retain a brief observation at the relevant work-graph node, identifying the observed conditions and manual section/edition used, its consequence and evidence where available. Link existing work/evidence; do not manufacture an observation when no such event occurred or require a report for every action. Relate the note to the applicable adopted basis from CLM-002. Basis: B2 V4-OPS-20/31; B4 Consolidated §§5.4/7.4.
- **REQ-007** — At the applicable selected-method decision or stage discussion, the manager shall bring the relevant practice notes to the owner and preserve their actual disposition as a proposed manual revision, a scoped project departure or no change, with actor, affected scope, reasons/conditions and follow-through where consequential. An undecided item remains pending; a proposed revision does not amend the manual, and a scoped departure does not silently govern other consumers. Carry CLM-006's unresolved owners and points of need; prepare concrete evidence before the applicable decision while independently authorized work continues. Basis: B1 SOW-222; B2 V4-OPS-21/22/23; B3 recommendations 01/07; B4 Consolidated §5.4.
- **REQ-008** — This contribution shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: project execution-basis/manual-adoption production remains App `DEL-10-01`'s contribution in CLM-002; production-DAG construction remains App `DEL-10-04`'s contribution in CLM-003; fleet product record implementation remains App `DEL-06-01`'s contribution and return/waiting/decision UI implementation remains App `DEL-06-02`'s contribution in CLM-004. These exclusions preserve local interface, evidence and feedback duties. Human consequential adoption/stage/DAG decisions remain with their actors in CLM-001, CLM-002 and CLM-003; actual host enforcement and independent examination remain with the actors in CLM-005; unresolved instruction/revision-policy decisions remain with CLM-006's owners. No recording or source-checking operation substitutes for those acts.

Begin with the applicable accepted basis, actual current graph and approved coordination choice. Recover work already performed, actual workers/shared resources, unintegrated returns, holds and the next useful contribution. Apply the conventions in the owning files under the manager's authority; use deterministic local operations where they reliably establish identity, containment or comparison. Read adopted manual sections as their relevant practice is exercised. Examine returns and update current state; record useful practice observations where the work occurs; prepare their consequential treatment at the applicable selected-method or stage discussion. Preserve original evidence and history rather than rewriting it to match later standing.

Confirm accepted-DAG currency is used when one exists, and that pre-DAG authorized definition is described on its own warranted basis. Check that file-native controls remain usable without fleet UI or PEC. Record scope/gaps; do not infer an executed product scenario from the walkthrough.

Reconcile parentage, role/context, permitted writes and required checks with the actual contribution and recipient; distinguish launch intent from observed execution and review/integration standing. Use available completed records or explicitly retain missing returns as pending.
- **VER-003** — Review the maintained record set against the reader/use table, following change and decision references to their authoritative sources. Examine a positive faithful-recording case: B5 preserves an actual reported owner response and exact subject, identifies parent-transcribed custody, and keeps recorder distinct from decision actor. Examine negative inferences such as treating an agent's successful check, authored file or merged PR as an unobserved human act; reject them. Confirm the same act is not reauthored into a competing authority record. This is record examination, not a new human-act policy or fabricated live event.
- **VER-004** — Compare the scoped brief with actual supplied host permissions, tool availability and checkout/shared-resource information for the identified run. State what is host-reported, directly observed, instruction-asserted or untested. Assess whether the claims match those grounds without probing prohibited operations; specifically challenge worktree-equals-sandbox and instruction-equals-enforcement inferences.
- **VER-005** — Follow the candidate/basis bindings through author checks, separate review and any changed-candidate backcheck. Inspect actual reviewer parentage/context and exposed model evidence; record unknown identity without claiming diversity. Check that findings remain open until their disposition is supported and that a review or successful rerun is not treated as proof of another act or of repair without examining the change.
- **VER-006** — Trace each applicable note to its node, observed conditions, evidence and exact manual section/edition. Check useful, ill-fitting and ambiguous descriptions against what happened; record which classes were actually observed instead of requiring invented examples of every class. Verify an existing central record is linked where it already carries the needed detail.
- **VER-007** — At the applicable discussion, compare the prepared notes with the actual decision evidence and resulting scoped follow-through. Distinguish a proposed revision from an adopted change, a project departure from universal adoption, and no change from an unmade decision. Verify undecided OI-019/020 treatment remains with the named owners and deadlines, and identify any required discussion still ahead without inventing its result.
- **VER-008** — Compare REQ-008's enumerated acts one-for-one with CLM-001 through CLM-006 and B1/B2/B5.
