# Proportionate undertaking controls and practice feedback

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

The manager of an undertaking on App v4 can choose authorized work, hand out bounded assignments, examine what comes back and pass the undertaking to a successor from files alone. The project says truthfully what its hosts enforced and what was independently checked. And what the work teaches about the selected practice reaches the owner as a decision to revise the manual, depart from it for this project, or change nothing. This deliverable contributes the controls that make those three things so, kept in the records that already carry the work.

It serves OBJ-006, by making the project's own bounded work and its decisions recoverable from file-based coordination records, and OBJ-010, by keeping working practice proportionate and grounded in its sources (OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-02, V4-OPS-03, V4-OPS-20 to V4-OPS-23, V4-OPS-30 to V4-OPS-32 and V4-OPS-34). The accepted decomposition assigns it to PKG-10 as a documentation deliverable of the App undertaking manager. These controls are used directly from files; the App's fleet views of PKG-06 may later show such records and are no precondition for them. No exit criterion of the draft 4.0.0 release target names this deliverable.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The work-graph control and the brief, return and reader conventions, carried in the undertaking's existing files: they relate selected work, bounded roles, usable inputs, actual returns, examination, integration and continuation. Covers SOW-212, SOW-213, SOW-226 and SOW-227; supports OBJ-006 and OBJ-010.
- **OUT-002** — The permission, isolation and independent-check account for an undertaking and its identified candidate: what was enforced apart from what a brief instructed, and what was checked apart from what was meant to be checked. Covers SOW-225; supports OBJ-010.
- **OUT-003** — The practice notes linked to work-graph nodes, and their dispositions at stage decisions. Covers SOW-221, SOW-222, SOW-226 and SOW-227; supports OBJ-010.

The three outputs share the records that already carry the undertaking. They imply no separate database, no standing register and no report per manual rule or per action.

Terms the product implements:

- **Undertaking.** One authorized body of work with a selected outcome and a manager.
- **Current work graph.** The one file-based graph of an undertaking. It gives the selected outcome, the basis the work stands on, the deliverables it maps to and, for each node, its inputs and holds, its owner, its write boundary, its completion check, the evidence returned, where review and integration stand, and the next action. The project's production dependencies, the route an undertaking selects and what is actually executing are three different things; a planned node or a written brief shows no execution.
- **Bounded brief.** An assignment that states its purpose and use, the basis and context actually supplied, the parent and the mechanism of delegation, the authority and tools, the exact write targets, the checks, the expected return and its recipient.
- **Return.** What an executor hands back: the outputs, the candidate they belong to, the checks actually run, the limitations, the unresolved matters and what should follow. The receiving manager examines it against the brief and the present state before treating it as integrated.
- **Enforcement standing.** Each stated boundary is one of four: reported by the host, directly observed, asserted by instruction only, or untested. A restriction that was followed is not thereby enforced, and a worktree is not a sandbox.
- **Practice note.** A brief observation that a selected practice proved useful, ill-fitting or ambiguous, held at the work-graph node where it arose, with the observed conditions, the manual section and edition, the consequence and the evidence. Observation is kept apart from inference and from any proposed change.
- **Disposition.** What the owner decides about a practice note at a stage or selected-method discussion: a proposed manual revision, a departure scoped to this project, or no change. Until decided it is pending. A proposed revision amends no manual, and a scoped departure governs no other consumer.

Readers and uses of the records kept under these outputs:

| Record | Reader | Point of consumption and use |
|---|---|---|
| The current work graph and the coordination choice it links | Undertaking manager, returning coordinator and successor | At entry, selection, each meaningful return and recovery: scope, ownership, actual state, holds and the next authorized work |
| Bounded brief and the return as received | Assigned executor, receiving manager and examiner | Before execution and at return: the boundary, and whether the contribution meets it |
| The capability and check account bound to a candidate | Manager and independent examiner; the owner where a decision warrants | At assignment, review, repair and recheck, and any reliance decision: what was allowed, what was enforced and what was examined |
| Git history and reviewed pull requests | Reviewer, integrator and successor | At change review, integration and recovery: what changed and what was checked, with no parallel change ledger |
| Practice note and its disposition | Undertaking manager and owner; the method's author or a receiving consumer where a change is selected | At the stage or selected-method discussion, then in the follow-through it authorizes |

The table names readers; it does not require five files. A record that preserves a human act or historical evidence keeps a recovery use when no run reads it. Any other register states its reader and use when it is created. The number of records kept, or an empty queue, shows no completion.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — The App undertaking manager owns these controls, coordinates the result and integrates returns. HELP_HUMAN keeps alignment and continuity with the owner, HELPS_HUMANS develops designs and proposed methods, WORKING_ITEMS organizes execution and its integrated return, and TASK executes one bounded assignment without delegating. The owner makes the stage decisions. A recorder may preserve an act that occurred without becoming its actor.
- **CLM-002** — `DEL-10-01` supplies the project execution basis and the selected manual and method editions, with the owner deciding consequential adoption or departure. This deliverable uses that basis and records how it worked in practice.
- **CLM-003** — `DEL-10-04` owns the project's production dependency graph, which the owner accepts. An undertaking's work graph selects and records execution; it neither establishes nor accepts production relationships.
- **CLM-004** — `DEL-06-01` owns the App's product formats for delegation and work-graph records, and `DEL-06-02` the product's return, waiting and decision views. Neither those views nor any coordination connector is needed to use these controls or to build the fleet software.
- **CLM-005** — The host enforces whatever permissions and isolation it has. A brief's author states the permitted scope, the executor observes it, and an independent examiner checks the identified contribution and reports within what it covered. An author's own check is not independent review.
- **CLM-006** — The owner with the execution manager decides how a consequential gap in the manual is treated. The owner decides who authors and approves manual revisions. The owner with the instruction owners decides how instructions are distributed and adopted. This deliverable records and routes observations; it chooses none of these and grants no edit of a manual or instruction.
- **CLM-007** — `DEL-09-12` routes the owner's method observations from validating use to this deliverable, where they enter as practice notes observed by the practitioner. Practitioner validation remains `DEL-09-12`'s.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — Each undertaking has one current file-based work graph, and authorized work is selected and carried through it. From it a reader recovers the selected outcome, the basis, the deliverable mapping, the actual inputs and holds, each node's owner, write boundary and completion check, the evidence returned, where review and integration stand, and the next action. An identified maintainer keeps it current at meaningful changes and returns. Work is selected from the accepted current dependency graph within its scope and currency; where no accepted graph covers the work, authorized definition proceeds on the conditions its own deliverables declare. A valid scope does not make an input available. [SOW-212; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-02; CLM-001; CLM-003]
- **REQ-002** — The four roles are used as CLM-001 gives them, with teams, delegated children or solitary work as the undertaking needs and no compulsory instance per role, package or node. Each assignment is a bounded brief and each return carries the elements of a return. The receiving manager examines a return against its brief and the present receiving state before claiming integration. [SOW-213; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-03]
- **REQ-003** — Each maintained record and each emitted register names its reader and point of consumption, and keeps only detail that serves a decision, reliance, examination or recovery. Git history and reviewed pull requests are the primary change record; existing evidence and decisions are linked, not copied into a parallel tracking system. A recorded human act keeps its content, scope, actor and custody, with the recorder apart from the actor. Execution, acceptance of a proposal, checking, approval and professional reliance each keep their own actor and evidence; none proves another, and no act is made to wait on another without a method that requires it. [SOW-226; SOW-227; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-31, V4-OPS-32; CLM-001]
- **REQ-004** — The enforcement account gives each filesystem, tool, network and approval boundary of the undertaking its enforcement standing, states the isolation actually available and the conditions of any shared checkout or resource, and names what is unknown or untested. A tool being available gives no authority beyond the brief. No restriction is described as enforced because it was followed, and neither a separate worktree nor a successful operation is described as a complete sandbox. [SOW-225; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-30; CLM-005]
- **REQ-005** — The independent-check account identifies, for each candidate, who reviewed it and how that reviewer was separate from its author, the basis supplied, the candidate's content identity, the checks performed, their results and the open findings, and it ties each repair and recheck to the candidate it affects. A model's identity is recorded only where the host exposes it, and an intended allocation is kept apart from what was observed; a fresh context shows neither a different model nor separate file access. For design units, a reviewer instance in the HELP_HUMAN session, of the same model, that did not author the work suffices, with its identity reported; such a review claims no independence of model family. For product candidates OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-34 stands, with the reviewer preference and the fallback it states. Author checks, independent review and the owner's decisions stay apart. [SOW-225; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-30, V4-OPS-34; CLM-005]
- **REQ-006** — When a selected practice proves useful, ill-fitting or ambiguous, a practice note is kept at the work-graph node concerned, related to the edition `DEL-10-01` records. Existing work and evidence are linked. No note is made where nothing was observed, and none is required per action. [SOW-221; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-20, V4-OPS-31; CLM-002]
- **REQ-007** — At the stage or selected-method discussion the manager brings the relevant practice notes to the owner and keeps each actual disposition with its actor, the scope it affects, its reasons and conditions and, where consequential, its follow-through. An undecided note stays pending. The matters CLM-006 leaves with the owner are carried with their owners and points of need, and evidence for them is prepared before the discussion while independently authorized work continues. [SOW-222; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-21 to V4-OPS-23; CLM-006; CLM-007]
- **REQ-008** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: producing the execution basis or adopting a manual edition (`DEL-10-01`, CLM-002), building the production dependency graph (`DEL-10-04`, CLM-003), implementing the product's fleet records (`DEL-06-01`) or its return, waiting and decision views (`DEL-06-02`), both under CLM-004, and practitioner validation (`DEL-09-12`, CLM-007). Stage, adoption and graph decisions stay with the owner (CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-003), enforcement with the host and examination with the independent examiner (CLM-005), and the undecided policy matters with the owners CLM-006 names. Recording or checking a source stands in for none of those acts.

- **AC-001** — From one current work graph and the sources it links, an examiner recovers the undertaking's selected route, the owners, the grounds of each input and hold, the meaningful returns and the next work. The graph keeps production dependencies, the selected route and actual execution apart, and it is usable as files with no fleet view and no connector. Verify by VER-001. [REQ-001; CLM-003; CLM-004]
- **AC-002** — For an actual assignment, the brief and the return each carry their elements, the arrangement shows its real parentage, mechanism and receiving owner, and a prepared assignment, an executing child, returned work, examined work and integrated work are told apart. No role, package or node has an instance that the work did not need. Verify by VER-002. [REQ-002]
- **AC-003** — Every maintained register names a reader and a use, changes resolve to Git history and reviewed pull requests, and no second current system repeats them. A recorded owner act shows its actor and custody apart from its recorder, and no acceptance, approval, check or reliance is claimed that its own evidence does not support. Verify by VER-003. - **AC-004** — For an identified run, the enforcement account gives each boundary its standing with the evidence for it, states instruction-only restrictions as such, includes the shared checkout or resource exposure, and nowhere turns compliance or a worktree into a claim of enforcement or sandboxing. Verify by VER-004. [REQ-004]
- **AC-005** — For an identified candidate, the check account ties the candidate and its basis to the author's checks, the reviewer's separation, the findings and the rechecks of what changed. A model identity appears only where the host exposed it, a finding stays open until its disposition is supported, and neither the owner's approval nor reliance is inferred from a review. Verify by VER-005. [REQ-005]

- **TBD-002** — How a consequential gap in the manual is treated is undecided (the owner with the execution manager). It is needed when such a gap affects selected work; until then the consequence and the affected reliance are stated, and no general rule for the manual's silence is assumed.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The controls live in the files that already carry an undertaking, under its manager's authority, and are applied where the work happens: a practice note is written at the node when the practice is exercised, not gathered afterwards. Evidence and earlier records are kept as they were and are not rewritten to match a later standing. A requirement whose decision or check lies ahead is shown as pending; it is not a failure.

- **VER-001** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: walks one undertaking's current work graph from a selected node through its input grounds, owner, permitted action, check and continuation, including an unresolved input, and checks that selection follows the accepted current dependency graph where one covers the work and that the files are usable without a fleet view or connector.
- **VER-002** — Comparison with a named reference: an actual arrangement, one bounded brief and its return are compared with the role and assignment sections of the selected edition of the manual, the Library's role files and its coordinated-knowledge-work method, reconciling parentage, context, permitted writes and required checks with the contribution actually received.
- **VER-003** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: reads the maintained records against the reader table, follows change and decision references to Git and the decision records, takes a recorded owner act as the positive case and searches for a check, an authored file or a merge presented as a human act.
- **VER-004** — Comparison with a named reference: for an identified run, the brief is compared with the permissions, tools and checkout conditions the host actually reported, each claim is classed as host-reported, observed, instruction-asserted or untested without probing a prohibited operation, and the claims that a worktree is a sandbox or an instruction is enforcement are looked for.
- **VER-005** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: follows one candidate from its author's checks through separate review to the recheck of what changed, inspects the reviewer's actual parentage, context and exposed model evidence, and checks that no finding closed without examination of the change.
- **VER-006** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: traces each practice note to its node, its conditions, its evidence and the cited section of the selected edition, and checks each description against what the work records show happened.
- **VER-007** — Comparison with a named reference: at a stage or selected-method discussion, the notes prepared are compared with the owner's decisions as recorded and with the follow-through, and the three undecided matters are checked against the decomposition's open-issue rows for their owners and points of need.
- **VER-008** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: reads the undertaking records against the acts REQ-008 excludes, looking for a neighbour's work implemented here and for an act of the owner, a host or an independent examiner, or a neighbour's output, presented as performed by these controls.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — A record earns its upkeep through an identified decision, reliance, examination or recovery use. Better means fewer records a successor must read to continue an undertaking; never by losing a human act, a hold or the grounds of an input.
- **AX-002** — Actors and evidence are reported as they were: permission, allowed scope, work done, checking, the owner's decision and professional reliance stay distinct. Better means fewer boundaries whose enforcement standing is unknown; never by describing an instruction, compliance or a worktree as enforcement.
- **AX-003** — The accepted composite applies at its own scope. Manual examples and older defaults create no product feature, no compulsory memory file, no order of precedence and no settled authorship of revisions. Better means more practice observations reaching a decision by the owner; never by treating a proposal as an adopted change, or one project's departure as binding on another consumer.
- **AX-004** — The decision at hand is made on current evidence. Better means less authorized work waiting on something it does not need, such as a product view, a connector or a later stage; never by counting a definition or a merge as a product, policy or acceptance not yet seen.
