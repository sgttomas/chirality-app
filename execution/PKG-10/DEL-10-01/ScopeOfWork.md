# DEL-10-01 — Project execution basis and manual application

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Someone arriving to work on App v4, whether an agent starting a session, a manager handing out work or an author writing a deliverable's scope, can learn from one maintained account what the project is built on and how it is worked: the basis and decomposition the owner accepted, the editions of the manual and of the shared roles and methods the project has selected, how packages and deliverables are set up and carried, and how the project applies the manual, including where it deliberately departs from it. This deliverable contributes that account and keeps it true as the selections change.

It serves OBJ-010: v4 work is defined and conducted through the accepted composite, manual-led practice and accountable technical contracts (OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-01, V4-OPS-04, V4-OPS-10 to V4-OPS-14 and V4-OPS-33; PRD V4-CST-04 and OQ-05). The accepted decomposition assigns it to PKG-10 as a documentation deliverable of the App project-definition manager. No exit criterion of the draft 4.0.0 release target names it; that target reads its direction from the execution basis this deliverable maintains.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The project execution basis: the account that names the accepted basis and decomposition the project works from, the selected edition of the manual and of the shared roles and methods with a recoverable identity for each, where each selection is made and what it covers, the inputs still unresolved with their owners and points of need, and the readers the account serves. Covers SOW-102, SOW-215, SOW-216, SOW-217 and SOW-228; supports OBJ-010.
- **OUT-002** — The setup and working handoff: how packages and deliverables are identified, where a deliverable's commitments, needs and work are found, what its working, checking and issued records are for, what is already prepared and what remains, and what the six development positions mean. Covers SOW-210, SOW-214 and SOW-228; supports OBJ-010.
- **OUT-003** — The manual application and departure account: for each practice the project relies on or sets aside, the purpose kept, the treatment actually in force and, where a departure is consequential, the owner's decision on it. It also covers the form of the project's maintained documents under the selected release. Covers SOW-102, SOW-215, SOW-216, SOW-218 and SOW-219; supports OBJ-010.

The three outputs may be parts of one account. They link to existing decisions and records where those are adequate.

Terms the product implements:

- **Accepted basis.** The composite the owner accepted for decomposition and project definition: the original seed set as revised by the seven decision recommendations and qualified by the owner's later directions. PRD §0 identifies it and where its acceptance records are archived.
- **Accepted decomposition.** The final decomposition snapshot of 11 packages, 41 deliverables and 262 scope items, read as amended by the accepted scope-change amendments.
- **The manual.** The Library's Project Management manual in its Consolidated edition, applied by relevant section as method reasoning. Current repository practice is carried by this repository's instructions (`AGENTS.md`), the Library's role files and the workflow selected for the work. The Field Book and the Agent User Manual are retired.
- **Selected edition.** The edition of the manual, or the release of the shared roles and methods, that the project relies on, named by release and revision so that its content can be recovered. A stable index or moving pointer serves discovery only. Reading an edition selects nothing. A changed edition takes effect only through a deliberate change of the selection.
- **Development position.** One of Conceptual, FEED, 30%, 60%, 90% and 100%, described by what the work has established. A position is not a share of effort, code or tasks, and parts of the project may stand at different positions. The project's dependency graph is established before the 30% position.
- **Departure.** A treatment that sets a manual practice aside. It is consequential when it affects an accepted commitment or another consumer's obligation, and the owner decides it.
- **Maintained document.** A document the project keeps current for its readers: the repository instructions, the session entry prompt, the documents under `docs/` and each tool's README. Frozen snapshots, accepted evidence and exact copies of release files are not maintained documents.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — The owner accepted the basis and the final decomposition, and each acceptance is recoverable from its record with its actor, subject and limits. Unchanged accepted definition is not put to the owner again.
- **CLM-002** — Candidate pins of the original seed, research revisions and editions read during earlier work are evidence of those runs. They select nothing for present work.
- **CLM-003** — A deliverable's scope states what it owes. Its existence, or its passing a format check, establishes neither that its inputs are met, nor that its outputs exist, nor that anything was accepted.
- **CLM-004** — The App project-definition manager prepares and integrates the three outputs and records the selections. The owner decides each consequential adoption or departure. An agent may record a human act that actually occurred, keeping the decision actor apart from the recorder; a record cannot supply an act that was not performed.
- **CLM-005** — `DEL-10-02` owns undertaking controls and practice feedback. `DEL-10-03` owns the account of shared commitments and their owners. `DEL-10-04` owns the project's production dependency graph and its acceptance account. `DEL-11-01` owns the inventory of preserved history and coexistence. `DEL-11-02` coordinates each consumer's adoption of a renewed basis, which that consumer's owner decides and performs. `DEL-02-04` owns the instructions packaged into the App, which keep their own source identities; selecting an edition for those who work on the project changes none of them.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The execution basis identifies the accepted basis, the later directions that qualify it, the accepted decomposition with the amendments it is read with, and what follows from them for present work. It leaves the original seeds, the thesis and earlier records as they were. It carries each unresolved input with its owner, its consequence and its point of need. A later material change reopens the decisions it affects and no others; accepted stages that did not change are not restarted. [SOW-102; SOW-228; OPERATING_METHOD §1, V4-OPS-33; PRD §0; CLM-001]
- **REQ-002** — The execution basis names each selected edition by release and by an identity from which its content can be recovered, and says where the selection is made and what work it covers. The selection is made in one place, and every other place that names it names the same edition. Editions that were only read, and historical candidates, are shown as such. Where the project keeps copies of a release's files for its own checks, each copy equals the released file byte for byte. [SOW-215; SOW-216; SOW-217; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-11; PRD OQ-05; CLM-002; CLM-004]

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The account is an index over the project's records. The records are the authority: the account restates none of their decisions, and where the account and a record differ the record governs and the account is corrected. The selection of the shared roles and methods is stated once, in the repository instructions, and changing it there is a deliberate change to the project; the execution basis and the project's reference record of the release repeat it for discovery and are changed with it. The manual's edition is selected in the execution basis, which is the project's adoption record; any other place that names it repeats that selection. A consumer that depends on exact content uses the named revision and does not assume a moving index still points at it. Only the manual sections that explain a selected practice are read into the account.

- **VER-001** — Comparison with a named reference: the basis, directions, decomposition and amendments the execution basis names are compared with PRD §0 and the accepted-decomposition pointer, and each acceptance, limit and unresolved input in it with the record PRD §0 or the accepted decomposition names for it, following each claimed acceptance to its actor, subject and limits.
- **VER-002** — Comparison with a named reference: each named release and identity is resolved against the Library's published release, the edition is compared across the repository instructions, the execution basis and the reference record wherever each names it, and each retained copy is compared with the released file's published identity.
- **VER-003** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: reads the handoff as a newly arrived author, resolves a package and a deliverable to their accepted identities and to their commitments, needs and work, and looks for a deliverable with a valid scope and an unmet input to see how the handoff shows it.
- **VER-004** — Comparison with a named reference: the explanation of the six positions is compared with the chapter on development positions in the selected edition of the manual and with OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-04, including a project with parts at different positions and a dependency graph prepared before the 30% position.
- **VER-005** — Comparison with a named reference: each treatment in the application account is read against the manual section or instruction file it cites and against OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-10 to V4-OPS-13 and §7, following purpose to mechanism and reader; each departure is checked for its effect on an accepted commitment and for the owner's decision.
- **VER-006** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: takes a recorded owner decision as the positive case, checking that actor, recorder and limits are kept apart, and searches the outputs for the negative cases of AC-006.
- **VER-007** — Comparison with a named reference: each output is traced to its readers and use, the outputs are compared with the nine assigned scope items, the anticipated artifacts and the package exclusions of the accepted decomposition, and what is handed to `DEL-11-02` is compared with the consequences the account identifies.
- **VER-008** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: for each act REQ-008 excludes, searches the three outputs for a claim that it was performed or a choice decided, including acts whose owner is a person.
- **VER-009** — Executable check (observes each maintained document's header): every maintained document is validated against the schema file of the selected release, constructed headers with a date, revision or status field and with incomplete template metadata are offered and must be refused, and the two entry documents are read for the template and edition they name.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — The owner's direction governs the undertaking, and where shared material is kept gives it no precedence. Better means fewer questions put to the owner that an existing record already answers; never by presenting text the owner did not see as accepted, or by letting a manual rule add or remove a product commitment.
- **AX-002** — Purpose, source identity and history are kept through change. Better means more of what the work relies on traceable to a named edition and revision; never by rewriting an earlier record, or by counting publication or a prepared notice as a consumer's adoption.
- **AX-003** — An edition that was read and a scope that is valid are evidence of themselves only. Better means an arriving reader needs to read less before starting work; never by dropping an unresolved input, its owner or its point of need.
