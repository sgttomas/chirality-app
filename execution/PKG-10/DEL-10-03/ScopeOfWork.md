## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Start with the accepted allocation and relevant current source clauses, read their original wording alongside the later decisions, and bound the subjects whose responsibility or supply is affected. Use existing source and consumer traces to locate evidence; expand only to settle an identified interface or placement question. Build the responsibility and promise trace together, then assess historical applicability and current consumer consequences. Keep discovered gaps with their real owners and points of need. Independent definition can proceed while a later placement, supply or adoption decision waits for its own input. A document's existence supplies neither that input nor an accepted production dependency.

Prepare the three outputs for the named contract/adoption readers. Compare the assembled account against its source population and connected contracts; retain source identities and focused evidence references rather than copy manuals or production contracts. Route substantive scope/ownership conflicts to the responsible manager/owner with precise source loci. Carry actual downstream decisions and evidence through their owning deliverables. This INIT creates only this production contract; the methods below define examination of later account production, not a report that those outputs already exist.

- **VER-001** — Inspect the assembled map against BASIS SOW-230 and the CLM-001–006 owner/interface statements. Follow each affected entry to its accepted contract source; compare meaning, ownership, consumer and open allocation. Record omissions, contradictory assignments and the candidate identity supporting AC-001.
- **VER-002** — Perform a two-way comparison between the bounded population of affected accepted promises and OUT-002. Follow each source obligation through later qualifying decisions to its SoW, derivative/interface and evaluation path, then reverse-check account entries for unsupported promises. Examine absent derivative and relocation cases explicitly; record retained obligations, missing links and owning points of need for AC-002.
- **VER-003** — Compare applicability entries to original records and later accepted direction, including SEED's Root-first precedence, thin-loop default and shared TypeScript wording against HTML/OPS/ARCH. Confirm original/thesis preservation references and identify any current consumer obligation that continues despite v4's different direction. Treat historical successful repairs as bounded evidence, not topology-only proof or a v4 qualification. Record the comparison supporting AC-003.
- **VER-004** — Inspect the selected current source/selector/tool-path chain for each affected placement or supply change, starting from relevant known leads such as CLM-007. Compare proposed placement with actual references and supplied-basis identity; name each affected Root/Runtime/App/Piping consumer and owner. Separate static trace from build/runtime evidence; an unavailable check records the gap and prevents the affected placement/supply reliance, without stopping independent definition. Return source identities, findings and limits for AC-004; execute no network experiment or consumer migration under this contract-initialization task.
- **VER-005** — Review the account against the PKG-02/03/04/05 accepted interface rows and ARCH decisions. Check workflow/checkpoint, identity, human-act, catalog and evidence coverage, the retained App/host direction, and whether proposed common implementation has a concrete repeated responsibility and named consumers. Compare all placement choices to TBD-001/002 so an open choice remains open. Record compatibility questions and owner decisions needed for AC-005; an interface comparison alone is not a joined-product pass.
- **VER-006** — Examine each exclusion in REQ-006 against the act its cited claim assigns, including non-deliverable human/host owners. Inspect actual-act statements and their custody. Use a positive documentary case of an actually performed human decision faithfully recorded with distinct actor/recorder and identified subject; use negative cases where a successful operation, check, prepared notice or silent interval is incorrectly presented as acceptance/adoption/reliance. Confirm rejection of those unsupported inferences without denying faithful recording or imposing an unaccepted order among distinct acts. Record the semantic comparison supporting AC-006.
