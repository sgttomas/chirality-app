# Shared commitments and consumer responsibility account

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

v4 renews what the Chirality projects share: concepts, working guidance and technical commitments move to new owners, new contracts and new routes of supply. This deliverable makes sure that nothing promised is lost on the way and that everyone affected knows who now owns what. It contributes one focused account: who is accountable for each shared subject and which contract consumes it; the route from each affected promise to the scope that answers for it; which historical statements still apply and to whom; and which consumers, packaging and tool paths a change of placement or supply would touch.

It serves OBJ-010: v4 work is defined and conducted through the accepted composite, manual-led practice and accountable technical contracts (PRD V4-CST-04 and V4-SHR-03; ARCHITECTURE §5 and V4-ARC-20; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-12 to V4-OPS-14). The accepted decomposition assigns it to PKG-10 as a documentation deliverable of the App/shared renewal definition owner, working with the owners of the affected consumers. Its readers are the contract owners of PKG-02, PKG-03, PKG-04 and PKG-05, the adoption and continuity owners of PKG-11, and whoever decides where shared sources are placed. It is a bounded renewal of responsibility, not a rearrangement of repositories.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The responsibility map. For each affected shared subject it gives the meaning, the standing of its source, the accountable owner, the consuming contract and any allocation still unresolved. Covers SOW-230 and SOW-234; supports OBJ-010.
- **OUT-002** — The promise trace. For each technical commitment the renewal affects it keeps the original identity and source, the wording or decision that now applies, the accountable scope and the derivative, interface or evaluation that carries it. A derivative that is promised is told apart from one that exists and from one that has been checked. Covers SOW-231; supports OBJ-010.
- **OUT-003** — The historical applicability and consumer account. It identifies the relevant historical statements with their present applicability and continuing obligations, the original records and current selectors, and the affected Root, Runtime, App and Piping consumers with the consequences of a placement change and the point at which each receives it. PKG-11 uses it as the basis for staged adoption; it performs no adoption. Covers SOW-232 and SOW-233; supports OBJ-010.

The three outputs may be parts of one account. It indexes the allocation and responsibility statements that the owning scopes already make and restates none of them. It is not a second inventory of the repository, a record of execution, or a document per field.

Terms the product implements:

- **Shared subject.** Something more than one project or expression relies on. It is one of three classes: a shared concept, working guidance, or a technical product commitment.
- **Five responsibilities.** For any shared subject the account keeps apart its conceptual home, its working practice, its technical obligation, its supply at run time and the human decision it involves.
- **Promise.** An accepted obligation. An incidental mechanism of an earlier arrangement is not a promise. A promise is affected when a relocation or a change of supply is proposed for it.
- **Consumer.** An identified project, or an executable or documentary route, that relies on the affected material.
- **Applicability.** For an identified use, a source is current, historical, superseded at a stated scope, or unresolved. The classification gives its reason, and the original record stays intact.
- **Facts kept apart.** That a source resolves, that it was supplied, that a provider adopted it, that behaviour was observed and that a receiving consumer adopted it are five separate facts.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The responsibility map and the promise trace are built together from the accepted allocation and the scopes that own each subject, reading original wording beside the later decisions; applicability and consumer consequences are assessed from them. Existing source and consumer traces locate the evidence, and the account widens only to settle an identified interface or placement question. Source identities and references are kept, and neither the manual nor a production scope is copied in. A gap found stays with its real owner and point of need. Independent definition proceeds while a placement, supply or adoption decision waits for its own input.

- **VER-001** — Comparison with a named reference: each entry of the map is compared with the accepted decomposition's row and with the owning scope's own allocation or responsibility statement, for meaning, owner, consumer and open allocation, recording omissions and contradictory assignments.
- **VER-002** — Comparison with a named reference: in both directions, the affected promises in the accepted sources are compared with the trace, following each through its later qualifying decisions to its scope, derivative and evaluation path, then checking each entry of the trace back to a source; the cases of an absent derivative and of a relocation are examined explicitly.
- **VER-003** — Comparison with a named reference: each applicability entry is compared with its original record and with the later accepted direction, including the three superseded defaults as worded in the original seed against PRD, OPERATING_METHOD and ARCHITECTURE, and the references to the preserved thesis and to continuing obligations are confirmed.
- **VER-004** — Comparison with a named reference: for each affected change, the chain of source, selectors and tool paths is inspected in the identified current source starting from a known trace such as CLM-007, each affected consumer and owner is named, and what the inspection shows is kept apart from build and run-time evidence; a check that cannot be made is recorded as a gap that holds the affected reliance.
- **VER-005** — Comparison with a named reference: the account is read against the accepted interface rows of PKG-02 to PKG-05 and the decisions of ARCHITECTURE, for coverage of the five contract kinds, the chosen direction, and a named repeated responsibility and consumers behind any proposed common implementation; each placement is compared with TBD-001 and TBD-002 so that an open choice stays open.
- **VER-006** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: each entry of the account that touches an act REQ-006 excludes is compared with the owner its claim assigns, including the acts owned by a person or a host; the reviewer takes a recorded human decision as the positive case, and searches for a successful operation, a check, a prepared notice or a silent interval presented as acceptance, adoption or reliance.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Renewal keeps promises and their owners. Better means fewer affected promises without an accountable scope and a derivative path; never by treating a moved file, an omitted mechanism or a passing check as a promise kept, and never by selecting a common service, a placement or an order of precedence its owners have not agreed.
- **AX-002** — Where a source sits, that it was published and that a file moved confer no human authority, no adoption and no closure of an obligation. Better means more affected consumers examined against current source before their placement changes; never by altering an original record, the thesis or its standing, or by recording an adoption its consumer's owner has not made.
- **AX-003** — The account is kept in proportion to its readers' decisions, interfaces and recovery needs. Better means a shorter account that points at the owning scopes; never by dropping a stable commitment, and never by letting a test or a fact of the present implementation create product scope.
