## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Use the selected project-dag method to prepare the future production result:
recover the recorded tracking mode and accepted allocation; establish the
inventory/objective; obtain usable local evidence through its owners; freeze
and examine closure; prepare basis checkpoint; resolve or hold coupled work;
assemble and independently examine the candidate; prepare version checkpoint;
publish only the actually accepted subject; and provide current-basis handoff.
Reuse suitable existing current evidence rather than rebuilding merely for a
new session. The two checkpoints may be decided together only under the
selected method's stated small-undertaking conditions, after independent
review. This INIT authors the contract; it executes none of those graph acts.
[B3, B5–B7, B10]

- **VER-001** — Compare the graph basis and node inventory to the accepted decomposition and recorded tracking mode. Check missing-register nodes, exemptions and coverage limitations as well as edge objective and direction. Evidence: identified inventory comparison and basis package, with discrepancies retained. [B1, B3, B5]
- **VER-002** — Trace represented rows to the frozen local source manifest, source identifiers and applicable contracts. Check contribution/condition/standing/satisfaction fields, original core-field fidelity, declared-origin mirroring, representative/mirror accounting and conflicts routed to both owners. Evidence: source manifest, row accounting/fidelity results and findings. [B3, B5]
- **VER-003** — Compare the interface account and excluded non-topological relationships with current relevant external and open-issue source rows. Inspect pending-input cases and confirm owners, point of need, limitations and distinct supplier-project identity survive. Specifically examine pending SWB delivery, optional/unqualified PEC, later Domains with unresolved allocation, and supplier/adoption dependencies; verify that graph acceptance neither waits for all external completion nor implies dependent-input satisfaction. Evidence: cited interface comparison and receiving limitations. [B1, B4, B8, B9]
- **VER-004** — Inspect the frozen-basis closure report, inventory coverage and SCC cases; run the selected `audit_dag.py --canonical --strict` on the admitted graph and preserve command, tool identity, exit/result and input hashes. Reconcile all active execution rows and copied-field fidelity. Check that held candidates are non-gating, affected missing-input work remains held, and resolved cases cite subsequent closure. Evidence: audit/closure/accounting outputs and case dispositions, with process success separate from subject pass. These future graph checks are not run against this INIT contract. [B5, B6]
- **VER-005** — An examiner separate from graph assembly compares the candidate with the confirmed basis, reruns applicable manifest/strict-audit/accounting/fidelity checks, examines semantics, exclusions, mirrors and held work, and records blocking/non-blocking findings. Compare each checkpoint package and decision subject with the checked candidate and any changed basis. Evidence: independent review, repairs/backchecks and presented subject identities; no claim of human acceptance from a review pass. [B5]
- **VER-006** — Reconcile the decision question, actual answer, actor and custody with the presented hashes and published snapshot/pointer. Positive case: a recording agent faithfully records an actually performed human acceptance or qualified acceptance, identifying the human decision actor and disclosed relay limits. Negative cases: an agent proposal/audit is relabelled acceptance, missing actor or date is invented, an unseen changed graph is published under an earlier act, or an observation pointer is treated as accepted. Evidence: actual decision record, presentation hashes and manifest comparison; unavailable decision evidence remains unresolved. [B1, B3, B5]
- **VER-007** — Examine currency comparisons against the accepted inventory, admitted/candidate arcs and SCCs. Check unchanged and evidence-only drift treatment, arc/inventory/SCC departures, unreadable sources, human acceptance/rejection, unchanged rejected rows, and later changed rows. Verify affected pending flags and no ready/blocked verdict while pending, unchanged accepted snapshots, scoped successor decisions, live satisfaction reading and handoff. Evidence: source comparison, currency report, decision and successor/predecessor links as applicable. [B5, B7]
- **VER-008** — Inspect the handoff and reliance statements against B3 and the owning production evidence. Trace the cases of an INITIALIZED contract with an absent artifact, pre-DAG definition, a held unresolved cycle, and a graph accepted with pending dependencies. Confirm each retains its actual next condition without a self-bootstrap prerequisite or invented 30%/lifecycle/release/reliance act. Evidence: source-grounded readiness/reliance examination. [B1–B3, B5–B7, B10]
- **VER-009** — Enumerate each excluded act in REQ-009 and match it to CLM-001, CLM-002 or CLM-003 and its cited source owner. Inspect actual assignments, decisions and records for responsibility transfer or fabrication; retain faithful recording of a performed act as a permitted case. Deterministic local-owner resolution assists this comparison but cannot authenticate actors or prove the semantic boundary. Evidence: owner-resolution report plus semantic examination and any routed conflict. [B1, B3–B9]
