# Host panel and shared interaction receiving

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Define the App/shared panel receiving contribution so conversation, workflow selection, proposals and checks remain connected to the host's actual objects, tables, views and results. This API_CONTRACT contributes to OBJ-004: App/shared host contracts and a coordinated SWB journey that preserve operation meaning, host authority and recovery. It does not discharge the whole objective or the external host's construction obligations.

SOW-019 includes the panel requirements and App/shared receiving work, with reusable components conditional on an agreed repeated responsibility. SOW-020 requires host work and proposed changes to appear in the host's own tables and views without an agent-private result surface. Both are covered locally by the outputs and evaluation below. Preparation of receiving requirements can proceed independently; corresponding host conformance depends on actual external inputs.

Source locators below are repository-root-relative. The frozen decomposition is accepted by its DECISION.md; historical pending labels inside the frozen CSVs do not undo that decision. Original seed clauses are read within the accepted composite, qualified by later owner directions and the clarification; they are not separate current commitments.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **CLM-001** — The App/shared panel receiving owner owns this deliverable's interface requirements, receiving contribution and conformance account. The external host implementation owner owns host-specific panel assembly, layout, tables/views, domain construction and conversation persistence; for SWBPIPE this is the outside implementation session. A shared contract or prepared handoff supplies no evidence of external agreement, delivery or adoption. Sources: D, I/DEP-001, B/J and O, C, A §4, H §1.
- **CLM-002** — Within the App-v4 project, `DEL-02-01` supplies portable workflow/checkpoint meaning; `DEL-03-01` supplies catalog/read-basis meaning; `DEL-03-02` supplies proposal, validation and outcome meaning; `DEL-04-01` defines and carries adopted operation-policy and human-act distinctions; `DEL-04-03` supplies content-bound decision/run-record meaning; and `DEL-05-01` supplies loop messages/tools/events/checkpoints and receiving requirements. Their checked definitions are consumed here, not independently redefined. These are App-v4 identities, not identically numbered external-project deliverables. Sources: D, H §§2–6 and 9, A §§4–5.
- **CLM-003** — The App/shared contract owners decide shared contract/component placement against actual consumer responsibilities; the shared contract owner with the SWB implementation owner agrees the host/common construction boundary. An agreed repeated responsibility is required before common implementation. Compatible local implementation, library or service is not selected by the word shared. Sources: I/OI-013 and OI-014, B/decision 02, C, A/V4-ARC-20.
- **CLM-004** — The owner with App/SWB contract owners decides unresolved reserved-act and classifier policy; the App/shared human-act contract owner carries the adopted decision through App-v4 `DEL-04-01`. The person sets autonomy and performs their actual checking, acceptance, approval or professional-reliance act as applicable. The host owns offering, recording and presenting those acts and enforcing adopted operation policy; it is not the person performing them. An agent may faithfully record evidence of an act actually performed while keeping decision actor distinct from recorder, and may not fabricate one. Sources: I/OI-001 and OI-002, D row DEL-04-01, B/decision 03, P/V4-AUT-01–05, H/V4-HI-25 and 30–42.
- **CLM-005** — The App manager owns prepared handoff and dependency accounting, and the human relays contracts, questions and returns to the external SWBPIPE owner through repository files. Actual host inputs and candidate evidence are needed before the corresponding joined integration/examination; a contract definition is not an implemented host or a readiness verdict. Sources: I/DEP-001, B/J and O, O, P/OQ-11, E scenario preamble.

- **OUT-001** — Document the conversation/workflow/proposal/check panel interface and App/shared receiving requirements, mapping each interaction to consumed definitions, host objects/results and responsible participants. This is a behavior contract; exact API/wire fields and host layout follow the responsible definitions and agreements. Scope: SOW-019, SOW-020; objective: OBJ-004. Sources: D, P/V4-HOST-04, H, CLM-001, CLM-002.
- **OUT-002** — Document justified reusable-component allocation, actual consumers, repeated responsibility, ownership, receiving agreement or unresolved placement, and the external host construction boundary. An allocation may remain open at its accepted point of need; it must not be represented as agreed common construction. Scope: SOW-019; objective: OBJ-004. Sources: D, I, C, A/V4-ARC-20, CLM-003.
- **OUT-003** — Provide panel-to-host-table receiving cases and their evidence expectations, then record actual candidate-bound results when the corresponding checked interfaces and external host inputs exist. Cases cover required interactions, host-visible proposals/checks and truthful standing; missing host evidence stays an explicit limitation. Scope: SOW-019, SOW-020; objective: OBJ-004. Sources: D, P/V4-HOST-04, H/V4-HI-10–42, E/V4-EXM-20–22, CLM-005.
- **OUT-004** — Provide selected reusable panel components only after responsibility agreement establishes their concrete repeated purpose, App/shared ownership and receiving consumers. Until then, preserve the conditional CODE obligation and its unresolved allocation without claiming code exists or silently selecting common execution. This setup run authors the contract only. Scope: SOW-019; objective: OBJ-004. Sources: D anticipated artifacts, I/OI-013 and OI-014, C, A/V4-ARC-20, CLM-003.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — OUT-001 shall define conversation, workflow selection, proposal queue and check interactions as App/shared receiving requirements, each connected to the relevant host objects/results and the checked workflow, catalog, proposal, standing/record and loop-event definitions in CLM-002. It shall identify unavailable or unresolved inputs without pretending they have been supplied. Sources: D, P/V4-HOST-04, H/V4-HI-10–12, CLM-001, CLM-005.
- **REQ-002** — Host work and proposed changes shall be presented through the host's own tables and views, without an agent-private result surface. Proposal presentation shall retain affected objects, old/new values and reason; check findings shall refer to host rows/results and their standing. The panel shall preserve the consumed basis, outcome and receipt references instead of inventing independent domain truth or an alternate mutation route. Sources: SOW-020 in D, H/V4-HI-10–12 and 20–25, E/V4-EXM-20–21, CLM-001, CLM-002.
- **REQ-003** — The receiving interface shall preserve execution, queued proposal, actual edit acceptance, application, checking, approval and professional reliance as distinct subjects with their own actors and evidence. Operation success alone establishes none of the human acts; evidence of one act does not establish another or impose a synthetic rule that acceptance must precede every independently evidenced act. Proposal decisions shall say accept rather than approve. The interface shall allow faithful presentation/recording of an actually performed human act with the actual decision actor distinct from the recorder, retain content binding and visible lapse after changed bound content, and never fabricate an act. It shall consume adopted policy and checkpoint distinctions without deciding OI-001/OI-002. Sources: H/V4-HI-25, 30–33, 40–42 and 70–71, P/V4-AUT-01–05, B/decision 03, E/V4-EXM-21–22, CLM-004.

OUT-004 shall become an implementation obligation only for the selected agreed contribution; neither a common service nor reuse of existing components is mandatory by default. Sources: D, I, C, A/V4-ARC-20, CLM-003.
- **REQ-005** — OUT-003 shall define receiving cases against the assigned panel contribution, using checked source contracts and identified host inputs; actual host conformance shall be claimed only for the candidate and evidence received. ## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

First compare the accepted local scope, composite qualifications and current source clauses. Develop the panel requirements against the checked input definitions in CLM-002, recording absent definitions and host evidence explicitly. Prepare the allocation account and receiving cases without choosing unresolved internals or declaring an external commitment. Carry concrete questions and proposed interfaces through the App manager and human-relayed repository-file route in CLM-005. On receipt of actual agreements and host inputs, bind the applicable cases to that candidate and record observed results and limits. Common components follow their separately agreed allocation.

- **VER-001** — Inspect OUT-001 against D/SOW-019 and P/V4-HOST-04; trace conversation, workflow selection, proposal queue and checks individually to CLM-002 input definitions, host objects/results and responsible receiving participants. Record the exact supplied definitions or missing inputs. This checks interface completeness, not host implementation.
- **VER-002** — Exercise or specify candidate-bound panel-to-host-view cases for a proposed edit and an agent check. Inspect affected rows, old/new values, reason, basis, outcomes, receipt references and result standing against the consumed catalog/proposal contracts; confirm check findings reference rows/results without themselves changing the tables. Include an attempted detached agent-private result or bypass route as a rejection case. Until the external candidate exists, label these designed/unexecuted; cite H/V4-HI-10–25 and E/V4-EXM-20–21.
- **VER-003** — Use the adopted human-act/record contract to examine separate traces for execution success, queued proposals, actual acceptance/application, agent findings and evidenced human checking/approval/reliance. Reject fabricated human acts and cross-act promotion. Include a positive actual human act faithfully recorded by an agent/host recorder with actor, evidence and bound content preserved, plus an independently evidenced human act without inferred prior proposal acceptance. Confirm proposal text says accept and changed bound content lapses visibly. Leave operation-specific unruled policy cases pending their accepted decision; cite H/V4-HI-25, 30–42 and 70–71, B/decision 03 and P/V4-AUT-01–05.
- **VER-004** — Compare the allocation account to D anticipated artifacts, C, A/V4-ARC-20 and I/OI-013/OI-014. Inspect the repeated purpose, actual consumers, agreement evidence or unresolved allocation, external ownership, and exact owner/timing for each issue. Do not infer agreement from authored files.
- **VER-005** — Review the receiving-case inventory for AC-001 through AC-003 coverage, consumed-definition identity, candidate/evidence binding and actual external contribution. Compare pending cases to DEP-001 and P/OQ-11. Record what is defined, executed, limited or awaiting input; a missing host or policy input is not a passed conformance case. This is case-accounting evidence for AC-005, not independent execution of the other criteria.
- **VER-006** — If common panel construction has an actual agreed allocation, inspect the component and receiving evidence against that exact selected responsibility and the applicable OUT-001 contract. Otherwise verify the recorded conditional state, unresolved owner allocation and point of need without marking implementation complete. Cite D, C, A/V4-ARC-20 and I/OI-013/OI-014.
- **VER-007** — Review every excluded act in REQ-006 one-for-one against its cited claim and source owner. Run schema and boundary-owner validation, then semantically inspect external/human owners beyond the tool's deliverable-ID grammar. Confirm scoped receiving work, truthful evidence standing and no external or policy-owner substitution; cite D, I, B/J and O, H §1 and O.
