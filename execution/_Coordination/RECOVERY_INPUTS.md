## Evidence availability limits

Raw private native screenshots, journals, original state and signing/build
records are not reproducible outputs. Their owning Work accounts retain private
locators and observed subjects. Git does not contain those raw inputs or establish their current availability.
App Agent 0 reports an unavailable input when owner-controlled access cannot be
obtained; a new run cannot replace its historical identity.

Issue 168 records unavailable historical objects and original inputs, including
the historical E5 fixture, unadopted `SOURCE_BASIS.json`, original DEL-11 source
commits and supplier-source drift. This reconciliation supplies none of them
and grants no acquisition or repin. Historical claims remain limited by those
missing inputs. DEL-11-03's receiving assessment and acceptance evidence cannot
be inferred from a replacement file or regenerated fixture. At a point of actual
reliance, the owning deliverable and App Agent 0 must identify the missing source,
seek its authorized recovery or carry the limitation into the owner decision
packet. Preserved frozen/accepted material is not rewritten to remove the gap.
