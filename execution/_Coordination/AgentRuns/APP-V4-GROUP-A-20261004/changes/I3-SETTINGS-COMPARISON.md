## Contract and implementation

Selected sources: AS `AUTONOMY_AND_STANDING_EXCHANGE.md` §6 (lines548–630) and RS `RECORD_SEMANTICS.md` §8 (lines674–758). These require per-settings-version displayed-versus-recorded match/mismatch/missing-in-record/missing-in-display, defects returned without either side correcting the other, separate operation grants and destination settings, known-element read-limited comparison, refused-version missing reason and preserved observation limits. Existing `AS_SETTINGS_IN.schema.json` is the unchanged representation and the unchanged RS record schema owns `settings_version.body`. Existing `records::read_log` returns `Vec<Value>, Vec<String>`; this unit reads that interface rather than adding a log parser/writer or native authority.

`standing::compare_settings_versions` receives display setting documents, existing reader entry Values, an explicit reader-supplied Written/ReadLimited/RefusedVersion fact and limits. It returns data-only observations with both sides' setting bodies, differences separately for metadata/operation grants/destination settings, defects and limits. Complete bodies use existing SchemaClaim shape validation; that does not admit acts, grants or controls. Refused-version bodies are not used. Read-limited bodies supply only known elements: omitted members do not establish absence; incomplete entry referents retain limits without positional matching. Settings arrays use their existing contract referents and scopes, never filename/time/entry ordering as precedence. Duplicate versions and duplicate/conflicting collection entries retain ambiguity instead of choosing a winner.

Required person/policy references are checked as readable reference claims; absent/invalid references on complete claims are defects, never established grants. This is not reference-resolution or proof of an A12. Destination/contact event entries are outside settings comparison; "destinations not observed" and supplied process/read limits remain verbatim. The output has no route to AdmittedAct, ConfirmedGrant, policy treatment or capture. The preliminary ACT not-set branch was expressly excluded; its source is untouched.
