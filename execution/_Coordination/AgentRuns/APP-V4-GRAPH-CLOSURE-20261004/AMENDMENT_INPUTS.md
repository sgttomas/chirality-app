These are the ScopeOfWork and register items the graph-closure amendment
must carry or respect. Every case's MOVES_PROPOSED CSV is an input in
full. Its S1 sentences are listed there, not repeated here. This file
records the conditions that cut across cases.

  sentence for DEL-09-11 is worded as supply only. It must not say that
  DEL-09-07 uses DEL-09-11's input-set classes; otherwise N13 gains a

  items") and G2's 25 missing relationships that close no cycle.

  no longer literal (C7A-n1).
