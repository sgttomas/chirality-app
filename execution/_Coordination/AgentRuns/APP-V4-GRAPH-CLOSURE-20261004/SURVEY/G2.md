**How a relationship was recognized.** A Design statement counts when it says that one deliverable receives, consumes, relies on or is handed a named contribution of another. Typical forms are a row in an Interfaces, Expected-from-suppliers or Provided-to-receivers table; a Receivers or Suppliers header line; "relies on"; and a hand-over section. The following do not count:

- version pins and sibling-label lists;
- owner or allocation maps, such as "X is DEL-yy's";
- examples and cross-references;
- values the Design itself labels a **runtime value / no row** (the R17-10 and R23-2 pattern), or "read as files".

Following the brief, a pin counts only where the Design says that the contribution is consumed.

**How a relationship was matched to a row.** A match needs the same consumer and supplier, with the direction normalized: a consumer UPSTREAM row and a supplier DOWNSTREAM mirror are the same arc. The contribution must also be compatible with the row's Statement. Package-level rows (TargetType PACKAGE, not topological) are reported separately. They are not counted as matches for a deliverable-level statement, because DAG-004 excludes them.

**Reach.** `PKG-10_…/DEL-10-04_…/Design/prototype/dag_reach.py` (sha256 `63259575…`) was run with `--dag DAG-004 CONSUMER SUPPLIER`, read-only. It printed "DAG read: DAG-004 (named with --dag); arcs over both layers: 212"; `_DAG/_LATEST.md` also names DAG-004. The tool reports **SCC-FORMING** whenever the supplier already reaches the consumer, which includes an addition inside an existing SCC. A scratch Tarjan pass over the same 212 arcs therefore separates three cases: "inside an existing SCC (membership unchanged)", "new SCC" and "merges SCCs".

**Coverage check.** Each of the 212 arcs is named, by `DEL-` ID, in at least one endpoint's Design. Of the 424 arc ends, 416 are named in the deliverable's own Design. The other 8 are stated by the partner's Design or by an abbreviated list (for example DEL-09-01's "DEL-09-02, 09-05 …").

## 8. Limits

- The survey read relationship sections and every cross-deliverable mention. It did not read every paragraph of the 4.9 MB of top-level Design text, so a relationship stated only in prose, without a `DEL-` ID or a section's own label (for example "RS R3" with no ID), could be missed.
- Observation files, schemas and prototypes under `Design/` were not surveyed, except where a top-level file cites them as consumed.
- "Matched" checks consumer, supplier and contribution against the row's Statement at the level of the Design's interface row. It does not compare `RequiredMaturity`. The three known mirror maturity differences are carried from DAG-004, not re-derived.
- Whether N22…N27 and the §5 items are production inputs, rather than runtime values, verification inputs or design-time joins, is a judgment. It is flagged for the owners; this survey does not decide it.
- `dag_reach.py` reports an addition inside an existing SCC as SCC-FORMING. The distinction between "inside", "new" and "merge" comes from the scratch Tarjan pass, not from the tool.
