### MAJOR

**M-1 (J5, J8) — the act observed on X reaches EXEC without an act or
record identity, and without a reliable capture time.**
- Consumer: EXEC §2.4.5 "A human act is cited by its RS §6 record identity
  with its kind, evidenced capture time and capturing surface";
  `checkpoint-record-entries.schema.json` `act_ref` requires `record_id`,
  `act_kind`, `captured_at`, `capturing_surface` (used by `act_counted`,
  `act_not_counted`, `item_decision`, `act_lapsed`). RC-7 and SP-6
  (SETTLED by DECISION-K1 K1-2) cite "the earlier act and its time".
- Supplier: ADAPTER §7.7 CO-2/CO-3 elements "Act kind; decision actor;
  recorder *App*; recording mode *faithful recording*; bound content
  identity with method; capture evidence reference, or *not supplied*;
  capture time where given". The schema's `act` object has no act or record
  identity and `capture_time` is optional; `observe_map.py` never fills it.
  P's recorded state carries the host's `act_reference` (`proposal_state`
  `act_ref`: `act_kind`, `actor`, `act_reference`,
  `capture_evidence_reference`) — ADAPTER drops it — and no capture time.
  ADAPTER S-9 step 3 says "R9 references the record; the observation goes
  to DEL-02-03", but the CO does not carry that R9/RS §6 record's identity.
- Proof: R-6 had to use `source_record#item` as `record_id` and the host
  read time as `captured_at`.
- Options: (a) CO-2/CO-3 carry the RS §6 record identity the faithful record
  received (and the host act reference), plus the capture time with its
  source (P's `act_ref` gains a capture time where the host gives one);
  (b) EXEC resolves the act through RS §6 by a stated key (the host act
  reference) and says so. No ruling decides it; (a) keeps EXEC reading one
  supplier.

**M-2 (J5, J6) — the host-recorded request read over X has no supplier.**
- Consumer: EXEC RC-5 records a request "only where it is a structured item
  the App observes: … or a request the host records and a read over X
  returns"; `request_observed.form` includes `host_recorded_request`; RC-4:
  the recorder consumes only native items and "the observations on X that
  DEL-03-03 passes (ADAPTER §7.7)".
- Supplier: ADAPTER §7.7: "The agent's request for the act (R9-1; K1-1) is
  a message in the App conversation, observed by EXEC's mechanism, not by
  the adapter." No CO is a request.
- The ADAPTER sentence also reads the request as a message, while EXEC RC-5
  says "The recorder never classifies agent message text as a request".
- Fix options: ADAPTER adds a CO "request recorded by the host" (from a host
  read that returns one), or EXEC drops the third RC-5 form for X until a
  host offers it. ADAPTER's "is a message" sentence should follow EXEC RC-5
  (EXEC owns the recording mechanism, EXEC §2.5).

**M-3 (J1, J3; R13-1 not yet applied) — the host read without lineage.**
