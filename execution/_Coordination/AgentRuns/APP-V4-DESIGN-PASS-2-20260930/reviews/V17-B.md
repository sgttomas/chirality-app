**B-1 — EXEC CH-20 and WD-EX R-9b give different results for the same cited case. DISPATCH records this as "no conflict".**

- EXEC §7.2 CH-20. Its input is "WD-EX R-9b "prior act" [E1b variant
  L-WDEX-7]: T2's A4 on S-2 precedes `CP-review`'s arrival at T4". Its
  governance-phase result: "S-2's T2 A4 … counts for S-2 … The arrival
  waits only for an A4 on the bound referents T2 does not cover (S-3): an
  A4 on S-3 after T4 → **performed**, answered by two acts (JA-1)".
- WD-EX E2 R-9b. Its input is "T2 with E1b variant L-WDEX-7 (`CP-review`
  binding S-2)". Its result: "`CP-review` (binding S-2): **performed** by
  T2's A4".
- CH-20 states no binding of its own. It cites R-9b by content, and it
  says in §7 that WD-EX local cases are "cited **by content**". Read that
  way, it assigns S-3 to an arrival that WD-EX says binds S-2 only. So for
  one case the candidate gives two answers: *performed* now (WD-EX), or
  still waiting for S-3 (EXEC).
- The mismatch already existed at the base (EXEC then read "waiting until
  A4 on S-2 and S-3"; WD-EX read "waiting until a new A4"). Before K1-2
  both results were *waiting*, which hid it. Recomputing under K1-2 makes
  the results diverge.
- A3 returned the mismatch as an undecided R10 candidate (`WAVE_A/A3.md`
  §3 item 2). DISPATCH's A3 row then says: "EXEC CH-20 and WD-EX L-WDEX-7
  bind different supports by their own statements (no conflict; no
  ruling)". That is not true of CH-20, which has no statement of its own
  on binding.
- No other file cites R-9b or L-WDEX-7 (checked by grep across all 16
  Design files). LOOP FX-C4b, PANEL PC-19b, RS E10, ACT FX-45 and AS F17
  each state their own binding, and each result follows from its own
  binding.
- **Fix (either one):**
  - (a) Restate CH-20's input as an EXEC local variant (a new L-EXEC
    label): E1b with `CP-review` binding S-2 and S-3 per the OP-C3
    findings, and T2's A4 on S-2. Cite R-9b only as the S-2-only reading.
  - (b) Align CH-20 to L-WDEX-7: *performed* by T2's A4.

  Then correct the DISPATCH A3 row.

  and includes "records the refusal". V4-ARC-12 records "every destination

  now in the accepted basis loses its label) covers only what the text
  actually says. The relabel follows S1-D item 2 and A1-D (`WAVE_A/A1-D.md`
  row "R9-4; LOOP 2").
- **Fix:** label NW-5 DERIVED (from V4-HOST-01's "the person chooses" and
  the always-off item), or SETTLED only for "no silent switch unless the
  person turns it on" and PROPOSED for the rest. In the enforcement bullet,
  mark "records the refusal" PROPOSED. PANEL ND-1 and ND-4 inherit nothing
