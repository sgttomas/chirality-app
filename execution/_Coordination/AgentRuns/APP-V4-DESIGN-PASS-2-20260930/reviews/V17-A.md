## Findings

### B-1 — BLOCKING — ACT AP-5 makes recording optional; EXEC PH-6 and R9-1 make it required

- **Where:** ACT §4.0, row **AP-5 Recording is observation** (line 671).
  This text did not change from the base.
- **ACT says:** "A checkpoint's arrival, the act that answers it,
  act-declined, act-lapsed, run-resumed and run-ended events may be
  **recorded** (DEL-04-03) … (EXEC PH-6; confirmed by R8-11 item 1)".
- **EXEC-v0.5 §2.1 PH-6, the rule it cites, now says (line 261):**
  "A checkpoint's arrival and the act that answers it are **recorded** (RS)
  where the arrival is observed … Recording there is required, not optional
  (R9-1): SoW REQ-002 requires that history 'preserve the checkpoint's
  identity and actual disposition'." EXEC's change table records the edit as
  "(was 'may be recorded')".
- **R9-1 says:** "REQ-002 requires that history 'preserve the checkpoint's
  identity and actual disposition', so recording is required where the
  arrival is observed, not optional." GUIDE-v0.4 §0 follows R9-1 ("required,
  not optional"), and its change table says so.
- **Why it is blocking:** the same rule says opposite things in two files,
  and ACT cites the other file for its own wording. ACT is also inconsistent
  with itself: AP-12 (iii), now SETTLED by DECISION-K1 K1-1, makes recording
  what the product observes part of the product's role. No other file in my
  share still says "may be recorded" (checked by a pattern search over all
  seven files and over EXEC and LOOP).
- **Proposed fix:** reword AP-5's first sentence: "… run-ended events are
  **recorded** (DEL-04-03) where the arrival is observed. Recording there is
  required, not optional (R9-1; DEL-02-03 SoW REQ-002; EXEC PH-6), so the
  person and the agent can see …". Add an R9-1 row to ACT's "Changes from
  v0.6" table.
