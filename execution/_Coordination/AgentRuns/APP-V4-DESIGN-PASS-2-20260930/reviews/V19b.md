### NOTE

- **n-1 — RS §13's resolution account follows Python's `urljoin`, not RFC
  3986.** RS says that against the URN `$id` "the reference stays the bare
  relative path". The `referencing` library does produce that, and I
  observed it. Strict RFC 3986 §5.2 merging against
  `urn:chirality:app-v4:del-04-03:rs-record:0.1` gives instead
  `urn:PKG-02_Workflow%20and%20role%20portability/…/checkpoint-record-entries.schema.json`.
  The conclusion stands either way: a standard validator cannot retrieve it
  without a loader mapping, and a file-relative retrieval rule works. The
  alternative, "EXEC's schema registered under that relative path", works
  only for a resolver that behaves as `urljoin` does. *Suggestion:* "the
  resolved URI depends on the resolver's handling of a URN base (the bare
  path in Python `referencing`; `urn:PKG-02_…` under RFC 3986 merging);
  either way a loader must map it".
- **n-2 — PANEL's return-input schema puts the not-started case in a field
  named `refusal`.** The enum is "run not started — no model selected", "no
  credential", "turn not active", …. The field description says it "is not
  a boundary refusal". RT-d sets the case "apart from these" refusals. The
  two are reconcilable: it is not a *boundary* refusal, and the field holds
  the loop's outcome for the message. A later revision might rename the
  field `outcome` or add a separate `notStarted` member.
- **n-3 — LOOP's "in a live run nothing is written for the message" covers a
  case §5.1 does not let arise.** LOOP §5.1 defines *Unconfigured* as "The
  person has not chosen" and lists no return to it after a choice. MS-02
  and GUIDE M7.3 state the not-started recording without that qualifier.
  The two do not conflict in practice. *Suggestion:* say "cannot arise once
  a model is chosen", or drop the clause.
- **n-4 — RQ applied two rulings beyond the files they name, correctly.**
  The m-2 wording went also to HOSTING L-3 and F-31, WD `mcp-tool-call`,
  and EXEC AW-1 and U-E26; the n-1 relabel went also to ACT, AS and RS. I
  checked each against the OBS record and the fence. Nothing false.

  outside RQ's fence. The work graph's OBS-1 row says "mechanism an
  inference". The integrator may align DISPATCH's wording.
