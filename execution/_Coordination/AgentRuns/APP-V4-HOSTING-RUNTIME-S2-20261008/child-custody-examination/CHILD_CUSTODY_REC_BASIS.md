## Consequences when continuity cannot be proved after reap

A private installed-source token preserves provenance/full H5 but does not establish that a reused numeric PID/group still belongs to it. Reaping may leave descendants; inability to prove continuity is neither “zero survivors” nor authority to signal a group. A cleanup-unavailable observation must retain that uncertainty. Source closure/request custody can remain established from actual child end even while descendant cleanup is unresolved. Preserve original loss cause, first counts, absent acknowledgment and no resend; do not reopen registers or reinterpret an earlier unexpected exit as caused by a later Stop.

For **ordinary explicit Stop**, refusing an unwarranted signal is a correct execution boundary, but if it prevents the promised whole-tree treatment it is an unmet capability/availability condition, not completed Stop. A design must state its exact state/result and surviving/unknown observations, including Stop racing EOF reaping and handshake-failure cleanup, rather than calling every path stopped/tree-ended. The existing wording permits recording actual survivors; it does not permit replacing unobserved ownership/census with fabricated zero. Choosing a representation for incomplete cleanup must be checked against existing schema/row meanings.

For **confirmed App quit**, preserve the actual confirmation and known cleanup attempts, unresolved survivors and persistence limits. Do not invent a second confirmation solely because an implementation primitive failed. Equally, do not silently change quit into indefinite waiting, silently continue with falsely complete cleanup, or claim clean session end proves tree extinction. REC acknowledges system termination and missing final record; those failure facts do not automatically choose the intentional App-quit policy when cleanup is unavailable. Parent/Host/NIR must identify whether the concrete design preserves existing quit semantics or changes the user-visible completion/failure contract.

For **restart after unexpected exit**, no-group-proof does not authorize automatic survivor killing or automatic same-home overlap. Nor does it erase the accepted bounded automatic-restart obligation. The unimplemented/unsafe remainder must be explicit: U16's proposed overlap rule cannot be adopted incidentally by a child mutex or retry timeout. A design that waits forever, refuses all future starts, or starts alongside unverified survivors makes a substantive availability/overlap choice that needs owning disposition. The current no-resend rule remains unconditional.

For **post-LT12 explicit Stop**, retain prior unexpected exit separately from later requested cleanup. A leader already reaped is not a reason to infer a fresh owned group. The earlier named lifecycle-row gap remains separately scoped; STEP1 must not allocate a new row or silently withdraw the broad Stop/Restart affordance as a side effect of safer signalling.

## Concrete decisions and engineering boundaries

1. **Engineering within existing meaning:** exclusive child observation/reap/signal ownership, short lock protocol, exact source capture, fail-closed stale identity and truthful unavailable results. Review actual OS lifetime warrants and signal/reap serialization. No new human act category is intrinsically required; no source-reading evidence here proves a particular OS primitive works.
2. **U16 source choice if needed:** unexpected-exit survivors are shown and ended only by person versus another treatment; whether/when same-home next generation may overlap; how continuity is established. REC + App implementation owner own the open treatment, with Host/NIR receiving. Do not present the proposal as already selected. If the chosen consequence changes accepted survivor authority, confirmation, availability or scope, parent prepares that precise human decision with alternatives and consequences.
3. **Shutdown availability choice if needed:** actual confirmed quit when cleanup ownership remains unresolved—what remains recorded, what completes, and what is reported. This cannot be chosen by interpreting a test grace value as permission. First prepare the executable mechanism and its concrete failure surface; then identify only the consequential reserved choice, not a blanket new permission prompt.
4. **Timing:** choose and justify implementation values through U05 ownership; distinguish stopping a wait from declaring cleanup successful. A finite wait does not prove a source ended, confer a signalling capability or settle U16.

Before implementation release, require a source/operation table for observe-exit, reap, signal, grace-check, replacement and shutdown, with exact authority/locking and failure outcome. The protocol must prevent check-unlock-signal versus reaping races, avoid blocking child-mutex/source-gate inversion, preserve REC closure independently of cleanup status and identify every availability change. Synthetic controls and later qualified native evidence are different standing; neither this source assessment nor a diagnostic pass is product qualification.
