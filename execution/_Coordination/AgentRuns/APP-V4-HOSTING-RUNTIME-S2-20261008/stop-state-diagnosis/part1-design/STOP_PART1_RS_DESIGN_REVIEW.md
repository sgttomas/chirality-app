**Verdict: NOT READY for implementation release.** Concur with explicit admission/no-child dispatch and separately once-captured closure/exit evidence. Two concrete ownership/liveness choices remain blocking, and their availability consequence must be explicit in the released fence.

1. **A captured installation identity is attribution, not an OS signalling capability.** It prevents wrong-attempt joins but cannot establish ownership of a reused PID/process-group number after reap. “Currently owned unreaped leader” is a suitable proposed necessary condition, not a finished proof: the unreaped check and actual signal must be serialized against every reaper, including EOF, Stop, handshake failure and local allocation-failure cleanup. A check followed by unlocked killpg still races. Specify who owns Child/wait/reap, when PID/group identity remains reserved, how the same group association is established, and the atomic handoff before signalling. Do not infer whole descendant membership or absence from leader custody alone. An independently warranted owned-group mechanism is not present merely because a Rust token exists.

2. **The exact nonblocking ownership/reap protocol is required before code.** A Child mutex held across wait cannot be the mechanism if Stop must acquire it to signal the child whose exit the wait needs. “Extract or snapshot” is insufficient without naming which actor then owns the wait and how Stop obtains safe signalling permission without a stale numeric snapshot. Specify bounded check/signal/reap critical sections, lock order, deadline handling and late exit observation. No source/Inner/REC locks across waits; no inverse Child/source-gate edge. The protocol must cover EOF before Stop, EOF during grace, force-after-grace, handshake failure and replacement, not only ordinary ready Stop.

## Compatibility and availability consequence

Refusing to signal when ownership cannot be established is faithful evidence/safety behavior; manufacturing tree-ended or killing a possibly unrelated group is not. However the proposed post-reap cleanup-unavailable branch is an **observable availability change**, especially for current post-LT12 Stop→start. The design cannot simultaneously promise unchanged residual availability and guarantee that this branch succeeds. Revise the selected fence to say that the existing unsupported post-LT12 mapping remains unresolved, while unsafe signalling is withheld and cleanup/Stop completion can remain unavailable. No false stopped/tree-ended/no-descendants claim may enable a new start or overlap merely by clearing the association.

This limitation is not selection of a new automatic descendant policy. It does not waive HOSTING §4.5 whole-tree deliberate Stop, §4.6 availability, or §4.3–4.4 restart completeness. Parent must explicitly accept this bounded incomplete implementation scope or commission a reviewed ownership solution before claiming complete behavior. A new human act is not inherently required for a protective unavailable outcome. A genuinely reserved consequence arises if the chosen remedy changes when survivors are ended automatically, live-work confirmation, allowed overlap/restart policy, or accepted delivery scope. Do not invent such a choice by implication. Part2 remains separate; no LT25 or policy is authorized here.

Also preserve the distinction between no supplier leader and no actionable owned tree. A reaped source with unresolved descendants must not satisfy a direct no-child Stop merely because its Child slot is empty. Retain truthful unresolved custody; no census or descendant-success assertion follows from group_alive Boolean or a missing PID.

## Receipt standing: concur with precise limits

Independent ClosureObservation and ExitObservation correctly permit first closure counts/cause before a genuine wait result, and late genuine exit evidence after an immutable unknown event. Closure-once is source-local effect custody, not durable atomic completion. Keep queued/error/partial persistence standing and poison-on-unwind; no retry that repeats already-applied closure. Unknown exit at a deadline is not a once-set real exit result. A contradictory duplicate adds a bounded limit rather than changing history. First supplier-exit cause cannot become supplier-stop because a later cleanup was requested.

LT23 with unknown exit fields is possible only when its actual stopping/tree-end-or-force guard and truthful descendant handling are otherwise satisfied; expiry of the exit-wait deadline alone does not establish that guard. If cleanup is unavailable, report unavailable and retain the actual event/receipt standing; do not emit LT23 merely to fit an expected terminal envelope. Historical same-H5 receipts and original LT09 references remain evidence, not renewed integrity, qualification or current signalling authority. Internal event/component binding may preserve provenance without changing closed serialized shapes, but readers must not be promised a new typed serialized link that the schema lacks.

Provide the concrete signal/reap ownership protocol and its race ordering; explicitly state post-reap unavailable state/return and future-start eligibility; bind LT23 emission to actual cleanup outcome rather than status-wait timeout; preserve the stated capture-once and partial-persistence limits. Add deterministic reaper-versus-signal and unavailable-cleanup controls to the existing regression plan with owned harmless processes or inert seams. This review runs none. All prior implementation, independent-review and receiving-adoption requirements remain. No extra human gate is added by this assessment.
