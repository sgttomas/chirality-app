## Exit facts, closure counts and LT23

on_eof reaps only the captured child and rechecks H5 under source gate. It calls close_generation before either branch. In stopping it journals exitFacts and notifies Stop, but discards the returned counts. In ready it embeds exitFacts and counts in LT12 but does not add the stopping-style exit journal fact. Stop later searches only that journal and calls close_generation again. Accordingly:

- Ordinary stopping EOF may close pending requests first; later Stop closure counts can be zero although the earlier closure ended requests. Do not present those recomputed residual counts as the original closure totals without defining their scope. The current table says closed-generation counts; the repair should preserve the first actual captured counts, not duplicate closure actions or manufacture totals.
- Stop after LT12 can miss already observed exitCode/signal, wait for an exit journal entry that will not arrive, and substitute unknown facts. Preserve LT12 as actual source. A repair may retain source-owned per-generation exit/closure observations at original capture and reuse them with explicit source identity, rather than rewrite past events or infer success from the process having disappeared. Define this source capture once, including repeated EOF behavior and first-observation/conflict handling, with REC owner review.
- For no-child direct Stops there is no new child exit, no invented successful exit code, no new H5 or closed-generation event. Do not call close_generation on a stale/absent generation merely to fill a schema. LT20/21/22 need actor/stop record under their existing shapes, not fabricated LT23 fields. Historical generation association, if any, must remain explicitly historical and must not claim a new closure.
- LT23 requires actual stopping plus child/tree-ended or forced-after-grace handling. Preserve surviving/unknown findings; `surviving` currently comes from a group_alive Boolean encoded as 0/1, not a census of all descendants. The record must not imply universal process-tree termination merely because state becomes stopped. No-child paths should not pass through LT23 and claim descendants checked/none surviving as a substitute for no child.

RS §9 says resolved references establish the source identity, not stronger act/change standing. Thus keep actual Stop request, execution-loss cause, original exit/closure observation, later cleanup observation and artifact-publication outcome distinct. A later deliberate Stop cannot retroactively make the earlier LT12 unexpected exit deliberate. Full session/home/spawn scope, actual event sequence, no-resend and independent publication status remain mandatory.

This is a missing contract case requiring a concrete named reviewed change and affected HOST/REC/NIR/RS/consumer adoption; not ordinary implementation divergence alone.
