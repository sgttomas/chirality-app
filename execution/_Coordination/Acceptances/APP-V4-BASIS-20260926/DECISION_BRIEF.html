A focused renewal, with a clear purpose

Embedded workflow capability in engineering and design applications is primary. The standalone App is the workflow-making exemplar, and methods can originate and evolve inside each host.

Recommend a renewal of shared meaning, responsibility and working contracts—then let the necessary implementation boundaries follow.

Use the three manuals as the core working governance the owner has selected. Express technical product commitments through Scopes of Work and their derivatives. Preserve the complete thesis unchanged. Account for real consumers of Root material before changing how that material is supplied.

Carry the recorded v4 direction into this work: stock Codex for the App; Tauri and the minimal host loop; a simpler local-first host experience. Define a first connected engineering activity and a staged transition. Proceed whether PEC is available or still being completed.

Why this course

The research found actual shared dependencies and distinct local responsibilities. It also found useful existing mechanisms, unestablished cross-application joins, and successful bounded repairs. Together they justify working on the shared contracts; they do not prove that the whole program must be rebuilt.

A new activity can warrant change even when an old arrangement works. Conversely, unfamiliar records or a missing witness do not establish a defective architecture.

Expected benefit: more capable work with clearer ownership and less repeated reconstruction. That is a reasoned expectation, not a measured saving.

The choice now is a design direction. The seven decisions below form one connected discussion. They are not seven mandatory approval gates, and the proposed behavioral evidence is not a prerequisite to conceptual discussion.

A short orientation

“Chirality” names both a body of ideas and a developing family of applications. The shared concepts, the software that carries them, and the records that authorize particular work are related—but they are different things.

Root

The shared conceptual and organisational home: roles, workflows, tools and documents. Today, some material here is packaged into applications. That technical dependency does not make Root the human decision-maker.

The Chirality App

The standalone place to create and refine workflows with an agent, and an exemplar for host experiences. A workflow is reusable guidance for doing work, with declared requirements and human checkpoints.

Piping / SWBPIPE

The engineering design/analysis application and first intended engineering host. It owns its model, domain validation and application of changes. An agent should work on those same meaningful objects and results.

Runtime

In the examined current system, an App-owned service hosts the stock Codex agent supplier. It handles execution and communication. Its present existence does not require the same service in v4.

PEC

Its intended purpose is to derive qualified coordination views from project files and add temporary presence information. Delivery and consumer qualification are separate. It helps people and agents see work; it does not own execution or make their decisions.

Scopes of Work (SoWs)

Production contracts: what a contribution must provide, its criteria, interfaces and examination. The manuals guide how to carry the work; SoWs and derivatives hold the product’s technical commitments.

Current versus intended: In v4’s recorded direction, the standalone App uses Tauri with stock Codex; engineering hosts use a minimal embedded agent loop and retain their domain responsibilities. That direction is not yet a demonstrated complete implementation. Domains is a separate evidence-retrieval connector; it is independent of PEC, and its current receiving contract remains a bounded question.

Seven decisions, with their consequences

The recommendations below are judgments grounded in the checked account. Each retains a plausible alternative and the condition that would make it preferable.

01
RecommendDirection now

Renew the shared responsibilities before redrawing the folders

How far should the renewal reach beyond the App?

Recommendation

Make a focused program-level account of shared concepts, working guidance, product commitments and their owners. Derive the necessary source and implementation changes from it.

The human’s new direction changes how Root, manuals and technical specifications should relate. At the same time, Root material has actual packaging, tool and instruction consumers. That combination warrants shared responsibility work, not a filesystem rearrangement treated as its own success. [S1 C01/C08; S2 C01–C04]

Focused shared renewalRecommendedA new activity changes shared obligations or ownership.Some coordination now; a coherent basis for local implementations.

Costs and risks

This adds upfront conception and consumer accounting. It can become a new documentation burden if the account duplicates SoWs or records with no reader. Keep it tied to the decisions, interfaces and recovery needs it serves.

Practical consequence

Keep the thesis intact; use manuals for practice; trace technical commitments into SoWs. Classify historical statements by present applicability. Check packaging and supplied-instruction consequences before moving active files.

Open: the exact distribution of shared guidance, project-specific instructions and technical contracts. This recommendation does not approve a new precedence tree.

Reconsider when: concrete cases show the shared account changes nothing useful, or a coupled new capability cannot be defined coherently within these boundaries.

Evidence and contrary finding

Current Root material is enumerated and packaged; changing its placement can affect executable checks. Yet historical state reconstruction also resolved apparent contradictions without architectural replacement. These findings support deliberate accounting of consumers and meaning, while keeping both local repair and broader renewal available.

02
RecommendDirection now · placement during definition

Share meaning and contracts; justify shared execution by use

Does common workflow capability require one common executable service?

Recommendation

Start from compatible workflow, identity, human-act and evidence contracts. Share implementation where a concrete repeated responsibility earns it. Retain the recorded Codex/Tauri/minimal-loop direction rather than reopening supplier selection.

Shared ideas or files do not establish a common runtime dependency. The existing App/Runtime chain is real, but the host’s domain application remains local. The examined stock-Codex replatform retained the App-owned service and its communication channels while repairing other causes; it is not evidence that a service is inherently wrong. [S2; S3 H1]

Alternatives

Compatible local implementations preserve host fit but require conformance work. A shared library can remove repeated logic with release/version coupling. A shared service can own a truly common lifecycle, but introduces process, availability and upgrade coordination.

Consequences

Contract-first work keeps host-specific behavior expressible and avoids assuming portability from matching file formats. Its cost is testing and maintaining the seams. A shared implementation may reduce duplication while making every consumer depend on its changes.

Open: where the workflow/checkpoint record, catalog types and reusable interface pieces should live. Resolve their placement against actual consumers and lifetime maintenance, not package names alone.

Reconsider when: multiple consumers demonstrably need the same stateful responsibility, or local implementations repeatedly diverge at a cost exceeding common-component maintenance.

Why the retained-service history matters

D-GOV-43’s A2 supplement retained service/socket/SSE and rejected their inherent wrongness as the diagnosis. Success followed a changed supplier and several integration repairs. A silence check already passed before the later App-proxy keepalive. There is no topology-only experiment or measured lifetime-cost result, and this history does not undo D19/D20’s later choices.

RecommendPrinciple now · operation-level choices next

Make autonomy practical without confusing human acts

What may an agent do directly, and what must remain an attributable human decision?

Recommendation

Keep graduated autonomy. Define it by operation and consequence, and keep proposing, applying, marking checked, accepting an edit and accepting professional reliance distinct.

The owner selected graduated autonomy and validation proportionate to the situation. Existing Piping proposal/Checked arrangements have their own scope. Neither that predecessor nor the new choice settles every pending reserved-act or classifier question. A tool’s success must not become an invented human act. [S1 C04/C13; decision lineage]

TreatmentProposed working approachConsequence
Read, examine, prepareAgents act within granted scope and expose grounds and limitations.Useful initiative with less routine prompting.
Apply a changeDirect application where the person grants it; otherwise a visible proposal. Preserve origin, basis, outcome and recovery.Autonomy gains require reliable validation and undo/recovery.
Attributed judgmentPrepare human checking, acceptance and reliance decisions; do not record them as performed by the person when they were not.Necessary human attention remains; its exact scope must be clear.

Fair alternatives

A proposal-only route is preferable for a consequential or poorly understood operation; it can overload review if universal. Broad direct application may suit well-bounded, reversible work but cannot collapse acceptance or professional reliance into execution.

Whole-course cost

Fewer prompts are useful only when decisions are genuinely delegated. Silent ambiguity shifts effort into incident reconstruction. The interface should show the active autonomy scope and ask for meaningful decisions, not every harmless step.

Open: the always-reserved list and the treatment of classifier-based routine permissions in the App. Discuss concrete operations; routine tool permission and a professional reliance act are different subjects. The table is a proposed interpretation, not an accepted new policy.

Reconsider when: consequences, reversibility, available examination or the human’s intended delegation change.

Preserve the meanings through the UI

A queued proposal is not an applied operation; an applied edit is not engineering approval. A receipt records the operation’s outcome, not an act of acceptance. The agent checking the engineer’s work is itself a desired activity and need not modify the model. Current authority in an existing project remains scoped there until an authorized transition.

04
RecommendContract definition · evidence on a candidate

Define parity by useful results, and qualify the extension promise

What does “the agent can do what the person can do” actually commit the host to?

Recommendation

Define parity across meaningful reads, operations, validation, results and recovery. Treat “a new catalog operation becomes available everywhere without separate work” as a distinct, still-unestablished candidate promise.

Runtime’s application-tool transport and Piping’s domain controller/applier are separately traced chains. Shared validation is valuable; it does not by itself join those chains, preserve an earlier inspection basis, create a UI or prove durable exactly-once mutation. [S2 C09–C14; S1 A3]

Alternatives and tradeoff

Curated adapters make per-operation work visible and can fit a specialist interface. Catalog-driven generation is preferable where it truly removes repeated work without losing meaning. Full automatic availability is a stronger promise with a larger compatibility and examination burden.

Recommended consequence

Specify the common semantics first, then identify what the catalog generates and what each adapter still implements. Trace one newly added operation across person, embedded agent and external agent before claiming the extension property or maintenance saving.

Open: whether to retain the absolute V4-PAR-05 wording, narrow it to defined generated surfaces, or make it a later capability. This brief recommends explicit qualification; it does not silently weaken an accepted requirement.

Reconsider when: an identified implementation demonstrates automatic all-actor extension, or a domain-specific interface makes generic generation less useful than deliberate adaptation.

A concrete parity case

Have each channel refer to the same model revision and operation. Compare availability reasons, invalid/stale refusal, non-mutating checking, old/new values, actual application and the receipt after interruption. Equal mouse gestures are unnecessary; equal operation meaning and truthful standing are essential. No such live three-channel witness was established by this research.

RecommendSelect the activity now · define before executing

Use one connected engineering activity to organize the first increment

Which activity will make the shared contracts concrete without attempting the whole program at once?

Recommendation

Prepare a bounded SWBPIPE model-adjustment and checking activity, linked to a reusable workflow. Use it to define the first App/host handoff and its examination after the implementation basis is accepted.

This follows the embedded-primary purpose and the recorded replacement direction. Existing registration protects reviewed workflow identity, and Piping has a domain application route. Their combination remains unestablished, so the activity tests a meaningful join instead of merely repeating isolated component demonstrations. [S1 A1/A3; S2; D11]

The proposed activity

On invented engineering material, inspect a model, propose an adjustment, ask for a non-mutating check, encounter an intervening edit, and recover the actual outcome and receipt. Carry the method between App and host with its assumptions, tool needs, revision and human checkpoints visible.

Keep its scope manageable

Define the joins together, then stage their implementation and examination. Add the external channel and catalog-extension check as bounded parts of the same contract when ready. Preserve the separate standalone core-loop obligation. This is not a single all-or-nothing launch test.

Alternative first activityPrefer it whenWhat it leaves open
App workflow loopHost prerequisites make the engineering slice impractical while useful App work is ready.Embedded parity and host-owned outcomes.
Long fleet undertakingImmediate review/recovery burden is the owner’s most consequential problem.Host operation semantics and the embedded replacement journey.

Open: the useful operation, permitted autonomy and exact candidate environment. Give the increment a SoW, owners and relevant checks before execution; no supplier experiment is launched by selecting its purpose.

Reconsider when: prerequisite readiness or the owner’s next valuable undertaking makes another activity more informative. A single successful slice will not prove all host or fleet behavior.

What recovery must actually establish

A selected workflow basis can be resolved before a provider is able to adopt it. Provider acceptance does not establish model obedience. Likewise, reconnecting a conversation is not recovery of a whole undertaking: returned work, independent findings, active workers, ownership and pending human choices remain separate facts. Later long-work examination should join those facts, without claiming the first host slice has done so.

RecommendPlan now · consume within qualified scope

Make PEC useful without making it a starting condition

How should work proceed when PEC is qualified, limited or unavailable?

Recommendation

Define a receiving contract for each application, and keep a deliberate file-based route. Begin v4 work with the available route; adopt PEC’s qualified capability as it becomes available.

PEC is intended across App, Piping and later hosts. Its adopted product definition permits bounded operational reliance after qualification. Current foundations and accepted product text do not establish a delivered response path, released reliance or adoption by a consumer. [S1 C14/C15; S2 C15–C18; owner E]

Qualified, current, enabled

Use covered record-tier claims within the released and adopted consumer envelope. Show the examined-through pin, coverage and freshness. Keep human acts and source authority in their owning records.

Stale, partial or failing

Show which conclusions are unsupported. Reconstruct affected questions from sources; do not infer empty work, readiness or permission from a failed feed. Continue independent work with a sound basis.

Unavailable

Agents locate and compare graphs, decisions and revisions. Managers maintain review and integration ownership. The human carries more cross-undertaking coordination. v4 can still proceed.

Figure 3 · Proposed handling of PEC’s condition. These are consumer paths to define and examine, not a claim that qualified delivery exists now. Limited or absent PEC changes who performs coordination work; it does not supply permission or cancel v4’s purpose.

Alternatives

Wait for PEC only if a particular undertaking cannot be carried responsibly with available coordination—not as a blanket v4 condition. Remain file-only when the current workload does not justify connector upkeep; retain PEC’s intended future value.

Costs and consequences

PEC adds provider, feed-version and consumer maintenance. Its absence adds repeated discovery and human reconstruction. It does not take over execution, review ownership, an execution queue or human rulings. No savings have been measured.

Open: the first supported consumer questions, release/adoption evidence and failure behavior. Domains is independent and needs its own source-admission, freshness and receiving contract.

Reconsider when: coordination load materially changes, a qualified consumer path becomes available, or file/feed changes invalidate earlier coverage.

Qualification is a separate fact from product definition

PEC’s CP2/CP3 records bind the product definition while retaining separate release, consumer-adoption and advertised-reliance conditions. A guarded store or valid response envelope can still contain untrue supplied metadata. An old App bridge test for a prototype does not qualify current PEC. A useful later examination follows one source pin through a response and receiving action, then checks the same question with absent or limited feeds.

RecommendTransition definition · human replacement later

Let old and new coexist while responsibility moves deliberately

How can a renewed basis become usable without erasing work or prematurely retiring the fallback?

Recommendation

Adopt the renewed basis by identified scope and consumer. Keep history intact, make current applicability easy to find, and retire old arrangements only when their continuing obligations have a clear disposition.

The historical cases show why a frozen header, a later ruling, an applied change and a lifecycle label are different facts. Some apparent conflicts disappear when their lineage is recovered. That supports clearer current navigation and explicit adoption rather than rewriting history to look uniform. [S3 H2/H3; S1 C09]

What to carry

Preserve complete thesis bytes, attribution and standing; old projects and archives; useful source and reviewed methods; actual work, pending obligations and evidence. Bind the new instructions supplied to agents to their own identity and adoption point.

What to account for

List affected consumers of active Root material, implementation dependencies, tool paths and instruction packaging. Carry technical changes through their owners’ SoWs and relevant checks. Verify later mainline changes before relying on this pinned research as a current implementation map.

What the continuation evidence does—and does not—show

Selected App and Piping histories show actual bounded merges, pause/resumption, retained partial work, independent review and correction. They do not prove complete migration, closure of every final-head check, or general long-run efficiency. Preserve those useful examples without turning them into a claim that the current program needs no renewal.
