# DEL-01-02 — Durable execution and request recovery

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Provide the App's durable execution and request-recovery contribution, owned by the **App execution/recovery owner**. Preserve ongoing work and outstanding requests when a window disappears, implement explicit interruption, and recover actual Codex state and prior conversation access after reconnect or relaunch. This is the accepted SOFTWARE `BACKEND_FEATURE_SLICE` in PKG-01. The contract defines future production and its evidence; its existence does not demonstrate implemented behavior or change lifecycle standing.

The accepted [decomposition decision][decision] gives the frozen [Deliverables row][deliverables], [ScopeLedger rows][scope], [Package row][packages] and [Objectives rows][objectives] their downstream standing. Historical pending labels in those frozen rows are read with that actual decision. The local contribution to OBJ-001 is recoverable native interaction within the standalone workflow-making loop; to OBJ-002 it is main-process execution/recovery within the chosen App architecture; to OBJ-005 it is truthful request/outcome evidence. This slice does not claim to fulfill the entirety of those shared objectives.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — Keep each Codex child (one per App-owned Codex home), its protocol session and outstanding-request register in the main process. Closing or reloading an observer window shall preserve ongoing work and outstanding approvals; hiding a window shall not be treated as a stop command. Window visibility and execution state shall remain distinct. Sources: SOW-060, SOW-122; CLM-002.
- **REQ-002** — Support the person's interruption of a turn as an explicit act and preserve its actual observed result. Interrupting a turn, ending a workflow run and stopping the Codex process are distinct operations; an interrupt, a quit or a supplier exit never ends a run. A turn ended by Codex after the person's cancel answer to a tool-permission request is recorded with that cause; items a turn opened and did not complete are stated as not completed. A stop request, its transmission and an observed interruption are distinguishable; loss of an observer alone shall not imply a stop, and primary-turn completion shall not imply every descendant has stopped. Sources: SOW-007, SOW-061; CLM-002; [Examination][examination] V4-EXM-11.
- **REQ-003** — Answer or explicitly decline every agent/server request through the selected native protocol. Outstanding requests remain recoverable across observation loss. Preserve the actual grant, denial, answer or explicit decline; silence, timeout, reconnection and restart shall never grant approval. No pending request is declined automatically after a period; a request still pending when the Codex process stops ends unanswered and is never answered afterwards. Sources: SOW-063, SOW-064, SOW-122; CLM-002 and CLM-004.
- **REQ-004** — Return an explicit error for an unknown server request using the supplied protocol boundary. The explicit error is written at receipt by the boundary of App-v4 DEL-01-01; this slice records its result and keeps it in custody across observation loss and relaunch. Preserve the difference between local settlement/reply writing and observed acknowledgment; an unavailable acknowledgment shall not be reported as received. Sources: SOW-123, SOW-065; CLM-002.
- **REQ-005** — On reconnection recover actual Codex state and outstanding requests instead of reconstructing authority from a Chirality transcript copy. After quitting/relaunching the App or restarting, support continuation of the conversation with access to what happened previously. When the person quits with live turns or pending requests, the App asks first and lists them; on confirmation the live turns are interrupted and recorded as interrupted by quit, and after relaunch they are shown with an offer to resume. The same applies when the person stops or restarts Codex from the App. These obligations do not assert that the old process keeps executing through an App quit or machine restart. Sources: SOW-008, SOW-062, SOW-066, SOW-124; CLM-002; [Examination][examination] V4-EXM-11.
- **REQ-006** — Supply observed events and explicitly unknown outcomes to the receiving UI and compact-evidence interface. Supplier facts (supplied guidance, model destination, tool-permission settlements) reach App-v4 DEL-04-03 directly from App-v4 DEL-01-01; this slice hands its own custody observations in its own format and keeps across relaunch only references and App-observed facts, never a copy of conversation content. Faithfully retain an actually evidenced human answer/act with its real actor and subject; distinguish that actor from the recorder. Execution, transport success and recovery shall not manufacture proposal acceptance, checking, approval or professional reliance. No artificial dependency requiring proposal acceptance before any other independently evidenced act is imposed. Conversation recovery shall not be represented as recovery of a whole branching undertaking's ownership, returned work, review, integration or active descendants. Sources: SOW-063, SOW-064, SOW-065; CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-004; [HTML decision 05][html].
- **REQ-007** — Preserve required restart and request-answering behavior regardless of whether old client logic is reused. Record any proposed reuse, its receiving-contract assessment and current candidate evidence, or the choice of another implementation. Keep unselected means explicit and use the supplied stock-Codex contract without restoring the excluded v3 Runtime service. Sources: SOW-125; CLM-005.
- **REQ-008** — Deliver the fixtures and candidate-bound evidence for this slice's coupled failure transitions, retaining passed, failed, blocked, not-run or inconclusive results as actually observed. Source paths, defined fixtures and historical checks shall not be claimed as current execution evidence. Sources: CLM-001, CLM-002; [Examination][examination] §2 and V4-EXM-11.
- **REQ-009** — This slice shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: supplier pin selection and protocol generation belong to App-v4 `DEL-01-01`; request-card and outcome-view implementation belongs to App-v4 `DEL-01-04`; compact run-file schema and record writer/reader construction belong to App-v4 `DEL-04-03`; adopted operation-policy contract construction belongs to App-v4 `DEL-04-01`. Each act and owner is stated in CLM-003. Unresolved policy choices remain the actual owner's and host policy owner's decisions in CLM-003 and CLM-004; consuming that policy does not decide it. This boundary preserves this slice's custody, state and evidence interfaces.

- **AC-001** — Closing, hiding and reloading an observer window leave ongoing work and outstanding approvals under main-process custody, with no stop or approval inferred from visibility loss. Verify REQ-001 with VER-001.
- **AC-002** — An explicit turn interrupt is distinguishable from observation loss and from a run end, and the reported outcome reflects the observed interruption or remaining uncertainty, including any separately observed active descendant. Verify REQ-002 with VER-002.
- **AC-003** — Known requests support actual grants, denials, answers and explicit declines; their outstanding state is preserved across observation loss, and silence, timeout or recovery never becomes approval. Verify REQ-003 with VER-003.
- **AC-004** — An unknown server request receives an explicit error through the selected supplier interface; missing acknowledgment remains unobserved even if a reply write or local settlement occurred. Verify REQ-004 with VER-004.
- **AC-005** — After loss and restoration of connection, the recovered execution and outstanding-request state is grounded in Codex observations; stale local rendered/transcript state cannot establish an outcome. Verify the reconnect part of REQ-005 with VER-005.
- **AC-006** — After App quit/relaunch or restart, the person can continue the conversation and access prior events, while recovered, currently outstanding and unavailable facts remain distinguishable, including turns interrupted by a confirmed quit. Verify the continuation part of REQ-005 with VER-006.
- **AC-007** — The UI/evidence handoff preserves observed events and unknown outcomes, including a faithfully recorded actual human response with decision actor distinct from recorder. Fabricated human acts and stronger unsupported outcome claims are rejected; no additional act is inferred from execution, settlement, acknowledgment or recovery, and no independently evidenced act is erased merely because another act is absent. Verify REQ-006 with VER-007.
- **AC-008** — The delivered fixtures and results cover this slice's window loss, explicit interruption, known/unknown requests, denial, timeout, reconnect, restart and missing-acknowledgment transitions against identified candidates, without substituting historical passes for current behavior. Verify REQ-008 with VER-008.
- **AC-009** — Documentation accounts for unknown outcomes, recovery/evidence interfaces, all four anticipated artifact classes, optional old-client reuse and the unresolved means, with the named receiving and decision owners retained. Required behavior remains covered whether reuse is selected or declined. Verify REQ-007 and REQ-009 with VER-009.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Work from the supplied stock supplier boundary and its selected-version types. The App execution/recovery owner integrates the coupled state transitions, then provides state/request interfaces to App-v4 DEL-01-04 and compact observation evidence to App-v4 DEL-04-03. Resolve relevant open inputs at their point of need. Define fixtures from the source-grounded criteria before relying on their evidence; do not invent wire fields, timing thresholds or a persistence technology here.

Use recorded native exchanges for deterministic seam checks where possible and deliberate native App witnesses for the interruption/relaunch behavior. Record candidate, selected supplier/configuration and date, with each result's actual outcome and limitations ([Examination][examination] V4-EXM-01…05). Coordinate the receiving UI's applicable WebKit/Chromium and packaged smoke evidence with its owner; browser evidence alone cannot establish a required native restart witness. Product scenario execution is future work under its own authorization, not an act performed by this INIT contract.

- **VER-001** — Observe main-process child/session/request custody while a run has ongoing work and an outstanding approval. Close, hide and reload its observer window; compare execution/request observations before and after and inspect any emitted stop/approval action. Sources: CLM-002; V4-EXM-11. Implements AC-001.
- **VER-002** — Interrupt a turn deliberately and trace the request and observed provider result. Contrast an observer loss and an unavailable stop acknowledgment. Include a separately active descendant where the native supplier exposes one; verify the report does not infer its stop from primary-turn completion. Sources: CLM-002; V4-EXM-11. Implements AC-002.
- **VER-003** — Exercise known request grant, denial, question answer and explicit decline using the selected native request types. Interrupt observation while a request is outstanding and restore it; exercise silence/timeout without granting permission. Compare actual replies and recovered outstanding state. Sources: SOW-063, SOW-064, SOW-122; V4-EXM-11. Implements AC-003.
- **VER-004** — Supply an unknown server request at the supplier boundary and inspect its explicit error. Exercise an unavailable acknowledgment after reply writing/local settlement and check that the report makes no acknowledgment or success claim unsupported by observation. Sources: SOW-123, SOW-065; CLM-002. Implements AC-004.
- **VER-005** — Disconnect and reconnect with outstanding work/requests and deliberately stale rendered or copied-transcript state. Compare recovered state with native Codex observations; retain uncertainty wherever actual outcomes cannot be recovered. Sources: SOW-062, SOW-124; CLM-002. Implements AC-005.
- **VER-006** — Quit with live work, confirm the question, relaunch the identified native App, then continue the conversation and inspect prior-event access and any outstanding/unknown state. Record actual supplier recovery behavior without assuming process survival during quit. Sources: SOW-008, SOW-066; V4-EXM-11. Implements AC-006.
- **VER-007** — Inspect the receiving state/evidence handoff for observed and unavailable outcomes. Include an actually supplied human answer/act recorded with its subject, actor and recorder distinguished, and negatives containing absent human evidence, transport-only success, lost acknowledgment and conversation-only recovery. Confirm execution, acceptance, checking, approval and reliance retain separate evidence; an independently evidenced act stays recordable without inventing other acts. Sources: CLM-004; REQ-006; V4-EXM-11. Implements AC-007 within this slice's handoff, not the sibling record schema.
- **VER-008** — Review the fixture inventory and candidate-bound result records against REQ-001…REQ-006 and the delivered code interfaces. Check coverage of the named transitions, actual executed/not-run standing and configuration identity; trace each asserted product result to its relevant observation. Sources: CLM-001; V4-EXM-01…05 and V4-EXM-11. Implements AC-008.
- **VER-009** — Review the documentation against the accepted DEL-01-02 artifact/interface row, SOW-125, the clarification and current Architecture §3. Check the optional-reuse rationale, receiving responsibilities and open choices; trace each excluded act to CLM-003 or CLM-004 and its actual owner. Confirm the receiving supplier boundary stays stock/directly hosted and no transcript copy or local settlement is given unsupported authority. Implements AC-009.
