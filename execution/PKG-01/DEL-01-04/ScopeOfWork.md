# Native requests, outcomes and attachments

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Provide the App's native approval/question cards and response interactions, turn/outcome and attachment presentation, and the receiving interface used by the workflow-making workspace. The responsible party is the **App native-interaction owner**. This SOFTWARE deliverable is the accepted UX_UI_SLICE in PKG-01, Native App and third-party harness integration. The contract defines future production and its evaluation; its existence does not establish implementation, passing product checks or lifecycle advancement.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — Native cards shall preserve the selected supplier's approval/question semantics and provide grant, deny, answer and explicit-decline interactions through CLM-003's execution interface. They shall expose received unsupported/unknown-request errors. Silence, elapsed time or closing the observer shall not be represented as approval or an answer. A translated Chirality event vocabulary shall not replace native requests. Where a request lists the answer forms it accepts, only those are offered. The explicit decline is the request's own negative form or, for a kind that has none, the native empty answer this slice's design names. No App rule declines a waiting request after any period, and a request the supplier resolves itself is shown as resolved by the supplier, never as an answer. The App shows how many requests wait for the person's answer, also when no conversation window is open. This slice composes the turns it sends, including the collaboration mode supplied by App `DEL-01-03` (sent explicitly after plan mode was used) and the run-start text and run-end line supplied by App `DEL-02-02`. Basis: SOW-005/SOW-014; N, R; CLM-002 and CLM-003.
- **REQ-002** — Turn and outcome presentation shall state only observed events from the execution interface. Unavailable outcomes remain unknown. Rendered state, provider state, request settlement, a reply-write attempt and received acknowledgment shall remain distinguishable; primary-turn completion shall not imply active descendants finished. Closing/reopening or reloading a view shall render the recovered actual state and outstanding requests supplied by the execution owner, without synthesizing completion or interruption. Interrupting a turn, ending a run and stopping the Codex process are shown as distinct, as App DEL-01-02 defines them; an interrupted turn is never shown as an ended run. The App offers Stop Codex and Restart Codex, each asking first when work is live. A new conversation shows that no model is selected until the person chooses one, from the selection state App DEL-01-05 reports; the person's last explicit choice for the project may be offered, and is never applied without the person's choice. A workflow run that cannot start for want of a model reads "run not started — no model selected"; an ordinary conversation reads "not started — no model selected". The Codex account App DEL-01-05 reports is the one used for the person's identity at act capture. A new conversation offers the roles App DEL-02-04 lists, with the registry's default preselected and clearable and no role allowed; the role is fixed for the conversation, and a different role opens a new conversation with a handoff summary the person can edit. An agent's proposal of the next workflow is offered for the person to confirm and starts nothing by itself. An agent's report that the workflow finished is offered as the person's "End run" and ends nothing by itself. Basis: SOW-129; R; CLM-003.
- **REQ-003** — Attachment/import and workflow-draft interactions shall preserve and expose the identity of the content actually supplied at the local interface. Content selected, submitted or referenced shall not be represented as different bytes or silently substituted by same-named material. A local selection or response attempt alone shall not claim provider adoption, review, registration or outcome. The receiving contract shall describe the identity carried and observation available without inventing supplier fields or choosing an unresolved storage implementation. Basis: D row DEL-01-04; SOW-129; R and W; CLM-004 and CLM-006.
- **REQ-004** — The native draft receiving interface shall carry source-qualified draft identity and explicit transitions supplied by the workflow workspace. It shall display a new or changed workflow as a draft until actual reviewed registration is received, preserve the reviewed/supplied content identity, and expose collision or refusal standing supplied by that workspace without silent overwrite or rebind. It shall identify the local contribution and the complete workflow-making integration in CLM-004. Basis: SOW-129; D and W.
- **REQ-005** — Request, outcome and draft UI shall keep execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance as distinct acts with their own actor, subject and evidence supplied under CLM-005. Success or evidence of one act shall imply none of the others, and no synthetic rule shall require acceptance before another independently evidenced act can be shown. A proposal decision shall say “accept”; native tool permission shall retain its native semantics without becoming engineering approval. An actually performed human act may be faithfully displayed or recorded through the designated recording interface with decision actor distinct from recorder; neither absent evidence nor agent output shall fabricate that act or professional certification. Where bound content changes, display the lapsed standing supplied by the evidence interface. The agent carrying out a workflow asks the person for a checkpoint's act; this slice offers the means to act as a standing facility and raises nothing because an arrival was recorded. An earlier act of the required kind on still-current content is shown as counting, with its time, and several acts may answer one arrival together. The person who acted is shown as the App observes them, marked identity not verified. Basis: H, D row DEL-04-03; SOW-129 as qualified by the assigned PKG-04 interface.
- **REQ-006** — This slice shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: supplier pin selection, generated-type production and protocol qualification belong to App `DEL-01-01` in CLM-002; durable session/request custody, settlement and execution recovery belong to App `DEL-01-02` in CLM-003; plan/tool/delegation-view production belongs to App `DEL-01-03` in CLM-003; the complete workflow-making workspace, the registration of a reviewed revision into the library and catalog selection belong to App `DEL-02-02` in CLM-004, while the person's registration act (A15) is captured by this slice's App act control; adopted-policy definition belongs to App `DEL-04-01` and durable act/run-record production belongs to App `DEL-04-03`, both in CLM-005. Unresolved policy decisions belong to the Owner with App/SWB contract owners, host act/receipt facilities to the host implementation owner, and actual human/professional acts to their person/accountable professional, as CLM-005 states. Native interaction, receiving and faithful presentation obligations remain here; this boundary does not prohibit recording an evidenced act through its owning interface. Basis: D; H; O.
- **REQ-007** — The receiving contract shall preserve required interactions independently of optional legacy UI reuse, record the relevant receiving obligations and candidate checks for any proposed reuse, and retain each applicable unresolved choice at its exact owner and point of need in TBD-001 through TBD-005. It shall not claim an adopted pin, fulfilled input, agreed common component or implemented capability solely from this definition. Basis: C; CLM-006; O.
- **REQ-008** — Provide the App act control: a dedicated control that only the person can operate — no agent tool, MCP operation, App rule, supplier request or interface script can operate it or produce its record — for one act kind at a time on App content. It covers App files and outputs, and A12 where an App control establishes the setting. It also covers A15 (register workflow revision), either on one reviewed workflow draft or on two or more library entries registered in place in one act. A15 is presented from one descriptor of the workflow workspace and bound to the exact reviewed content of each entry, so that a draft or entry changed after review needs a new review. Presenting or operating it answers no pending supplier request. It is used by DEL-02-02 (A15) and DEL-02-03 (App-side positive capture fixtures), each of which declares it upstream in its own register; it takes its act wording and kinds from DEL-04-01 and writes its records through the writer of DEL-04-03, as CLM-005 states. Basis: SOW-129 as qualified by the assigned PKG-04 interface; H; CLM-004, CLM-005; `APP-V4-DESIGN-PASS-2-20260930-DECISION-K1` K1-4; `APP-V4-DESIGN-PASS-3-20261001-DECISION-K3` K-8.
