# Native plans, tools and delegation views

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

DEL-01-03 supplies the App's native plan/revision, substantive tool-use and subagent-activity interaction so the person can direct work within and for professional applications. The responsible party is the **App native-interaction owner**. This UX_UI_SLICE contributes to OBJ-001, the complete standalone workflow-making loop with native tool use and recoverable interaction, and to OBJ-002 through the selected App architecture's native presentation and supplier-version visibility. It does not alone establish either objective's completion.

The production basis is the accepted Group3 decomposition, whose decision supersedes historical pending labels in its frozen tables. The source keys below identify the clauses used in this contract; current consolidated clauses are read with the accepted composite and its later clarification, not as retroactively human-reviewed bytes.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **CLM-001** — The accepted local contribution is native presentation and interaction in the chosen App stack, consuming stock pinned Codex items without a Chirality event translation vocabulary. Codex owns the agent engine and credentials; the App owns its receiving integration. Source: D, P V4-APP-01/04, A M-2 and §3.
- **CLM-002** — App `DEL-01-01`, the App supplier-integration owner, owns the stock child/protocol boundary, generated native types, selected supplier identification and qualification. App `DEL-01-02`, the App execution/recovery owner, owns durable execution, outstanding-request state, explicit stop, reconnect and relaunch. Their types and actual execution state are inputs here. Source: D named rows; A §3.
- **CLM-003** — App `DEL-01-04`, the App native-interaction owner, owns native request/response, turn/outcome, attachment and workflow-draft-transition interactions. App `DEL-02-02`, the App workflow-experience integration owner, owns the complete create/trial/review/register/reuse/refine journey, receiving this deliverable's plan/tool interaction. App PKG-06 owns file-based fleet coordination and consumes native delegation identities from this slice; it owns work-graph, return, waiting and decision views. This slice's plan-mode element, item anchors and delegation availability are received by App `DEL-01-04`, which composes turns and request cards from them; its delegation identities are received by App `DEL-06-01`; and its views and checks are received by `DEL-09-02` and `DEL-09-05` before their witnesses. Each declares it upstream in its own register. Source: D named deliverable/package rows. Local view delivery alone does not qualify that joined journey.
- **CLM-004** — The person directs work, sets the permitted autonomy and remains accountable. Execution, accepting a proposed edit, checking, approval and professional reliance are distinct acts with their own actor, subject and evidence. The host owns the acts it offers and records; it does not become the person performing them. Faithful recording of an actually performed human act is permitted. For this scope the owner decided them by `APP-V4-FIRST-INCREMENT-20260928-DECISION-1` D2 (reserved acts) and D3 (routine tool permission is the person's own Codex setting); any always-reserved class or classifier matter beyond that ruling remains a decision of the owner with affected App/host contract owners, not a decision made by this presentation slice. Source: P §1, V4-AUT-01…05 and V4-REC-03/05; H decision 03; I §1 and §5; O OI-001/002.
- **OUT-001** — CODE: native plan/revision and tool/delegation views, including selected-version visibility through the supplier input, truthful activity/outcome presentation and the receiving interactions in CLM-002/003. Covers SOW-001, SOW-003, SOW-004, SOW-006, SOW-014, SOW-128, SOW-129; supports OBJ-001 and OBJ-002. Source: D accepted anticipated artifacts and verification; P V4-APP-01/04; A §3; H clarification.
- **OUT-002** — TEST: pinned-native-item presentation fixtures and candidate-bound results for representative plans/revisions, tool outcomes, descendants and the truthful-actor distinctions exercised by these views. Covers SOW-001, SOW-003, SOW-004, SOW-006, SOW-014, SOW-128, SOW-129; supports OBJ-001 and OBJ-002. Source: D anticipated artifacts; E §1–3; A M-7. Covers SOW-003, SOW-004, SOW-006, SOW-014, SOW-128, SOW-129; supports OBJ-001 and OBJ-002. Source: D anticipated artifacts/interfaces; A §3; H clarification; O.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The App shall support native plan creation and revision interaction and keep the observed plan and its revisions visible in native form, preserving the required plan behavior while leaving registry storage and implementation open. Plan revisions Codex does not keep in its history are not copied; after a relaunch or a supplier restart they are shown as not recoverable, while plan items are recovered from Codex history. Sources: P V4-APP-01/04; D SOW-003/014/128/129; H clarification.
- **REQ-002** — The views shall expose substantive native tool activity and its observed outcomes using supplied native items. Unobserved outcomes shall remain unknown; a tool result shall not be translated into a separate Chirality event vocabulary or silently treated as proof of a different act. Sources: P V4-APP-01/04 and V4-EXE-03; A V4-ARC-05; CLM-001/004.
- **REQ-003** — Native subagent activity and descendant identities shall remain visible and usable by the fleet receiving interface. A completed primary turn shall not imply that active descendants completed, returned, were reviewed or were integrated. Delegation by a task agent is recorded and shown, labelled "stated, not enforced"; the App does not override the person's Codex configuration to prevent it. Sources: P V4-APP-01/04; D local interfaces; A §3; E V4-EXM-11/13; CLM-003.
- **REQ-004** — The native views and their fixture evidence shall identify the selected Codex version through the supplied supplier boundary and use its generated native types, with experimental supplements distinguished as supplied. They shall not claim verification against an unselected or historical version. This local obligation consumes the supplier's identity and qualification evidence in CLM-002; it does not select a pin or prescribe a checker. Experimental supplier surfaces the views use (at 0.158.0, plan mode) are labelled "experimental" where they appear; the App remains fully usable without them, the corresponding views being absent. Sources: D SOW-128 and local verification; A V4-ARC-05 and §3; O OI-012/DEP-005.
- **REQ-005** — Presentation shall preserve the separate actor, subject and evidence of execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance in CLM-004. Tool success, elapsed time or a native session entry alone shall establish none of the other acts. When an actual human act is supplied with its identified content, scope and purpose, the views may faithfully display it with the person as decision actor and the system/agent as recorder where applicable. No universal ordering requiring proposal acceptance before another independently evidenced act is imposed. An act the App captures names the person as observed, marked "identity not verified". An instruction to carry out a plan is ordinary conversation input, not a reserved act, unless a workflow checkpoint names an act. Sources: P V4-AUT-01…05 and V4-REC-03/05; H decision 03; I V4-HI-30…33.
- **REQ-006** — The native feature and optional-reuse map shall account for every required local plan/tool/delegation interaction, the workflow-making and fleet receiving contributions, supplier identity and relevant open choices. Existing plan-registry, version-check and UI code shall be assessed only as reuse candidates against the receiving contract and actual candidate checks; source similarity or a historical pass alone shall not qualify reuse. Sources: D, A §3, H clarification, E V4-EXM-03; CLM-002/003.
- **REQ-007** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable or package: stock supplier hosting/type generation/pin qualification belong to App `DEL-01-01` and durable execution/request recovery to App `DEL-01-02`, as stated in CLM-002; request/response, turn/outcome, attachment and draft-transition interaction belong to App `DEL-01-04`, complete workflow-making/reviewed registration integration to App `DEL-02-02`, and file-based fleet graph/return/waiting/decision views to App PKG-06, as stated in CLM-003. It shall not substitute a new agent engine for supplier-owned Codex in CLM-001, decide the unresolved reserved-act/classifier policy, or perform the person's professional-reliance act in CLM-004. These exclusions retain the named supplying/receiving seams and faithful-recording contribution here.
- **REQ-008** — Verification evidence shall bind the actual candidate, selected supplier/configuration and date, use recorded real exchanges for deterministic presentation checks where possible, and distinguish pass, fail, blocked, not run and inconclusive. Interface examination shall cover WebKit and Chromium and the relevant packaged App smoke witness; prior results and browser-only evidence do not replace the required native witness. Sources: E V4-EXM-01…05; O OI-015; D anticipated TEST artifact.

- **AC-001** — Representative native plans and their revisions remain visible and interactive under REQ-001, with the required behavior preserved irrespective of the assessed registry implementation. After a relaunch or a supplier restart, plan revisions Codex did not keep are shown as not recoverable and plan items are recovered from Codex history. Verify using VER-001.
- **AC-002** — Representative substantive tool activity and successful, failed or unavailable outcomes render from native items under REQ-002; missing observations remain unknown and no Chirality event translation supplies invented meaning. Verify using VER-002.
- **AC-003** — Representative delegated work retains native descendant identity/activity and the fleet receiving seam under REQ-003, including a completed primary turn with an active descendant. No unobserved return, review or integration is inferred. Verify using VER-003.
- **AC-004** — Presented selected-version identity and the native types/fixtures correspond to the supplied supplier basis under REQ-004, and a missing or mismatched basis cannot be reported as verified compatibility. Verify using VER-004.
- **AC-005** — Tool execution alone produces no invented human acceptance, checking, approval or professional-reliance status; a separately evidenced actual human act can be faithfully shown with its decision actor distinct from the recorder and its content/scope/purpose intact under REQ-005. Verify using VER-005.
- **AC-006** — The feature/reuse map covers all assigned scope and anticipated artifacts, preserves required behavior while recording conditional reuse, and resolves the enumerated exclusions to their named owners under REQ-006/007. Open means and policy stay assigned to their actual decision owners and points of need. Verify using VER-006.
- **AC-007** — The pinned-native-item fixture set and its reported results meet REQ-008 and account for the local plan/tool/delegation observations without claiming the full workflow-making, fleet or host journey passed. Verify using VER-007.

Completion evidence is evaluated against the identified implemented candidate. This INIT contract defines that work; it supplies no product code, executed product tests, qualified supplier input, accepted dependency graph, lifecycle promotion or professional reliance. A defined upstream contract is not evidence that its required input exists. ## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The App native-interaction owner first resolves the native type, supplier-identity and execution-state inputs with their owners, then defines the presentation and receiving seams against those inputs. It develops the required local interactions, assesses historical reuse against them, and records only candidate-bound observations. The App workflow-experience integration owner integrates the joined workflow-making journey; fleet coordination consumes native delegation identities. These are responsibility/input relationships, not an accepted execution schedule.

- **VER-001** — Replay selected-version native plan items and revisions through the views and inspect the person's planning/revision interaction. Compare the displayed plan and revision sequence with the source items, including updated content. Retain candidate-bound fixture/input identities and observations for AC-001. Sources: D verification; P V4-APP-01/04; E local V4-EXM-10 observations.
- **VER-002** — Replay recorded substantive native tool activity and observed success/failure/unavailable-result cases. Compare displayed activity/outcomes with the native source, inspect the presentation path for event renaming/semantic translation, and confirm unavailable outcomes are still unknown. Record evidence for AC-002. Sources: D; A V4-ARC-05; E V4-EXM-02/11.
- **VER-003** — Exercise a native delegated-work fixture and its receiving identity linkage, including a primary-turn completion while a descendant remains active. Compare source identities/activity to both local display and the defined fleet seam; inspect for invented return/review/integration completion. Record evidence for AC-003. Sources: D; A §3; E V4-EXM-11/13 local observations.
- **VER-004** — Compare visible supplier version and fixture/type identities with the actual supplier input from CLM-002. Exercise missing/mismatched identity evidence and verify it is not reported as confirmed compatibility. Record the supplier qualification reference and this slice's observation separately for AC-004. Sources: D SOW-128/local verification; A §3; O OI-012/DEP-005.
- **VER-005** — Inspect presentation fixtures for (a) execution success without any human-act evidence, (b) an attempted inference of approval/reliance from a session entry and (c) a separately evidenced human act with identified actor, content, scope and purpose recorded by the system/agent. Verify negatives do not fabricate an act and the positive faithfully renders the actual actor/recorder distinction. Use synthetic material for fixtures; do not report synthetic acts as real owner decisions. Confirm no invented acceptance-first ordering for independently evidenced acts. Record evidence for AC-005. Sources: P V4-AUT-03/05 and V4-REC-03/05; H decision 03; I §5.
- **VER-006** — Review the native feature/reuse map against D's assigned scope, artifacts and interfaces, H's conditional-reuse clarification, CLM-001…004 and the O issue rows. Trace each excluded act in REQ-007 to its cited actual owner; check local versus joint contributions, required behavior versus reuse choices, and each retained choice's point of need. Record evidence for AC-006.
- **VER-007** — Inspect fixture provenance and the candidate/configuration/date/outcome record, confirm coverage of AC-001…005 and cross-engine/native evidence under REQ-008, and mark unavailable witnesses with their actual status. Confirm local fixture results are not advertised as full workflow-making/fleet/host qualification. Record evidence for AC-007. Sources: D; E §1–3; O OI-015.
