# Operation-policy and human-act distinctions

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable gives every part of the product one meaning for two things: what an agent may do directly, what it must propose and what only the person may do; and which act happened, who performed it, what it concerned and what evidence supports it. It contributes that meaning as a contract, the policy-class records that carry each adopted value to the catalogs and controls that apply it, and the conformance cases that show an agent can neither perform nor be credited with an act reserved to the person. It performs no human act and builds no host facility.

It covers SOW-074, SOW-079, SOW-082, SOW-179, SOW-180, SOW-181, SOW-182 and SOW-235, for OBJ-004 (host contracts that preserve operation meaning and host authority) and OBJ-005 (useful autonomy with attributable human acts). Its sources are PRD V4-AUT-01, V4-AUT-03, V4-AUT-04 and V4-AUT-05, and HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-22, V4-HI-25, V4-HI-30, V4-HI-31, V4-HI-33, V4-HI-40 to V4-HI-42, V4-HI-51 and V4-HI-52.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The operation-policy and human-act contract: the act kinds, operation classes, grants, treatments and resolution rule defined below, as the one meaning that the catalog, checkpoint, proposal, autonomy-display, record and host-receiving deliverables implement. Covers SOW-074, SOW-079, SOW-082, SOW-179, SOW-180, SOW-181, SOW-182 and SOW-235; supports OBJ-004 and OBJ-005.
- **OUT-002** — The policy-class records: one versioned data document holding a record for each operation class or act class, each carrying its adopted value with the decision that fixed it and the consumers that apply it, and naming each value still undecided with its owner. A consumer cites a record by policy revision and record identity. Covers SOW-074, SOW-179, SOW-181 and SOW-182; supports OBJ-004 and OBJ-005.
- **OUT-003** — The reserved-act and label conformance cases, with their results on an identified candidate: false attribution, an agent's attempt at a reserved act, professional standing claimed for agent output, default and widened autonomy, and the separation of independently evidenced acts. Covers SOW-079, SOW-082, SOW-179, SOW-180, SOW-182 and SOW-235; supports OBJ-004 and OBJ-005.

Terms:

- **Act kinds.** Every consumer uses these names. Evidence of one kind never establishes another, and the absence of one never invalidates an independently evidenced act of another.

  | Kind | Name | Performed by | Does not establish |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | A1 | propose | an agent, or the person | acceptance, application, checking, approval or reliance |
  | A2 | apply | the person; an agent under an effective direct treatment; the host's route after acceptance | that anyone accepted, checked, approved or relied |
  | A3 | examine | an agent | any act of the person |
  | A4 | mark checked | the person | acceptance, approval or reliance |
  | A5 | accept (identified change items of a proposal) | the person | checking, approval or application |
  | A6 | approve (engineering approval) | the accountable person | reliance or any certification |
  | A7 | rely (professional reliance) | the accountable professional only | anything about agent output |
  | A8 | request (prepare or ask for a person's act) | an agent | anything; a request is never the act |
  | A9 | record (direct capture, or faithful recording of a performed act) | the capturing surface, the App, a host facility or an agent, as recorder | the act it records |
  | A10 | reject | the person | more than non-acceptance of those items |
  | A11 | withdraw | the proposer only | a decision on the proposal's merit |
  | A12 | set grant, including allowing a host's agent a network destination | the person | that an agent's request established anything |
  | A13 | enable external access, or disable it | the person | a grant for any operation class |
  | A14 | answer tool permission | the person, or the person's own Codex permission mode | any other act, or a grant for a host operation |
  | A15 | register workflow revision | the person | that the revision was examined, checked or approved |
  | A16 | decide (choose one alternative of a decision package) | the person | that the alternative was carried out, or any other act |

- **Person's acts.** A4 to A7, A10, A12, A13, A15 and A16. No agent is ever their decision actor and no grant covers them. The *decision actor* is the person who performed the act; the *recorder* is whoever wrote the record; a record that names its recorder as the decision actor is not conformant.
- **Reserved acts.** For the first increment's App and shared contracts these acts are reserved to the person: marking work checked; accepting a proposal wherever the active autonomy requires a proposal; engineering approval; relying on a result for a professional purpose; and changing the autonomy grant or enabling external-agent access. Rejecting is reserved wherever accepting is. An operation whose effect is to perform one of these acts is itself reserved. No grant widens past a reserved act or a declared checkpoint. A host names and enforces its own list.
- **Routine tool permission.** In the App, tool-permission and sandbox modes, classifier-based modes included, are the person's own Codex setting. A host has no classifier permission mode. A tool-permission answer governs tool execution only and never stands in for a reserved or professional act.
- **Operation class.** Each catalog operation has one class value: *none*; *may apply within granted autonomy*; *proposal only*; *reserved to the person*; or *no policy basis*, with its reason (omitted, unassigned, or awaiting a named decision). An operation with an effect is not classed *none* without its own decision basis.
- **Grant.** For a class, a value of *direct* or *propose* with a scope (workspace or model, object set, run, period, consequence). A grant is effective when the person set it and the control confirmed it, or when an adopted policy default applies. A requested, unconfirmed or refused setting is not effective. The value *propose* is the person's choice and can be widened; the class *proposal only* cannot.
- **Treatment.** What the host's route resolves for one operation: *execute*; *apply directly*; *propose*; *request the person's act*; or a stated refusal (*channel not enabled*, *not permitted*, *no policy basis, held*).
- **Resolution rule.** Applied at validation and again at application; the first matching line governs. (1) An external agent while external access is not enabled: channel not enabled. (2) An operation that performs a person's act, or whose class is reserved: request the person's act. (3) No policy basis: proposing stays available and confers no permission; direct application is not permitted; a grant that would widen the class is refused. (4) Proposal only: propose. (5) May apply within granted autonomy: apply directly only under an effective grant of value direct whose scope covers the whole operation; otherwise propose, and a direct request without such a grant is not permitted and is never converted into a proposal. (6) None: execute. The person's own operations are not gated by an agent's grant.
- **Declared checkpoint.** A workflow's declared pause for a named act of the person. Whatever the grant, the act is requested and is recorded as done only when the person performs it. In the current phase a checkpoint is plan guidance and nothing holds the run; holding the run until the act is a later governance layer, which the owner takes up for a workflow that needs enforced checkpoints.
- **Policy-class record.** Record identity; class identity (host-named, or defined by an act); the operations or acts it covers; class value and, for no policy basis, its reason; default grant value; whether the person may widen it; acceptance granularity; actors covered; consequence; decision basis; and whether the host's adoption is evidenced. The adopted records are: the reserved acts (not widenable); operations that perform a reserved act (reserved, not widenable); SWBPIPE model changes (may apply within granted autonomy, default propose, accepted row by row, by several rows or as a whole batch, widenable by the person); routine tool permission (a setting, never a class value); and the no-policy-basis treatment.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — This deliverable defines meaning and carries adopted values. The owner, with the host policy owner, decides any class or reserved act not yet ruled; this deliverable decides none.
- **CLM-002** — `DEL-02-01` declares checkpoints and `DEL-02-03` records arrivals and acts at them. `DEL-03-01` assigns a class to each catalog operation. `DEL-03-02` owns the proposal lifecycle and outcomes. `DEL-04-02` owns the display of grants and standing. `DEL-04-03` owns the format, writer and reader of act and run records. `DEL-01-04` owns the App act control that captures a person's act on App content, and `DEL-02-02` owns workflow registration. `DEL-05-01` and `DEL-05-02` own the host loop and panel receiving contracts.
- **CLM-003** — SWBPIPE's own project owns the host: its classes, validation and application, receipts, origin marks, undo, the facilities that offer, record and present human acts, and the enforcement of its reserved list.
- **CLM-004** — The person performs each act attributed to that person. An agent may prepare or request one and never performs one.
- **CLM-005** — The accountable professional owns every statement of certification, sealing, professional approval or code compliance.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — An agent applies a change directly only within a scope the person set for that operation class; otherwise the change is a proposal. A directly applied change carries its origin, a route to undo it and a route to check it later. A declared checkpoint's act is requested and recorded as done only when the person performs it, whatever the grant. [SOW-074; PRD V4-AUT-01; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-22, V4-HI-40, V4-HI-42]
- **REQ-002** — Proposing, applying, agent examination, marking checked, accepting an edit, engineering approval, professional reliance and registering a workflow revision stay distinct, each with its own actor, subject and evidence. Operation success or a receipt establishes only that the operation ran. Evidence of one act establishes no other, and acceptance is never made a prerequisite of another independently evidenced act. An agent may prepare or request a person's decision; it never represents or records an act as performed by the person when it was not. A faithful record of a performed act keeps the decision actor apart from the recorder and grants the recorder nothing. Each act kind A1 to A16 has one name and a stated performer, and the resolver, the policy-class records and the conformance cases use only those names. [SOW-079; SOW-235; PRD V4-AUT-03; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-25, V4-HI-31]
- **REQ-003** — No label, record or case outcome presents agent production as certified, sealed, approved or code-compliant. A separately evidenced professional statement stays attributed to its accountable professional and its actual subject, and is never inferred from execution, checking or edit acceptance. [SOW-082; PRD V4-AUT-05]
- **REQ-004** — The contract requires a host to name and enforce the acts its adopted policy reserves, against embedded and external agents alike. The policy-class records carry each adopted value with its decision basis and mark each undecided value. An operation with no ruled class has no policy basis: omission or an undecided value is never permission, and nothing that depends on the value proceeds before its decision. This deliverable supplies no reserved list or classifier treatment beyond the adopted rulings. [SOW-179; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-30, V4-HI-51]
- **REQ-005** — A decision on a proposal is labelled "accept", never "approve", and accepting a proposed edit claims no engineering approval. [SOW-180; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-33]
- **REQ-006** — Consequential host operations default conservatively. SWBPIPE model changes default to proposals accepted row by row, by several rows or as a whole batch, and the person may widen that grant. Widening never fabricates a human act, passes a declared checkpoint or authorises a reserved act. [SOW-181; SOW-182; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-41, V4-HI-42, V4-HI-51]
- **REQ-007** — This deliverable performs no act owned by another. Deciding an unruled class or reserved act belongs to the owner with the host policy owner (CLM-001). Declaring checkpoints belongs to `DEL-02-01`, recording arrivals and acts at them to `DEL-02-03`, assigning classes to catalog operations to `DEL-03-01`, the proposal lifecycle to `DEL-03-02`, displaying grants and standing to `DEL-04-02`, the record format, writer and reader to `DEL-04-03`, capturing a person's act on App content to `DEL-01-04`, registration to `DEL-02-02`, and the host loop and panel receiving contracts to `DEL-05-01` and `DEL-05-02` (CLM-002). Host validation and application, receipts, origin marks, undo, the facilities for human acts and enforcement belong to SWBPIPE's own project (CLM-003). Performing a human act belongs to the person (CLM-004). Professional statements belong to the accountable professional (CLM-005).

- **AC-001** — For every actor, class, grant state, scope and intent, the resolved treatment is *apply directly* only for a class that may apply within granted autonomy under an effective grant of value direct covering the whole operation; an agent's request, an unconfirmed or refused setting, an operation outside the grant's scope and an operation result each yield no direct treatment; no grant records a checkpoint's act as done; and every *apply directly* treatment requires the change to carry its origin, a route to undo it and a route to check it later. Verify REQ-001 by VER-001.
- **AC-002** — In every case each act keeps its own actor, subject and evidence: operation success and evidence of one act establish no other; an independently evidenced act is not refused for want of an acceptance; a decision an agent prepared is not recorded as the person's; and a performed act recorded faithfully shows the person as decision actor and the recorder apart, with no permission or authority gained by the recorder. Verify REQ-002 by VER-002.
- **AC-003** — No agent-output case carries certification, sealing, professional approval or code compliance, and a separately evidenced professional statement is attributed only to its actual actor and subject. Verify REQ-003 by VER-003.
- **AC-004** — An embedded or external agent's attempt at a reserved act resolves to a request for the person's act and leaves no record of the act as performed or as the person's, and the host refuses the attempt on its own route. An operation with no ruled class is shown and recorded as held, with its reason and the decision it awaits, and no value is guessed for it. Verify REQ-004 by VER-004.
- **AC-005** — Every proposal decision in the contract, the policy-class records, the cases and the interface labels says "accept" for edit acceptance, and none presents it as engineering approval. Verify REQ-005 by VER-005.
- **AC-006** — With no grant set, an SWBPIPE model change resolves to a proposal that can be accepted row by row, by several rows or as a whole batch; after the person widens the grant the same change applies directly within the widened scope only; a widened grant still leaves reserved acts and checkpoint acts with the person; and a consequential host operation with no adopted default is never applied directly without the person's grant: it resolves to a proposal, or is held with no policy basis. Verify REQ-006 by VER-006.
- **AC-007** — Each value in the policy-class records has its decision basis and its consumers; each undecided value is marked with its owner and the decision it awaits; a record with no decision basis, a reserved record marked widenable, a class value on the tool-permission setting and a repeated record identity are each refused; and the records hold no reserved act or classifier treatment beyond the reserved acts and routine tool permission as PRD V4-AUT-03 and V4-AUT-04 state them and the reservations this scope states as following from them. Verify REQ-001 and REQ-004 by VER-007.
- **AC-008** — No resolved treatment, policy-class record or conformance case performs or records an act that REQ-007 assigns elsewhere; each record's host-adoption element reads evidenced only where the host's evidence is cited; and no case records a person's act it holds no evidence of. Verify REQ-007 by VER-008.
- **AC-009** — The conformance cases cover REQ-002 to REQ-006, each with an identified case and its result on an identified candidate; a case that depends on an undecided value or on host evidence not received is reported as held, never as passing. Verify by VER-009.
- **AC-010** — Each act kind A1 to A16 appears once, with one name and a stated performer; no two kinds share a name; and every act that the resolver, a policy-class record or a conformance case names is one of those kinds under that name. Verify REQ-002 by VER-010.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Every name in the contract is a name of meaning; field names, transport, storage and the placement of shared components are chosen by the deliverables that implement it. The meaning is fixed before any operation-specific value is selected, and a value enters the policy-class records only with the decision that fixed it. Treatment is resolved on the host's route, at validation and again at application, so that a grant changed in between governs the application; the loop and the adapter relay intent and decide nothing. The policy-class records are data, checked by a schema and by the rules of AC-007, and the same records feed the conformance cases. A case's expected result is written from the PRD and HOST_INTEGRATION clauses before the product is run.

- **VER-001** — Executable check (observes the treatment resolved for each combination of actor, class, grant state, scope and intent, at validation and again at application, and the origin, undo route and later-check route an *apply directly* treatment requires): compare each with the treatment that PRD V4-AUT-01 and HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-22, V4-HI-40, V4-HI-42 and V4-HI-52 require as this scope's resolution rule states them, including an operation outside the grant's scope, a requested, an unconfirmed and a refused setting, and a checkpoint reached under a direct grant.
- **VER-002** — Executable check (observes the actor, recorder, subject and evidence of each act recorded or shown): run cases of a proposal alone, application success alone, agent examination, marking checked, accepting, approval, reliance and registration; a performed act recorded faithfully by a separate recorder; and fabricated attribution; and confirm no case creates an act it holds no evidence of or refuses an evidenced act for want of another.
- **VER-003** — Comparison with a named reference (PRD V4-AUT-05): inspect every label, record and outcome produced for agent output and for a separately evidenced professional statement, and confirm success, examination, checking and acceptance confer no professional standing.
- **VER-004** — Executable check (observes the treatment and the record left by an embedded and an external agent's attempt at each reserved act, and the standing shown for an operation with no ruled class), combined with comparison with a named reference (the host's own evidence of refusing the same attempts): where the host supplies none, the host half is reported as not established.
- **VER-005** — Executable check (observes the wording of every proposal decision in the contract, the policy-class records, the cases and the interface labels): find each occurrence and confirm "accept" is used and "approve" is not.
- **VER-006** — Executable check (observes the treatment of an SWBPIPE model change with no grant, with the default, and after the person widens the grant, of a reserved act and a checkpoint act under the widened grant, and of a consequential host operation that has no adopted default and no grant from the person), combined with comparison with a named reference (the host's own behaviour for the same changes, where the host supplies it).
- **VER-007** — Comparison with a named reference (the reserved acts and routine tool permission as PRD V4-AUT-03 and V4-AUT-04 state them, HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-41 for the SWBPIPE default, and the reservations the Ontology states as following from them): trace each recorded value to its decision and its consumers, and run the refusals of AC-007 on records built to break each rule.
- **VER-008** — Executable check (observes each resolved treatment, each policy-class record's host-adoption element with the evidence it cites, and the acts each conformance case records): confirm that none performs or records an act REQ-007 assigns elsewhere, that host adoption reads evidenced only where the host's evidence is cited, and that no case records a person's act without evidence of it. Combined with judgment by a named role (a reviewer who did not write the contract): compare the allocation in REQ-007, act by act, with the decomposition's deliverable rows and its external dependency on SWBPIPE.
- **VER-009** — Executable check (observes which requirement each case exercises and each case's result on the identified candidate): confirm REQ-002 to REQ-006 are each covered and that every case depending on an undecided value or on absent host evidence is reported as held.
- **VER-010** — Executable check (observes the act kinds the resolver defines and every act named in the policy-class records and the conformance cases): compare them with the act-kind table of this scope, and report a kind that is missing, repeated, unnamed or without a performer, and any act named outside the table.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — A value is carried only with the decision that fixed it; an earlier draft, a fixture's expectation or a pending recommendation fixes nothing, and what is undecided stays visible as undecided. Better means fewer operations resolving to no policy basis because more classes carry an adopted value; never by carrying a value the owner or the host policy owner has not decided.
- **AX-002** — Useful autonomy does not supply authority. Acceptance, checking, approval and reliance each need their own attributable evidence, and no act is promoted into another. Better means the person is asked for fewer acts that a grant they set already covers; never by letting any agent action, tool permission or operation result stand as a person's act or a professional statement.
- **AX-003** — The host keeps its authority. The conservative defaults are commitments at the shared interface, not a transfer of SWBPIPE's implementation. Better means the host's own evidence of enforcement covers more of the reserved acts; never by the App stating enforcement, adoption or a default the host has not shown.
- **TBD-001** — Operation-specific additions to the reserved acts, and the class of the first connected operation, are undecided (the owner, through the SWBPIPE session, with the App owner). Until decided, such an operation has no policy basis.
- **TBD-005** — No consequence vocabulary for operation classes is adopted (the owner with the host policy owner). Until one is, no policy-class record assigns a consequence and a grant's consequence dimension matches nothing.
