# Content-bound decisions and compact run records

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable keeps what the person decided and what each run did recoverable from files alone, without the conversation that produced them. Each recorded human act names who performed it, who recorded it, and the exact content, scope and purpose it concerned, and is shown as lapsed when that content changes. Each run leaves a compact record that links the host's own evidence instead of copying it. The App keeps these records in its own store, outside the working tree an agent edits, and does not rely on version control to manage them. It contributes the record format, the App's writer and reader with the store they keep, the handling of lapse, the cases that examine them, and the shared meaning a host's own run recording uses.

It covers SOW-092, SOW-093, SOW-094, SOW-095, SOW-096, SOW-143 and SOW-186, for OBJ-004 (a shared record contract that preserves host authority) and OBJ-005 (attributable acts, standing and reconstructable provenance). Its sources are PRD V4-REC-01 to V4-REC-06, V4-AUT-03, V4-HOST-02 and V4-PM-06, and HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-25, V4-HI-30 to V4-HI-33, V4-HI-70 and V4-HI-71.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The versioned human-act and run-record format, with the rules of record authority: record identity, the binding of an act to content, scope and purpose, and the run inventory with its references. Supports SOW-092, SOW-093, SOW-094, SOW-095, SOW-143 and SOW-186; OBJ-004 and OBJ-005.
- **OUT-002** — The App's record writer and reader, the records store they keep, and the visible handling of lapse when bound content changes. Supports SOW-092, SOW-093, SOW-094, SOW-095, SOW-096 and SOW-186; OBJ-005.
- **OUT-003** — The identity, reference and lapse cases, with their results on an identified candidate, including a performed act recorded faithfully and the refusal of a fabricated one. Supports SOW-092, SOW-093, SOW-094, SOW-095, SOW-096 and SOW-186; OBJ-005.
- **OUT-004** — The shared record meaning for consumers and hosts: what each consumer reads from a record, and what a host's own recording of a host-agent run carries, with its receipts by reference. Supports SOW-092, SOW-093, SOW-094, SOW-095, SOW-143 and SOW-186; OBJ-004 and OBJ-005.

Terms:

- **Record authority.** The authority that a person performed an act on given content for a given purpose is an act record, with the evidence it references, in the App's records store or, for a host's act, in the host's own store. A harness session, a transcript, agent memory, a derived view, a search index, a tool-permission settlement or an answer to a supplier's question may locate evidence and creates no act.
- **Run record.** That a workflow run occurred, with: run identity and who started it; the workflow's source-qualified identity and revision; the guidance supplied (role guidance and the run's text, each by reference with the state of its supply check); the conversation by reference; the model used and its observed destination for each turn, the requested and the reported effective one kept apart; the autonomy settings versions; each requested operation and its observed outcome; checkpoint arrivals, requests and dispositions; references to human acts; agent examination findings; tool-permission settlements; evidence limits; and, for a host's agent, each network destination contacted with the grant or list entry that allowed it, each destination grant, request, decline and refusal.
- **Human-act record.** One act of one kind: mark checked, accept, approve, rely, reject, set grant, enable external access, register workflow revision, decide, or a withdrawal by the person as proposer. It carries the decision actor; the recorder and whether the record is a direct capture or a faithful recording; the bound subject with its content identity and the method that produced it; scope; purpose; at least one reference to capture evidence; what the evidence does not show; and its relations (run, arrival answered, request answered; for a grant, the control's effect; for a registration, the reviewed draft and the prior revision; for a decision, the decision package it decides and the alternative chosen, which must be one the package names). A person's own proposals and applications are operations in the run record, not human-act records. A tool-permission answer is never a human-act record.
- **Other entries.** An *act request* (an agent asked the person for a named act; never the act); an *act-declined event* (the person decided not to perform a required act; never the act and never a run end); an *act-lapsed event*; a *run-ended event* (the person or the run's owner ended the run, or an observer reports its end; an interrupt, a quit or a lost observation is not one). A run that has ended is never resumed; an act captured afterwards is marked as after run end and changes nothing in that run.
- **Decision actor as observed.** For an act captured in the App the actor is recorded from what the App can observe (the name the person set in the App, the operating-system account, and the Codex account when Codex reports one, by its reported email or as an account with no email reported; the plan type is not recorded) and is marked *identity not verified*.
- **Lapse.** The current content identity of exactly the bound subject and scope is compared with the bound one. The states are: *not lapsed*; *lapsed*; *lapsed (subject absent)*; *partially lapsed* (evaluated element by element); *matches again after observed lapse*; *unknown (incomparable)* when the two identities were not produced by the same method; *unknown (unavailable)*; and *not yet evaluated*, which is never shown as not lapsed. Where a host supplies only one identity for a whole model, every act bound through it lapses on any change to the model. Lapse never edits or deletes the act record. A grant setting and an enablement are not lapse-evaluated: a later one that the control establishes supersedes the earlier. A registered revision is not lapse-evaluated: a changed definition is a new revision that needs its own registration.
- **Correction.** A written entry is never edited. A correction is a new entry naming the one it corrects, and both stay readable.
- **Read state.** How a reader finds an entry: written; corrected; partial (a torn line, kept and not read); read limited (a newer minor version: known elements read, the rest shown as present and unread); refused (an unknown format or major version); nonconformant (it breaks the schema or a reader rule, and is never used for a disposition or a count).
- **Records store.** One store for each installation, in the App's own data folder or a folder the person chose, with one subtree for each project keyed by a stable project identity. It holds run logs, act logs, capture evidence, workflow selection and run-text records, connector route accounts, the agents' read log, role-supply observations and the App's own coordination observations. Records the App alone writes join the store; working files that the person and agents edit with ordinary tools stay in the project.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — Workflow definitions are ordinary files in the user's project, workspace or workflow library. The person's decisions, accepted records and run records are ordinary append-only files in the App's records store, linked to their project. The one exception is a workflow library's registration acts: their append-only log and linked capture evidence stay with the library and travel with it, and a project's run cites the library evidence that applies; an in-project library's evidence therefore sits inside the working tree an agent can edit. The exception changes location and carriage only. A host keeps its domain truth in its own store, and a host-agent run's record stays with the host project.
- **CLM-002** — A run record links the host's receipts, hashes and origin marks, which are the evidence of what changed, and copies none of them. It holds a reference, the identity claimed, the method that produced it and whether the reference resolved.
- **CLM-003** — The decision actor and the recorder are different roles. A faithful record of a performed act is permitted; false attribution is not. Execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance are separate subjects, and an operation's success is not a human act.
- **CLM-004** — This deliverable owns the format, the App's writer and reader, the records store, lapse handling and its cases. It receives: act kinds and class values from `DEL-04-01`; settings versions from `DEL-04-02`; subject content identities and their method from `DEL-03-01`; operation outcomes, change-item content identities and receipt references from `DEL-03-02`; the bodies of checkpoint entries (arrival, act request, act counted, lapse, run end) and compatibility reports from `DEL-02-03`; the workflow identity and the checkpoint dispositions from `DEL-02-01`; the run's text with its supply check, the selection record and the registration relations from `DEL-02-02`; role-supply records from `DEL-02-04`; external dispatch entries from `DEL-03-03`; observed supplier facts (supplied guidance, model and destination, tool-permission settlements) from `DEL-01-01`; custody facts about unanswered requests and lost acknowledgements from `DEL-01-02`; capture evidence and the person's observed identity from `DEL-01-04`; and a host agent's network-destination events (destination contacted, destination grant, destination declined) from `DEL-05-01`. `DEL-06-01` owns the fleet record and `DEL-07-02` the connector route accounts that the store holds for them. `DEL-09-11` owns the later reconstruction witness, made by a separate reader.
- **CLM-005** — SWBPIPE's own project owns the host's receipts, domain store, domain changes and its recording of host-agent runs, and offers, records and presents host human acts. The person performs each act. The accountable professional owns professional reliance and certification.
- **CLM-006** — A recorded act binds to identified content, scope and purpose, and a change to that content causes visible lapse. A reader keeps the scope of the acts actually recorded and what remains unknown; a resolved source or a successful transport never fills an outcome the host did not report.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

A record is an append-only log of entries, one log for each run and writer, and an entry is written whole or not at all. The format is versioned: a reader limits what it reads of a newer minor version and refuses an unknown one, and infers nothing from what it cannot read. Host evidence is held by reference with the method that produced each identity, and two identities are compared only when the same method produced both; the App never computes a replacement identity for host content. An act is written only from capture evidence that the person performed it. The store is plain files so that it can be backed up, moved and checked with ordinary tools, and every derived structure can be thrown away and rebuilt. Names in the format's meaning are names of meaning; serialization is chosen in the implementation, and a host's own persistence follows its owner.

- **VER-001** — Executable check (observes where each kind of record is written and what a written run record contains): write and read a run and its acts, read a selection and a run record made under each approval policy and one older record that carries neither, compare the locations with REQ-001 and the run's contents with the inventory HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-70 lists, resolve each receipt, hash and origin reference to its supplied source, and confirm that a session statement or a derived view label yields no act.
- **VER-002** — Executable check (observes the actor, recorder, subject, evidence and standing recorded for each case): run a performed act recorded by a separate recorder, a proposal alone, an operation success alone, a permission grant alone, and an independently evidenced act with no other act, and compare each record with what the case supplied.
- **VER-003** — Executable check (observes the lapse state shown for an act against the content identities the case supplies): bind an act, leave the content unchanged, change it, remove the subject, return it to its first content, and supply an identity of another method and none at all.
- **VER-004** — Executable check (observes the standing recorded and read for a missing receipt, an unobserved host outcome and a transport or source resolution with no host report).
- **VER-005** — Executable check (observes which acceptance criterion each case exercises and its result on the identified candidate), kept apart from the witness of `DEL-09-11`.
- **VER-006** — Judgment by a named role (a reviewer who did not write the shared meaning): trace each consumer of REQ-005 and each exclusion of REQ-006 to the decomposition's deliverable rows and its external dependency on SWBPIPE, and report any host contribution or adoption stated without the host's evidence.
- **VER-007** — Executable check (observes the file system after recording under the default placement and a chosen one, and the App's response to a refused placement, an unavailable store, and a missing, changed, duplicated and moved project link).
- **VER-008** — Executable check (observes the reader's report after a written line is changed, removed or reordered and after a unit is left partly present, and the index's answers before and after it is deleted and rebuilt), combined with comparison with a named reference (the SHA-256 of each preceding line, recomputed by a verifier that imports no product code).
- **VER-009** — Executable check (observes the App's response to a disposal attempted on an open project, without a verified export and under a legal hold, and the administrative history that remains after a permitted disposal).
- **VER-010** — Comparison with a named reference (BagIt, RFC 8493, and a verifier that uses no product code): verify an exported package and an altered copy outside the App, compare payload bytes with the store, and import into an empty store, a store holding the same records and a store of another installation.
- **VER-011** — Executable check (observes what the records capability returns to an agent for the current project and for another project, whether any call can write, the read log's entry for each call, and what the App's guidance to agents says of the capability and of a direct file read).
- **VER-012** — Executable check (observes the read state the reader returns for each entry supplied to it, and the log's bytes before and after a correction): supply an entry of a known version, of a newer minor version, of an unknown major version and of an unknown format, a torn line and a nonconformant entry; write a correction; and compare each read state with the one the case states.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-002** — Faithful recording, never fabrication. Acts and actors stay separate, and graduated autonomy brings neither automatic trust nor a chain of approvals. Better means a larger share of the acts a person actually performed are found in the records with their capture evidence resolving; never by writing an act from anything other than evidence that the person performed it.
- **AX-003** — Evidence is proportionate to the reader who must reconstruct the work. Links and content identities serve attribution and lapse; copies of host receipts or of private transcripts do not. Better means a reader needs fewer sources outside the run record to say what happened; never by copying host truth into the record or by letting an index, a view or a session stand in for a record.
- **TBD-001** — Operation-specific additions to the reserved acts are undecided (the owner, through the SWBPIPE session, with the App owner). An act through an operation with no ruled class is recorded with the class "no policy basis" and its reason.
- **TBD-002** — Where a host keeps its agent's run records, and whether any reader or writer is built as one shared with a host, are undecided (the shared contract owner with the SWBPIPE implementation owner; the App and shared contract owners). The App's own writer and reader are local to the App.
- **TBD-003** — Which key signs log checkpoints and export manifests is undecided (the App implementation owner, with the signing of the App). Until a key is chosen the chain shows an edit only where the links after it were not recomputed, and a package is unsigned.
