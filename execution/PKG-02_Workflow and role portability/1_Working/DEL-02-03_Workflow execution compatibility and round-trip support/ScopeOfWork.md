# Workflow execution compatibility and round-trip support

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This BACKEND_FEATURE_SLICE makes selected workflow requirements actionable in the current host, holds declared human checkpoints, and supports App/host workflow transfer and refinement. The responsible party is the **App/shared workflow-execution owner; external host owner supplies host execution**. This contract defines the local contribution; it does not establish implemented behavior or a joined host witness.

The accepted Group3 snapshot named in frontmatter supplies the following row references: **G3** means its `canonical/` directory; **G3 decision** means its `DECISION.md`. **Current seeds** means `projects/chirality-app-v4/docs/`; **Composite** means `projects/chirality-app-v4/execution/_Coordination/Acceptances/APP-V4-BASIS-20260926/`. Source citations below identify files, clauses or rows within those exact roots. Original seed clauses are read with Composite `ACCEPTANCE.md`, `OWNER_DIRECTIONS.md` J–O and `DECISION_BRIEF.html` decisions 02, 03 and 05; current consolidated meanings qualify historical defaults.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — Check the selected workflow's declared required tools against the current host's capability information, and tell the person which requirements are missing or unsupported. Expose the affected workflow/host basis; a portable file or registration alone is insufficient evidence of compatible execution. Sources: SOW-051; PRD.md V4-WF-04; EXAMINATION.md V4-EXM-14; CLM-001/CLM-002.
- **REQ-002** — At a declared checkpoint, request the required human act and hold the run at that checkpoint until the person performs that act, even where the applicable operation autonomy otherwise allows direct application. Interrupted or replayed history must preserve the checkpoint's identity and actual disposition. Sources: SOW-052/SOW-053; PRD.md V4-WF-05; HOST_INTEGRATION.md V4-HI-42; EXAMINATION.md V4-EXM-14/22.
- **REQ-003** — Receive and faithfully record evidence of an actually performed checkpoint act using PKG-04's content/scope/purpose binding, retaining the human decision actor separately from the recorder. Execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance each require their own actor/evidence; success, a queued proposal or evidence of another act supplies none of them by inference. An independently evidenced act does not gain a synthetic prerequisite that proposal acceptance must occur first. If bound content changes, receive the visible lapse rather than carry the old act onto revised content. Sources: SOW-053; current PRD.md V4-AUT-03, V4-REC-03/05; HOST_INTEGRATION.md V4-HI-31/32/33; Composite HTML decision 03; CLM-002.
- **REQ-004** — Support carrying an App-authored workflow into a host for adaptation, retaining source-qualified identity, original/revised content identity, declared tool/checkpoint meaning and the trace from selected/resolved content to actually supplied/provider-adopted content and observed behavior. Record unavailable receiving capability explicitly. Sources: SOW-054; PRD.md V4-WF-03/04/06; EXAMINATION.md V4-EXM-14; CLM-001/CLM-003.
- **REQ-005** — Support opening and refining a host-authored workflow in the App, preserving its origin and revision/replay history and routing changed drafts through the existing reviewed-registration contract. Adaptation must not silently rebind a selection to a same-named source or manufacture past checkpoint acts. Sources: SOW-055; PRD.md V4-WF-02/03/05/06; EXAMINATION.md V4-EXM-14; CLM-001.
- **REQ-006** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: declaration/shared-allocation design belongs to `DEL-02-01` and workflow review/registration construction to `DEL-02-02` (CLM-001); catalog semantics belong to `DEL-03-01`, adopted operation-policy definition to `DEL-04-01`, and act/run-record format and writer/reader construction to `DEL-04-03` (CLM-002); host-loop receiving design belongs to `DEL-05-01`, and the joined activity witness/external commitment account to `DEL-09-06` (CLM-003). External host construction remains with the external SWBPIPE implementation owner, unresolved policy decisions with the owner and host policy owner, and actual human judgments with the person, as CLM-002/CLM-003 state. Local compatibility behavior, receiving duties and fixtures remain this deliverable's work.
- **REQ-007** — Carry open decisions at their separate points of need in TBD-001 through TBD-005. Use only the identified adopted operation policy for dependent cases and agreed receiving/allocation choices for common implementation; independent local definition and checking can proceed without declaring external delivery or filling unresolved choices. Sources: G3 Open_Issues.csv OI-001/002/013/014/021; External_Dependencies.csv DEP-001; CLM-002/CLM-003.

No successful compatibility claim rests on file portability or registration alone. Verify by VER-001. Sources: REQ-001; SOW-051.

Execution, acceptance, checking, approval and reliance remain independently attributable, with no invented acceptance prerequisite. Verify by VER-003. Sources: REQ-003; SOW-053.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Prepare receiving behavior against identified portable declaration, capability and record inputs; retain missing inputs as explicit limitations. Develop the transfer/adaptation contract with the named consumers, then exercise local fixtures against the actual candidate. Forward identified local evidence to the connected-activity integration owner when the coordinated host inputs are ready. No exact parser, wire field, transport, new engine, service, numerical threshold or external construction is selected by this definition.

- **VER-001** — Exercise required-tool checks with an available-tool case and a missing/unsupported-tool case against identified workflow and host capability fixtures. Inspect the person-facing result and evidence basis, including the case where registration succeeds but a requirement is unavailable. Implements AC-001 using PRD.md V4-WF-04 and EXAMINATION.md V4-EXM-14.
- **VER-002** — Exercise checkpoint arrival under identified direct-application autonomy with no human act, then interruption/replay and later performance of the required act. Inspect request, waiting state and advancement evidence; direct permission or successful unrelated work must not release the checkpoint. Implements AC-002 using V4-WF-05, V4-HI-42 and V4-EXM-14/22.
- **VER-003** — Inspect checkpoint evidence in a positive faithful-recording case where the person performs the act and a separate recorder preserves actor/content/scope/purpose. Compare cases of absent act, successful execution, another kind of human act and changed content. Include independently evidenced checking/approval/reliance without assuming proposal acceptance as a prerequisite; require only the applicable act's evidence, and retain visible lapse on changed bound content. Implements AC-003 using V4-AUT-03, V4-REC-05 and V4-HI-31/32/33; uses PKG-04's format without creating it.
- **VER-004** — Trace an App-authored source through local transfer/adaptation fixtures and inspect original/revised identity, tool/checkpoint semantics, unsupported receiving outcome and the separate selection/resolution/supply/adoption/behavior evidence. Supply this local contribution to DEL-09-06 for its externally coordinated witness; label any unavailable live-host evidence. Implements AC-004 using V4-WF-03/04/06 and V4-EXM-14.
- **VER-005** — Exercise host-origin opening/refinement and return to the App's draft/registration receiving interface, with same-name source collision and interruption/revision/replay cases. Compare preserved original/revised bytes and actual act history; registration remains with DEL-02-02. Implements AC-005 using V4-WF-02/03/05/06 and V4-EXM-14.
- **VER-006** — Review output/requirement ownership against CLM-001 through CLM-003 and the accepted Deliverables.csv rows; compare TBD-001 through TBD-005 and DEP-001 against frozen and current registers. Check that no policy carrier is promoted into the decision actor and no local contract implies an external commitment. Implements AC-006 using the accepted interfaces and Composite HTML decisions 02/03/05.
- **VER-007** — Inspect the candidate-bound fixture set and actual run report for the behavior coverage in VER-001 through VER-005, identifying candidate/source versions, outcomes, received evidence and missing external contributions. Check that the local evidence account names DEL-09-06 as joined witness owner and does not claim that witness occurred. Implements AC-007 using G3 Deliverables.csv DEL-02-03/DEL-09-06 and DEP-001.

These are production verification obligations. Authoring and schema validation of this contract satisfy none of the future code, fixture-execution or external-witness obligations by themselves.
