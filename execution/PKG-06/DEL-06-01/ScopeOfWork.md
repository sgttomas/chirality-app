Managers retain review and integration ownership; a returned artifact alone is not their integration act. Source: BASIS rows DEL-06-02/DEL-09-05, COMPOSITE decisions 05/06 and EXAM V4-EXM-13.
- **CLM-004** — `DEL-07-01`, owned by the App PEC receiving owner with PEC provider qualification evidence supplied by the PEC owner, owns the receiving contract, adapter and adoption evidence. `DEL-07-02`, owned by the App connector-receiving owner, owns connector limitation/fallback behavior; managers and the human retain fallback coordination. Present repository work can use its file-native controls immediately; this future product is not a prerequisite to that practice or to its own construction. Source: BASIS rows DEL-10-02/DEL-10-04, OPS V4-OPS-02 and SETUP.

An undertaking work graph records selected work and continuation in a particular undertaking; a project DAG records production relationships across the project. A graph's saved plan, a native descendant's observed execution, a returned artifact, an independent finding and a manager's reviewed integration are different facts. This model carries their relationships without replacing the source evidence or the responsible actor.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The bounded brief shall identify purpose, accepted or otherwise stated basis, supplied context, authority, available/declared tools, write scope and expected return. It shall preserve which limits are instructional and which are actually host-enforced; declaring a capability or write path supplies no absent permission. Association to a child shall retain the actual native identity and parent relationship when observed, without representing preparation of the brief as executed dispatch. Sources: SOW-083, BASIS, PRD V4-PM-01/V4-ROLE-03 and OPS V4-OPS-12/14; CLM-001.
- **REQ-002** — Each undertaking shall have one identifiable current file-based work graph carrying selected work, prerequisites, actual owners, results and continuation. Its content shall let a reader distinguish a ready independent contribution from a contribution awaiting its identified input; selected work and actual executing state remain separate. Identifying the current graph shall not erase earlier basis or result evidence needed to recover the undertaking. Sources: SOW-084, BASIS, PRD V4-PM-02, OPS V4-OPS-02 and EXAM V4-EXM-13.
- **REQ-003** — Record read/write and native association shall preserve the distinction between planned, actually executing, returned, reviewed and integrated work. They shall retain actual ownership, pending review and the relevant basis/continuation when the basis changes or observation is interrupted. Missing native evidence or reconnection alone shall not establish an executing child, a received return, review or integration. Sources: BASIS, ARCH §3, COMPOSITE decisions 05/07 and EXAM V4-EXM-13; CLM-001 and CLM-003.
- **REQ-004** — Where coordination references evidence or human acts, it shall preserve the identified source content, scope, purpose and actor through the PKG-04 interface described by CLM-002. Faithful recording of an actually performed human act is allowed with recorder and decision actor distinct. Applying, checking, accepting an edit, approval and professional reliance shall retain their separate evidence: success at one act proves none of the others and creates no automatic prerequisite ordering among independently evidenced acts. Sources: PRD V4-AUT-01…04/V4-REC-02…05, COMPOSITE decision 03 and BASIS.
- **REQ-005** — Coordination formats shall identify their versions and consumers and remain stable for identified consumption. A proposed format change shall account for affected PEC feed coverage and receiving adoption before relying on that changed consumer path. The writer remains governed by its source-record contract; an observing projection does not become writer authority. File-based read/write and recoverable coordination shall work without PEC availability. Sources: SOW-185, HOST V4-HI-61…63, COMPOSITE decision 06; CLM-004 and CLM-005.
- **REQ-006** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: native plan/tool/descendant presentation belongs to `DEL-01-03` in CLM-001; human-act/run-file format and content-change lapse handling belong to `DEL-04-03` in CLM-002; return-review/waiting/decision-package views belong to `DEL-06-02` and the joined fleet examination belongs to `DEL-09-05` in CLM-003; PEC receiving/adoption belongs to `DEL-07-01` and connector fallback behavior belongs to `DEL-07-02` in CLM-004; current undertaking practice belongs to `DEL-10-02` and project-DAG construction/acceptance accounting belongs to `DEL-10-04` in CLM-005. PEC provider construction remains with the PEC owning project in CLM-004. Actual human decisions remain with their human actor in CLM-002; manager review/integration acts remain with their actual managers in CLM-003. Local record consumption, association and compatibility duties remain included. Sources: BASIS interface allocations and COMPOSITE decisions 03/05/06.

- **AC-001** — A representative brief preserves all seven bounded-assignment elements in REQ-001, and distinguishes declared limits from observed host enforcement; a prepared brief without native dispatch evidence makes no executed-child claim. Verify by VER-001. Grounding: SOW-083 and BASIS verification.
- **AC-002** — A record fixture has one identifiable current undertaking graph showing selected work, prerequisites, owners, results and continuation, including independent ready work and dependent work whose input is still outstanding. Recovering the graph preserves those facts and its distinction from a project DAG. Verify by VER-002. Grounding: SOW-084, OPS V4-OPS-02 and EXAM V4-EXM-13.
- **AC-003** — App record round trips preserve the observed native child/parent association and source evidence without translating the native identity into a fabricated execution event. A no-dispatch case and an unknown-outcome case remain distinguishable from observed execution. Verify by VER-003. Grounding: BASIS CODE artifact and native-identity interface, ARCH §3.
- **AC-004** — After changed basis and interrupted observation, record recovery retains actual worker/resource ownership, returned work, independent findings and continuation, including a return awaiting review and the separate evidence for reviewed/integrated work. It does not promote a return or a reconnected conversation to integration. Verify by VER-004. Grounding: BASIS TEST artifact, COMPOSITE decisions 05/07 and EXAM V4-EXM-13.
- **AC-005** — The record/evidence interface faithfully carries a referenced, actually performed human act with its actor, content, scope and purpose, while preserving a separate recorder identity. Negative cases do not manufacture human acceptance, checking, approval or reliance from a successful tool action or returned artifact. Independently evidenced acts retain their actual order without an invented universal acceptance gate. Verify by VER-005. Grounding: CLM-002, REQ-004 and COMPOSITE decision 03.
- **AC-006** — The compatibility account identifies record versions and consumers and traces a proposed format change to its affected PEC coverage and adoption consequences, leaving provider response/wire details and receiving qualification with their actual owners. Verify by VER-006. Grounding: SOW-185, HOST V4-HI-63 and CLM-004.
- **AC-007** — A file-only record exercise with PEC absent still supports brief/graph read/write, actual ownership and continuation; absence supplies no empty-work, readiness or permission conclusion and does not block independent file-native practice. Verify by VER-007. Grounding: BASIS phase/interface limits, HOST V4-HI-61…63 and COMPOSITE decision 06.

Local fixtures claim only their record coverage; the joined fleet witness and receiving adoption remain separately evidenced. Verify by VER-008. Grounding: BASIS deliverable/artifact allocation, OPEN and CLM-001 through CLM-005.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Develop the record semantics against the accepted rows and named native/evidence interfaces, then select their technical encoding and version handling within the responsible implementation scope. Prepare the App reader/writer and native association against identified supplier/evidence inputs, retaining unknown and partial facts rather than filling them from plans. Exercise the local fixtures against the identified candidate and give the returned records and compatibility account to the adjacent owners. Code layout, wire fields, numerical budgets and a protocol pin are not selected by this contract.

- **VER-001** — Inspect a populated brief against the seven source elements and its actual native/enforcement evidence; contrast an observed dispatch with a written but undispatched brief and a declared limit unsupported by host enforcement. Preserve fixture, candidate identity and observed distinction for AC-001.
- **VER-002** — Save and recover a current graph fixture from files; compare the selected work, outstanding prerequisite, independent ready contribution, owners, result references and next work against the source fixture. Inspect its current-selector meaning and separate project-DAG reference. Record the recovered comparison for AC-002.
- **VER-003** — Exercise the candidate App reader/writer and native-delegation association using the identified native supplier interface from DEL-01-03; compare the saved and recovered child/parent identity and evidence links. Include no-dispatch and unknown-outcome inputs and examine that no event is invented. Bind results to the candidate and supplier basis for AC-003.
- **VER-004** — Exercise local record continuation with a changed basis and interruption, preserving an active worker, returned work pending review, an independent finding and separately evidenced review/integration. Compare pre-interruption evidence and recovered facts. These are record fixtures contributing to DEL-09-05's joined witness, not a claim to have performed it. Retain the comparison for AC-004.
- **VER-005** — Exercise the PKG-04 reference interface with a positive actual-human-act record and a distinct recorder, then fabrication negatives based only on successful execution or return. Include independently evidenced acts in their actual order and check that no extra acceptance prerequisite appears. Inspect content/scope/purpose and actor references against the source records for AC-005.
- **VER-006** — Review the identified format/consumer account and a bounded proposed-change example with the responsible format and receiving owners' contributions distinguishable; trace the example's affected feed coverage and required receiving adoption evidence. Confirm unresolved response details remain at OI-022 rather than invented locally. Retain the version/coverage comparison for AC-006.
- **VER-007** — Run the local file record exercise without PEC and compare persisted assignment, current graph, ownership and continuation against the same source facts. Check that missing observation does not alter record authority or create a readiness/permission/empty-work conclusion. Retain observed file-route behavior for AC-007.
- **VER-008** — Compare the candidate artifacts and evidence to the accepted SOW rows, anticipated artifact classes, interface claims and open-issue table. Check each excluded act against its named owner one-for-one and distinguish local test results from DEL-09-05 examination and PEC release/adoption. Record gaps and the exact candidate basis for AC-008.

Verification results must identify the candidate and material input basis actually exercised. A proposed fixture, a source reading or a schema pass is not an execution witness. Missing supplier or receiving evidence holds the particular dependent claim at its point of need while independent definition and file-route work continue.
