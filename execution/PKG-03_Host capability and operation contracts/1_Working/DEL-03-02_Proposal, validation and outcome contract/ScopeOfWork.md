# DEL-03-02 — Proposal, validation and outcome contract

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Define the App/shared contract for changes proposed or applied through a host, so human, embedded-agent and external-agent consumers preserve the same validation, application, outcome and error meanings. This API_CONTRACT contributes to OBJ-004 (shared host contracts and a coordinated journey preserving operation meaning, host authority and recovery) and OBJ-005 (useful autonomy with attributable human acts, result standing and reconstructable provenance). The App/shared operation-contract owner produces these definitions and fixtures; the host owner produces domain implementation and evidence of actual host behavior.

The accepted scope is the twelve frontmatter scope items. This contract defines their local contribution; it neither establishes that the described product artifacts already exist nor records a lifecycle promotion. The source kit's historical draft labels remain historical under the actual accepted composite and Group3 decisions.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The contract shall route changes by the person, embedded agent and App external-agent consumer through the same host validation and application path, preserving equivalent outcomes and errors for equivalent operation inputs, basis and applicable authority; it shall specify no alternate agent mutation route. Source: SOW-070; PRD V4-PAR-04; HOST V4-HI-20; CLM-002 and CLM-005.
- **REQ-002** — The contract shall retain domain truth and validation in the host's store and ownership, and shall never represent agent output as a host-accepted result without the host's recorded act. Source: SOW-090/SOW-091; PRD V4-REC-01/V4-CST-05; CLM-002.
- **REQ-003** — An agent change shall carry its origin, conversation and workflow-run attribution and the basis relied on, preserving the catalog's operation and basis meaning through submission and outcome reporting. Source: SOW-170; HOST V4-HI-21 and V4-HI-11; CLM-003 and CLM-004.
- **REQ-004** — Outside granted direct autonomy, the contract shall represent drafted → validated → queued → accepted → applied (receipt), with rejected, withdrawn and stale dispositions from the queued proposal as stated in the source. Granted direct application remains distinct from a proposal and does not manufacture acceptance. Source: SOW-171; HOST V4-HI-22–23; CLM-004 and CLM-006.
- **REQ-005** — Whenever a proposal/application outcome cannot be observed, the contract shall report outcome unknown, including when execution may have occurred; it shall not infer application, failure or acceptance from missing observation. Source: SOW-172; HOST V4-HI-23; CLM-006.
- **REQ-006** — A proposal whose relied-on basis no longer holds shall be refused as stale with the reason; the contract shall permit re-drafting against the current basis without silently treating that as the unchanged original proposal. Source: SOW-173; HOST V4-HI-23; CLM-003 and CLM-006.
- **REQ-007** — A later selection change shall never retarget a proposal; its affected objects remain those bound when it was drafted. Source: SOW-174; HOST V4-HI-23; CLM-006.
- **REQ-008** — Repeated submission of the same proposal shall have only one effect. The contract shall make this obligation testable while leaving implementation mechanics explicit for agreement; shared validation alone is not evidence that it holds. Source: SOW-175; HOST V4-HI-23; DECISIONS #d4; CLM-006.
- **REQ-009** — The contract shall provide the host's own proposal views with old and new values, affected objects and reasons for the change, retaining host ownership of presentation and actual receipts. Source: SOW-176; HOST V4-HI-24; CLM-002.
- **REQ-010** — Reported operation success shall mean that the operation ran, never that the person accepted anything; execution and human-act evidence shall remain distinguishable. Source: SOW-177; HOST V4-HI-25; CLM-004 and CLM-006.
- **REQ-011** — A submitted proposal shall report queued until the host records acceptance and application. The lifecycle may carry the actual accepted stage separately, but neither accepted alone nor a successful submit shall be reported as applied; applied reporting shall refer to the host receipt. Source: SOW-178; HOST V4-HI-23/V4-HI-25; CLM-002 and CLM-006.
- **REQ-012** — The contract shall consume adopted operation/autonomy and human-act distinctions without assigning the unresolved global reserved list or routine-classifier policy. It shall keep proposal acceptance, checking, engineering approval and professional reliance distinct and never attribute an unperformed act to a person. Source: CLM-004; ISSUES OI-001/OI-002; DECISIONS #d3; HOST V4-HI-33. This qualifies SOW-171/SOW-177/SOW-178 without adding policy scope.
- **REQ-013** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable or external host: catalog/read-basis definition belongs to `DEL-03-01` (CLM-003); operation-policy definition and unresolved-policy receiving belong to `DEL-04-01`, autonomy/standing receiving controls to `DEL-04-02`, and human-act/run-record production to `DEL-04-03` (CLM-004); external receiving adapter construction belongs to `DEL-03-03`, integrated host-guide production to `DEL-03-04`, and joined external-control/catalog-extension examination to `DEL-09-09` (CLM-005). Host domain object/store construction, catalog implementation, validation/application implementation, receipt production, actual human-act recording and host UI construction remain the external host owner's acts (CLM-002). Definitions and local fixtures required by OUT-001–OUT-003 remain this deliverable's contribution.
