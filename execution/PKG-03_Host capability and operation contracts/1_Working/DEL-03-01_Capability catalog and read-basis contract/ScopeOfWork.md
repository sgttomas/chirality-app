# DEL-03-01 — Capability catalog and read-basis contract

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Define the App/shared contract for one host capability catalog: the meaning of operations, their declared inputs/results, availability, and the basis and standing of reads. This is the API_CONTRACT contribution owned by the **App/shared capability-contract owner**. The host owner implements its catalog and supplies actual domain tables, results and diagnostics. The local contribution to OBJ-004 is common operation meaning across human, embedded-agent and external-agent consumers; its contribution to OBJ-005 is faithful read standing and attributable basis, without stronger claims than the host evidence warrants.

The accepted Group3 deliverable row defines a schema and semantic-fixture context, not implementation of every host operation. The expected products are CONFIG catalog/read-basis schemas, a DOC generated-surface and adapter-responsibility map, and TEST availability/read-standing/basis contract fixtures. This contract defines those products; its existence does not establish that they have been built, adopted or integrated.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — Define meaningful read and change operations once and require the human interface, embedded agent and external agent to use that same catalog meaning. Semantic parity covers reads, operations, validation, results and recovery subject to separately identified human acts; this deliverable defines the catalog/read interfaces into those paths, with proposal/application and runtime behavior received from CLM-001 and CLM-002. Equal UI gestures are unnecessary. Sources: SOW-018/SOW-067/SOW-068; H V4-HI-01/03; B #d4.
- **REQ-002** — Each entry shall declare stable identity and version; purpose readable by person and agent; argument schema; preconditions and unavailable reasons; affected objects, if any; result schema including standing; meaningful errors; human-act/autonomy class drawn from the adopted operation policy; and host-declared exposure per consumer surface, independent of class. Preserve the declared none/may-apply/proposal-only/reserved distinctions, and represent an operation without an adopted class explicitly (no policy basis) rather than silently applying the historical blanket reserved list. The first-increment App/shared reserved acts and routine-permission treatment are those adopted by `APP-V4-FIRST-INCREMENT-20260928-DECISION-1` D2/D3 and carried by DEL-04-01; operation-specific additions remain OI-021 and host adoption remains DEP-001. - **REQ-003** — A read shall return the tables, results and diagnostics available to the person with the same standing. An operation unavailable to the person shall be unavailable to the agents with the same availability result and reason. Contract cases shall compare the same operation and described basis across channels. Sources: SOW-069/SOW-166; H V4-HI-04/10; B #d4.
- **REQ-004** — Every read shall carry the workspace identity, generation, model revision and canonical content hash (received as a canonical content identity with its identity-method designation; the algorithm stays unselected under TBD-003) of the basis it describes. Each object or row in a read result also carries a host-supplied subject content identity with its method designation, distinct from the read-level identity, for per-item basis checks, act binding and lapse; where a host supplies only a whole-model identity, that identity is received as the identity of every subject it covers, and the App never computes identities itself. Every later action shall cite the basis it actually relied on; the catalog/read contract shall expose that association for the App v4 DEL-03-02 receiver, without substituting a later queue-time or newly selected basis for the original relied-on basis. Concrete serialization and canonicalization details require an identified host/consumer agreement. Sources: SOW-167/SOW-168; H V4-HI-11; B #d4; E V4-EXM-25.
- **REQ-005** — Read results shall expose current or historical standing, checks passed and known limitations. Agent-facing representations shall not strengthen host claims. Execution, checking, proposal acceptance, approval and professional reliance remain separate subjects with their own actors and evidence: a success value alone establishes none of the other acts, and no blanket acceptance-before-checking dependency is introduced. A faithful report of a human act actually evidenced by the host is permitted with the person as decision actor and any host/agent recorder distinct; unperformed acts shall not be invented. Sources: SOW-169/SOW-018; H V4-HI-12/25/30–31; P V4-AUT-03; B #d3.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Start from D's exact local scope and the composite-qualified H/P meanings. Develop the catalog/read schemas without adding operation behavior or provider wire fields absent an adopted receiving agreement. Maintain the consumer responsibility map alongside schema decisions so a changed meaning is traceable to its receiver. Obtain concrete host contributions through the human-relayed external coordination path before claiming host conformance. Verification implements the source obligations below; its examples do not create additional product scope.

- **VER-001** — Inspect the catalog definition and representative read/change examples against H V4-HI-01–02 and the assigned scope rows. Check each declared field, operation identity/version and adopted/unresolved class treatment. Record the candidate schema identity and relevant policy source, or the precise held assignment. Evidence: field-to-source comparison and schema/example checks for AC-001.
- **VER-002** — Compare the corresponding read contract fixtures for human, embedded and external consumers on an identified operation and basis. Compare table/result/diagnostic meaning and host standing, recording consumer versions and whether evidence is a schema fixture, adapter result or actual host observation. Evidence: comparison with mismatches/absent integrated paths explicit for AC-002; matching types alone cannot close an actual consumer claim.
- **VER-003** — Exercise an operation whose stated precondition does not hold in the shared contract fixtures. Compare unavailable result and reason across all channels and check that a success/empty-result encoding does not hide refusal. Evidence: availability comparison bound to candidate/operation/basis for AC-003. The concrete precondition comes from the identified host example, not an invented universal rule.
- **VER-004** — Inspect each fixture read for workspace identity, generation, model revision and canonical content hash (as a canonical content identity with its identity-method designation), then trace a subsequent action to that received basis. Include an intervening basis change and demonstrate that the reference remains the original relied-on basis; proposal refusal/application behavior is received from App v4 DEL-03-02 rather than implemented here. Evidence: read-to-action basis comparison and named unresolved representation decisions for AC-004.
- **VER-005** — Compare current and historical result fixtures, checks passed and known limitations with host standing. Include a successful operation and an agent finding that do not invent human checking/acceptance/approval/reliance. Include a separately evidenced actual human act faithfully carried in the read result, retaining the person's attribution and distinguishing the recorder. Do not require one independent act as a synthetic prerequisite to another. Evidence: standing/attribution cases for AC-005, with actual versus illustrative evidence labeled.
- **VER-006** — Review the map against the three consumer surfaces, D's named interfaces and CLM-001 through CLM-003. For every listed generated or checked portion, inspect its declared conformance route and remaining adapter responsibility; confirm allocation remains open where not agreed. Evidence: catalog-to-surface and owner/receiving-point map for AC-006.
- **VER-007** — Compare the map's extension treatment with S V4-PAR-05/V4-HI-03, B #d4, I OI-003 and E V4-EXM-24. Check that original promise, available evidence, missing trace and decision owner are explicit. A future operation trace records generated behavior, additional work, availability reasons and semantic outcomes across the three surfaces; only the explicit retain/narrow/defer disposition fixes the corresponding later criterion. Evidence: source/disposition account for AC-007, not a claimed automatic-extension demonstration.
- **VER-008** — Audit each excluded act against its cited ownership claim and accepted row/source; inspect the open-input account for owner, point of need and consequence. Distinguish schema/fixture evidence, actual host/consumer delivery and human decisions. Evidence: local boundary and unresolved-input review for AC-008; no inferred external adoption or product completion.
