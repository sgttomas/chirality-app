## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The App receiving path shall use Codex's native tools against the host's catalog-derived MCP or CLI interface and consume the catalog/read-basis and proposal/outcome meanings in CLM-002. It shall preserve operation identity, availability reason, meaningful read results, standing and original inspected basis through submission, including a non-mutating check where that operation is exposed. Sources: SOW-148, SOW-183; consolidated V4-ARC-21, V4-HI-01…12, V4-HI-20…25 and V4-EXM-25.
- **REQ-002** — External host access shall remain machine-local and off unless the person enables it. The receiving behavior and documentation shall distinguish disabled access from enabled but unavailable host/operation access; enabling the channel shall not create an operation/autonomy grant. Host content read over the channel may flow to the model the person selected for the App conversation, cloud included; the App gates neither enablement nor requests on that destination and adds no other destination (no App relay, telemetry or remote endpoint) (`APP-V4-FIRST-INCREMENT-20260928-DECISION-2` D5). The App records the observed model destination and shows it in the channel status as information, not as a gate. A host may restrict its own channel (DEP-001); PRD V4-HOST-02, as revised by `APP-V4-SWBPIPE-INTAKE-20260928-DECISION-5`, governs the host's embedded agent. This requirement selects no model or transport. Sources: SOW-184; accepted DEL-03-03 verification; consolidated V4-HI-04, V4-HI-40, V4-HI-50…52 and PRD V4-HOST-02; DeliverableFocus.
- **REQ-003** — Enabled requests shall preserve the same host validation/application path and actual adopted autonomy and reserved-act rules as the other catalog consumers. Direct application is permissible only under an effective direct treatment within the person's grant and operative host policy; otherwise a direct request is *not permitted* — never silently converted — and the agent may propose instead. Workflow checkpoints retain their required human act. In the current phase a checkpoint on this channel is plan guidance: its act is recorded only when the person performs it and the App claims no hold (`APP-V4-SWBPIPE-INTAKE-20260928-DECISION-4` D4-1); hold support and enforced holds on this channel are `DEL-02-03`'s governance-phase definition (CLM-002). Transport success, queued status and receipt observation shall not fabricate acceptance, checking, approval or professional reliance. Faithful recording of an actually evidenced human act is permitted with the decision actor distinguished from the recorder and the act bound to its content. Execution, edit acceptance, checking, approval and reliance retain separate evidence; one act supplies neither another act nor a synthetic requirement that acceptance precede every independently evidenced act. ## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Production begins from the identified App catalog and proposal/outcome contracts, adopted PKG-04 policy, actual native-tool capability and externally supplied endpoint contract. Resolve affected open choices at the points below before depending on them. Configure the App receiving boundary and implement only necessary App adaptation; document enablement and shared-operation behavior; exercise consumer fixtures against identified local candidates. Give App DEL-09-09 the local evidence and remaining external requirements for its joined witness. These are future production obligations: this INIT run creates the contract and author evidence only.

- **VER-001** — Examine native-tool configuration/adapter wiring and exercise catalog inspection, a declared non-mutating read/check and proposal submission using an identified endpoint or explicit fixture. Compare identity, availability, standing and original basis with App DEL-03-01/02 contract expectations; record each source and candidate. Evaluates AC-001.
- **VER-002** — Exercise disabled, person-enabled and enabled-but-unavailable cases; inspect local configuration/request destinations and actual grant accounting. Confirm no host call while disabled, truthful unavailability and no implicit remote/data/autonomy expansion. Mark a fixture's reach and leave actual host local-operation traffic examination to its responsible joined examination. Evaluates AC-002.
- **VER-003** — Against identified adopted policy, exercise permitted direct operation, proposal-only, reserved-act refusal and a checkpoint case whose act is recorded only when performed, with no hold claimed (a hold case only for a workflow in the governance phase). Trace the receiver's route to the shared host contract, including refusal, and inspect App code/configuration for a bypass route; use external host evidence for host enforcement claims. Evaluates AC-003 without deciding the policy.
- **VER-004** — Inject an intervening edit, later selection, duplicate submission and lost acknowledgment/interruption into external consumer fixtures. Compare expected preserved basis/target and stale/duplicate/unknown outcomes with App DEL-03-02; identify actual versus simulated host receipts and defer durable joined conclusions to App DEL-09-09. Evaluates AC-004.
- **VER-005** — Inspect received human-act mapping and exercise fabrication negatives plus a positive case of an actually performed human act recorded with actor, relevant content identity and evidence distinct from recorder. Check that execution, edit acceptance, checking, approval and reliance retain their separate standing, including independently evidenced acts without an edit-acceptance predecessor. Evaluates AC-005.
- **VER-006** — Review documentation against the accepted DEL-03-03 artifact/interface/owner row, Group3 PKG-03/04, OI-001/002/003/013/014/021 and DEP-001, current source qualifications, CLM-001…CLM-004 and each open matter below. Check excluded acts one-for-one, local/privacy and grant limits, exact unresolved ownership/timing, and no inferred host delivery or new technical choice. Evaluates AC-006.
- **VER-007** — Inspect the fixture inventory, results and receiving handoff for complete AC-001…AC-005 coverage, candidate/contract/policy identities, original-basis/outcome evidence and explicit simulation/host limits. Check App DEL-09-09's required join remains separate and that generated/adapted work does not settle automatic extension. Evaluates AC-007.
