## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

1. The App connector examination owner identifies the candidate and receiving consumer, the applicable PKG-07 receiving/fallback contribution, any PKG-08 source-contract contribution and the actual provider evidence available. Carry TBD-001…TBD-003 at their points of need. Missing witness input constrains that examination, not independent work.
2. Prepare invented-material cases and use recorded real protocol exchanges where suitable under EXAM V4-EXM-02. Record whether evidence is replayed, directly observed or supplied by a provider; do not equate replay or a written handoff with operational delivery. Use actual qualified input for the required joined witness.
3. Execute the required connector cases on the identified candidate. Preserve the same question/source relation through response, receiving action and source fallback. Applicable interface scenarios follow EXAM V4-EXM-04 in WebKit and Chromium with targeted packaged-application smoke evidence; browser evidence does not replace a required native observation.
4. Assemble OUT-002 from actual results and contributions, retaining limitations, absent evidence and affected rechecks. Return it for independent examination with the responsible owners and open matters visible. Production methods and exact fixture/API details remain governed by their received contracts; this initialization runs no product scenario.

- **VER-001** — Inspect the qualified-case execution record against the selected consumer's adopted envelope: follow the actual source pin, extraction and response to the observed receiving action, compare citations/standing/coverage/freshness, and check the identified provider qualification/release and consumer-adoption evidence. An envelope-only check or missing link cannot verify AC-001.
- **VER-002** — Compare the absent, stale, partial and failing observations with the qualified case's coordination question. Follow the actual fallback sources and actor actions, inspect unsupported conclusions and any unavailable basis, and check that no inferred empty-work/readiness/permission conclusion appears. This verifies AC-002 without treating honest blocked evidence as a completed qualified case.
- **VER-003** — Inspect the original PEC-stopped/Domains-stale execution and its file recovery; compare connector availability cases to confirm independent availability/limits and independent supported work. Check that later research-to-design claims remain App DEL-08-02's contribution. This verifies AC-003 without inventing an exhaustive combination count.
- **VER-004** — Audit the candidate/configuration/date and evidence standing of each result, its capture/replay/native/browser provenance as applicable, and any affected re-examination after change. Compare completion claims with actual joined observations and protected source criteria. This verifies AC-004.
- **VER-005** — Review the dossier against CLM-002…CLM-004, CURRENT and the open matters below. Check release/adoption evidence and D108 limitations, one-for-one ownership of excluded acts, and faithful-recording cases where human acts actually occur alongside negative checks for fabricated acts or inferred authority. Keep actual actor distinct from recorder; verify AC-005 without requiring a new or favorable human decision to pass the connector scenario.
