# Later run reconstruction witness

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Demonstrate that a named reader, separate from the host-run author, can reconstruct one identified host journey from the project's files a week after that journey. The reader establishes what was requested, proposed, accepted by the engineer and actually changed, follows host receipt references, and preserves what remains unknown. This is the local SOW-206 contribution to PKG-09, supporting OBJ-005 (attributable human acts, standing and provenance) and OBJ-008 (candidate-specific examination without overstating partial evidence).

This contract defines the TEST_SUITE contribution. Its existence does not establish that a host journey, elapsed interval or reconstruction has occurred. The App evidence examination owner integrates the witness; a separate reconstructing reader performs it. The accepted context is one host-run record set and its receipt references, not a survey of every run.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The witness shall use one identified V4-EXM-20 host-run record set from App DEL-09-07 and the compact records from App DEL-04-03, with a named reconstructing reader separate from that run's author. Record the host-journey date and reconstruction date so the required week-later observation can be examined. A plan, an immediate reread, or elapsed time without an actual reconstruction does not fulfill OUT-001. [S1, S3, S6]
- **REQ-002** — The reconstruction shall recover from project files the request, proposal, engineer's actual acceptance and actual changes, and trace the workflow source identity/version, conversation, model, autonomy settings, requested operations and observed outcomes through the compact record to the host's receipts, hashes and origin marks. It shall identify the sources and content basis used, including historical standing when relevant. It shall retain receipt references rather than create a replacement copy of host domain truth. [S1, S4 V4-REC-01…05; S5 V4-HI-70…71; S6 V4-EXM-31]
- **REQ-003** — Each reconstructed human act shall retain the actual human actor distinct from the recorder, the identified content, scope and purpose, and any visible lapse when bound content changes. Faithful recording of an actually performed human act is permitted. Proposing, applying an edit, accepting an edit, checking, engineering approval and professional reliance are separate acts with their own evidence: evidence of one establishes none of the others, and no invented sequence requires acceptance before another independently evidenced act. Tool success, silence, source resolution or transport success shall not be promoted into a human act or an unobserved host/provider outcome. [S2 decision 03; S4 V4-AUT-01…05, V4-REC-03/05; S5 V4-HI-25, V4-HI-30…33; S6 V4-EXM-31]
- **REQ-004** — The reader shall rely on project source records and linked host evidence for the reconstruction. Private agent memory, a harness transcript/session store, and a derived view may help locate evidence but shall not become authority for a human act or replacement host truth. Missing, conflicting, inaccessible or unobserved evidence remains identified as such. Record partial, failed, blocked, not-run and inconclusive results honestly; those records do not satisfy the completed week-later witness required by REQ-001. [S1, S4 V4-REC-01…04; S6 §§1–2 and V4-EXM-31]
- **REQ-005** — Each result shall name the candidate, configuration including harness/model versions and model server, date, reader and source-run identity, and distinguish observations from inferences and limits. An earlier candidate's pass supports that candidate only; relevant changes reopen affected examination. A successful reconstruction concerns the actual scope of the recorded acts, without requiring a new favorable human approval, validation-in-use finding or professional reliance act. [S1, S6 §§1–2; S2 decision 03; S4 V4-AUT-02…05]
- **REQ-006** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: production of the shared record format, App writer/reader and lapse implementation belongs to App `DEL-04-03`, and execution/qualification of the source host journey belongs to App `DEL-09-07`, as assigned in CLM-001. It also excludes host implementation, domain mutation, solve and receipt production, which belong to the external SWBPIPE implementation owner in CLM-002; performance of human acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance, which belongs to the actual person/accountable professional in CLM-002; and deciding the unresolved operation policies/activity selection, which belongs to the exact owners in CLM-003. Examining and faithfully recording these acts is this deliverable's contribution, not performance of them. [S1–S2, S4–S7]

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The App evidence examination owner identifies the source journey and compact records, confirms actual input availability and assigns a separate named reader. The required week-later observation is a dependency of execution; this contract selects no calendar date. The reader reconstructs from the supplied project files and linked host evidence, records the account and unknowns, and returns it for integration against this contract. Relevant supporting checks may use identified reference/lapse fixtures from App DEL-04-03; fixture evidence and live-journey reconstruction keep distinct identities and cannot substitute for one another.

- **VER-001** — Examine source-journey evidence, reader/run-author identities, input-set identity and both observation dates; inspect the reader's actual reconstruction return. Confirm the V4-EXM-31 week-later witness occurred on the named journey, rather than accepting a schedule, test definition or synthetic clock advancement as the witness. Record incomplete timing, missing journey or absent reader execution as an unfulfilled witness. [S1, S3, S6]
- **VER-002** — Trace each reconstructed request, proposal, engineer acceptance and actual-change assertion to identified project records and authoritative host receipt/hash/origin references. Check the compact record's workflow/version, conversation, model, autonomy, operations and outcomes against the sources. Resolve references without replacing host truth; record unresolved claims and their effect on completion. [S4 V4-REC-01…05; S5 V4-HI-70…71; S6 V4-EXM-31]
- **VER-003** — Compare a faithfully recorded actual human act with its source, actor, recorder and bound content/scope/purpose. Use identified content-change evidence to examine visible lapse; retain supporting fixture provenance separately when the source journey does not contain that change. Check fabrication negatives in the record/read path: tool or transport success, a receipt, agent findings, or silence cannot establish an unperformed human act. Check any independently evidenced acts individually without creating an acceptance prerequisite, and distinguish edit acceptance from engineering approval and reliance. These checks implement the record/act criteria; they do not select the open reserved-act policy. [S2 decision 03; S4 V4-AUT-03, V4-REC-05; S5 V4-HI-25, V4-HI-31…33; S1 App DEL-04-03]
- **VER-004** — Compare the reader's account with source gaps and available outcomes, including an unobserved outcome despite a resolved source or successful transport, using identified supporting evidence where needed. Verify that no private recollection, transcript or derived view fills the gap; distinguish truthful negative/partial evidence from fulfillment of the actual week-later reconstruction. Preserve the evidence's actual result without forcing a pass. [S1, S4 V4-REC-01…04; S6 §§1–2 and V4-EXM-31]
- **VER-005** — Inspect result metadata and findings against the candidate and input basis actually examined. Check configuration/date/reader identity, observed outcome, limitations and applicability after changes. Verify that the stated conclusion reaches only the reconstructed journey and contains no inferred favorable approval, validation-in-use or professional reliance. [S6 V4-EXM-01/03/05 and §§1–2; S4 V4-AUT-02…05]
- **VER-006** — Compare the input and responsibility account with S1, S5 §1 and S7. Check each excluded act one-for-one against CLM-001 through CLM-003; check the exact unresolved owners and points of need and distinguish proposed, received, adopted and executed contributions. Return any missing input or unresolved consequential policy to the App evidence examination owner, without claiming it supplied or deciding it. [S1–S2, S4–S8]
