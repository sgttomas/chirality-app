# Standalone App candidate qualification

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable qualifies one identified standalone build of the App by having a person carry out its user journeys on that build and recording what was observed. It contributes the journey and scenario definitions with their candidate-bound result records, and one dossier that joins those results with the feature owners' focused evidence and hands them, with their limits, to DEL-11-03 for the owner's decisions. It builds no product feature.

It serves OBJ-001 (a complete standalone loop that recovers), OBJ-002 (explicit choice between account and local access), OBJ-003 (source-identified workflows and truthful checkpoints) and OBJ-008 (candidate-specific verification). Its sources are PRD V4-APP-01/02/04, V4-WF-01…05, V4-EXE-01…04, V4-AUT-03/04 and V4-REC-02…05, and EXAMINATION §§1–3 and §7.

The two standalone journeys the current execution basis keeps in scope for 4.0.0, J1 and J2 (publication punchlist P1 and P2), are observed here on the candidate (AC-009, AC-010), beside the request, interruption, recovery and access results of punchlist P3 (AC-004…AC-006). V4-EXM-10 and V4-EXM-11 passed on the candidate are the standalone evidence that the App supports the core loop at least at v3.0.1's level (PRD V4-REP-01; EXAMINATION §7), shown by AC-001…AC-005 and mapped in AC-008. Installed custody and keychain protection (punchlist P4) also names this deliverable; this scope holds no criterion for it (TBD-005).

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The journey and scenario definitions (J1, J2, V4-EXM-10, V4-EXM-11, V4-EXM-12) and one result record for each step planned on the candidate. Supports SOW-195, SOW-196 and SOW-197; OBJ-001, OBJ-002, OBJ-003 and OBJ-008.
- **OUT-002** — One standalone qualification dossier: the candidate's identification, every step's result, the feature owners' focused evidence linked with its own identity and limits, and the return to DEL-11-03. Supports SOW-195, SOW-196 and SOW-197; OBJ-001, OBJ-002, OBJ-003 and OBJ-008.

Terms:

- **Candidate.** One build of the App identified by its source revision, build identity and supplier pin, using the DEL-09-01 candidate identification record. All journeys and scenarios of one qualification run on the same candidate. Whether the journeys must be observed on the installed package or may be observed on an identified development build is undecided (owner).
- **J1 and J2.** The two journeys `DEL-02-02` defines and provides as product: J1, use an existing workflow; J2, create, save, return and reuse. This deliverable observes them on the candidate, J2 under the person's workflow approval policy ("Allow disk records" or "Require verified approval").
- **Run A.** One continuous journey that is V4-EXM-10 with V4-EXM-11 carried out inside it. Its steps are: J-1 start from an empty project folder, develop a plan with the agent and revise it; J-2 execute with substantive real tool use, including one delegated sub-task; J-3 create a workflow draft; J-4 trial the draft; J-5 review it, with an entry of the same name from another origin present; J-6 the person registers it at the act control, after the unperformed-act negatives; J-7 select the registered revision and run it on new inputs; J-8 refine it a first time and J-9 a second time, giving two further registered revisions while the earlier selection and run still name revision 1. The V4-EXM-11 steps taken during it are: S11-1 stop a turn while a delegated child is active; S11-2 close and reopen the window with a request waiting; S11-3 deny one request; S11-4 grant a later one; S11-5 quit with live work and relaunch; S11-6 continue the conversation.
- **Run B.** V4-EXM-12 on the same candidate: sign in with a ChatGPT account, configure a local model server with its credential, confirm that a new conversation shows no model selected until the person chooses, hold one conversation with each mode and switch between them. No credential appears in any record.
- **Stimulus.** A condition the examiner arranges so that a step can show its criterion: a revision condition; a same-name entry from another origin; the four unperformed-act negatives (the control left unconfirmed, the agent claiming registration, a tool reporting success, and an act of a different kind or on a different subject, such as a request grant, a trial or a review without registration); a delegated child outliving its parent's turn; a lost acknowledgment. A stimulus the candidate cannot produce is supplied by its recorded-replay counterpart, or the step is blocked. It is never passed.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — The feature owners own what is examined and keep their focused checks. `DEL-02-02` owns the journeys as product and the complete create, trial, review, register, reuse and refine result. `DEL-01-01` owns supplier hosting, `DEL-01-02` execution and request recovery, `DEL-01-03` and `DEL-01-04` the native interaction views and the act control, `DEL-01-05` account and provider access, `DEL-01-06` the package. This deliverable joins their contributions on one candidate.
- **CLM-002** — `DEL-02-01` owns workflow declarations, `DEL-02-03` checkpoint behaviour, `DEL-04-01` the operation policy and `DEL-04-03` the act and run records. The person performs each review, registration, grant and denial; the App offers, records and presents it. Five acts are reserved to a person: marking checked, accepting a proposal where autonomy requires it, engineering approval, professional reliance, and changing the grant or enabling external access. Routine tool permission and sandbox modes are the person's own Codex setting and never stand for a reserved act.
- **CLM-003** — `DEL-09-01` owns the examination support, record formats and review protocol this deliverable uses; there is no second record protocol. Within `app/examination/`, this deliverable owns standalone preparation and fixtures, the native-step forms, native operation support and the receiving of qualification records; DEL-09-01 owns record checking, admission, candidate and support identity, package and distribution receiving and the interface runner. `DEL-09-05` owns the fleet and longer-work recovery witness, `DEL-09-12` the owner's validation in use, and `DEL-11-03` the decision packet. A standalone result is no joined host witness, release, replacement or professional reliance.
- **CLM-004** — The App workflow-experience integration owner integrates this dossier and an independent examiner assembles it. The three scenarios are cases of this one deliverable. Examination takes place when the candidate's needed contributions are available; it is not a late testing phase. Agents do development testing; the owner's product testing begins after readiness is recommended, and a control operated by an agent establishes no act of the owner.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — Every result names the candidate, the harness, model and model-server configuration and the observation date. A changed candidate reopens the affected steps, and an earlier pass stays with the candidate it was observed on.
- **REQ-002** — V4-EXM-10 is observed as one journey through steps J-1…J-9, showing the workflow's declared inputs, tools, human checkpoints, outputs and evidence, its source-qualified identity, the review and registration standing of each changed draft, and protection against silent overwrite and rebinding of a selection.
- **REQ-003** — Review, registration and checkpoint evidence distinguishes an act a person actually performed from execution, a proposal, a routine tool permission, checking, approval and professional reliance. Tool success, silence, a timeout or another act supplies no missing act, and acceptance is not required before every independently evidenced act.
- **REQ-004** — V4-EXM-11 is observed during V4-EXM-10 through steps S11-1…S11-6. Losing the observer is kept distinct from stopping the work; work and outstanding requests survive window closure.
- **REQ-005** — Recovery observations distinguish provider state from rendered state, completion of the primary turn from active descendants, and an attempted reply from a received acknowledgment. An unavailable outcome stays unknown, and continuing a conversation is not reported as recovery of the whole undertaking.
- **REQ-006** — V4-EXM-12 keeps ChatGPT sign-in and local model-server access configured together on the candidate, with one conversation using each and observed selection between them. The local server's required credential and its custody by Codex in the App's dedicated local home are examined separately from account sign-in, and switching modes keeps the other mode's configuration. Standalone OpenAI API-key access is outside this qualification.
- **REQ-007** — Each planned step ends as passed, failed, blocked, not run or inconclusive according to what was executed and observed. A missing candidate, a source inspection, a test definition or a missing configuration is neither a pass nor a failure. Native packaged observations are kept apart from WebKit and Chromium interface evidence, and browser evidence never replaces a native witness. Verification material is invented.
- **REQ-008** — This deliverable performs no act owned by another deliverable, as allocated in CLM-001, CLM-002 and CLM-003, and links focused evidence without copying feature tests into a second suite.
- **REQ-009** — The qualification carries the adopted reserved-act policy of CLM-002 and decides no further policy. The steps a person must perform are never automated: review, registration, answers to requests, quit confirmation, sign-in and key entry.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The examiner identifies the candidate and the feature contributions it needs, prepares the invented project material and stimuli, and has the person operate the candidate while the examiner records each step on a native-step form. Before a request step the examiner confirms that the person's permission settings make the supplier ask; if no request arises, the step is blocked. V4-EXM-11 runs inside V4-EXM-10, and V4-EXM-12 runs on the same candidate. Seams that a recording can show are examined by replay; live runs are kept for the journeys themselves. A missing input goes to its owner and its step is recorded as blocked or not run. Records use the DEL-09-01 formats and are received only after they pass its checks.

- **VER-001** — Observe run A from the empty project through J-9 and compare each step with V4-EXM-10. A scripted definition or component pass does not stand in for the joined run.
- **VER-002** — Inspect the declared part and the selected and registered bytes through the journey. Apply the revision and other-origin stimuli and compare selection and registered content before and after.
- **VER-003** — Compare the act the examiner watched the person perform with the candidate's record of it: actor, content, scope and purpose. Check that each observed act is recorded as its own kind. Apply the four negatives and inspect what the candidate shows and records.
- **VER-004** — Carry out S11-1…S11-6 during run A and record stop against window closure, the state of work and requests after reopening, the two request outcomes, and the relaunch and continuation.
- **VER-005** — At each recovery point compare the supplier's actual events with the rendered state, including the child that outlives its parent's turn and the lost acknowledgment, using recorded seam evidence where the candidate cannot produce the condition.
- **VER-006** — With both modes configured, select each for one conversation, observe the switch and the result, and inspect the records for credentials. Examine the local server's credential and its custody by Codex in the dedicated local home apart from account sign-in, and check after each switch that the other mode's configuration is still in place. A mode that cannot be obtained is a named input gap or an observed failure, never a pass by the other mode.
- **VER-007** — An independent reviewer checks the assembled dossier against the step list and the DEL-09-01 record rules: identity, applicability of linked evidence, execution against definition or history, outcome classification, native against browser evidence, and reopened steps.
- **VER-008** — Trace each statement in the return to DEL-11-03 to a step record, and check the allocation of acts against CLM-001, CLM-002 and CLM-003.
- **VER-009** — Observe a person carry out J1 on the candidate without help from an agent in reading the App's controls, and record where the person found the results.
- **VER-010** — Observe a person carry out J2 on the candidate, with the saved workflow run in a later session, and record which approval policy the person chose and what approval evidence the candidate applied.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Criteria come from the accepted scope, the PRD and the examination scenarios. A passing test neither creates scope nor weakens a failed criterion. Better means more of the journey observed on the one candidate as the person actually experiences it; never by counting a component pass, a replay or an agent-operated control as the person's journey.
- **AX-002** — Each record stands for its own observed subject only. Better means fewer blocked and not-run steps on the candidate; never by recording an unobserved step as passed or by relaxing a criterion.
- **AX-003** — Definition, execution, checking, acceptance, approval and reliance each have their own actor and evidence. A truthful record of a performed act is welcome; an inferred act is refused. Ordinary uncertainty creates no new human checkpoint. Better means more recorded acts traceable to the person who performed them; never by inferring an act or merging two acts into one.
- **AX-004** — Standalone qualification stands on its own: no external host, connector or Domains result is a starting condition for it, and it settles nothing about them. Better means fewer standalone steps waiting on host, connector or Domains inputs; never by drawing a host conclusion from a standalone result.
- **TBD-001** — Additions to the reserved acts for a specific connected operation are undecided (owner, with the App and SWBPIPE contract owners). They do not bear on the standalone criteria above.
- **TBD-003** — The set of access modes is not settled between the records: this deliverable's decomposition row, OI-010's stated consequence and publication punchlist P3 name three modes, while PRD V4-APP-02 and V4-EXM-12 require the account and local modes and omit standalone API-key access from this increment. AC-006 follows the PRD; reconciling the other records is the owner's. The supplier pin the qualification is bound to is the one the candidate was built with.
- **TBD-004** — Signing, notarisation and the supplier entitlements of the packaged candidate are undecided (App implementation owner with the SWBPIPE owner). They are needed before the packaged distribution witness.
- **TBD-005** — Publication punchlist P4 (installed custody and keychain protection on the installed candidate) names this deliverable among its owners, but no criterion here covers keychain enrolment, the keychain-held approval policy or admission of a sealed workflow after a cold start. Whether this deliverable carries such a criterion is for the owner.
