## Recoverable failures versus unsupported terminal conditions

Ordinarily recoverable: question grammar/transcription or corroborating image
fails while exact independent sheet/Cancel binding remains valid; use one
Cancel, verify dismissal and Quit. A transient pre-action AX error may be read
again only inside the fixed bounded read allowance before any uncertain input;
there is no input retry or target reselection.

Not recoverable under this method: sheet absent from the permitted hierarchy,
missing row/parent/Cancel, persistent AX timeout, changed incarnation, unknown
Cancel delivery without verified dismissal, or unexpected new live work at
Quit. These may be ordinary accessibility/presentation failures, **not** an
App crash; an untimed dialog can still require a person's Cancel. Report them
immediately with retained ownership and exact remaining deadline. Do not call
them catastrophic to excuse the gap.

Catastrophic conditions include a stalled AppKit loop, native abort or system
sleep. No normal UI-only method can promise fixed closure in those cases.
Observe actual process state without signals/cold-PID action; never infer exit
or supplier settlement. No speculative hard-closure guarantee is made.

## Residual selection required before first sheet

focus, enabled Cancel accessibility or delivery/dismissal timing. These are the
smallest missing native observations. The candidate cannot be fully

not open the sheet: a different reviewed shipped-path cancellation capability
is required first. No such capability or exception is authorized here.

After a successful Cancel witness, ordinary AX recovery has real supporting
evidence but is still not infallible. A failed witness returns concrete facts
and closure status, not another automatic preparation/launch cycle. Fresh
candidate/state admission, current notice gaps and identity remain explicit;
no result transfers automatically to the retained fdcf or release subject.
