This DOC_UPDATE defines the App adoption coordinator's contribution to carrying renewed guidance and technical contracts into identified consumers. ## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

The three anticipated artifacts are an adoption/source account, current applicability checks, and received technical-change/verification evidence. Sources: S1 and S2 `#d7`.

- **CLM-005** — Publication, selected/resolved source, actual instruction supply, provider adoption, observed behavior and a receiving consumer's adoption have different evidence. A portable file, source integration, registration or publication does not establish the other facts. The checked research pin `e548d4cfada4d2105de6231516dc6e5fc4bd4689` is historical and must be compared with relevant later changes before current implementation reliance. - **OUT-001** — Consumer/scope/source-identity/adoption-point account identifying the renewed basis, prior applicable basis, actual publication/supply/adoption evidence and its limits for each affected consumer. - **OUT-002** — Current-source applicability account for affected Root/Runtime/App/Piping consumers, instruction packaging, tool paths and implementation dependencies, with relevant later-mainline/consumer comparisons against pinned research and the checks needed before dependent reliance. ## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The App adoption coordinator starts from the identified DEL-10-03 responsibility account and relevant PKG-02/03/04/05 contracts, follows active consumer references and requests or receives owner evidence through the authorized coordination route. Identify the applicable old/new basis and unsettled choices before dependent supply or transition. Compare current source and receiving consequences at the point of reliance, then record actual notices, decisions, changes and checks with their source identities. Return unresolved consequences to their existing owners; report adoption status to DEL-11-01 and the relevant replacement-evidence preparation without altering those owners' records by implication. This describes future production; INIT authors only this contract.

- **VER-001** — Inspect OUT-001 against the affected-consumer inventory and DEP-006. For each claimed consumer/scope adoption, follow the named owner's decision/action evidence to its actual subject and adoption point; compare pending or partial rows with the claimed overall coverage. Confirm that App-only acceptance or prepared notices cannot supply the missing receiving evidence. Evidence: reviewed coverage/receipt references and specific absent or partial contributions. Verifies AC-001.
- **VER-002** — Examine the account with distinct supported and unsupported states: publication with no supply evidence; resolved content with no provider-adoption evidence; provider adoption with no behavior witness; and App adoption with no other consumer adoption. Also inspect a faithful record of an actually performed human adoption act, keeping its human actor distinct from the coordinator recorder and preserving any reported-custody limits. Reject fabricated decisions and any inference that independent checking requires an unrelated acceptance first. Clearly identify illustrative cases as such; no fabricated case is a real adoption witness. Evidence: source-bound distinction/custody review and case outcomes. Verifies AC-002.
- **VER-003** — Trace newly supplied instruction examples from the source-qualified origin, edition/revision and exact content identity through selected/resolved and actual supply evidence to the receiving adoption point. Revisit a prior basis or revised same-named source and confirm the historical identity survives; compare a run-only selection with any separately evidenced broader adoption. Evidence: identity/supply/history trace with missing evidence identified. Verifies AC-003.
- **VER-004** — Follow affected active-source references to actual consumers and their packaging/tool paths/dependencies. Compare the relied-on pinned account with relevant identified later mainline and consumer sources, inspect the affected owners' applicable checks and distinguish source/test definitions from performed checks or deployed behavior. Report comparison coverage and any currency gap before the related reliance. Evidence: current-source applicability comparison and referenced check results, including unaffected or unresolved consequences where warranted. Verifies AC-004.
- **VER-005** — Follow each affected technical promise from its source and responsibility allocation to the responsible SoW/derivative, received change and relevant candidate-bound verification evidence. Check that no relocated promise is silently dropped and no owner is replaced by the App coordinator; distinguish received proposals, owner dispositions, applied changes and actual checks. Evidence: reviewed promise/change/check trace and remaining obligations with owner and point of need. Verifies AC-005.
- **VER-006** — Review the account against CLM-002 through CLM-004, REQ-007 and TBD-001 through TBD-006. Check each excluded act against its cited owner, carry consequential departures to the actual owner, and verify that pending distribution/placement/transition choices remain pending rather than becoming invented architecture. Confirm that continuity/replacement consumers receive truthful status and that independent definition remains possible. Evidence: boundary and open-matter review with any conflict's exact source loci. Verifies AC-006.

These methods verify the accepted scope; they do not add thresholds, wire fields, a new distribution mechanism or supplier implementation. Where real supply, adoption or runtime behavior evidence is absent, record that limit and the owning point of need rather than claim a demonstration passed.
