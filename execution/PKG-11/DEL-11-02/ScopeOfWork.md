# Consumer-specific renewed-basis adoption

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable shows, for each consumer of the renewed guidance and technical contracts, whether that consumer has actually adopted them and what currently applies to it. It contributes one adoption account in three parts: for each renewal and consumer, the prior and the renewed basis, the source identity of what was supplied, and the adoption point or its absence; a check that the sources, packaging and tool paths a consumer relies on are current; and a trace of each affected technical promise through the responsible owner's scope to the change and the check that owner returned. Each receiving consumer's owner decides and performs that consumer's adoption. This deliverable coordinates, accounts and records, and adopts nothing on anyone's behalf.

It serves OBJ-009 (staged adoption and an owner-decided replacement while preserving historical content and continuing obligations). Its sources are recommendations 01 and 07 of the accepted decision brief; PRD §8 and V4-SHR-03; OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-11…14 and the closing paragraph of §5; the closing paragraph of ARCHITECTURE §5; and EXAMINATION V4-EXM-14 for the separation of resolved, supplied, provider-adopted and observed. No exit criterion of the draft 4.0.0 release target names this deliverable; its adoption status reaches DEL-11-03's decision packet directly and through DEL-11-01's continuity account.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The adoption account: for each renewal and each affected consumer, the responsible receiving owner, the prior and the renewed basis, the eight adoption facts with the evidence for each, and the adoption point or its unestablished state, together with the notices prepared, the decisions received and any consequential departure. Supports SOW-250 and SOW-254; OBJ-009.
- **OUT-002** — The current-source applicability account: the affected consumers of active shared material, their instruction packaging, tool paths and implementation dependencies, and the comparison of later mainline and consumer changes with the pinned research. Supports SOW-255 and SOW-257; OBJ-009.
- **OUT-003** — The received-change account: each affected technical promise traced from its source through the responsible owner's scope to the change and the check that owner returned, or to the stated gap. Its adoption status goes to DEL-11-01 and DEL-11-03. Supports SOW-256; OBJ-009.

Terms:

- **Renewal.** A change to shared guidance or to a technical contract that consumers may adopt: the renewed v4 basis as a whole, or one instruction or contract change.
- **Consumer.** A project or lane that works from the shared material. DEL-10-03's account names the affected ones; the decomposition names Root, Runtime, App and Piping.
- **Adoption facts.** Eight facts for one renewal and one consumer, each with its own evidence: a notice was prepared; it was delivered; the change was published; its content was selected and resolved; it was actually supplied to an agent; the provider adopted it; behaviour was observed; the consumer adopted it. Evidence for one establishes none of the others.
- **Fact state.** A fact is established only by evidence of its own kind. Every other fact reads as not established, and the account may say why (no evidence found, unknown, or not applicable). An unknown is never filled in by inference.
- **Adoption point.** The identified act, by the consumer's own owner, from which the renewed basis applies to that consumer.
- **Pinned research.** The source-checked account made at revision `e548d4cfada4d2105de6231516dc6e5fc4bd4689` of `sgttomas/chirality`. It is historical.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — The App adoption coordinator integrates this account. Each receiving consumer's owner decides and performs that consumer's adoption.
- **CLM-002** — `DEL-10-03` owns the allocation of shared responsibilities and technical promises, its account of the active consumers that a change of placement or supply affects, and the check of their packaging and tool paths against current source before such a change is relied on. The applicability account starts from that account and makes its own current-source checks of what each affected consumer relies on when it adopts a renewal (REQ-003), citing a `DEL-10-03` check where that check covers the same source at the same revision. The contracts whose adoption is accounted for belong to their own deliverables in PKG-02 (workflows and roles), PKG-03 (catalog and proposals), PKG-04 (operation policy, autonomy and records) and PKG-05 (host loop and panel), and host-specific implementation belongs to the host's own project. `DEL-02-04` owns the supply record of the role guidance composed for a conversation's start; `DEL-01-01` observes what is carried to the supplier, and `DEL-04-03` owns the record format and writer. `DEL-10-01` owns the project execution basis and the selected editions of the manual and of the shared roles and methods.
- **CLM-003** — The owners of the affected consumers keep their own scopes and checks, and supply their adoption, their packaging and tool-path consequences and the dispositions of their continuing obligations. SWBPIPE is built by its own project. The coordinator may prepare notices and record the evidence received; neither a prepared notice nor App's own acceptance is another consumer's adoption.
- **CLM-004** — `DEL-11-01` carries preserved history, current selectors and continuing-obligation dispositions. `DEL-11-03` prepares the replacement evidence and the owner decides the replacement. The owner with the affected consumers decides each staged adoption and retirement.
- **CLM-005** — Publication, a resolved source, instructions actually supplied, provider adoption, observed behaviour and a consumer's adoption are different facts with different evidence. A portable file, a source merge, a registration or a publication establishes none of the others.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The adoption account identifies each affected consumer and adoption scope, the responsible receiving owner, the prior and the proposed or current basis, and the adoption point or its unresolved state. It shows each of the eight adoption facts in the state its evidence supports; a state without evidence is not established.
- **REQ-002** — Newly supplied instructions stay traceable to their source-qualified origin, edition or revision and content identity, the project entry or method they apply to, the receiving scope and the adoption point. Identities supplied earlier and their history are kept, and an old selection is never rebound to current bytes. An edition selected or read for a run is evidence of that selection or reading only.
- **REQ-003** — The applicability account identifies the affected active shared material and its actual consumers, instruction packaging, tool paths and implementation dependencies, and records the scope, source revisions and results of the current-source checks made. Before a consumer relies on the pinned research as a current implementation map, the relevant later mainline and consumer changes are compared with it and their effect on the relied-on claim is stated. Currency that was not checked is shown as unchecked, and source inspection establishes neither a running deployment nor observed behaviour.
- **REQ-004** — The received-change account traces each affected technical change from DEL-10-03's responsibility account and the contracts named in CLM-002 into the responsible owner's scope or its derivative, keeping the promise, the receiving scope, the actual owner, the affected check and the evidence returned or the gap. A prepared handoff, an accepted change, an applied change and a performed check each keep their own subject and evidence, and evidence of one discharges none of the others.
- **REQ-005** — Adoption records keep who actually decided or acted, the subject and scope, the evidence for the act, and who recorded or relayed it. A human adoption decision that was actually made is recorded with the human as actor and the coordinator as recorder; a draft notice, a successful check or the act of recording makes no decision. No act is required before another independently evidenced act.
- **REQ-006** — Each undecided matter that an adoption depends on is carried with its owner and point of need (TBD-002, TBD-005). A consumer's missing adoption or missing check stays visible to the coordinator and to that consumer's owner before the consumer transitions or relies on the renewed basis.
- **REQ-007** — This deliverable performs no act owned by another, as allocated in CLM-002, CLM-003 and CLM-004: it allocates no shared responsibility, defines no contract, adopts or changes nothing in another consumer, builds nothing in SWBPIPE, produces no preservation or replacement evidence, and decides no replacement or retirement. Its own acts are coordination, accounting for sources and applicability, and faithful recording of what was received.

- **AC-001** — The adoption account covers every affected consumer and scope with its responsible owner, prior and renewed basis, and adoption point or unestablished state, each with its evidence. A consumer's adoption is established only by evidence of an adoption decision or act by that consumer's owner, with its subject and scope: either a record the consumer's owner keeps, or that owner's decision faithfully recorded by the coordinator with the owner as actor (REQ-005). A prepared or delivered notice, the wording of a notice written by its sender, the owner's approval of the change, or App's own adoption establishes no other consumer's adoption. Verify by VER-001. [REQ-001; CLM-001; CLM-003]
- **AC-002** — Each of the eight adoption facts is shown as established only on evidence of that fact's own kind, and otherwise as not established. Publication without supply, resolved content without provider adoption, provider adoption without observed behaviour, and App's adoption without another consumer's each read as exactly that. A human decision that was actually made is recorded with the person as actor and the coordinator as recorder, and no decision appears that only a publication, a registration, a check or a notice could have supplied. Verify by VER-002. [REQ-001; REQ-005; CLM-005]
- **AC-003** — Each newly supplied instruction traces to its source-qualified origin, its edition or revision and content identity, the receiving scope and the adoption point. The identities supplied earlier remain retrievable, and an edition selected or read for one run is not shown as wider adoption. Verify by VER-003. [REQ-002; CLM-005]
- **AC-004** — The applicability account names the affected consumers of active shared material with their packaging, tool paths and implementation dependencies, and the current-source checks made with their revisions and results. Where a consumer relies on the pinned research as a current implementation map, a comparison of the later mainline and consumer changes with that claim precedes the reliance; where none does, the account says so. Unchecked or unsupported claims are shown as such. Verify by VER-004. [REQ-003; CLM-003; CLM-005]
- **AC-005** — Each affected technical promise keeps its source, the responsible owner's scope or derivative, the receiving owner, and the change and check that owner actually returned or the specific gap. A notice, an acceptance or a source inspection does not appear as an applied or verified change, and no promise drops out of the trace. Verify by VER-005. [REQ-004; CLM-002; CLM-003]
- **AC-006** — The account keeps each undecided matter with its owner and point of need and each consumer's own responsibility, and the status it gives DEL-11-01 and DEL-11-03 equals what the account itself shows. It selects no order of precedence among instruction sources, no mandatory form for a project's entry file, no shared implementation and no retirement, and it contains no act that REQ-007 excludes. Verify by VER-006. [REQ-006; REQ-007; CLM-002; CLM-003; CLM-004]

One consumer's adoption may be established while another's is not. The other consumer is named as not established and does not become adopted because the account is otherwise complete.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The outputs are linked views of the records the sources and the consumers already keep; this deliverable introduces no register, transport, distribution mechanism or storage format. Each fact is read from the repository that holds it, at an identified revision. The coordinator starts from DEL-10-03's consumer and promise account, follows each consumer's references, and records notices, decisions, changes and checks with their source identities; current sources are compared at the point of reliance. A consumer's later record, such as an adoption, a declined adoption or a revised scope, revises the account, and an unresolved consequence goes back to its owner. Where the account is kept is undecided (owner).

- **VER-001** — Inspect the adoption account against DEL-10-03's consumer list and the affected consumers' own records. For each claimed adoption follow the named owner's decision to its subject and adoption point, in the record that holds that owner's decision, and compare rows that are pending or partial with the coverage claimed. Include a notice delivered without a decision, a sender's wording offered as the receiver's act, and the owner's approval of the change offered as a consumer's adoption; none may establish adoption.
- **VER-002** — Examine the account with supported and unsupported states side by side: publication with no supply evidence; resolved content with no provider adoption; provider adoption with no observed behaviour; App's adoption with no other consumer's. Inspect one human adoption decision recorded by the coordinator and check that the person is the actor. Check that no check is refused for lack of an unrelated acceptance. Invented cases are labelled as invented and are never an adoption witness.
- **VER-003** — Trace supplied instructions from their source-qualified origin, edition or revision and content identity, through what was selected, resolved and supplied, to the receiving adoption point, computing the content identity from the source at its named revision. Revisit an earlier basis and a same-named source that was later revised and confirm the earlier identity is still retrievable; compare a selection made for one run with any separately evidenced wider adoption.
- **VER-004** — Follow the references to active shared material to its actual consumers and their packaging, tool paths and dependencies. Compare the relied-on part of the pinned research with the later mainline and consumer sources, read the affected owners' own check results, and tell a defined test apart from a performed check and from deployed behaviour. State the coverage of the comparison and any currency gap.
- **VER-005** — Follow each affected technical promise from its source and its allocation in DEL-10-03's account to the responsible owner's scope, the change received and the check evidence for it. Check that no relocated promise is dropped and no owner is replaced by the coordinator, and that a proposal, an owner's disposition, an applied change and a performed check are each shown as what they are.
- **VER-006** — Review the account against CLM-002, CLM-003 and CLM-004, REQ-007 and the undecided matters below. Check each excluded act against its owner, that a consequential departure is carried to the owner who decides it, that an undecided distribution, placement or transition is still shown as undecided, and that the status given to DEL-11-01 and DEL-11-03 matches the account.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Adoption belongs to the consumer that adopts. Better means more affected consumers whose adoption state rests on their own recorded decision; never by recording an adoption the consumer did not make, or by reading a publication or a notice as one.
- **AX-002** — Human direction governs, the accepted basis states v4's scoped commitments, manuals and selected methods organise the work, and a library location gives no precedence. The earlier Root-first order and the thin-loop-file default are not requirements, and nothing here puts a new order in their place or changes another consumer's instructions. Better means fewer supplied instructions whose source identity or adoption point cannot be traced; never by rebinding an earlier selection to current bytes or by rewriting history to look uniform.
- **AX-003** — Evidence is kept in proportion to the decision or reliance it serves, using the source and act records that exist. Enforcement by a host is a different fact from what an instruction says, and holding a record does not authenticate it. Better means less new record-keeping for the same adoption facts shown; never by dropping a fact's own evidence.
- **TBD-002** — How changed shared instructions are distributed to and adopted by projects other than the App is undecided (owner with the shared and project instruction owners; needed before instruction changes or dependent supply to those consumers). Manual-led practice is settled, and so is the arrangement for the App itself.
- **TBD-005** — First adopters of the renewed basis, continuing obligations and the conditions for ending coexistence are undecided (owner with the affected consumers; needed before each adoption or retirement decision). Until then AC-001 shows each consumer's adoption of the renewed basis with that owner and point of need, and a replacement of the fallback decides none of it.
