# Owner replacement evidence packet

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable puts before the owner the concrete evidence for deciding whether an identified v4 candidate replaces the v3.0.1 fallback, and records what the owner then decides. It contributes two things: a comparison showing, element by element, whether the candidate supports the core loop at least at v3.0.1's level; and the decision package with the record of the owner's actual disposition. It produces no qualification of its own and makes no decision.

It serves OBJ-009 (staged adoption and an owner-decided replacement while preserving historical content and continuing obligations). Its sources are PRD §8 (V4-REP-01 and the paragraphs after it), V4-AUT-03, V4-AUT-05, OQ-08 and OQ-12; EXAMINATION §§1–2, V4-EXM-10, V4-EXM-11, §6 and §7; and HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-32. The release list of the current execution basis and the publication punchlist also put the owner's publication decision in this packet, separately from replacement, with the exact final candidate, the joined results, the limits and the continuing obligations, which AC-007 states.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The core-loop comparison for the identified candidate: for each of the seven core-loop elements, the V4-EXM-10 and V4-EXM-11 results from the DEL-09-02 dossier that bear on it, with their evidence and limits, and the element's standing. Supports SOW-111; OBJ-009.
- **OUT-003** — The decision package and the disposition record. The package presents the replacement condition with its standing, the candidate and the exact evidence, the proposed scope of replacement, the practitioner's observations, the gaps, the continuity account and what the decision leaves undecided. For the 4.0.0 decisions the package also puts the publication decision, separately from replacement, with the exact final candidate, each result the publication decision rests on on that candidate, the release's limitations and the continuing obligations (REQ-007). The disposition records the owner's actual response. Supports SOW-113, using the evidence for SOW-111; OBJ-009.

Terms:

- **Fallback.** Chirality v3.0.1, the published product that stays in place until the owner decides v4 has replaced it, identified in `reference/REFERENCES.md`.
- **Candidate.** One identified build of the App, identified as the DEL-09-02 dossier identifies it. A result observed on another candidate is joined only when an assessment of the scenarios a change reopens covers the difference (EXAMINATION V4-EXM-03).
- **Core-loop elements.** Seven: plan, execute, save a workflow, reuse it, approvals, interruption, restart.
- **At least at v3.0.1's level.** V4-EXM-10 and V4-EXM-11 passed on the candidate. v3.0.1 is named as the baseline and is not measured again.
- **Element standing.** Met when every step that bears on the element passed on the candidate; not met when one failed; otherwise not evidenced. A blocked, not-run or inconclusive step is missing evidence, neither a pass nor a failure.
- **The replacement condition.** The core loop at v3.0.1's level. V4-REP-01 allows replacement when it holds.
- **Decision package.** What is put to the owner. For the replacement decision: the decision subject (the candidate and the exact evidence), the proposed scope of replacement, the replacement condition's standing, the gaps with their owners, the continuing obligations, and what the decision does not decide. For the 4.0.0 decisions the same package also puts the publication decision, as a separate decision: the exact final candidate, each result the publication decision rests on on that candidate, the release's limitations and the continuing obligations. The results that decision rests on are those the release list of the execution basis and the publication punchlist name, until the owner accepts a release target whose exit criteria name them. A live SWBPIPE journey is SWBPIPE's requirement and bears on neither decision: App v4 is released on working through the interface it ships with.
- **Disposition.** The record of the owner's response to one package: not presented; presented without a decision; or decided, naming what the owner chose. Silence, elapsed time, a tool receipt or an agent's statement moves it nowhere. It answers the package it was given: once the candidate or the evidence changes, the package is a new one and the earlier disposition does not carry over.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — The App replacement-evidence coordinator assembles the packet. The owner alone decides the replacement; a recorder preserves that act and its subject without making it. v3.0.1 remains the fallback until that act.
- **CLM-002** — `DEL-09-02` owns standalone qualification and its dossier. A prepared file or a component pass is neither a delivery nor qualified behaviour on the candidate.
- **CLM-003** — `DEL-11-01` owns the continuity account, `DEL-11-02` the adoption account, `DEL-09-12` the owner's validation in use, and `DEL-01-06` the package, its identity record, the terms position and the stated limitations. The owner with the affected consumers decides each adoption and retirement; the owner decides public release; the accountable professional keeps professional approval and reliance. None of these follows from a replacement, a merge or a successful operation.
- **CLM-004** — `DEL-04-01` carries the operation policy: five acts are reserved to a person (marking checked, accepting a proposal where autonomy requires it, engineering approval, professional reliance, and changing the grant or enabling external access).

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The comparison shows, for the identified candidate against the named v3.0.1 baseline, the applicable V4-EXM-10 and V4-EXM-11 results, accounting separately for each of the seven core-loop elements with evidence locators, observations and limits. A general claim of functionality stands for no missing element.
- **REQ-003** — Every result in the packet names its candidate, its configuration (harness and model versions, model server), its date, its source and the observed outcome, and keeps the outcome as passed, failed, blocked, not run or inconclusive. A pass on an earlier candidate needs an assessment of the affected scenarios when the candidate changes. A source inspection, an accepted contract, a provider's acceptance, a component pass, a definition or a Git merge is never shown as live qualification or as replacement. Practitioner validation and its limits are kept apart from agent-operated verification, and the packet supplies no validation period and no favourable outcome.
- **REQ-004** — The package puts the replacement condition and its standing before the owner, identifies the exact candidate and the proposed scope of replacement, and links the supplied evidence and the continuing obligations. A disposition is recorded as decided only when an attributable owner act exists; it keeps the actor, the exact subject, the response, its source and custody limits, the recorder apart from the decision-maker, and the time of recording apart from a time of the act that is not available. A pending, deferred or negative response is recorded as such. A favourable result is never a condition of completion, and without the owner's act the decision stays pending and the fallback stays available.
- **REQ-005** — The package carries the current continuity account, the remaining claims and each undecided matter with its owner and point of need. Replacement retires no old project, archive, active work or continuing obligation and asserts no consumer's adoption. Domains and PEC are no condition of the replacement, and their availability establishes no readiness, release or adoption. Practitioner observations are carried with their limits and make no additional condition.
- **REQ-006** — This deliverable performs no act owned by another, as allocated in CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-003 and CLM-004: it runs and assembles no qualification, produces no continuity, adoption or validation account, chooses no operation policy, performs no person's acceptance, and decides no replacement, adoption, retirement, public release or professional reliance. It records the evidence received and the decisions actually made.
- **REQ-007** — For the 4.0.0 decisions the packet identifies the exact final candidate and gives, for each result the publication decision rests on, the owning deliverable's result on that candidate, together with the release's limitations and the continuing obligations. Unrun, failed, blocked and limited results are shown as such. Publication and replacement are put to the owner as two separate decisions, and professional reliance as neither.

- **AC-001** — The comparison names the v3.0.1 baseline and the candidate and shows each of the seven elements with the V4-EXM-10 and V4-EXM-11 steps that bear on it, their outcomes, evidence and limits, and the element's standing. The replacement condition reads as met only when all seven elements are met; a blocked, not-run or inconclusive step reads as missing evidence, never as a failure and never as a pass. Verify by VER-001. [REQ-001; CLM-002]
- **AC-003** — Every result in the packet carries its candidate, configuration, date and outcome. A result from another candidate is joined only with an assessment naming the scenarios a change reopened. A historical pass, a definition, a merge or a source inspection appears only as the limited claim it supports, with the remaining scope stated, and practitioner validation is shown with its own standing apart from verification. Verify by VER-003. [REQ-003; CLM-002; CLM-003]
- **AC-004** — The package identifies the exact decision subject and the proposed scope and states, for the replacement condition, what is and is not established. The disposition records a received owner response with the owner as actor, the recorder separate, the response's own words, its source and its custody limits. An absent, deferred or negative response is recorded as such with v3.0.1 retained, and no favourable decision is needed to meet this criterion. A disposition answers only the package it was given. Verify by VER-004. [REQ-004; CLM-001]
- **AC-005** — The package links the current continuity account and its continuing obligations, shows replacement, adoption, retirement, public release and professional reliance as separate matters of which the packet establishes none, carries each undecided matter with its owner and point of need, and names neither Domains, PEC nor any validation period as a condition of replacement. Verify by VER-005. [REQ-005; CLM-003]
- **AC-006** — Every item in the packet is the coordinator's comparison, evidence link or decision record. Each act excluded by REQ-006 stays with its owner and appears only as that owner's evidence shows it. Verify by VER-006. [REQ-006; CLM-001; CLM-002; CLM-003; CLM-004]
- **AC-007** — The packet put to the owner for the 4.0.0 decisions names the exact final candidate as its package identity record identifies it and shows, for each result the publication decision rests on, the owning deliverable's result on that candidate with its standing, the release's limitations and the continuing obligations. Each unrun, failed, blocked or limited result is shown as such, and publication and replacement appear as two separate decisions. Verify by VER-007. [REQ-007; CLM-003]

A packet with gaps is a finished account of its candidate and may be put to the owner. It states that the condition for replacement holds only when the core loop at v3.0.1's level is established on the candidate.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The coordinator receives the dossiers and accounts, establishes which candidate each concerns, follows their evidence links, and prepares the comparison and the package from them. Nothing in the packet is filled in from a definition. Each supplied item is named with its source and content identity, so that a later change in a supplier's record makes a new packet. A gap goes back to the owner responsible for it and stays visible. The coordinator puts the package to the owner, records the owner's response as given, and returns the disposition and the continuing obligations to DEL-11-01 and the affected consumers. Where the packet is kept is undecided (owner).

- **VER-001** — Inspect the comparison against the DEL-09-02 dossier and the named baseline. Trace each element to the candidate's V4-EXM-10 and V4-EXM-11 step results, including reviewed registration and reuse, the granted and the denied request, and the interruption and restart. Withdraw the evidence for one element, and put a blocked step in place of a passed one, and confirm that the condition no longer reads as met and the blocked step does not read as failed.
- **VER-003** — Reconcile candidate, configuration, date and source identities across the comparison and the package, and inspect any candidate change for the scenarios it reopens. Present a historical pass, a definition, a Git merge and a source inspection as wider fulfilment and confirm each is reduced to the claim it supports. Inspect the practitioner evidence separately, including the case where no validation has been agreed.
- **VER-004** — Compare the disposition with the owner's actual response and with the exact package presented. Include a decision inferred from a tool receipt, from silence, and from an approval of something else; a response that names no choice; and a package changed after it was presented. None may read as a decision on the current package, and a missing, deferred or negative response does not retire the fallback. No act is asked of the owner merely to run this check.
- **VER-005** — Compare the packet's continuity and undecided-matter statements with DEL-11-01's account and with the open-issue rows for their owners and points of need. Check that the packet asserts no retirement, release or reliance, no Domains or PEC readiness or condition, and no validation period the owner did not agree.
- **VER-006** — Review each act excluded by REQ-006 against CLM-001, CLM-002, CLM-003 and CLM-004, including the difference between a record of an act and the person performing it.
- **VER-007** — Comparison with named references (each owning deliverable's own record of its result on the final candidate, DEL-01-06's package identity record and limitations list, DEL-11-01's account): for each result the publication decision rests on, compare what the packet shows with the owning deliverable's own record of its result on the final candidate; compare the candidate's identity with DEL-01-06's package identity record, the limitations with DEL-01-06's list, and the continuing obligations with DEL-11-01's account. Check that every unrun, failed, blocked or limited result appears as such and that publication and replacement are put as separate decisions.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — The replacement condition is shown as it stands. A packet may report partial or adverse evidence truthfully; it states that the condition for replacement holds only when it is established on the candidate, and even then the owner's act is separate. Better means fewer elements without candidate-bound evidence; never by counting a component pass, an earlier candidate's result or a definition as that evidence.
- **AX-002** — Source identity, the actual actor and the scope of each act are preserved; execution, checking, acceptance of a proposal, approval and professional reliance each mean only what they are. Better means the owner can decide from the packet with less reading outside it; never by leaving out a gap, a limit or an adverse result.
- **AX-003** — The packet is evidence for a decision and never the decision, and an honest negative or deferred answer completes its work as fully as a favourable one. Better means a disposition tied more exactly to the candidate and the evidence presented; never by presuming, prompting or recording a decision the owner did not make.
- **TBD-001** — The period and the activities of validation in use are undecided (owner; needed before validation in use). The validation account reaches this packet through DEL-09-12 and is shown with its standing.
- **TBD-003** — First adopters, continuing obligations and the conditions for ending coexistence are undecided (owner with the affected consumers; needed before each adoption or retirement decision). A replacement settles none of them.
- **TBD-004** — The 4.0.0 release target is a draft. Whether the core loop at v3.0.1's level (its criterion 11) gates publication or only replacement is undecided (owner); AC-001 states the result either way. The publication decision's place in this packet comes from the release list of the execution basis and the publication punchlist; no decomposition objective covers it (OBJ-009 names replacement only). AC-007 takes the results that decision rests on from the release list and the punchlist; the packet does not wait for a release target to be accepted.
- **TBD-005** — What replacing the fallback changes once 4.0.0 is published is stated in neither the PRD nor the release target (owner). The package states the scope it proposes, and AC-004 holds the disposition to that scope.
