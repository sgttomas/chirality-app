The source hierarchy is the accepted normalized rows read with the original seed, accepted HTML decisions and later owner directions, then the consolidated clauses. A source's existence or this contract's initialization is not a completed replacement witness. [S1–S6, S10]

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The replacement-evidence coordinator receives identified source dossiers, compares their candidates and applicable scope, follows the actual evidence links, then prepares the two obligation accounts and a concrete decision package. Gaps remain visible and return to the responsible owner; independent definition can continue while external live evidence is absent. The coordinator incorporates a later owner's response faithfully and returns the disposition and continuing obligations to the App continuity owner and affected consumers. Human-relayed files preserve the external SWB route; this method creates no direct-message or host-implementation authority. [S1, S3, S5–S8]

- **VER-001** — Inspect the core-loop comparison against the App qualification dossier and named v3.0.1 reference. Trace each REQ-001 element to the relevant candidate's V4-EXM-10/11 observations, including reviewed workflow registration/reuse, grant/deny and interruption/restart outcomes. Reject a conclusion of at-least-v3.0.1 coverage when any required element lacks applicable evidence. [S1, S2, S4, S5]
- **VER-002** — Inspect the live local host dossier and trace the original request/basis to proposed/reviewed content, the actual person's acceptance and recorded outcomes/receipts on the named candidate. Check local model/server identity and joined execution evidence. Check that source-only traces, independent component passes, stale/unknown outcomes or operation receipts without a human act cannot pass the required journey. Faithful recording of an actually evidenced person's acceptance is valid when actor and recorder are distinguished; it does not imply approval or reliance. [S1–S6]
- **VER-003** — Reconcile candidate/configuration/date and source identities across the two accounts and the decision package. Inspect any candidate changes for affected-evidence applicability. Examine negative cases consisting of a historical pass, initialized contract, Git merge, source inspection and isolated first-host success presented as broader fulfillment; require the supported limited claim and explicit remaining scope. Inspect practitioner evidence separately and retain absent/unagreed validation standing. These are packet checks, not new product scenarios or new scope. [S3–S5, S7, S10]
- **VER-004** — Compare the decision record directly with the attributable owner's response and the exact presented candidate/evidence subject. A positive faithful-recording case must preserve an actually performed act with the human as decision-maker and coordinator/host as recorder; reject fabricated acceptance from a tool receipt, favorable result inferred from silence, or substitution of a decomposition/setup/Git approval. Check a missing, deferred or negative response remains accurately labeled and does not retire the fallback. No new human act is requested merely to run this check. [S1, S3–S5]
- **VER-005** — Compare the packet's continuity and unresolved-input statements with the received App continuity account, accepted/current open-issue rows and external boundaries. Verify the exact OI-001/016/021/024 owners and points of need, current selectors and obligations, no automatic all-project retirement/release/reliance, and no PEC/Domains readiness assertion or initial gate. Confirm any validation period comes from actual agreement. [S1, S3–S8]
- **VER-006** — Review each excluded act in REQ-006 against CLM-001/002/003/004 and their source owners, including the difference between a host recording an act and the person performing it. Inspect the packet for delegated policy choices, unauthorized host/qualification production, professional claims or unsupported delivery/adoption assertions; return those to their actual owners while preserving valid local comparison and recording work. [S1, S3–S8]

- **AX-001** — The two replacement obligations are preserved independently. Setup/decomposition acceptance, contract validation and ordinary Git integration grant no replacement, product release or professional reliance. [S1–S5, S10]
- **AX-002** — Preserve source identity, actual actor and the scope of each observed act. Evidence for execution, checking, proposal acceptance, approval and professional reliance carries its own meaning. The settled prohibition is false attribution; the unresolved global reserved-act policy is not filled by a new blanket list. [S3, S4, S6, S7]

This claim further specifies the operation-policy owner used in REQ-006. [S7, S8]
- **TBD-001** — OI-016 remains with the Owner before practitioner validation in use: agree the period and owner-selected activities. App DEL-09-12 supplies that actual validation account; this packet consumes its standing without inventing dates, scores or a new replacement condition. [S1, S5, S7, S8]
- **TBD-002** — OI-021 remains with the Owner via outside SWB session and App/shared owner before connected-activity SoW and execution: choose useful operation, permitted autonomy and exact candidate environment. DEP-001 is required before the corresponding connected witness and fallback-replacement decision. The supplied current observation reports SWBPIPE building before agent-action integration; it supplies no joined witness. [S3, S6–S8]
- **TBD-003** — OI-024 remains with the Owner with affected consumers before each adoption/retirement decision: identify first adopters, continuing obligations and end-of-coexistence conditions. DEP-006 requires actual receiving adoption and obligation disposition at the consumer transition. Replacement does not itself settle these matters. [S3, S4, S7, S8]
- **AX-003** — Selected source editions and this run's basis do not constitute broader project adoption. Complete history, useful work and the fallback remain governed by their actual continuity and owner acts. Technical realization stays open where the accepted source is open; no API, provider delivery, validation duration or favorable disposition is created by this contract. [S1, S3–S10]
