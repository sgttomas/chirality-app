# Preserved history and coexistence account

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable keeps what came before v4 recoverable and findable while the old and new arrangements coexist, and shows what still has to be settled before any old arrangement is retired. It contributes one account in three parts: a preservation inventory that gives each preserved item its standing, its current selector and a route to recover it; identity checks showing that preserved material is still what its source record says it is; and the disposition of each continuing obligation by the owner accountable for it. It retires, migrates, freezes and deletes nothing.

It serves OBJ-009 (staged adoption and an owner-decided replacement while preserving historical content and continuing obligations). Its sources are PRD §6 (no import of v3 chats is selected), §8 (the old projects and archives are kept until the owner decides the replacement; staged coexistence preserves current selectors, source identity, active work and continuing obligations), §9 OQ-12 and §11 (the thesis); OPERATING_METHOD V4-OPS-11, V4-OPS-14, V4-OPS-31, V4-OPS-32 and the closing paragraph of §5; the closing paragraph of ARCHITECTURE §5; and recommendation 07 of the accepted decision brief.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The preservation inventory with current selectors: for each preserved class in the coexistence scope, the records that hold it, its standing, its current selector, its recovery route, and what was not examined. Supports SOW-114, SOW-251, SOW-252 and SOW-253; OBJ-009.
- **OUT-002** — The identity checks of the preserved material named in OUT-001 and their results, each with the source and the content compared, the coverage and the gaps. Supports SOW-253; OBJ-009.
- **OUT-003** — The continuing-obligation account: continuing users, active work, archives and pending obligations in the coexistence scope; each obligation's disposition by its accountable owner, or its open owner and point of need; the owner's acts that bear on preservation, as recorded; and the replacement standing. It is the continuity input to DEL-11-03's packet. Supports SOW-114, SOW-253 and SOW-258; OBJ-009.

Terms:

- **Coexistence scope.** The arrangements that continue beside v4: the published v3.0.1 fallback, the earlier projects and their active work, the Git-ignored archives, and the consumers of shared material that DEL-10-03's account names (the decomposition names Root, Runtime, App and Piping).
- **Preserved classes.** The v3.0.1 fallback; old projects and archives; useful source and reviewed methods; actual work; pending obligations; evidence; and the thesis.
- **Linked view.** The account links the records that already hold preserved material (the reference and archive records, Git history and tags, owner-decision records) by their identity, and adds only what none of them carries: standing, current selector, recovery route, owner and point of need. It copies no preserved material and repeats no list those records already hold.
- **Standing.** What a preserved item is at present: the current fallback, work still active in its own lane, or preserved history. No standing makes an item a v4 requirement, and an active lane keeps its own rules at its own scope.
- **Current selector.** The place a reader goes to find what applies now to a named scope or consumer, kept apart from historical pins and superseded material.
- **Identity check.** A comparison of preserved content with the identity its source record gives it (a Git object, a published release digest, a recorded archive digest), reporting that it matches, has changed, is missing or was not checked.
- **Disposition.** For one continuing obligation, what its accountable owner decided will happen to it, with the act and the evidence that show it. An empty record is no disposition.
- **Replacement standing.** Whether the owner has decided that v4 replaces the v3.0.1 fallback. Until DEL-11-03 returns the owner's decision it reads pending, with v3.0.1 retained.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — History is evidence, not current commitment. Historical defaults, unselected alternatives and superseded material create no v4 requirement by being preserved, and this account sets no order of precedence among sources.
- **CLM-002** — The App continuity owner integrates this account with the affected project owners. It reads the applicability and consumer account of DEL-10-03 and the adoption status of DEL-11-02, and supplies the continuing obligations to DEL-11-03.
- **CLM-003** — `DEL-10-03` owns the account of historical applicability, active consumers, packaging and tool paths, and any reallocation of shared commitments. `DEL-10-01` owns the project execution basis and the selected editions of the manual and of the shared roles and methods, with the owner deciding consequential adoption or departure.
- **CLM-004** — `DEL-11-02` coordinates adoption, and each receiving consumer's owner decides and performs it. A published instruction, a prepared notice, an edition selected for a run or a relocated file is no evidence of wider adoption or of instructions actually supplied to an agent.
- **CLM-005** — `DEL-11-03` prepares the replacement evidence and the owner decides the replacement. This account supplies the continuing obligations and repeats none of that qualification.
- **CLM-006** — The owner with the affected consumers decides each adoption and retirement, the end of coexistence and any migration of v3 chats. Professional reliance stays with the accountable person. A replacement of the fallback retires no other project.
- **CLM-007** — The thesis is maintained in the Chirality Library at `docs/thesis/` (`sgttomas/chirality-library`, selected edition `library-v3.3.0`). App may export or package it from there and holds no maintained copy; the copy App formerly held remains in this repository's Git history, identified in PRD §11. The thesis keeps its attribution and its nonbinding standing: citing its purpose makes none of its historical implementation choices a v4 requirement. This deliverable compares no thesis bytes.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The v3.0.1 fallback, the old projects and the archives stay available until the owner decides the replacement, and useful reviewed methods, actual work, pending obligations and evidence are preserved with their standing. Existing rules continue at the scope they actually apply to: preservation neither imposes them on v4 nor erases them from another active lane, and no new v4 file is treated as the retirement of anything.
- **REQ-002** — Current selectors distinguish, for each identified scope or consumer, the source that applies now from historical and superseded material, traced to the applicability and adoption evidence supplied, with its gaps. An edition selected for one run is kept apart from adoption by another consumer and from instructions actually supplied to an agent.
- **REQ-003** — Preserved material named in the inventory is checked against the identity its source record gives it, with the compared set, the coverage and the gaps stated. A changed or missing item is reported, never normalised. The Git-ignored archives are read only: a check changes nothing in them and the account repairs nothing in them.
- **REQ-004** — Each continuing obligation relevant to an intended retirement has a clear, evidenced disposition from its accountable owner before that arrangement retires. Unresolved obligations, continuing users and work, and unavailable evidence stay visible; neither the fallback replacement nor an empty record closes an obligation.
- **REQ-005** — This deliverable performs no act owned by another, as allocated in CLM-003, CLM-004, CLM-005 and CLM-006: it selects or departs from no manual or method edition, reallocates no shared commitment, performs no consumer's adoption, prepares no replacement qualification, and decides no replacement, migration, retirement or professional reliance. Preservation authorises no deletion, no import of v3 chats, no freezing of another active lane and no instruction edit. Source history and recovery stay available while any later migration choice is made.
- **REQ-006** — The account keeps historical content, supplied current applicability, observed preservation checks and actual human decisions apart. An adoption, a retirement or the replacement that was actually performed is recorded faithfully with its actor, subject, scope, source and custody limits, the recorder distinct from the actor. A proposed, absent or inferred act is not recorded as performed, and no act is inferred from another or placed in a sequence the sources do not state.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The account is a linked view assembled at one identified revision of each repository it reads, so that its links resolve to fixed content; only the archive check depends on a checkout that holds the Git-ignored archives. The coexistence scope is identified before its content is checked. Existing records are reused by reference, investigation widens only where a stated gap affects a specific preservation or retirement claim, and an input that was not received is shown as not received. The account is revised when a lane moves, an obligation is supplied, adoption status changes or the owner's replacement disposition is returned. Where the account is kept is undecided (owner).

- **VER-001** — Compare the inventory with the preserved classes of REQ-001 and the "what to carry" list of the decision brief's recommendation 07. Follow each recovery route to the material and read its standing; record what was examined and what was not; check that each active lane is still shown at its own scope and none as replaced.
- **VER-002** — Starting from the inventory, follow the current selector of each listed scope or consumer to its source and compare it with DEL-10-03's consumer and applicability account and DEL-11-02's adoption status. Include a historical pin and a consumer whose adoption is absent or uncertain, and check that neither reads as current authority because a file is present or an edition was selected for a run.
- **VER-004** — For each named item, read or recompute the content's identity and compare it with the identity in its source record: Git objects and tags by their ids, the fallback against the published release's own digest, the archives with the archive digest tool in a checkout that holds them. Apply a changed item and a missing item to a copy of the account's inputs and confirm each is reported, without altering preserved content. Confirm that results carried from an earlier revision are labelled as such.
- **VER-005** — Trace each obligation to the consumer record it comes from and to its accountable owner. For each intended retirement inspect the dispositions and the evidence of the deciding act. Include a lane whose obligations have not been supplied and check that it appears with its owner and point of need although no retirement is intended. Include an obligation with no disposition, an empty disposition, and a decided fallback replacement with nothing else; none may show an arrangement as eligible to retire.
- **VER-006** — Compare each recorded owner act with its source record: actor, subject, scope, text and custody. Include an act whose text is not in its record, an altered text, a proposed act, and an act that the account's recorder attributes to itself as the deciding actor; none may read as performed. Check that replacement, adoption, execution, checking, approval and professional reliance are neither merged nor ordered into a sequence the sources do not state. Use existing records or labelled invented cases, never a fabricated live act.
- **VER-007** — Review each act excluded by REQ-005 against CLM-003, CLM-004, CLM-005 and CLM-006 and the accepted decomposition's allocation, and check that the account only refers to the contributions owned elsewhere.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — History is preserved as evidence with its original identity, attribution and standing. A frozen header, a later ruling, an applied change and a lifecycle label are different facts, and current navigation may clarify them without rewriting them to look uniform. Better means more preserved items whose recovery route reaches them and whose identity has been checked; never by altering, normalising or relocating preserved content so that it checks.
- **AX-002** — Staged coexistence is the accepted approach; neither a single cutover nor indefinite duplication is chosen in its place, and existing rules keep the scope they had. Better means fewer continuing obligations without a disposition from their accountable owner; never by reading silence, an empty record, new files or the fallback replacement as a disposition.
- **AX-003** — A usable route and focused evidence serve recovery and decisions. Better means a shorter path from a reader's question about what applies now to the source that answers it; never by copying preserved material into a second inventory, opening a whole-corpus audit, or stating coverage that was not examined. An unexamined area is a stated limit: neither a failure of preservation nor closure.
- **TBD-001** — First adopters, continuing obligations and the conditions for ending coexistence are undecided (owner with the affected consumers; needed before each adoption or retirement decision), and so is any migration of v3 chats (the same owner; before any migration or retirement act). Until they are decided, AC-005 holds each affected obligation open with that owner.
