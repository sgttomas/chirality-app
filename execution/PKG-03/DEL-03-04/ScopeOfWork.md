# Host boundary and integration guide

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable contributes the one guide an application builder follows to add a host. For each thing a host needs (a catalog, one route for changes, a basis, origins and undo, human acts and records, autonomy, a loop and panel, its own methods, and optionally external access and connectors) it says what the App and shared contracts supply, what the host must supply, who receives each contribution and where, and what evidence supports relying on it. The App/shared host-contract integration owner answers for its completeness. It restates none of the contracts it joins and builds no host.

It serves OBJ-004 (App/shared host contracts and a coordinated SWBPIPE journey that preserve operation meaning, host authority and recovery). Its sources are PRD §§2.2–2.4 and §3.1, V4-WF-01…06, V4-PAR-01…05 and V4-AUT-01…05; HOST_INTEGRATION §§1–11, above all the ownership table of §1 and the new-host checklist of §10; ARCHITECTURE §§4–5; EXAMINATION V4-EXM-20…25 and V4-EXM-30…32; and OPERATING_METHOD §6.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The new-host integration checklist: the catalog, the single route, the read and action basis, origins, undo and proposals, human acts, autonomy, loop and panel, and host methods, with optional external access and selected connectors as conditional paths. Each entry names a supplier, a receiving owner and interface, the evidence required and any unresolved input at its point of need. Supports SOW-187; OBJ-004.
- **OUT-002** — The App/shared versus host responsibility and interface matrix: for each contribution, who constructs it, who the human actors are, who receives it and where, what is held externally and which choices remain open. Supports SOW-156; OBJ-004.
- **OUT-003** — The guide completeness checks and their recorded comparison against the catalog and proposal, workflow, loop and panel, and act and record contracts. They find missing, conflicting and unsupported entries. Supports SOW-156 and SOW-187; OBJ-004.

Terms:

- **Contribution.** One of the ten rows of the receiving map below, which follow the ten items of HOST_INTEGRATION §10.
- **Receiving point.** The named owner and interface at which a contribution is taken up, and the moment it is first relied on.
- **Conditional path.** External catalog access and connectors: required only where a host offers or selects them, never to begin.
- **Coordination states.** A file prepared, relayed, answered, agreed, delivered, examined on a candidate, and adopted by its receiver are seven different facts.

Each host owns its domain objects and truth, catalog, validation and application route, receipts, origin and undo, interface, and the offering, recording and presenting of human acts. - **CLM-002** — `DEL-03-01` owns the catalog, the read basis and the map of generated surfaces; `DEL-03-02` the proposal, validation and outcome contract; `DEL-03-03` the App's external receiving. The guide takes their definitions and the limits of their evidence.

- **CLM-004** — `DEL-04-01` owns the operation policy and the distinctions between human acts; `DEL-04-02` autonomy and standing receiving; `DEL-04-03` the act and run records. ## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The checklist and the matrix are written together from the receiving map and the contracts they join, by citation: where a contract's meaning changes, the guide entries that cite it are checked again. A missing artifact or decision is not a gap in the guide; the guide names the input and where it is needed. Guide checks are documentary and change nothing in a host.

- **VER-001** — Comparison with a named reference (HOST_INTEGRATION §10 and the receiving map): every checklist entry for supplier, receiver, evidence and point of use, including the conditional paths.
- **VER-002** — Comparison with a named reference (the scopes and definitions of `DEL-03-01` and `DEL-03-02`, and HOST_INTEGRATION §§2–4): reads and unavailable reasons, the original basis, the one route, origin and undo, proposal differences, stale, retarget, duplicate and unknown outcomes, and receipt references.
- **VER-003** — Comparison with a named reference (HOST_INTEGRATION §§5–6 and §9 and the policy of `DEL-04-01`): the act and autonomy entries, read against an execution-only success, a fabricated act, a faithful record with a separate recorder, a content change, an acceptance against an approval, and a declared checkpoint.
- **VER-004** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: walks the method, tool, checkpoint, record, loop and panel receiving path and checks who receives each contribution, its source identity, the network boundary, the host's views and each open placement.
- **VER-005** — Comparison with a named reference (HOST_INTEGRATION §8 and §8.1, EXAMINATION V4-EXM-30 and V4-EXM-32): the selected, absent and limited connector paths and the later Domains inputs.
- **VER-006** — Comparison with a named reference (the open issues and external dependencies of the decomposition): each open matter's owner and timing as recorded there, and each stated coordination state against its evidence, including a file prepared but not relayed, an answer received without delivery, and a definition checked without host adoption.
- **VER-007** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: lists every act REQ-008 excludes and compares its named owner with CLM-001…CLM-006 and the decomposition's rows.
- **VER-008** — Judgment by an independent reviewer: reads the whole guide candidate against the contracts it cites at their identified revisions and records, for each criterion above, pass, fail, blocked, not run or inconclusive.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — Shared meaning follows real responsibilities and consumers. Better means more contributions with a named receiver and an agreed receiving point; never by choosing a common service or moving a host's construction to the App to close a row.
- **AX-002** — The person's accountability and truthful standing govern every interface. Better means more entries whose evidence names the act, its actor and its content; never by attributing an act nobody performed or by deciding a policy that is someone else's to rule.
- **AX-003** — A definition, a checked candidate, a relayed handoff, a delivered capability, a receiver's adoption and a human act are separate facts. Better means more entries stating exactly the state their evidence shows; never by letting a guide check stand for a host's conformance.
- **TBD-003** — Whether a new catalog operation reaches every actor without separate work is undecided: retained, narrowed to generated surfaces or deferred (owner with the host contract owner). Semantic parity is required either way.
- **TBD-004** — Per-host loop placement, parsing, persistence and panel assembly, and any reusable common contribution, are unagreed (shared contract owner with SWBPIPE's implementation owner).
- **TBD-005** — Where shared contracts and components are placed is unagreed (App/shared contract owners). No common service is presumed.
- **TBD-006** — The operation, the autonomy permitted and the candidate environment of the first connected activity, and any operation-specific reserved act, are undecided (owner, through the SWBPIPE session, with the App owner). SWBPIPE's evidence is needed before the connected journey is examined; neither holds up the guide.
- **TBD-008** — The Domains receiving contract and its timing are undecided: source admission, query, provenance and freshness, the research workflow, and a deployment and data boundary compatible with PRD V4-HOST-02 (owner with the Domains, SWBPIPE and App receiving owners). An admitted source basis is needed before query results are relied on, the query interface before a dependent tool is built, the research workflow before the research-to-candidate increment, and the host's use and the person's approval route before the connected witness.
- **TBD-009** — Who builds the Domains provider, and where, is undecided (owner with the App, Domains and SWBPIPE definition owners). Domains develops in parallel and is needed for the later Domains-enabled increment, not the first activity.
