## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Develop the source-grounded receiving definition and decision account from the accepted composite and decomposition. Resolve relevant interface/admission choices with their identified owners at the points of need. Prepare fixtures only when their identified query contract and admitted source inputs exist, then exercise the focused receiving cases. Supply the resulting contract and explicit limits to the later integration owner through the manager's human-relayed coordination route. Preserve actual execution results separately from the contract's intended behavior; no source, provider, external commitment or human act is inferred from a drafted handoff.

- **VER-001** — Review the query definition against the assigned scope and V4-CON-01/V4-HI-60. Trace a candidate query result back to its source reference and inspect a successful hit whose membership/standing/suitability is unestablished; confirm the contract does not promote retrieval into authority. Identify unresolved query meanings before dependent implementation. Evidence: source-to-contract comparison and query/reference case result or an explicit unsupplied-input limit.
- **VER-002** — Examine the admission definition and source evidence for an admitted test source and a located but unadmitted source. Check that applicability/standing/limitations arise from the identified admission basis rather than search or PEC availability; repeat the contract assessment with PEC absent. Evidence: admission/source comparison, responsible-party/open-detail account and case observations.
- **VER-003** — Compare displayed/recorded freshness and receiving treatment with the identified source/admission basis for current, stale and unknown-freshness cases. Check that no retrieval timestamp or responsive feed alone substitutes for source currency and no unsupported age limit is imposed. Evidence: source/freshness basis and observed receiving limits.
- **VER-004** — Inspect candidate query/result fixtures against their identified contract and admitted source inputs. Account for each REQ-004 case, retained reference/standing/freshness/limitation, and any missing input or unexercised case. Evidence: fixture-to-source/contract comparison and actual preparation/execution status; fixture identity is not live provider qualification.
- **VER-005** — Exercise the local unavailable, unsuitable and stale-source cases when the required inputs exist; inspect source-based fallback and responsible coordination through the common receiving policy. Include absent PEC with stale Domains and independent connector availability. Evidence: identified candidate/fixture and observation linking the input limit to the permitted receiving action, with any unsupported or unexercised outcome stated.
- **VER-006** — Compare the decision account to OI-023/OI-026, DEP-003 and V4-HOST-02/PKG-05. Check positive faithful recording of the owner's reported external knowledge-development location with its attributed custody, and reject inference of selected provider ownership, transport, deployment, an additional data destination or readiness. Where an actual later resolution is supplied, check its subject, actor and evidence separately from the recorder; otherwise retain the exact open owner and point of need. Assess the selected query/tool arrangement's compatibility only when that arrangement and evidence exist. Evidence: decision/source comparison, unresolved-choice account and applicable compatibility evidence or identified missing input.
- **VER-007** — Review the receiving handoff against App v4 DEL-08-02's accepted interface and DEP-003. Trace source/admission/freshness limits and actual readiness to the later contribution, and check that a prepared handoff or successful query cannot be evidence of outside agreement, host execution or a human approval. Verify the separate points of need and initial-activity independence. Evidence: bounded handoff/interface comparison, supplied-return references and explicit outstanding inputs.
- **VER-008** — Audit the output boundary against the accepted deliverable/package rows and CLM-002 through CLM-005. Enumerate each excluded act and verify its owner one-for-one; check that unresolved provider allocation remains open without being dropped from program intent. Evidence: artifact-coverage and owner-resolution comparison, including semantic checking of any deterministic boundary-tool limitation.
