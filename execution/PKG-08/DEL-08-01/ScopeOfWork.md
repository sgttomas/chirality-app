# Domains query, admission and freshness contract

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable contributes the receiving side of a Domains query: what a consumer of a search over the domain knowledge database must be given, must record and must show before a located passage is relied on as research context. It lets a research workflow tell a search hit from an admitted source, a source as it was indexed from the source as it now stands, and an answer from no answer, so that nothing retrieved becomes authority by being retrieved. It contributes the receiving contract, the receiving record that carries it, the fixtures and limit cases that exercise it, and the account of which query-tool arrangements meet the limit on where a host's agent may send data. It builds no provider, database or search tool.

It covers SOW-023 (Domains is consumed by querying a domain knowledge database and search tool through research workflows), SOW-024 (search locates evidence and establishes neither corpus membership nor authority), SOW-025 (freshness is exposed and checked), SOW-247 (admission is independent of PEC) and SOW-249 (the admission and receiving contract, provenance and freshness, and the later integration point, with provider allocation, tool deployment and the data boundary kept explicit). It serves OBJ-007: optional connectors and the later Domains capability are consumed through explicit receiving contracts with truthful fallback. The accepted decomposition assigns it as the API contract of PKG-08. Its sources are PRD V4-CON-01, V4-CON-03, V4-CON-04, V4-HOST-02, §3.1 and OQ-03; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-60, V4-HI-62, V4-HI-64 and §8.1; ARCHITECTURE V4-ARC-22; and EXAMINATION V4-EXM-30 and V4-EXM-32. The draft 4.0.0 release target places Domains in a later increment and names no exit criterion for this deliverable.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The Domains receiving contract: the meanings of request, result, admission, freshness and standing defined below, and the receiving record that carries them for one query. Covers SOW-023, SOW-024, SOW-025, SOW-247 and SOW-249; supports OBJ-007.
- **OUT-002** — Query and result fixtures: receiving records prepared against an identified query contract and admitted test sources, covering an admitted current source, a located source that is not admitted or not suitable, a stale source and no response. Covers SOW-023, SOW-024, SOW-025 and SOW-247; supports OBJ-007.
- **OUT-003** — The limit cases and their results: unavailable, unsuitable and stale Domains input, admission judged with PEC absent and with PEC present, and PEC absent together with stale Domains. Covers SOW-023, SOW-024, SOW-025, SOW-247 and SOW-249; supports OBJ-007.
- **OUT-004** — The arrangement and decision account: for each way a host's agent could reach a Domains query tool, whether it meets the destination rule of CLM-003 and why; and each undecided matter with its deciding parties and the point at which it is needed. Covers SOW-249; supports OBJ-007.

Terms:

- **Request.** A research question together with its intended use. The intended use is what admission is judged against.
- **Result.** One located source passage: its source reference, the revision of the source the index was built from, the passage, and the admission state supplied with it. A result locates evidence. It establishes no corpus membership, standing, applicability or authority.
- **Query contract.** The identified interface through which requests and results pass. While none is identified, a receiving record says so and no result is relied on.
- **Admission.** A source is admitted for an intended use, at an identified revision, by an admission basis that the receiving decisions establish. A result is *admitted* when its admission is supplied and is found in that basis for the request's intended use; *located, not admitted* when it is supplied as not admitted, or when its admission is for a use other than the request's intended use, in which case it is *unsuitable* and is recorded as located, not admitted, with that reason; otherwise *unknown*. Admission at one revision does not carry to a later one. The state of PEC or of any other connector plays no part.
- **Freshness.** Judged against the source: a result is *current* when the source stands at the revision that was indexed, *stale* when the source has been revised since, and *unknown* when the source cannot be read. No response at all is *absent*. A response is as fresh as its least fresh result. No age threshold applies unless the freshness criterion of TBD-001 sets one.
- **Standing and reliance.** Each result and each response has a standing in the connector standing vocabulary that `DEL-07-02` owns; this deliverable defines the Domains inputs to it. A result supports reliance only when the query contract and admission basis are established, the result is admitted, and it is current. A stale admitted result supports nothing, because what was admitted is not the source as it stands. Standing is derived from Domains inputs alone.
- **Receiving record.** What a receiver records for one request: the question; the response or its absence; the query contract's identity, or that none is identified; what the index was built from; each result with its identity, source reference, indexed revision, current revision as the receiver read it, admission and standing; the standing of the response; the conclusions the response supports and does not support; whether the source-file route is needed; the recorder; and whether any input was simulated, and which.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — This deliverable owns the meanings above, the receiving record, its fixtures and limit cases, and the arrangement and decision account. It selects no provider, database, corpus, transport, deployment or wire field; the element names of the receiving record are labels for meanings.
- **CLM-002** — Who builds the Domains provider and its query tool is unallocated, and the party that admits sources comes with that allocation and the receiving decisions (TBD-001, TBD-002). Domains knowledge is developed outside this repository.
- **CLM-003** — `DEL-05-01` owns the host-loop receiving contract, including the destination rule: a host's agent sends data only to the model service the person selected and to destinations the person has allowed, and every destination contacted is recorded and shown. The external SWBPIPE implementation owner builds the loop and enforces the rule.
- **CLM-004** — `DEL-08-02` owns the research method that turns receiving records into research context and a design candidate, the host handoff and the later witness of the joined activity. The external SWBPIPE implementation owner builds the host's use of the query tool, and the person performs any approval.
- **CLM-005** — `DEL-07-02` owns the connector standing vocabulary, the App's presentation of connector limits and the source-file route. `DEL-09-10` owns the connector consumption witness. PEC and Domains are independent, and neither is a condition of the first connected activity.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — The contract defines Domains consumption as a request made through a research workflow and results that each keep a reference back to the source evidence, with their availability and limitations. Successful retrieval establishes no corpus membership, standing, applicability or authority.
- **REQ-002** — Before a result is relied on as research context, its receiving record shows the source's identity, its admission state with the basis and revision of admission, and its limitations. A located hit is distinguished from a source admitted for the intended use. Admission is established without regard to PEC.
- **REQ-003** — The freshness of each result is exposed and is checked by the receiver against the source as it stands. A stale or unknown result carries a visible limit on reliance. A retrieved or responsive source is never treated as current for that reason, and no age threshold is applied that the freshness criterion of TBD-001 has not set.
- **REQ-004** — Fixtures are receiving records prepared against an identified query contract and admitted test sources. They distinguish an admitted current result, a located result that is not admitted or not suitable, a stale result and no response. A fixture whose query contract was not supplied by the provider, or whose admission basis was not supplied by the admitting party, is marked simulated, says what was invented, stands for no live query and counts toward none of the four cases; a case with no supplied fixture is reported as not exercised. Test sources may be invented material.
- **REQ-005** — With Domains input unavailable, unsuitable or stale, the receiving record states why the input cannot supply the research context asked for, keeps the limitation and marks that the source-file route of CLM-005 is needed. No context, empty result, readiness or permission is produced in its place, and work that does not depend on the input continues. The case of PEC absent together with stale Domains is kept, and neither connector is a prerequisite of the other.
- **REQ-006** — The account names each arrangement by which a host's agent could reach a Domains query tool and says whether it meets the destination rule of CLM-003, with the reason: an arrangement meets the rule when it adds no destination or contacts the tool only as a destination the person has allowed, recorded and shown. Before any implementation or reliance that depends on the query tool, the arrangement in use meets that rule, or the account names the explicit decision needed on the affected constraint and who decides it. The account states each undecided matter of CLM-002 with its deciding parties and its point of need. A matter that has been decided is stated with who decided it, apart from whoever recorded it. Where Domains knowledge is developed grants no destination.
- **REQ-007** — A receiving record gives the research method of CLM-004 what it cites: the record's identity and, for each result, an identity, the source reference, the revisions and the standing. Nothing in the contract makes Domains a condition of the first connected activity.
- **REQ-008** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable or party: the host-loop receiving contract and its destination rule are those of `DEL-05-01`, and building the loop and enforcing the rule are the external SWBPIPE implementation owner's (CLM-003); the research method, the host handoff and the later witness are those of `DEL-08-02`, and any approval is the person's (CLM-004); the standing vocabulary, the presentation of connector limits and the source-file route are those of `DEL-07-02`, and the connector witness is that of `DEL-09-10` (CLM-005). Building the provider and admitting sources are unallocated (CLM-002), and nothing here assigns them.

- **AC-001** — In every receiving record each result carries its source reference and can be followed to the passage in the source, and a result whose admission is not established is recorded as located and not admitted, or as unknown, with no conclusion resting on it. Verify by VER-001. [REQ-001]
- **AC-002** — For an admitted test source and a located source that is not admitted, the receiving record shows each one's admission state, and for the admitted one the basis and the admitted revision; only the admitted one supports reliance. The record is the same with PEC absent and with PEC present. Verify by VER-002. [REQ-002]
- **AC-003** — For a source standing at its indexed revision, a source revised since it was indexed and a source that cannot be read, the receiving record shows current, stale and unknown respectively, and the stale and unknown results support no reliance. While no freshness criterion beyond a change of the source is set, the outcome does not change with the time elapsed since indexing. Verify by VER-003. [REQ-003]
- **AC-004** — Each fixture is a valid receiving record, names the query contract and the admission basis it was prepared against, and the set covers the four cases of REQ-004. A fixture whose contract or admission basis was invented is marked simulated, says what was invented and counts toward no case, and each case not covered by a supplied fixture is reported as not exercised. Verify by VER-004. [REQ-004]
- **AC-005** — With no response, the receiving record has no results, the standing absent and the source-file route marked as needed. With an unsuitable or a stale result it keeps the limitation and supports no reliance. In no case does it state empty work, readiness or permission. PEC absent with stale Domains gives the same Domains standing as stale Domains alone. Verify by VER-005. [REQ-005]
- **AC-006** — The account lists each arrangement by which a host's agent could reach a query tool as adding no destination, as contacted only as a destination the person allows, or as depending on a destination the person has not allowed, each with its reason; for the last it names the decision needed on the affected constraint and its deciding party, and the arrangement in use is not of that kind unless that decision is recorded. It lists each undecided matter with its deciding parties and its point of need, and a decided matter with who decided it, apart from the recorder. Verify by VER-006. [REQ-006]
- **AC-007** — A consumer holding only a receiving record can cite any of its results by record and result identity and read that result's source reference, revisions and standing. The fixtures of AC-004 serve as that consumer's inputs unchanged. Verify by VER-007. [REQ-007]
- **AC-008** — The outputs select no provider, database, corpus, transport, deployment or wire field and perform no act allocated in CLM-002 to CLM-005. Verify by VER-008. [REQ-008; CLM-001]

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The contract is a set of meanings and one receiving record format, an open JSON schema whose element names are labels and not a provider's wire fields. The receiver reads the source's current revision itself and does not take currency from the response. Standing is expressed in the one vocabulary shared with other connectors, so a consumer treats a Domains limit as it treats any other connector limit. A simulated input is marked in the record that uses it. Cases use invented material; no real engineering or client data is needed to examine the contract (PRD V4-CST-06).

- **VER-001** — Comparison with a named reference (the source files that a fixture's results cite, read directly): follow each result's source reference to its passage, and inspect a retrieved result whose admission is not established for any conclusion drawn from it.
- **VER-002** — Executable check (observes the admission state, basis, admitted revision and reliance recorded for an admitted source and for a located source that is not admitted, each with PEC absent and with PEC present).
- **VER-003** — Executable check (observes the freshness and reliance recorded for a source at its indexed revision, a source revised since and a source that cannot be read, and that, while no freshness criterion beyond a change of the source is set, changing only the time elapsed since indexing changes nothing).
- **VER-004** — Executable check (observes each fixture's validity against the receiving record schema, its simulation marking, the coverage of the four cases by fixtures that are not simulated and the report of each case not exercised), combined with comparison with a named reference (the admitted test sources and the identified query contract that each fixture names).
- **VER-005** — Executable check (observes the receiving record produced for no response, for an unsuitable result and for a stale result, each alone and with PEC absent).
- **VER-006** — Judgment by a named role (a reviewer who did not write the account): compare each arrangement's listing with PRD V4-HOST-02 and the destination cases of `DEL-05-01`, the arrangement in use with any decision recorded for it, and each undecided matter with TBD-001 and TBD-002.
- **VER-007** — Executable check (observes that a reader given only the fixtures resolves each cited result by record and result identity and reads its source reference, revisions and standing).
- **VER-008** — Judgment by a named role (a reviewer who did not write the outputs): read the contract, record format, fixtures and account for any selection CLM-001 excludes, and trace each act named in REQ-008 to its owner in the accepted decomposition.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-002** — References, admission, freshness and limitations come before reliance. Better means fewer results reach a research context with their source reference, admission or freshness unknown; never by treating a retrieved or responsive source as admitted or current.
- **AX-003** — The contract holds whoever builds the provider and however the query tool is deployed. Better means fewer assumptions about provider, transport or deployment in the receiving record; never by adding a destination the person has not allowed.
- **AX-004** — A search result, an admission, a check, a person's approval and professional reliance are different facts. Better means each is stated with its own evidence and actor; never by inferring one from another.
- **AX-006** — Domains is optional to everything that does not depend on it. Better means less work is held when Domains input is missing or limited; never by fabricating context or by making Domains or PEC a condition of starting independent work.
- **TBD-001** — Undecided, with the owner and the Domains, SWBPIPE and App receiving owners (decomposition open issue OI-023), before the Domains-enabled increment: who admits sources and on what basis; the provider's query contract; any freshness criterion beyond a change of the source; and the query tool's deployment and data boundary. Each is needed before the implementation or reliance that depends on it.
- **TBD-002** — Undecided, with the owner and the App, Domains and SWBPIPE definition owners (decomposition open issue OI-026), before provider production is allocated and its integration committed: who builds the Domains provider and query tool, and where.
