# DEL-08-02 — Later research-to-design receiving activity

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable contributes the App and shared side of the later activity in which SWBPIPE's agent queries Domains through a research workflow, builds research context and produces a design candidate for a person to decide on. It makes that context inspectable: what was retrieved, what standing each piece has, what was inferred from it and what is missing. The person deciding on a candidate can then see what it rests on, and no context, candidate standing or decision is supplied from anything that did not happen. It contributes the research method with its research context account, the receiving integration and handoff to the host's owner, and the witness of the joined activity on an identified candidate. It builds no provider, query tool or host.

It covers SOW-026 (support the later SWBPIPE agent activity that queries Domains through a research workflow, builds source-grounded context and produces a design candidate for human approval) and SOW-027 (develop the Domains receiving relationship in parallel with the connected host activity and integrate it in a subsequent increment). It serves OBJ-007: optional connectors and the later Domains capability are consumed through explicit receiving contracts with truthful fallback. The accepted decomposition assigns it to PKG-08 as the later receiving activity and its evidence. Its sources are PRD V4-CON-01, V4-CON-03, V4-CON-04, V4-CON-05, §3.1, V4-AUT-03, V4-AUT-05, V4-ROLE-03, V4-HOST-04, V4-HOST-05, V4-SHR-03 and V4-WF-01; HOST_INTEGRATION V4-HI-25, V4-HI-31, V4-HI-32, V4-HI-60, V4-HI-62, V4-HI-64, V4-HI-65 and §8.1; and EXAMINATION V4-EXM-32. The draft 4.0.0 release target places Domains in a later increment and names no exit criterion for this deliverable.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

- **OUT-001** — The research method and contribution account: the research path and the research context account defined below, the workflow package that carries the method to a host, and, for each contribution the later activity needs, who provides it and the point at which it is needed. Covers SOW-026 and SOW-027; supports OBJ-007.
- **OUT-002** — The receiving integration and host handoff: the research context account format, the requirements on the candidate decision, and the questions the host's owner must answer for the activity to be joined, with answers received kept apart from questions asked. Covers SOW-026 and SOW-027; supports OBJ-007.
- **OUT-003** — The V4-EXM-32 witness: the observed result of the joined activity on an identified candidate, with an identified provider and query contract, admitted test sources, the research workflow and the host integration, including unavailable, unsuitable and stale research input and the person's actual decision. Covers SOW-026 and SOW-027; supports OBJ-007.

Terms:

- **Research path.** A research question with its intended use; a query; evidence classed by its standing; inferences and gaps; a candidate; a request for the person's decision; the person's decision. Applying a change, accepting change items, marking work checked and professional reliance are separate acts, and none follows from the decision.
- **Evidence use.** Each evidence item cites one result of a Domains receiving record and takes the use that result's standing allows: *admitted evidence* where the result supports reliance; *located only* where it is located and not admitted; *limited* otherwise, which includes an admitted source that is stale.
- **Inference and gap.** An inference names the evidence or inferences it rests on and any limit that follows from them. A gap names what is missing, its effect and who is responsible for it.
- **Research context account.** For one question: the receiving records used; the evidence items, each with its source, revision, statement and use; the inferences; the gaps; the candidate's reference, the host's content identity for it and what it rests on; the account's standing; and the recorder.
- **Account standing.** *Supported*: every evidence item the candidate ultimately rests on is admitted evidence. *Limited*: the candidate rests on at least one located-only or limited item, and the account names the limit. *Held*: Domains is absent, or nothing retrieved can carry a candidate; no candidate is produced from the account and the gap is named.
- **Candidate decision.** The person's approval or decline of one candidate, identified by the host's content identity, for the scope and purpose the request stated. Silence, a timeout, an agent's statement, a tool's success or a host receipt is not a decision. A candidate without a content identity cannot be offered for decision. A change to the candidate's content ends the applicability of an earlier decision.

Boundary:

- **CLM-001** — The SWB Piping Designer is an application expression of this activity that uses the four standing roles and the selected workflow. It adds no fifth role and no separate knowledge-browser interface.
- **CLM-002** — Domains develops alongside the first connected activity and joins it in a subsequent increment. The first connected activity depends on neither Domains nor PEC.
- **CLM-003** — `DEL-08-01` owns the Domains receiving contract, the receiving record and its fixtures. `DEL-02-01` owns the portable workflow contract that carries the method to a host. `DEL-04-01` owns the act kinds and `DEL-04-03` the act and run record format.
- **CLM-004** — The external SWBPIPE implementation owner builds the host's use of the query tool, candidate creation and presentation, the facility that captures the person's decision, application, validation and receipts. The host owns its domain truth and the candidate's content identity.
- **CLM-005** — Who builds the Domains provider and its query tool, and who admits sources, are unallocated (TBD-001, TBD-002).
- **CLM-006** — The person performs the candidate decision, and professional reliance stays with the accountable professional. The acts reserved to the person in the first increment are those `DEL-04-01` carries. Whether a host reserves the candidate decision on its own list is undecided (TBD-003).

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — A research context account traces an identified, admitted query and source basis through retrieved evidence, inferences and gaps to an identified candidate and the decision asked of the person. Source references, standing, freshness and limits of suitability stay inspectable in it. Retrieval alone gives an item no standing as evidence.
- **REQ-002** — Unavailable, unsuitable, stale or otherwise limited research input is shown with its effect on the context and the candidate: the account is held or limited as the terms define, and no context is produced in place of a missing input. Work that depends on the missing input waits; work with its own sound basis continues.
- **REQ-003** — The contribution account states each contribution the later activity needs with who provides it, or that it is unallocated, and its point of need: an admitted source basis and query access before research context is relied on; a provider and query arrangement compatible with the host-agent destination rule before a dependent tool or consumer is built; the research workflow before the research-to-candidate increment; and host integration with the person's approval route before the connected witness.
- **REQ-004** — The handoff states the App and shared contribution and the host's contribution separately, and carries questions asked and answers actually received as different things. It asks the host's owner how the agent reaches the query tool, what content identity a candidate carries, which host facility presents the candidate and captures the person's decision and what evidence reference it returns, whether the host reserves the candidate decision, and where the research context account is kept with the host project. A question or proposal is never shown as agreed, built or adopted. A common implementation is proposed only for a concrete repeated responsibility. The handoff names no API field, transport, corpus, database, deployment or date.
- **REQ-005** — Evidence of a candidate decision identifies the candidate by its content identity, the decision's subject and scope, the person who decided and the evidence that the act occurred, apart from whoever recorded or presented it. An act the person performed is recorded faithfully. No act is recorded from an agent's result, a tool's success, a host receipt or silence. A candidate changed after a decision shows that decision as lapsed. The candidate decision, applying a change, accepting change items, marking work checked and professional reliance stay distinct, and none is required before another unless the policy in force says so.
- **REQ-006** — The witness binds the provider and query contract, the admitted sources, the research workflow, the host integration and the candidate examined. It is complete only when the whole research path and the person's actual decision with its scope have been observed on the joined candidate, together with the limit cases. A favourable decision is not required, and professional reliance is not part of it. A part whose inputs are missing is reported as not run, with the inputs named. A rehearsal on fixtures, a prepared handoff or a partial observation never counts as the completed witness, and a missing external input holds nothing in the first connected activity.
- **REQ-007** — This deliverable shall perform no act owned by another deliverable or party: the Domains receiving contract, its record and fixtures are those of `DEL-08-01`, the portable workflow contract is that of `DEL-02-01`, the act kinds are those of `DEL-04-01` and the act and run record format is that of `DEL-04-03` (CLM-003); host construction, candidate presentation, decision capture, application and receipts are the external SWBPIPE implementation owner's (CLM-004); building the provider and admitting sources are unallocated (CLM-005); the candidate decision is the person's and professional reliance the accountable professional's (CLM-006).
- **REQ-008** — The research method is a portable workflow package under the contract of `DEL-02-01`: prose that gives the research path, and a declared part stating the inputs the method expects, the tools it needs, the checkpoints where a human act is required and the outputs and evidence it returns. The package states nothing that TBD-003 or TBD-004 leaves undecided as though it were settled.

- **AC-001** — For an account built over receiving records, every evidence item's use is the one its cited result's standing allows, every inference and the candidate rest on items in the same account, and the account's standing is supported, limited or held as the terms define. An account is refused when it is supported while resting on a located-only or limited item; uses a stale admitted result as admitted evidence; has an inference that cites an item not in the account; is limited and names no limit; is held and has a candidate; or offers a candidate for decision without a content identity. Verify by VER-001. [REQ-001; REQ-002]
- **AC-002** — The contribution account names, for the provider, the query tool, the research workflow, the host integration and the App and shared contribution, who provides each or that it is unallocated, with the points of need of REQ-003. Nothing in it makes Domains or PEC a condition of the first connected activity. Verify by VER-002. [REQ-003; CLM-002; CLM-005]
- **AC-003** — The handoff shows the App and shared contribution and the host's separately, contains the questions of REQ-004, and shows each answer with who gave it. A question without an answer is shown as unanswered, and no proposal appears as a commitment, a capability or an adoption. Verify by VER-002. [REQ-004; CLM-004]
- **AC-004** — On the joined candidate, the host's agent calls the query tool through the research workflow, and one question is followed from the query, through evidence with its source references, standing and freshness, inferences and gaps, to a candidate with its content identity, a request, and the person's actual decision with its scope. The witness is complete whether the person approves or declines. Verify by VER-003. [REQ-001; REQ-006]
- **AC-005** — On the joined candidate: with Domains unavailable, the account is held, no candidate is produced from it and independent work continues; with a hit that is not admitted, the item is not used as admitted evidence and the account is limited and names it; with an admitted source revised after it was indexed, the account is limited. In none of the three is context, candidate standing or a decision supplied. Verify by VER-004. [REQ-002; REQ-006]

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The research context is one account for each question, a JSON document under an open schema, with the rules a schema cannot express checked over whole accounts. An account cites results of receiving records by identity and takes each item's use from the result's standing; it never derives admission or freshness again. The method reaches a host as a portable workflow package under the contract of `DEL-02-01`. The witness uses invented material, declares each stimulus before the run and labels a rehearsal on fixtures as a rehearsal.

- **VER-001** — Executable check (observes, for accounts written by hand over receiving-record fixtures, which are accepted with which standing and that each breach listed in AC-001 is refused). It observes hand-built accounts, a proxy for the accounts a research run produces; those are observed under VER-003.
- **VER-002** — Judgment by a named role (a reviewer who did not write the outputs): read the contribution account and the handoff against HOST_INTEGRATION §8.1, PRD §3.1 and the accepted decomposition, check each provider or unallocated contribution and point of need, each question against what the host's owner actually returned, the four-role expression, and each act named in REQ-007 against its owner.
- **VER-003** — Judgment by a named role (the examiner of the joined candidate, with the external SWBPIPE implementation owner): observe the path of AC-004 once the provider and query contract, admitted sources, research workflow and host integration exist, keeping the identity of each, and observe the person decide. A missing input or an act that was not observed is reported as such.
- **VER-004** — Judgment by a named role (the examiner of the joined candidate): apply the three stimuli of AC-005 under the actual query contract — the query tool made unavailable, a source that is not admitted, a source revised after indexing — and observe the account and what the candidate shows. Each observation says whether it came from fixtures or from the host's path.
- **VER-005** — Judgment by a named role (the examiner of the joined candidate): apply the three stimuli of AC-006 — the agent claims approval, the request is left unanswered, the candidate is edited after the decision — and compare the decision record with what the person was seen to do.
- **VER-006** — Executable check (observes what `DEL-02-01`'s reader reports for the research workflow package: the reading of each category and element, and the declared inputs, required tools, checkpoints, outputs and evidence).

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-002** — Evidence and acts keep their identities: search locates evidence, a candidate is a candidate, a receipt records an outcome, and a decision needs evidence that the person made it. Better means more of what a candidate rests on is visible to the person at the moment of decision; never by showing located or stale material as admitted evidence, or a decision the person did not make.
- **AX-003** — The receiving relationship is shared meaning, not shared machinery, and the means of reaching Domains stay open until their owners choose them. Better means fewer assumptions about provider, transport or host internals in the method and the handoff; never by adding a destination the person has not allowed or by making Domains a condition of independent work.
- **AX-004** — A witness is worth what was observed. Better means more of the research path observed on the joined candidate with its real inputs; never by counting a rehearsal, a prepared handoff or a partial run as the completed witness.
- **TBD-001** — Who admits sources, the provider's query contract and the query tool's deployment and data boundary are undecided, as `DEL-08-01` states them. Until they are settled the parts of the witness that need them are not run.
- **TBD-002** — Who builds the Domains provider and query tool, and where, is undecided, as `DEL-08-01` states it.
- **TBD-003** — No shared act kind names the person's decision on a design candidate, so no workflow checkpoint can require it and no act record can carry it: whether it is engineering approval or a kind of its own is undecided (the owner, with the act-kind owner of CLM-003). Whether a host reserves the decision on its own list is undecided (the owner with the affected App and host design owners, PRD OQ-02).
- **TBD-004** — How a research workflow declares the Domains query tool among its required tools, and where the research context account is kept with the host project, are undecided (the owners of the Domains-enabled increment with the host's owner).
