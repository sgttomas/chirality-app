# Operation-policy and human-act distinctions

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Define the App/shared contract for graduated autonomy and attributable human acts. The person sets direct-application or proposal requirements by operation and consequence. The contract carries adopted decisions into consuming catalogs, workflow checkpoints and host interfaces while preserving the distinct meanings of execution, checking, edit acceptance, approval and professional reliance.

This API_CONTRACT contributes to OBJ-004, compatible App/shared host contracts preserving host authority, and OBJ-005, useful autonomy with attributable acts and reconstructable evidence. It defines the work and its completion evidence; creating this ScopeOfWork does not itself adopt operation-policy values, supply implementation or establish a human act.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — OUT-001 and OUT-002 shall express direct application only within the scope the person sets, otherwise proposal, by operation class and consequence; preserve origin, undo and later-checking interface obligations without implementing those sibling/host facilities. Declared workflow checkpoints continue to require the person's actual act regardless of granted autonomy. Sources: SOW-074; S/original-seed/PRD.md V4-AUT-01; S/original-seed/HOST_INTEGRATION.md V4-HI-22, V4-HI-40, V4-HI-42; CLM-002 and CLM-003.
- **REQ-002** — The contract and fixtures shall distinguish proposing, applying/executing, agent examination, human marking checked, accepting an edit, engineering approval and professional reliance. Registering a workflow revision (A15) is likewise a person's act with its own actor, subject (the revision) and evidence. This contract supplies that distinction to App v4 `DEL-02-02`, whose registration it governs (`DEL-02-02/REQ-002`, `DEL-02-02/AC-006`), and to `DEL-01-04`, whose App act control captures it (`APP-V4-DESIGN-PASS-3-20261001-DECISION-K3` K-8); it consumes nothing from either. Each asserted act retains its actual actor, subject and supporting evidence. Operation success or a receipt establishes only the observed operation outcome; evidence of one act does not establish another. Acceptance shall not become a synthetic prerequisite for any other independently evidenced act. Agents may prepare or request a human decision; they shall not represent or record a human act as performed by the person when it was not. Faithful recording of an actually performed human act retains the human decision actor separately from any agent recorder; this distinction grants no recording permission or reserved-act authority. Sources: SOW-079 and SOW-235; S/DECISION_BRIEF.html#d3; current `projects/chirality-app-v4/docs/PRD.md` V4-AUT-03 and `projects/chirality-app-v4/docs/HOST_INTEGRATION.md` V4-HI-31; S/original-seed/HOST_INTEGRATION.md V4-HI-25, V4-HI-31, V4-HI-33 as historical source within that composite; CLM-004 and CLM-005.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Start from the accepted scope rows and the actual source clauses above. Define the semantic contract and consumer responsibilities before selecting any operation-specific unresolved value. Obtain the recorded decision for each concrete case at its point of need, then carry its adopted value into the policy representation and consuming catalog interface. The policy representation's concrete syntax and placement are design work; this ScopeOfWork invents no wire fields, service topology, thresholds or permission classifier.

Prepare the reserved-act and label fixtures from the grounded requirements. Bind verification to the exact delivered document/configuration/fixture revision and report observed outcomes, limits and missing external inputs. Local contract conformance can proceed independently; a host enforcement assertion requires actual host evidence from DEP-001. Tests supply evidence for these obligations and do not expand them.
