# Native requests, outcomes and attachments

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

Provide the App's native approval/question cards and response interactions, turn/outcome and attachment presentation, and the receiving interface used by the workflow-making workspace. The responsible party is the **App native-interaction owner**. This SOFTWARE deliverable is the accepted UX_UI_SLICE in PKG-01, Native App and third-party harness integration. The contract defines future production and its evaluation; its existence does not establish implementation, passing product checks or lifecycle advancement.

| Accepted reference | Local contribution |
|---|---|
| SOW-005 / OBJ-001 | Present and handle native approvals and questions through the execution owner's request interface. |
| SOW-014 / OBJ-001 | Preserve native Codex request/response semantics in this slice. App DEL-01-03 supplies the jointly mapped plan, tool and delegation views. |
| SOW-129 / OBJ-001, OBJ-002 | Retain request, draft, turn/outcome and attachment interactions, including supplied-content identity. App DEL-01-03 supplies the jointly mapped plan interaction; old UI reuse is optional. |

OBJ-001 is the complete standalone workflow-making loop with native tool use and recoverable interaction. OBJ-002 is the chosen maintainable macOS App architecture and three user-selectable Codex access modes. This slice contributes native receiving behavior within that architecture; it does not acquire account-mode implementation or claim to complete either objective alone.

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

- **REQ-001** — Native cards shall preserve the selected supplier's approval/question semantics and provide grant, deny, answer and explicit-decline interactions through CLM-003's execution interface. They shall expose received unsupported/unknown-request errors. Silence, elapsed time or closing the observer shall not be represented as approval or an answer. A translated Chirality event vocabulary shall not replace native requests. Basis: SOW-005/SOW-014; N, R; CLM-002 and CLM-003.
- **REQ-002** — Turn and outcome presentation shall state only observed events from the execution interface. Unavailable outcomes remain unknown. Rendered state, provider state, request settlement, a reply-write attempt and received acknowledgment shall remain distinguishable; primary-turn completion shall not imply active descendants finished. Closing/reopening or reloading a view shall render the recovered actual state and outstanding requests supplied by the execution owner, without synthesizing completion or interruption. Basis: SOW-129; R; CLM-003.
- **REQ-003** — Attachment/import and workflow-draft interactions shall preserve and expose the identity of the content actually supplied at the local interface. Content selected, submitted or referenced shall not be represented as different bytes or silently substituted by same-named material. A local selection or response attempt alone shall not claim provider adoption, review, registration or outcome. The receiving contract shall describe the identity carried and observation available without inventing supplier fields or choosing an unresolved storage implementation. Basis: D row DEL-01-04; SOW-129; R and W; CLM-004 and CLM-006.
- **REQ-004** — The native draft receiving interface shall carry source-qualified draft identity and explicit transitions supplied by the workflow workspace. It shall display a new or changed workflow as a draft until actual reviewed registration is received, preserve the reviewed/supplied content identity, and expose collision or refusal standing supplied by that workspace without silent overwrite or rebind. It shall identify the local contribution and the complete workflow-making integration in CLM-004. Basis: SOW-129; D and W.
- **REQ-005** — Request, outcome and draft UI shall keep execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance as distinct acts with their own actor, subject and evidence supplied under CLM-005. Success or evidence of one act shall imply none of the others, and no synthetic rule shall require acceptance before another independently evidenced act can be shown. A proposal decision shall say “accept”; native tool permission shall retain its native semantics without becoming engineering approval. An actually performed human act may be faithfully displayed or recorded through the designated recording interface with decision actor distinct from recorder; neither absent evidence nor agent output shall fabricate that act or professional certification. Where bound content changes, display the lapsed standing supplied by the evidence interface. Basis: H, D row DEL-04-03; SOW-129 as qualified by the assigned PKG-04 interface.
- **REQ-006** — This slice shall perform no act owned by another deliverable: supplier pin selection, generated-type production and protocol qualification belong to App `DEL-01-01` in CLM-002; durable session/request custody, settlement and execution recovery belong to App `DEL-01-02` in CLM-003; plan/tool/delegation-view production belongs to App `DEL-01-03` in CLM-003; the complete workflow-making workspace, reviewed registration and catalog selection belong to App `DEL-02-02` in CLM-004; adopted-policy definition belongs to App `DEL-04-01` and durable act/run-record production belongs to App `DEL-04-03`, both in CLM-005. Unresolved policy decisions belong to the Owner with App/SWB contract owners, host act/receipt facilities to the host implementation owner, and actual human/professional acts to their person/accountable professional, as CLM-005 states. Native interaction, receiving and faithful presentation obligations remain here; this boundary does not prohibit recording an evidenced act through its owning interface. Basis: D; H; O.
- **REQ-007** — The receiving contract shall preserve required interactions independently of optional legacy UI reuse, record the relevant receiving obligations and candidate checks for any proposed reuse, and retain each applicable unresolved choice at its exact owner and point of need in TBD-001 through TBD-005. It shall not claim an adopted pin, fulfilled input, agreed common component or implemented capability solely from this definition. Basis: C; CLM-006; O.

- **AC-001** — The identified candidate renders native approvals/questions and routes grant, deny, answer and explicit decline with their actual meanings; supplied unknown-request errors remain explicit and neither silence nor observer closure becomes a response. Verify by VER-001. Grounds: REQ-001; N and R.
- **AC-002** — Outcome and recovered-view cases distinguish observation from execution, preserve outstanding requests, separate settlement/write attempts from acknowledgment, retain unknown outcomes, and do not turn primary completion into descendant completion. Verify by VER-002. Grounds: REQ-002; R.
- **AC-003** — Attachment and local draft-transition cases preserve actual supplied-content identity across selection, handoff and display, detect mismatched or same-named substituted content, and make no unsupported provider-adoption or outcome claim. Verify by VER-003. Grounds: REQ-003; D row DEL-01-04 and R.
- **AC-004** — The draft receiving contract and candidate surface preserve source-qualified content identity, distinguish draft from actual reviewed registration, and carry workspace collision/refusal results without silently overwriting or rebinding; the full workflow-making owner remains identified. Verify by VER-004. Grounds: REQ-004; W and CLM-004.
- **AC-005** — Act-presentation cases retain execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and professional reliance as separate evidenced subjects, label proposal decisions “accept”, show an actually performed human act faithfully with decision actor and recorder distinct, show received lapse after bound-content change, and reject fabricated acts or inferred professional standing. Verify by VER-006. Grounds: REQ-006, REQ-007; D, C and O.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

Produce this bounded native slice against the actual supplier/session interfaces and the workflow/evidence receiving contracts described in CLM-002 through CLM-005. Define receiving obligations first where inputs remain open; implement only when authorized and when the relevant actual input or decision exists. The App native-interaction owner coordinates interface readiness with the named owners; a defined contract is not supplied code or a satisfied runtime dependency. Avoid guessing wire fields, UI layout, attachment storage or state persistence. Optional UI reuse requires its receiving comparison and affected checks, not a mandatory port.

- **VER-001** — On an identified App candidate and selected native supplier basis, exercise approval grant and denial, question answer and explicit decline, supplied unknown-request error, silence and observer closure. Compare visible native semantics and routed response evidence with the session/request interface. Retain fixture inputs, rendered observations and response evidence; distinguish a sent attempt from received acknowledgment. Checks AC-001 only.
- **VER-002** — Drive the native presentation with observed turn outcomes, unavailable outcomes, outstanding requests, settlement with missing acknowledgment, observer close/reload/reopen and primary completion with an active descendant. Compare displayed state to the execution owner's available evidence and record unknowns. This verifies receiving/presentation behavior, while App DEL-01-02 retains durable recovery qualification and the joined witness retains its own evidence. Checks AC-002 only.
- **VER-003** — Follow distinguishable attachment contents and local draft content through selection, actual handoff and rendered reference. Include changed content and same-named different content; compare identity to the actual supplied material, including an unavailable provider-adoption observation. Record actual content identity and observation limits using the receiving contract's agreed representation. This method prescribes no hash algorithm, storage location or supplier field. Checks AC-003 only.
- **VER-004** — Inspect the receiving document with the workflow-experience integration interface and exercise draft, changed draft, reviewed/registered, collision/refusal and same-named source alternatives. Compare visible transitions and identities with the actual workflow-owner evidence; confirm no local transition invents review or registration and identify where the complete journey is verified. Checks AC-004 only.
- **VER-005** — Compare UI labels and attribution against the adopted PKG-04 interface. Use separately evidenced execution, proposal acceptance, checking, approval and reliance cases, including an independently evidenced act without a preceding acceptance record. Include a positive faithful-recording case: the person actually performs the act on identified content and a separate recorder preserves it; then a content-change/lapse case. Negative cases include success alone, missing human-act evidence and agent text claiming professional approval. Confirm none is promoted into another act or stronger standing. A concrete unresolved policy-dependent operation remains subject to its own OI-001/OI-002 ruling; test construction does not decide it. Checks AC-005 only.
- **VER-006** — Review the receiving document against D, C, H and O. Trace every REQ-006 exclusion one-for-one to CLM-002 through CLM-005 and the actual owner; verify local coverage, optional-reuse treatment and each distinct open point of need. Compare proposed shared placement or process division only against an actual recorded decision, reporting absence explicitly. Checks AC-006 only.
- **VER-007** — Inspect and run the delivered interaction fixture suite against its declared candidate/basis; trace the cases to AC-001 through AC-005 and their methods, preserving results and limitations. Report what was simulated, what was observed in the native App and what joined checks remain with integration owners. The fixtures substantiate this slice and do not qualify the full V4-EXM-10/V4-EXM-11 journeys alone. Checks AC-007 only.

Verification records identify the artifact revision, native supplier basis, supplied cases, actual observations and limits. These are future product checks, separate from schema/checklist validation of this contract. Project-level examination owns the complete candidate witness and the owner retains practitioner validation; an interaction fixture pass does not establish those acts.
