## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

The following describes later authorized production and qualification, not acts performed by this INIT assignment. Confirm the accepted receiving sources; resolve the two named choices at their stated points of need with their recorded owner; implement the stock boundary and generate from the selected supplier; qualify the candidate and retain the actual outcome/limits. A changed pin or affected interface requires the affected checks against the changed candidate. Tests realize the source-grounded criteria and cannot add scope. [D, I, A, C]

- **VER-001** — Inspect candidate stack/configuration, stock binary identity and process/protocol responsibility; use a recorded protocol exchange or controlled candidate fixture to demonstrate stdio handling and the explicit unknown-request error. Evidence names supplier/App candidate identities and observed outcome; durable recovery is a separate receiver's witness. Verifies AC-001.
- **VER-002** — Regenerate or compare the protocol types against the selected supplier's generation output, inspect the experimental supplement and check representative native field/item exchanges consumed by the seam. Bind output/provenance and fixture results to that candidate. Verifies AC-002.
- **VER-003** — Trace selected-version identification/check evidence and a representative native plan/revision exchange through the supplier contract to the named plan receiver; inspect the allocation of presentation and the still-optional registry/checker design. This verifies the local contribution without asserting the receiving UI witness passed. Verifies AC-003.
- **VER-004** — Review the responsibility/optional-reuse account against [A] fixed invariants, [I] OI-008, the accepted priority order and mainstream-stack constraint, and [K]'s outcome/means distinction. Record the actual decision source and any unresolved value; do not mark the completed-allocation criterion met while the choice is open. Verifies AC-004.
- **VER-005** — Compare the chosen Codex embedding behavior against its identified published protocol using recorded exchange fixtures and the applicable candidate witness; inspect local-provider requirements handed to the App account/provider owner against the selected protocol. Record unsupported/unobserved portions explicitly; native sign-in and server-substitution evidence is received from its own owner when needed. Verifies AC-005.
- **VER-006** — Review pin-selection evidence and run the selected-version check plus applicable recorded protocol regressions on the candidate; for a supplier upgrade compare the candidate's generated schema, experimental supplement and exchange outcomes with the prior qualified basis. Record the actual pin, candidate, checks and failures rather than assuming an upgrade from a version label. Prefer recorded exchanges for seam regressions, with live checks only where they establish a required fact. Verifies AC-006.
- **VER-007** — Perform a one-for-one boundary review of the enumerated acts against CLM-004 through CLM-006 and the accepted owner rows. Inspect candidate/qualification statements for any transfer of sibling/supplier/host work or unsupported human-act claim. Confirm that offering/recording/presenting an act is distinct from performing it and that one act's evidence is neither another act's proof nor an invented universal prerequisite. Verifies AC-007.
