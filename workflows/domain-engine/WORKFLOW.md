# domain-engine

Integrate a deterministic domain engine with explicit computational coverage and human judgment.

WORKING_ITEMS coordinates this undertaking and assigns bounded contributions to TASK.

## Method

1. Discover the engine and classify its integration level, protected paths, capabilities, and coverage gaps.
2. Develop the profile and adapter contract; distinguish engine-checkable propositions from premises requiring human judgment.
3. Validate the schema and bounded operation proposals against test evidence, then present adoption decisions.
4. Record the adopted profile and route domain operations through its approved paths, preserving proposal-versus-adopted status.
