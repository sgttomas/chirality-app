# audit-governance

Compare governance rules, references, terminology, and authority claims with their evidence.

TASK executes this bounded contribution and returns evidence to its caller.

## Method

1. Inventory the accepted governance suite, role registry, workflow inventory, and applicable tools.
2. Run count, reference, invariant, terminology, inventory, and hierarchy checks.
3. Apply the seventh pass to calibrate claim strength against its warrant and actual enforcement.
4. Emit schema-consistent findings with severity, category, IssueType, evidence, coverage, and recommendations.
