# audit-scope-closure

Follow an accepted amendment through implementation, reruns, and remaining closure obligations.

TASK executes this bounded contribution and returns evidence to its caller.

## Method

1. Bind each amendment action to its recorded implementation evidence and affected scope.
2. Verify downstream reruns against the amended input basis and matching scope, then inspect retired references, supersession, and KTY remediation.
3. Reconcile readiness claims with unresolved findings, human dispositions, and derivative status.
4. Return one closure verdict with severity and uncertainty recorded separately, plus precise rerun requirements.
