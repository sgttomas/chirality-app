# reconciliation

Reconcile claims, artifacts, implementation, evidence, lifecycle, and remaining work with accepted project truth.

WORKING_ITEMS coordinates this undertaking and assigns bounded contributions to TASK.

## Method

1. Freeze the accepted source basis and inventory the corpus and representation of each scope unit.
2. Plan bounded review waves and independently calibrate claims against their actual support.
3. Validate returns and synthesize cross-package discrepancies, routing decisions to the human and repairs to their authorized owners.
4. Backcheck repairs against frozen evidence and record closure, deferred derivatives, and remaining blockers.
