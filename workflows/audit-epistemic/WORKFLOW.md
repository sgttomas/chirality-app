# audit-epistemic

Inspect what claims assert, what warrants them, and what uncertainty or conflict affects reliance.

TASK executes this bounded contribution and returns evidence to its caller.

## Method

1. Identify claims within the declared production representation and distinguish claim, warrant, status, gap, conflict, and ruling.
2. Inspect label visibility, citation presence, substantive source support, lifecycle evidence, and cross-document consistency.
3. Keep indeterminate audit assessments separate from the four warrant states; document semantic judgments and coverage limits.
4. Return evidence-linked findings and recommendations without promoting claims or changing the audited content.
