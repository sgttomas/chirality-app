# review

Guide evidence review and human disposition for a deliverable lifecycle transition.

WORKING_ITEMS coordinates this undertaking and assigns bounded contributions to TASK.

## Method

1. Confirm the transition, current lifecycle state, accepted criteria, and exact source bindings.
2. Populate the checklist, capture findings, and obtain human dispositions through the five gates.
3. Recommend issuance only when every CRITICAL finding is resolved and MAJOR findings are resolved or explicitly deferred.
4. Record the lifecycle decision, finalize a new immutable review snapshot, and update only the authorized pointer.
